# Azura Dawnveil

Azura Dawnveil is [[Twizzle]]'s grandmother and the only known survivor of the attack on the [[Dawnveil Family]].

According to the confessions of [[Fuski and Springmaul]], Azura was last known to have been taken to [[Lord Zulpair]]. Her current location and condition are unknown.
