# Bashekk Ortallis

Bashekk Ortallis was a Zhentarim sellsword killed in the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]].

Under Speak with Dead, he said the [[Stone of Golorr]] was to be delivered to [[Gralhund Villa]], described it as an artifact that could make them rich as kings, and named [[Darkhold Castle]] as their main hideout. He identified [[Wern Malkrave]] and [[Urstul Floxin]] as associates.
