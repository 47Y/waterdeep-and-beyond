# Fala Legaliir

Fala Legaliir owns [[Corellon's Crown]].

During the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]], Fala was watering plants in the shop's second-floor greenhouse. Through the smoke, Fala saw a badly burned, cloaked man take something from [[Dalakhar]] and limp toward [[The Bent Nail]], looking behind him as though he were being followed.
