# Jezrynne Hornraven

Jezrynne Hornraven is a noblewoman who was leaving [[Tiger's Eye]] when the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]] occurred.

She reported seeing a puppet-like figure on a rooftop throw something into the crowd, causing the explosion. She witnessed halflings burn to death.
