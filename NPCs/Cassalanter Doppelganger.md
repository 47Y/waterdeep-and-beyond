# Cassalanter Doppelganger

An unidentified doppelganger posed as a well-dressed tiefling and served as head servant at [[Cassalanter Villa]].

He brought [[Lilith D Gluv]] into the villa after she claimed to be delivering the [[Stone of Golorr]], then confronted her in the cellar. Lilith killed him, causing him to revert to his true form.

[[Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[Ammalia Cassalanter]] later hid his body in one of several cells behind a secret door.
