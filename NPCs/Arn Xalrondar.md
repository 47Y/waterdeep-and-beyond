# Arn Xalrondar

Arn Xalrondar was captured at the [[Abandoned Windmill]] with [[Seffia Naelryke]].

Under questioning, Arn said he and Seffia had taken the [[Stone of Golorr]] as ordered and had killed [[Vaelle]], [[Holiver]], and [[Kaeth]] on [[Victoro Cassalanter]]'s orders. He identified the attacking creatures as spined devils serving Victoro.

Arn agreed to surrender to the City Watch. The outcome after his departure for the guard station is unknown.
