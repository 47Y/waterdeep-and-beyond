# Yagra

During the troll fight at [[The Yawning Portal]] in [[Session 1]], Yagra worked with [[Durnan]] and [[Lilith D Gluv]] to push the unconscious troll back into the well.

## Session 6

- Identified herself as a middle-ranking [[Zhentarim]] member.
- Denied knowing about the recent bank robberies attributed to the organization.
- Testified under Zone of Truth before [[Laeral Silverhand]] that the Zhentarim were not responsible for those robberies.
- Received Laeral's assurance of safety during the meeting.
