# Seffia Naelryke

Seffia Naelryke was captured at the [[Abandoned Windmill]] with [[Arn Xalrondar]].

## Information Provided

- Rumors connected the disappearance of the oldest Cassalanter child and danger to the other two children with a deal involving [[Asmodeus]].
- The [[Stone of Golorr]] was important to [[Victoro Cassalanter]].
- Cult leaders ordered the kidnapping of destitute people for sacrifice.
- Something significant was planned for [[Founders' Day]], then one tenday away.
- The Stone was a transformed creature that knew the location of the [[Vault of Dragons]].

Seffia surrendered to the City Watch and repeated substantially the same information she had given [[Dick Mixon]].
