# Cassalanter Children

According to rumors shared by [[Seffia Naelryke]], the oldest Cassalanter child disappeared several years ago.

The two remaining children are [[Elzerina Cassalanter]] and [[Terenzio Cassalanter]]. [[Ammalia Cassalanter]] confessed that if the family fails to deliver 999,999 gp and 99 souls to [[Asmodeus]], the twins' souls will be taken instead.

The [[Stone of Golorr]] said a toy unicorn was kept in the children's room at [[Cassalanter Villa]].
