# Terenzio Cassalanter

Terenzio Cassalanter is one of the Cassalanter twins and a child of [[Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[Ammalia Cassalanter]].

If his parents failed to pay [[Asmodeus]] 999,999 gp and 99 souls, his soul and [[Elzerina Cassalanter]]'s soul were to be taken instead. He was detained with the household during [[Session 6]].

During [[Session 7]], he was enrolled in [[Eilistraee]]'s church and received a coin bearing [[Dick Mixon]]'s sigil that could secure an audience with him. On [[Founders' Day]], he appeared possessed during an apparent supernatural attack. The party carried the twins to the [[Temple of Eilistraee]], where Eilistraee saved their souls from Asmodeus.
