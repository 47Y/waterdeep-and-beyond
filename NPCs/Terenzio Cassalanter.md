# Terenzio Cassalanter

Terenzio Cassalanter is one of the Cassalanter twins and a child of [[Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[Ammalia Cassalanter]].

If his parents fail to pay [[Asmodeus]] 999,999 gp and 99 souls, his soul and [[Elzerina Cassalanter]]'s soul will be taken instead. He was detained with the household during [[Session 6]], and the party intends to find a way to protect him.
