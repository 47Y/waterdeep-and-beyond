# Seris Vaelith

Seris Vaelith is [[Cordan]]'s younger sister.

- More socially graceful and conventional than Cordan.
- Close with Cordan despite their differences.
- Interested in Cordan's travels.
- Probably knows more about Cordan than anyone else.
- Still lives in [[Astraelith]].
