# Dalakhar

Dalakhar was a gnome killed in the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]]. Dried waste on his boots suggested he had recently traveled through the sewers.

## Speak with Dead

- Suspected the [[Zhentarim]] had killed him.
- Had been unable to leave Waterdeep.
- Was coming to [[Trollskull Manor]] to entrust the [[Stone of Golorr]] to the party after hearing that they had saved the Open Lord's son.
- Had stolen the Stone from the underground lair of [[Xanathar]].
