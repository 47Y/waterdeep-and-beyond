# Spectral Bartender

[[Twizzle]] discovered a spectral bartender in [[Trollskull Manor]] using Divine Sense during [[Session 1]]. The party described it as a poltergeist in [[Session 2]].

[[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]] received permission to study it for his upcoming book in exchange for crediting the party and providing an advance copy.
