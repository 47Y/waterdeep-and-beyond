# Hlam

Hlam is a monk who lives in a cave on the side of [[Mount Waterdeep]].

## Session 2

- Shared the warning: "Evil's twin hides its face for now, expect that to change before winter's end."
- Offered the party water.
- Told [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] that he was welcome to return.
- Tathlynd later returned with books and tea as gifts.

## Session 7

- Sent Tathlynd a letter asking the party to help a stranded choirmaster named [[Gwendolan]].
- Received Tathlynd after the party completed [[Recover Gwendolan's Campestri]], when Tathlynd returned with tea, books, and news of the choirmaster.
