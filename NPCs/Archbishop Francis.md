# Archbishop Francis

Archbishop Francis is associated with [[Waukeen]]. [[Dick Mixon]] approached him while seeking divine help for the [[Cassalanter Children|Cassalanter twins]]. Francis relayed that Waukeen had no interest because money was not involved and had little power in the situation. Dick then spoke with him about money.

The exact spelling of his name, title, and institutional position have not been independently confirmed.
