# Floon Blagmaar

[[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]] hired the party to find Floon during [[Session 1]]. The party traced him through the Dock Ward, found him in the [[Xanathar Hideout]], and returned him safely to Volo at [[The Yawning Portal]].
