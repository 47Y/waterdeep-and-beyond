# Wentworth

Wentworth is [[Dick Mixon]]'s butler.

He informed Dick of a string of bank robberies being attributed to the [[Zhentarim]], though Wentworth believed the organization might not actually be responsible.

At Dick's request, Wentworth obtained an [[Amulet of Asmodeus]] overnight for the meeting with [[Victoro Cassalanter]]. It was made in the same or a similar style as the Cassalanter amulets but did not come from their villa.
