# Elyndra Moonbough

Elyndra Moonbough is a dryad connected to the [[Starroot]].

## Details

- Inhabits and guards the Starroot near [[Astraelith]].
- Knew [[Cordan]] before he traveled to the Material Plane.
- Could not leave the Starroot to confront the fomorian who trapped a fallen star fragment in its roots.
- Worked with Cordan to guide the star fragment back into the heavens.
- Gave Cordan the [[Fey Branch]] after the Starroot bloomed.

## Quote

"Carry a piece of the Starroot, and wherever you walk, the heavens will remember the way."
