# Volothamp Geddarm

Known to the party as Volo.

## Session 1

- Hired the party to find [[Floon Blagmaar]].
- Offered the deed to [[Trollskull Manor]] after Floon was rescued.
- Agreed to pay the deed transfer fee after negotiating with [[Dick Mixon]].

## Session 2

- Accompanied the party to collect the deed and paid the 25 gp transfer fee.
- Discussed his upcoming book over drinks at [[The Yawning Portal]].
- Received permission to study the [[Spectral Bartender|poltergeist]] in [[Trollskull Manor]].
- Agreed to credit the party in the book and provide an advance copy.

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “Chapter 9: Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion”; related sections where noted).

- Author of *Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion*, a visitor's guide to [[Waterdeep]] covering its history, laws, wards, landmarks, customs, and celebrations.
- In the introduction, he promotes a planned updated edition of his out-of-print *Volo's Guide to Waterdeep*.
- His opinions, anecdotes, and reported rumors are his published account, rather than events witnessed by the party.
