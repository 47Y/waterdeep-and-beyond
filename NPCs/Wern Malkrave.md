# Wern Malkrave

Wern Malkrave was a Zhentarim sellsword killed in the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]].

Under Speak with Dead, he said the [[Stone of Golorr]] was to be delivered to [[Gralhund Villa]] and would make them rich. He named [[Bashekk Ortallis]] and [[Urstul Floxin]] as associates. He said the main hideout was in the sewers, contradicting Bashekk's claim that it was [[Darkhold Castle]].

Wern did not know who threw the fireball.
