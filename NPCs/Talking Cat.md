# Talking Cat

A cat approached [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] and spoke to him, offering a job and directing him to [[Phaulkonmere]]. It was a familiar or other spell effect used by [[Melannor Fellbranch]], and its message led to the Emerald Enclave's [[Scarecrow Trouble]] assignment.

During [[Session 6]], a cat messenger associated with the [[Emerald Enclave]] brought Tathlynd the [[Stop the Grells]] assignment. It is not confirmed whether this was the same manifestation.
