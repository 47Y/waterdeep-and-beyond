# Saeth Cromley

Saeth Cromley is a member of the City Watch.

## Details

- Sent [[Joe Bauers]] to meet the party at [[The Yawning Portal]] before [[Session 1]].
- Declined to tell Joe whether fireballs had been used in other attacks because the party was near the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]] and therefore treated as suspect by default.
- Serves as [[Joe Bauers]]' supervisor in the City Watch.
- Suggested that Joe retire from regular Watch service and instead perform contract work when needed.
- Prepared paperwork for Joe to retire within a week and informed him that his uniform, guard-summoning horn, and other Watch equipment must be returned.
- Directed Joe to another, unnamed supervisor when asked about [[Fuski and Springmaul]].
