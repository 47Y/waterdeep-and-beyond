# Dirlem

Dirlem is an ancient dragon connected to [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]]'s past.

## Details

- Killed the drow pursuing Tathlynd after he deserted.
- Spared Tathlynd after sensing he was unlike most drow Dirlem had met.
- Offered Tathlynd a chance to leave his old life behind.
- Took Tathlynd to an elven settlement and vouched for him.
- Later checked in on Tathlynd from time to time.
