# Lord Zulpair

Lord Zulpair is a Waterdavian noble whose family operates a merchant shipping company. The business is profitable through most of the year but less so during winter.

[[Twizzle]]'s downtime investigation identified Zulpair as the person responsible for destroying her home and attacking her family.

## Meeting at The Black Key

Zulpair approached [[Dick Mixon]] during [[The Black Key]]'s grand opening and expressed interest in shipping agreements. He said other obligations had kept him from Dick's investiture ceremony. Dick interpreted this as evidence that Zulpair might be one of Waterdeep's concealed Lords, but this is unconfirmed.

When Dick asked whether the shipping business was entirely above board, Zulpair said that it was beyond reproach. He agreed to meet Dick at [[Dick's Manor]] at first light and was asked to bring the previous year's shipping ledgers.
