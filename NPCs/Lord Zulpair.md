# Lord Zulpair

Lord Zulpair is a Waterdavian noble whose family operates a merchant shipping company. The business is profitable through most of the year but less so during winter.

[[Twizzle]]'s downtime investigation identified Zulpair as the person responsible for destroying her home and attacking her family.

## Meeting at The Black Key

Zulpair approached [[Dick Mixon]] during [[The Black Key]]'s grand opening and expressed interest in shipping agreements. He said other obligations had kept him from Dick's investiture ceremony. Dick interpreted this as evidence that Zulpair might be one of Waterdeep's concealed Lords; Zulpair later confirmed this during his meeting with Dick.

When Dick asked whether the shipping business was entirely above board, Zulpair said that it was beyond reproach. He agreed to meet Dick at [[Dick's Manor]] at first light and was asked to bring the previous year's shipping ledgers.

## Session 9

- Brought several boxes of [[Zulpair Business Records|business records]] to Dick's Manor with a guard and butler.
- Said his family worships [[Umberlee]] primarily for ancestral reasons.
- Described the [[Dawnveil Family]] as pirates disguised as merchants and admitted participating in the operation against them.
- Claimed the family was killed during an attempted capture and agreed to provide the [[Dawnveil Investigation Papers|investigation papers]].
- Was contradicted by the confessions of [[Fuski and Springmaul]], who said Zulpair paid guards to provoke the confrontation and that the Dawnveils were legitimate merchants.
- Purchased [[Skyhorn]] for much less than its expected value.
- Confirmed to Dick that he is one of Waterdeep's Secret, or [[Lords of Waterdeep|Masked]], Lords.
