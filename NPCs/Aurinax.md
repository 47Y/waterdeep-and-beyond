# Aurinax

Aurinax is an adult gold dragon who guarded the gold in the [[Vault of Dragons]] while disguised as an elderly dwarf.

## Session 5

- Guarded the hoard on behalf of [[Dagult Neverember]].
- Carried the [[Dragonstaff of Ahghairon]] as payment for his service.
- Willingly submitted to [[Dick Mixon]]'s Zone of Truth.
- Suspected that Neverember had embezzled the gold from Waterdeep.
- Agreed to release the 500,000 gp hoard and thirty gemstones worth 100 gp each after Dick promised to return the money to the city.
