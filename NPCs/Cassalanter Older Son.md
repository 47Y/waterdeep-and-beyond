# Cassalanter Older Son

Earlier rumors said the oldest Cassalanter child had disappeared several years ago. During [[Session 7]], a privately handled continuity addition established that a chain devil was chained in the attic of [[Cassalanter Villa]] and was the missing older son.

The session notes record his name as “El Waldo.” The spelling and whether he was transformed into the chain devil or connected to it in another way remain unconfirmed.
