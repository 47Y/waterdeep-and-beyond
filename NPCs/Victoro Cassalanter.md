# Victoro Cassalanter

Victoro Cassalanter resides at [[Cassalanter Villa]].

## Observed Actions

- Attacked and helped capture [[Lilith D Gluv]] after her infiltration was discovered.
- Decided to turn Lilith over to the City Watch.
- With [[Ammalia Cassalanter]], concealed a dead [[Cassalanter Doppelganger|doppelganger]] in a cell behind a secret door.
- Put on a red robe and devil mask before joining a ritual in an underground chamber resembling a temple to [[Asmodeus]].
- Ended the ritual by declaring, "Founder's Day approaches."
- Was named by captured cultists as a leader who ordered the kidnapping of destitute people for sacrifice.
- Ordered [[Arn Xalrondar]] and [[Seffia Naelryke]] to kill [[Vaelle]], [[Holiver]], and [[Kaeth]] as a security measure.
- Was said to command spined devils and to urgently want the [[Stone of Golorr]].

## Confession and Arrest

During a recorded meeting at [[Dick's Manor]], Victoro confessed that:

- The family had sacrificed peasants to [[Asmodeus]] for years.
- The final payment due on [[Founders' Day]] was 999,999 gp and 99 souls.
- The souls were the true prize.
- He believed the [[Stone of Golorr]] would enable him to fulfill the bargain.

He swore by Asmodeus that receiving the Stone would let him honor the contract. [[Laeral Silverhand]] then entered, and [[Joe Bauers]] placed him in manacles. Victoro was taken into custody without further resistance.
