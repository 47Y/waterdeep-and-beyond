# Laeral Silverhand

Laeral Silverhand was the Open Lord of Waterdeep through [[Session 6]]. After [[Dick Mixon]] interviewed with the [[Lords of Waterdeep]] and became Open Lord at the end of [[Session 7]], Laeral became an advisor. No other role was disclosed to the players.

## Session 6

- Met the party at [[Castle Waterdeep]].
- Accepted a 250,000 gp banknote representing the first portion of the 260,000 gp allocated for return from funds embezzled by [[Dagult Neverember]].
- Heard evidence regarding the Cassalanters' relationship with [[Asmodeus]].
- Guaranteed [[Yagra]] safe testimony under Zone of Truth.
- Participated in the hidden observation of [[Dick Mixon]]'s meeting with [[Victoro Cassalanter]].
- Ordered Victoro and the Cassalanter household detained after his confession.
- Showed genuine interest in [[Cordan]]'s Feywild star map and the possibility of creating equivalent Material Plane star-map crystals.

Dick directly told Laeral that he wanted to become the next Open Lord and believed he could perform the role better because he actively wanted it.

## Session 7

- Helped arrange a meeting involving [[Elzerina Cassalanter]], [[Terenzio Cassalanter]], and [[Ammalia Cassalanter]] while the party sought a way to protect the twins.
- Was described as helping where she could with the effort to save the children.
- Worked with [[Cordan]] at various points during the six-month downtime to discuss and study the stars.
- Became an advisor after Dick's appointment as Open Lord; no other role was disclosed to the players.
