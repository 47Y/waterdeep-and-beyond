# Osvaldo Cassalanter

Osvaldo Cassalanter is the oldest child of [[Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[Ammalia Cassalanter]]. Earlier reports said that he had disappeared several years ago.

During [[Session 7]], a privately handled continuity addition established that Osvaldo had been transformed into a chain devil and chained in the attic of [[Cassalanter Villa]].
