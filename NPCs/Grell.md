# Grell

The [[Xanathar Guild]] released grells in Waterdeep as part of an effort to distract the City Watch and City Guard.

During [[Session 6]], the party tracked droppings to an alley in the Dock Ward and killed two grells. [[Cordan]] dissected one and discovered that grells produce paralytic poison after being exposed to it himself.
