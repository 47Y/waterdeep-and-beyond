# Durnan

During the troll fight at [[The Yawning Portal]] in [[Session 1]], Durnan worked with [[Yagra]] and [[Lilith D Gluv]] to push the unconscious troll back into the well.

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “Castle Ward”; related sections where noted).

- Proprietor of [[The Yawning Portal]].
- According to Volo, Durnan and Mirt voluntarily explored Undermountain and returned with treasure, using magic to extend their lives.
- Durnan built the tavern over the Well and has been charging for descents nearly two centuries later, as of the guide's account.
