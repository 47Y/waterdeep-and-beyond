# Elzerina Cassalanter

Elzerina Cassalanter is one of the Cassalanter twins and a child of [[Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[Ammalia Cassalanter]].

If her parents fail to pay [[Asmodeus]] 999,999 gp and 99 souls, her soul and [[Terenzio Cassalanter]]'s soul will be taken instead. She was detained with the household during [[Session 6]], and the party intends to find a way to protect her.
