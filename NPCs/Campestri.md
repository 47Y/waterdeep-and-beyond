# Campestri

Campestri are sentient mushroom creatures encountered during [[Recover Gwendolan's Campestri]]. Dick's Identify spell established their species and found that they were not under any spells.

The eight missing Campestri were members of [[Gwendolan]]'s choir and were not slaves. They traveled in four singing pairs and communicated by repeating words in song. The first rescued pair wanted to return to Gwendolan and appeared happy after doing so.
