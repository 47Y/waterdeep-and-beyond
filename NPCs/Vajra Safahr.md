# Vajra Safahr

Vajra Safahr is the Blackstaff and is based at [[Blackstaff Tower]].

## Party Interactions

- Mentally contacted [[Joe Bauers]] and summoned the party to Blackstaff Tower.
- Invited the party to join the [[Gray Hands]].
- Assigned the party to [[Consult Hlam]].
- Agreed to keep [[Twizzle]] informed if she learned anything relevant to corruption in the guard.
- Told Twizzle that Waterdeep did not have a consistently reliable magic-item shop.
- Directed [[Lilith D Gluv]] to a secretary for information about [[Damien L Gluv]].

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “The Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors”; related sections where noted).

- The guide identifies the Blackstaff as head of the Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors.
- The order encourages resident arcane spellcasters to join and calls on members to assist with investigations, city defense, fires, and other disasters.
