# Fireball at Trollskull Manor

A Fireball spell detonated outside [[Trollskull Manor]] during [[Session 3]]. The dead included [[Dalakhar]], halflings, and people bearing [[Zhentarim]] tattoos.

## Evidence

- Dalakhar had dried waste on his boots, suggesting he had recently been in the sewers.
- [[Dick Mixon]] found a broken [[Necklace of Fireballs]] with two remaining beads in a rain barrel.
- [[Twizzle]] found five high-quality gems worth 100 gp each, for a total of 500 gp.
- [[Fala Legaliir]] saw a badly burned, cloaked man take something from Dalakhar and limp toward [[The Bent Nail]].
- [[Jezrynne Hornraven]] saw a puppet-like figure on a rooftop throw something into the crowd immediately before the explosion.
- The rooftop figure resembled an automaton from the [[Day of Wonders]] parade supplied by the [[Temple of Gond]].

## Investigation

- [[Barnabus Blastwind]] took charge of the official investigation.
- Dick surrendered the necklace and gems to the authorities.
- Speak with Dead revealed that Dalakhar had intended to bring the [[Stone of Golorr]] to the party.
- Two dead Zhentarim sellswords said the Stone was to be taken to [[Gralhund Villa]].
- The sellswords gave conflicting locations for their main hideout: [[Darkhold Castle]] and the sewers.
