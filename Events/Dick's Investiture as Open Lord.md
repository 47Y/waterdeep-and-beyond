# Dick's Investiture as Open Lord

At the [[Palace of Waterdeep]], [[Laeral Silverhand]], the previous Open Lord, introduced [[Dick Mixon]] before Waterdeep's assembled nobles. Dick used Disguise Self to appear slightly thinner and without visible blemishes, knelt, and placed the [[Crown of Silver Tongues]] on his head.

Dick promised to make [[Waterdeep]] a major center of commerce and trade and to spread the resulting prosperity throughout the population. Nobles gave him unspecified gifts. He gave each of his five companions an [[Earring of Message]] and two [[Clockwork Amulet|Clockwork Amulets]], with additional individual gifts that included a [[Survival Mantle]] for [[Cordan]]. Joe's additional gift remains unidentified.

Afterward, the attendees were encouraged to join a procession to the grand opening of [[The Black Key]].
