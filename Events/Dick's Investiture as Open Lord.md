# Dick's Investiture as Open Lord

[[Laeral Silverhand]], the previous Open Lord, introduced [[Dick Mixon]] before Waterdeep's assembled nobles. Dick used Disguise Self to appear slightly thinner and without visible blemishes, knelt, and placed the [[Crown of Silver Tongues]] on his head.

Dick promised to make [[Waterdeep]] a major center of commerce and trade and to spread the resulting prosperity throughout the population. Nobles gave him unspecified gifts, and he gave unspecified gifts to each member of the [[Party]].

Afterward, the attendees were encouraged to join a procession to the grand opening of [[The Black Key]].
