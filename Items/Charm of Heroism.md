# Charm of Heroism

[[Melannor Fellbranch]] gave one Charm of Heroism to each party member belonging to the [[Emerald Enclave]] after the grell mission in [[Session 6]].

As an action, a bearer can expend the charm to gain the benefits of a Potion of Heroism once.

Recipients:

- [[Cordan]]
- [[Joe Bauers]]
- [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]]
- [[Twizzle]]
