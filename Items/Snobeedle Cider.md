# Snobeedle Cider

A halfling farming family offered the party cider after the [[Scarecrow Trouble]] assignment. The party liked it and proposed that the family supply cider and hard cider for the tavern at [[Trollskull Manor]].

[[Dick Mixon]] plans to negotiate exclusive selling rights. No agreement has yet been recorded.

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “Undercliff”; related sections where noted).

- Volo recommends the **Snobeedle Orchard and Meadery** in Undercliff, east of [[Waterdeep]], owned and run by the Snobeedle halflings.
- It has a drinking hall, a shop accommodating larger visitors, and seasonal pick-your-own fruit.
- The guide supplies public background on the Snobeedle business; it does not establish a supply agreement with [[Trollskull Manor]].
