# Crown of Silver Tongues

The Crown of Silver Tongues is the crown worn by [[Dick Mixon]] during [[Dick's Investiture as Open Lord]]. It is made of orichalcum and set with purple, blue, and red gemstones.

Its magical properties, if any, and the obligations associated with wearing it are unknown.
