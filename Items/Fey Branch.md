# Fey Branch

The Fey Branch is [[Cordan]]'s living staff.

## Origin

- It came from the [[Starroot]] after Cordan and [[Elyndra Moonbough]] guided a fallen star fragment back into the heavens.
- Elyndra gave it to Cordan as a reward.

## Details

- Remains alive and connected to the Feywild.
- Leaves change with Cordan's Eladrin season.
- On clear nights, its markings rearrange into unfamiliar constellations.
- Those constellations may reveal omens of events yet to come.
