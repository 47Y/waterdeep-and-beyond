# Dawnveil Investigation Papers

[[Lord Zulpair]] sent these papers to [[Dick's Manor]] after being questioned about the [[Dawnveil Family]].

The papers listed Twizzle's murdered relatives and contained circumstantial accusations that the Dawnveils were pirates, but no concrete evidence. [[Twizzle]] said most of the account was false.

The list of guards in the paperwork matched the names provided during the confessions of [[Fuski and Springmaul]].
