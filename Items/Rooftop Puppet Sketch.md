# Rooftop Puppet Sketch

The party received a copy of a sketch artist's depiction of the puppet-like rooftop figure seen by [[Jezrynne Hornraven]] during the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]].

Its closest resemblance was an automaton supplied by the [[Temple of Gond]] for the [[Day of Wonders]] parade.
