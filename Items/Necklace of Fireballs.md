# Necklace of Fireballs

[[Dick Mixon]] found this necklace in a rain barrel after the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]]. It had a broken clasp and two remaining orange beads.

Dick initially concealed it in [[Dick's Magical Jacket]]. [[Twizzle]] later recognized it as a Necklace of Fireballs, after which Dick surrendered it to the authorities as evidence.
