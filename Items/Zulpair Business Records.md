# Zulpair Business Records

[[Lord Zulpair]] brought several boxes of business records to [[Dick's Manor]]. Zulpair's butler remained with them so they could be returned when the review ended.

[[Dick's House Wizard]] began memorizing and reproducing the books, a process expected to take several days.

The records contained multiple references to [[Skyhorn]], a functional lighthouse Zulpair purchased for much less than its expected value. Further findings remain pending.
