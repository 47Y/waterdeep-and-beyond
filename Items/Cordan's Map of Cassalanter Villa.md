# Cordan's Map of Cassalanter Villa

[[Cordan]] completed a map of the portions of [[Cassalanter Villa]] he explored in spider form during [[Session 4]].

He used Eladrin Trance to gain temporary proficiency with cartographer's tools before drawing it the following morning.

Cordan provided the map to [[Laeral Silverhand]]. City guards later used it to sweep the hidden cellar and temple beneath Cassalanter Villa.
