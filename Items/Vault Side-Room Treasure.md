# Vault Side-Room Treasure

Recovered from a secret room in the [[Vault of Dragons]].

- Five copper urns worth 25 gp each.
- Five tourmalines worth 100 gp each.
- 200 cp.
- [[Ring of Warmth]].
- Ten ordinary gold rings worth 25 gp each.
- 650 sp.
- 250 gp.
- Thirty-three blue quartz gemstones worth 10 gp each.
- A nine-inch silver statuette of a dwarf priest of Moradin with amethyst eyes, worth 250 gp and weighing 10 pounds.

The party initially took everything except the urns and copper, then collected those later. The final division or storage of this treasure was not recorded.
