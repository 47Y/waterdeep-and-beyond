# Dick's Magical Jacket

[[Dick Mixon]] owns a magical jacket described as a modified version of a Handy Haversack.

After the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]], he secretly placed the recovered [[Necklace of Fireballs]] inside it before later surrendering the necklace to the authorities.
