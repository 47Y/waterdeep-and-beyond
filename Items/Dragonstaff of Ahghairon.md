# Dragonstaff of Ahghairon

[[Aurinax]] received the Dragonstaff of Ahghairon from [[Dagult Neverember]] as payment for guarding the [[Vault of Dragons]].

Among its abilities, the staff permits Aurinax to move freely within Waterdeep despite the city's ward against dragons. Aurinax still held the staff at the end of [[Session 5]].
