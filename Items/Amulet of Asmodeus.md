# Amulet of Asmodeus

[[Wentworth]] obtained this amulet overnight at [[Dick Mixon]]'s request. It was created in the same or a similar style as the Cassalanter amulets but was not taken from [[Cassalanter Villa]].

Dick displayed it while deceiving [[Victoro Cassalanter]] into confessing during [[Session 6]].
