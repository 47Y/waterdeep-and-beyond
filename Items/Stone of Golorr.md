# Stone of Golorr

## Known History

- [[Dalakhar]] said he stole it from [[Xanathar]]'s lair deep beneath Waterdeep.
- Dalakhar intended to entrust it to the party at [[Trollskull Manor]].
- A badly burned, cloaked figure took something from Dalakhar immediately after the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]].
- The dead Zhentarim sellswords said they had obtained the Stone and were meant to take it to [[Gralhund Villa]].
- [[Bashekk Ortallis]] called it an artifact that could make them rich as kings.
- [[Yalah Gralhund]] claimed it had already been sent to its next owners, the Cassalanters.
- The party attempted to intercept it at [[Cassalanter Villa]] but did not find it.
