# Sunbeam Tea

- Cost: 5 gp.
- Effect: adds 1d4 radiant damage to unarmed attacks for 24 hours.
- Drawback: the drinker gains one level of exhaustion afterward.

[[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] bought the tea before returning to visit [[Hlam]] during the downtime after [[Session 2]].
