# Old Xoblob Curios

[[Dick Mixon]] paid 10 gp at [[Old Xoblob Shop]] for a toy unicorn and the following unusual items.

## Shirt of Throwing

A simple green shirt sized for an average halfling, gnome, or dwarf. When worn, the wearer can be used as a thrown missile, with the throwing range increased by 50 feet.

## Flying Pan

Hovers above a fire just high enough to avoid heating up.

## Pipe of Intellectual Looks

Makes its holder appear thoughtful and clever, granting advantage on Bluff checks made to look smart. It does not improve the holder's behavior, speech, or intelligence, and creatures that have heard the holder speak are immune to the effect.

## Rod of Wander

Causes its owner to have no sense of direction.

## Mantle of Awe

Entering a room while carrying the mantle produces doves, fanfare, and an angelic chorus. The effect becomes grander in larger rooms and activates every time the bearer enters any room.

## Gauntlets of the Mighty Arms of the Dinosaurs

The wearer gains:

- An enlarged, tooth-filled mouth with a bite attack dealing 1d12 damage.
- A spiked tail attack usable as a bonus action for 1d8 damage.
- Scales granting natural AC 15.
- +4 on Intimidate checks.

The wearer also suffers:

- Cannot wear armor, helmets, backpacks, capes, or cloaks.
- Arms become tiny and weak with effective Strength 3; hands cannot carry anything or use shields.
- Charisma is halved, rounded down.
- Intelligence is halved, rounded down.
- Cannot speak or cast spells with verbal components; can only roar loudly.
