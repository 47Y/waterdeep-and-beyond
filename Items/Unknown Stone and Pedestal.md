# Unknown Stone and Pedestal

During the fight in the [[Xanathar Hideout]] in [[Session 1]], a mind flayer placed a stone on a pedestal and teleported away.

After the fight, [[Dick Mixon]] ritually cast Identify on the pedestal and stone. He learned that the two combine to teleport one individual to an unknown destination.

Dick kept the stone. The pedestal remained attached to the floor in the Xanathar Hideout.
