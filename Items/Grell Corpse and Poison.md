# Grell Corpse and Poison

[[Cordan]] retained and dissected one of two grell corpses recovered in the Dock Ward during [[Session 6]].

He discovered through accidental exposure that grells produce paralytic poison. He plans to attempt collecting some of the poison on the following night, but that collection has not yet happened.
