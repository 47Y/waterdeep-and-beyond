# Grell Corpse and Poison

[[Cordan]] retained and dissected one of two grell corpses recovered in the Dock Ward during [[Session 6]].

He discovered through accidental exposure that grells produce paralytic poison. His first collection attempt failed, but he learned how to handle the poison properly in the future.
