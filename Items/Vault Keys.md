# Vault Keys

The [[Stone of Golorr]] identified three keys required to open the [[Vault of Dragons]]:

- A silvered warhammer. The party used a decorative one made by [[Steam and Steel]].
- Sunlight. The party redirected sunrise through twenty steel mirrors.
- A unicorn. A toy unicorn purchased at [[Old Xoblob Shop]] was accepted.
