# No-Hands Pretzel

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A row of giant pretzels dangles on strings from a pole. Hands behind your back. Catch it in your teeth, chew through all that dough, and finish the last bite without it falling to the floor. The crowd times you with a big brass hourglass.*

**Description:** A weekly eating race. A big soft pretzel hangs on a string at face height, and contestants eat it **without using their hands**. There are **three stages**: catching it, eating through it, and finishing the last bite. **Every attempt takes 15 seconds**, and a failed attempt means trying again. The fastest time wins.

## How to Play

### The stages

| Stage | What you're doing | Check | DC |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Catch the swinging pretzel in your teeth | Dexterity check | 10 |
| 2 | Eat through the dough without stopping | Constitution check | 14 |
| 3 | Finish the last bite before it drops | Dexterity (Acrobatics) | 18 |

### Step by step

1. Start at stage 1.
2. **Each attempt takes 15 seconds.** Make the stage's check.
   - **Success:** move on to the next stage.
   - **Failure:** try that stage again.
3. Finishing stage 3 stops the clock. The fastest possible time is **45 seconds**.

### Winning

- The fastest time wins.
- **Ties: a pretzel-off.** The tied eaters each make one more stage 3 check. The highest total wins.

## Dice Examples

**Eaters:** Quill (PC, Dex +3, Con +1, Acrobatics +5) and "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Dex +0, Con +4, Acrobatics +0).

**Quill**
- Stage 1 (DC 10): **14** + 3 = **17**. ✓ (15 seconds)
- Stage 2 (DC 14): **9** + 1 = **10**. ✗ (30 seconds). Again: **16** + 1 = **17**. ✓ (45 seconds)
- Stage 3 (DC 18): **11** + 5 = **16**. ✗ (60 seconds). Again: **15** + 5 = **20**. ✓ (75 seconds)
- **Quill: 75 seconds.**

**Oswin**
- Stage 1 (DC 10): **7** + 0 = **7**. ✗ (15 seconds). Again: **12** + 0 = **12**. ✓ (30 seconds)
- Stage 2 (DC 14): **13** + 4 = **17**. ✓ (45 seconds)
- Stage 3 (DC 18): **18** + 0 = **18**. ✓ (60 seconds)
- **Oswin: 60 seconds. Oswin wins.**

**Edge case: a tie**
- If both had finished in 60 seconds, each would make one more stage 3 check. Quill (+5) would be the favourite.

- **Entry:** 5 sp per eater (the pretzel is included).
- **Winner:** 3 gp and a **free pretzel every visit** for a month.
- **The House keeps:** the rest of the entries and a great deal of mustard and beer.

| Bonus | Stage 1 | Stage 2 | Stage 3 | Perfect 45 seconds | Average time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +2 | 65% | 45% | 25% | 7% | about 2 minutes |

**Stage 3 decides most races.** A clumsy eater can be stuck there for a minute or more.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any chewing technique, any amount of mess.
- Hands behind your back, or tied there with a ribbon if you don't trust yourself.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *mage hand*, *guidance*, *enhance ability* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Touching the pretzel with your hands, a shorter string, or a partly cut pretzel.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steady the pretzel with your chin and shoulder | Eater | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on stage 3 |
| Bribe the baker for a pre-cut pretzel (2 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Advantage on stage 2 |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Pass one stage automatically |
| Swing a rival's string | Anyone nearby | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's next attempt has disadvantage |

### Cheating dice example

On stage 3, Quill pins the pretzel against his shoulder.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **5** + 7 = **12**. The Watcher: **11** + 5 = **16**. **Caught.**
- Quill is disqualified, banned for 3 months, and pinned to the notice board. The sketch shows him with pretzel crumbs on his collar. To clear his name: 5 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The baker, Old Kringle,** bakes the pretzels with a secret ingredient that makes them stretchier every week.
- **A visiting ogre** holds the record of 45 seconds. He ate the string too.
