# Silent Toast

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Draw a card, read the phrase, and act it out for your team without a word or a sound. "Last orders!" "Who spilled my ale?" "The goblin owes me money!" The quicker they get it, the more points you score.*

**Description:** A daily miming game for two teams. Each player takes a turn as the **mimer**: draw a tavern phrase and act it out in silence. The mimer gets **three tries**, each harder than the last. **The sooner the team guesses it, the more points it scores.** The losing team buys the round, and everyone drinks a toast in silence.

## How to Play

### A mime, step by step

1. **Draw a phrase card.** Show it to the other team, but not your own.
2. **Mime it.** No speaking, no sounds, no pointing at the card. Make a **Charisma (Performance)** check:

   | Try | DC | Points if your team gets it |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | 12 | 3 |
   | 2 | 15 | 2 |
   | 3 | 18 | 1 |

   - **Success:** your team guesses it. Score the points.
   - **Failure:** your team guesses wrong. Go on to the next try. (It gets harder because they've latched on to the wrong idea.)
   - **Three failures:** 0 points.
3. **The teams take turns** until every player has mimed once.

### Winning

- **The highest team total wins.**
- **Ties:** each team's best mimer goes once more. The higher score wins; if still tied, call it a draw.

## Dice Examples

**Team 1:** Mirela (PC, Performance +7) and Thokk (NPC, Performance −1).
**Team 2:** Lark Thistlewhite (NPC, Performance +6) and Quill (PC, Performance +1).

| Mimer | Phrase | Try 1 (DC 12) | Try 2 (DC 15) | Try 3 (DC 18) | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mirela | "Last orders!" | **14** + 7 = 21 ✓ | | | 3 |
| Lark | "Who spilled my ale?" | **4** + 6 = 10 ✗ | **11** + 6 = 17 ✓ | | 2 |
| Thokk | "The goblin owes me money!" | **9** − 1 = 8 ✗ | **15** − 1 = 14 ✗ | **19** − 1 = 18 ✓ | 1 |
| Quill | "My horse is drunk." | **12** + 1 = 13 ✓ | | | 3 |

**Team 1: 3 + 1 = 4. Team 2: 2 + 3 = 5. Team 2 wins.** Team 1 buys the round.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 sp per player.
- **The winning team** splits the pool, minus 10% for the House.
- **The losing team** buys the toast round.
- **The House keeps:** the 10% and the round.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any gestures, faces and acting, as long as it's silent.
- Using props from the table.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *message*, *minor illusion*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Mouthing the words, sounds of any kind, or a teammate peeking at the card.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mouth the words | Mimer | Cha (Deception) | +5 to one Performance check |
| Peek at the card | A teammate | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | One try succeeds automatically |
| Hidden spell (*message*, *minor illusion*) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | One try succeeds automatically |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Silent Toast is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. The cheater's team loses the game.

### Cheating dice example

On Thokk's second try, he mouths the words "goblin" and "money."
- Thokk: Deception **10** − 1 = **9**. The Watcher: **6** + 5 = **11**. **Caught.**
- Team 1 loses the game. Thokk is banned from Silent Toast for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 1 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The phrase cards** are written by the regulars. One card, in a hand nobody recognises, reads *"The cellar door is open."* It isn't supposed to be.
- **A troupe of travelling mimes** have challenged the tavern to a match. They have never lost, and they have never spoken.
