# Silver Shuffle

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A long slate board with five painted beds, each narrower than the one before. You set a silver piece at the edge, strike it with the heel of your hand, and watch it slide... and slide... and stop. Just inside the line. Or just short.*

**Description:** A weekly league of shove-ha'penny, played with silver pieces. Two players take turns shoving coins up a board with **five beds**. The far beds are harder to reach. Fall a little short and your coin lands in the bed below; fall well short and it slides into the gutter. **The first to land a coin in all five beds wins.**

## How to Play

### Setting up

- Two players. Toss a coin to see who goes first.
- Each player needs to fill **5 beds**, numbered 1 (nearest) to 5 (farthest).

### Your turn, step by step

You shove **3 coins**, one at a time. For each coin:

1. **Name a bed.**
2. **Make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check** against that bed's DC.

   | Bed | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | DC | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 18 |

   - **Meet or beat the DC:** the coin lands in the bed you named.
   - **Miss by 1 to 4:** the coin stops short, **one bed lower**. (Short of bed 1 means off the board.)
   - **Miss by 5 or more:** the coin slides into the **gutter**. Nothing.
3. **Mark the bed** if you didn't have a coin in it yet. A coin that lands in a bed you've already filled does nothing.

### Winning

The first player to have a coin in **all five beds** wins, right away (even partway through a turn).

## Dice Examples

**Quill** (PC, Sleight of Hand +7) vs **Mirela** (PC, Sleight of Hand +2). Quill goes first.

**Quill's turn 1**
- Names bed 5 (DC 18): **13** + 7 = **20**. **Bed 5.** ✓
- Names bed 4 (DC 16): **6** + 7 = **13**. Misses by 3, so it stops one bed lower: **bed 3.** ✓
- Names bed 4 again: **11** + 7 = **18**. **Bed 4.** ✓
- Quill has beds 3, 4, 5.

**Mirela's turn 1**
- Names bed 5 (DC 18): **10** + 2 = **12**. Misses by 6. **Gutter.**
- Names bed 5: **15** + 2 = **17**. Misses by 1: **bed 4.** ✓
- Names bed 5: **18** + 2 = **20**. **Bed 5.** ✓
- Mirela has beds 4, 5.

**Quill's turn 2**
- Names bed 2 (DC 12): **3** + 7 = **10**. Misses by 2: **bed 1.** ✓
- Names bed 2: **9** + 7 = **16**. **Bed 2.** ✓
- **All five beds filled. Quill wins** without needing his third coin.

**Edge case: a wasted coin**
- If Mirela names bed 2, misses by 3, and lands in bed 1, but she already has bed 1, that coin does nothing.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Stake:** 1 gp per player per game. The winner takes both stakes, minus 10% for the House.
- **Coins shoved** are returned after the game. (The House supplies polished silver for the board.)
- **League champion** (the most wins over a season): their name painted on the board's rail.

## Odds

**Chance to land each bed:**

| Sleight of Hand | Bed 1 | Bed 2 | Bed 3 | Bed 4 | Bed 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +2 | 65% | 55% | 45% | 35% | 25% |
| +4 | 75% | 65% | 55% | 45% | 35% |
| +7 | 90% | 80% | 70% | 60% | 50% |

**Average turns to fill all five** (aiming at the farthest empty bed each time): about 3.7 at +2, 3 at +4, 2.4 at +7. A +7 player beats a +4 player about 7 times in 10.

**Aim high.** A coin that falls short of a far bed still lands in the one below it.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any shoving technique: palm, thumb or the knuckle flick.
- Chalking your hand from the House chalk pot.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *mage hand*, *guidance*, *prestidigitation* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A shaved or weighted coin, a damp sleeve on the board, or nudging the table.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shaved coin | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) to swap it in | +2 to every shove this game |
| Nudge the table on a rival's shove | Anyone at the table | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's shove has disadvantage |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand*) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Move one coin up or down one bed |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Silver Shuffle league is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A caught cheater forfeits the game and their stake.

### Cheating dice example

On her second turn, Mirela's coin stops in bed 2, just short of bed 3. She quietly casts *mage hand* to nudge it over the line.
- Mirela has no Subtle Spell, so she rolls Deception without advantage: **18** + 7 = **25**. The Watcher: **12** + 5 = **17**. Not caught.
- Quill is staring at the board: Perception **16** + 6 = **22**. Not enough to beat 25. The coin "slides on its own", and the crowd gasps.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The slate board** came from a temple floor. The beds are carved with a prayer that nobody can read, and coins in bed 5 sometimes come back tarnished green.
- **An old soldier** pays his rent by winning the league every season. This season, he can't afford to lose, and he's frightened.
