# Nine-Pin Kegeln

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Down in the cellar, a long wooden lane runs to nine pins set in a diamond. The ball is solid oak, a bit lopsided, and heavier than it looks. Roll it hard enough and straight enough, and all nine go down with a crash the whole tavern can hear.*

**Description:** A daily bowling game on the cellar lane. Each **frame**, a bowler gets **two balls** to knock down **nine pins**. The better your roll, the more pins fall. Knock all nine down with your first ball for a **strike**, worth bonus points. The highest total after **three frames** wins.

## How to Play

### A frame, step by step

1. **First ball:** make a **Strength (Athletics)** or **Dexterity** check (your choice, each ball). **Pins knocked down = your total − 8** (from 0 up to 9).
   - **All 9 pins (a total of 17 or more):** a **strike**. The frame scores **12**, and you don't roll again.
2. **Second ball:** if any pins are left, roll again. Pins knocked down = total − 8, but only up to the number still standing.
3. **The frame scores** the number of pins knocked down (or 12 for a strike).

### A game

- **Three frames** each (or ten, for a long game).
- The highest total wins.
- **Ties:** one extra frame each.

## Dice Examples

**Bowlers:** Thokk (NPC, Athletics +5) and Quill (PC, Dex +3).

**Thokk** (uses Athletics)
| Frame | First ball | Second ball | Score |

| 3 | **9** + 5 = 14. 6 pins | **3** + 5 = 8. 0 pins | 6 |
| | | **Total** | **27** |

**Quill** (uses Dexterity)
| Frame | First ball | Second ball | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **12** + 3 = 15. 7 pins | **6** + 3 = 9. 1 pin | 8 |

| 3 | **15** + 3 = 18. **Strike** | — | 12 |
| | | **Total** | **32** |

**Quill wins, 32 to 27.**

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Choosing Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity for each ball.
- Any bowling style: two hands, between the legs, or the "gnome spin".
- Rage and other features that don't use magic.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *mage hand*, *gust*, *guidance*, *enhance ability* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A hollow pin, a weighted ball, or a pin-boy paid to set the pins loose.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bribe the pin-boy to set loose pins (2 sp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | +2 to your next first ball |
| Bribe the pin-boy to set a rival's pins tight | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | −2 to the rival's next first ball |
| Hidden spell (*gust*, *mage hand*) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Knock down 1 extra pin |

### Cheating dice example

Before frame 3, Thokk slips the pin-boy 2 sp to set Quill's pins tight.
- Thokk: Persuasion **17** − 1 = **16**. The Watcher: **5** + 5 = **10**. Not caught.
- Quill's frame 3 first ball: **15** + 3 = 18, minus 2 = **16**. That knocks down 8 pins. No strike. His second ball: **2** + 3 = 5, 0 pins. Frame 3 scores 8. Quill's total is 28. **Thokk still loses**, by one pin.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The pin-boy, Tam,** sees everything that happens at the far end of the lane, including the secret door behind the pins.
- **The lopsided ball** is said to be carved from a giant's knuckle bone. A giant has come asking for it back.
