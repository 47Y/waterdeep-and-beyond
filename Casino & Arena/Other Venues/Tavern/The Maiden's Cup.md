# The Maiden's Cup

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *On Midsummer night, couples and partners step forward to the silver maiden: a little statue whose skirt is one cup and whose raised arms hold a second, swiveling bowl. Two people must drink from one figure, at the same time, without spilling a drop.*

**Description:** A yearly contest for pairs, using an old wedding cup. The silver figure's skirt holds wine, and a tiny bowl swivels in her raised hands. Both partners drink at once, one from each end, and the one holding the figure must keep the bowl level. Each round the wine gets stronger and the cups harder to handle. Spills cost the pair; too much wine and a partner drops out.

## How to Play

### Setting up

- Each pair gets a **maiden cup** and decides who holds it first.
- Each partner keeps their own place on the Drunk Track.
- Each pair starts with **0 faults**.

### A round, step by step

All pairs play each round at the same time.

1. **Both partners drink.** Each partner makes a **Constitution saving throw** at **DC 9 + the round number** (round 1 is DC 10, round 2 is DC 11, and so on). Failures go on the Drunk Track.
2. **Keep it level.** The partner **holding the figure** makes a **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check at **DC 10 + the round number** (round 1 is DC 11).
   - **Success:** no spill.
   - **Failure:** a spill. The pair gains **1 fault**.
3. **Swap.** The partners swap who holds the figure for the next round.

### Out of the contest

A pair is out when **either**:
- the pair has **3 faults**, or
- **either partner** is **Out** on the Drunk Track.

### Winning

- The last pair still in wins.
- **Sudden death:** if every remaining pair goes out in the same round, the pair with the **fewest faults** wins. If they're tied, the pair whose holder had the higher Sleight of Hand total that round wins.

### Drunk Track

![[Drunk Track#Rules]]

**Being Drunk hurts here:** a Drunk partner has disadvantage on the Sleight of Hand check.

## Dice Examples

**Pairs:**
- **Mirela and Quill** (PCs): Mirela Con save +1, Sleight of Hand +2; Quill Con save +1, Sleight of Hand +7. Quill holds first.
- **Thokk and "Fat" Oswin** (NPCs): Thokk Con save +5 with advantage, Sleight of Hand −1; Oswin Con save +4, Sleight of Hand +0. Oswin holds first.

**Round 1** (drink DC 10, level DC 11)
- Mirela **12** + 1 = 13, pass. Quill **6** + 1 = 7, **1 failure** (Tipsy).
- Quill holds: **9** + 7 = **16**. No spill.
- Thokk **4** and **17** = 22, pass. Oswin **11** + 4 = 15, pass.
- Oswin holds: **8** + 0 = **8**. **Spill: 1 fault.**

**Round 2** (drink DC 11, level DC 12)
- Mirela **15** + 1 = 16, pass. Quill **13** + 1 = 14, pass.
- Mirela holds: **10** + 2 = **12**. No spill.
- Thokk **8** and **12** = 17, pass. Oswin **5** + 4 = 9, **1 failure**.
- Thokk holds: **14** − 1 = **13**. No spill.

**Round 3** (drink DC 12, level DC 13)
- Mirela **3** + 1 = 4, **1 failure**. Quill **natural 1**: **2 more failures**, so Quill is **Out**. **Mirela and Quill are out.**
- Thokk passes, Oswin **14** + 4 = 18, pass. Oswin holds: **13** + 0 = **13**. No spill.
- **Thokk and Oswin win** with 1 fault.

**Edge case: sudden death**
- If Oswin had rolled a natural 1 in round 3, he'd have 3 failures and be Out too. Both pairs would go out in the same round. Mirela and Quill have 0 faults and Thokk and Oswin have 1, so **Mirela and Quill would win.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 2 gp per pair.
- **Winning pair:**
  - A silver maiden cup (worth 25 gp).
  - The title **"Wedded Luck"**: the House chaplain casts *bless* on the pair once, any time in the next year, for free.
- **The House keeps:** all entries, wine sales, and its cut of spectator bets.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Natural traits, including Dwarven Resilience.
- Any pairing: couples, friends, rivals forced together by fate.
- Holding hands, whispering and counting "three, two, one, drink!".

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *lesser restoration*, *protection from poison*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration from someone outside the pair.
- A weighted or glued swivel bowl, or watered wine.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glue the swivel bowl | Either partner, before the contest | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on every Sleight of Hand check |
| Water the wine | Either partner or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Both partners pass this round's drink automatically |
| Hidden spell | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |
| Jostle a rival pair | Anyone in the crowd | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival holder has disadvantage this round |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Maiden's Cup is a **special public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A cheating pair is disqualified together.

### Cheating dice example

Before round 1, Quill dabs glue on the swivel bowl.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **16** + 7 = **23**. The Watcher: **12** + 5 = **17**. Not caught by the Watcher.
- Thokk is standing next to them: Perception **19** + 1 = **20**. Not enough to beat 23.
- Quill and Mirela get advantage on every Sleight of Hand check for the rest of the contest.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The silver maiden** used every Midsummer is three hundred years old. A noble family claims it was stolen from a wedding that never happened.
- **A jilted lover** has entered with a new partner, and has a plan to make sure their ex's pair spills.
