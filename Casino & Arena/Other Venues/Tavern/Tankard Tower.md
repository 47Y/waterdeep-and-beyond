# Tankard Tower

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Empty wooden tankards, a minute on the sandglass, and a wobbly table. Stack them as high as you can. Every tier is harder than the last, a slip knocks off the top one, and a real fumble brings the whole tower crashing down.*

**Description:** A daily stacking game. Builders have **one minute** (6 attempts) to stack empty tankards into a tower, one **tier** at a time. Each tier needs a harder **Sleight of Hand** check. A failed attempt knocks off the top tier; a **natural 1** topples the whole tower. The tallest tower standing when the sand runs out wins the night's champion tankard.

## How to Play

### Your minute, step by step

1. You get **6 attempts** (10 seconds each).
2. **Each attempt:** add the next tier with a **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check at **DC 8 + 2 × the tier you're adding**.

   | Tier | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | DC | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 18 | 20 |

   - **Success:** the tier stays. Your tower is one tier taller.
   - **Failure:** the **top tier falls off**. Your tower is one tier shorter (it can't go below 0).
   - **Natural 1:** the **whole tower topples**. Back to 0.
3. **Your score** is the height of your tower after the 6th attempt.

### Winning

- The tallest tower of the night wins.
- **Ties:** each tied builder gets one more attempt. The taller tower wins; if still tied, they share the title.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** free with any three drinks bought that night.
- **Night's champion:** drinks from the **silver champion's tankard**, with free refills for the rest of the night.
- **The House keeps:** the drink sales. (That's the point.)

## Odds

| Sleight of Hand | Average height | Chance of 4 or more |
|---|---|---|
| +2 | 1.5 | 9% |

A tower of 4 usually takes the night. Six tiers almost never happens.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any stacking style, as long as it's tankards on tankards.
- Using both hands.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *mage hand*, *guidance*, *mold earth* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Sticky rings on the tankard bottoms, or bumping the table during a rival's minute.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beer-sticky tankard bottoms | Builder | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | A natural 1 only knocks off the top tier |
| Bump a rival's table | Anyone nearby | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's next attempt has disadvantage |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand*) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on one attempt |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Tankard Tower is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. The fine is set as if the entry were 1 sp (the price of the drinks).

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 1 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

On Quill's 6th attempt, Mirela bumps the table.
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **9** + 2 = **11**. The Watcher: **13** + 5 = **18**. **Caught.**
- Mirela is banned from Tankard Tower for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. The bump is stopped, so Quill's attempt is rolled normally. To clear her name: 1 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The silver champion's tankard** is engraved with the names of past champions. Several of them are now dead adventurers. Superstitious regulars refuse to drink from it.
- **A gnome tinker** has offered the House a "self-stacking tankard." The House is tempted.
