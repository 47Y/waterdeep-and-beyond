# Giant's Chess

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The pit floor is a giant board. Eighteen human-sized constructs stand on it, in two colours. High above on the second-floor balcony, two captains shout orders down. It's chess, more or less, if chess were a battle of nerves.*

**Description:** A monthly strategy game. Two **captains** stand on the second-floor balcony and command **nine constructs** each on a board laid out on the pit floor. Every capture is a battle of wits: **your Investigation against your rival's Insight**. **Capture four pieces, then take the enemy's crown, to win.**

## How to Play

### The board and pieces

- **A 5 × 5 grid** of 10-ft squares on the pit floor.
- **Each captain has 9 constructs:** 8 pieces and 1 **crown**.

### A game, step by step

1. **Toss a coin** to see who moves first. Then take turns.
2. **On your turn**, move one piece onto an enemy piece to **try a capture**: your **Intelligence (Investigation)** against the rival captain's **Wisdom (Insight)**. **Ties go to the defender.**
   - **Win:** the enemy piece is captured.
   - **Lose:** your piece goes back where it came from.
3. **The crown can only be attacked once you've captured 4 pieces.**
4. **Capture the crown to win.**

**Running it fast:** in practice, captains attack every turn. The DM can run the game as a simple race: take turns making capture contests, and **the first captain to 5 captures** (4 pieces + the crown) wins.

No spells and no magic items. The constructs never attack anyone.

## Dice Examples

**Orrin Vex** (NPC wizard, Investigation +5, Insight +1) against **Magistra Ilse Varn** (NPC scholar, Investigation +3, Insight +4).

Orrin wins the coin toss and moves first.

| Turn | Captain | Roll | Result | Captures (Orrin–Ilse) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orrin | **14** + 5 = 19 vs **11** + 4 = 15 | Capture | 1–0 |
| 2 | Ilse | **9** + 3 = 12 vs **12** + 1 = 13 | Fails | 1–0 |
| 3 | Orrin | **10** + 5 = 15 vs **13** + 4 = 17 | Fails | 1–0 |
| 4 | Ilse | **17** + 3 = 20 vs **8** + 1 = 9 | Capture | 1–1 |
| … | … | … | … | … |
| 13 | Orrin | **16** + 5 = 21 vs **18** + 4 = 22 | **Crown attempt fails** | 4–3 |
| 15 | Orrin | **15** + 5 = 20 vs **9** + 4 = 13 | **Crown captured!** | **Orrin wins** |

**Edge case: a tie.** If Orrin had rolled 15 + 5 = 20 and Ilse 16 + 4 = 20, **the defender wins**, so the capture fails.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per captain.
- **Purse:** 8 gp to the winner.
- **The House keeps:** the entries, the betting cut and the balcony tables (sold at a premium on chess nights).

## Odds

**The edge** is your Investigation minus the rival's Insight. When both captains have the same edge:

| Edge | −2 | 0 | +2 | +4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Each capture attempt works | 38% | 47.5% | 57% | 66% |
| The first mover wins the game | 23% | 55% | 83.5% | 96% |

Moving first is worth about 5% between equal captains.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Bluffing, feints and talking to your rival between turns.
- Moving any piece in any direction, one square.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic** (no *detect thoughts*, no *guidance*).
- A spotter signalling the rival captain's tells, or a construct tampered with.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| A spotter reading the rival's face | A friend on the balcony | Dex (Sleight of Hand) for hand signals | +2 to your capture checks |
| Tamper with a rival construct | Anyone in the workshop | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's crown can be attacked after 3 captures instead of 4 |
| Hidden spell (*detect thoughts*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Quill acts as a spotter for Orrin, signalling Ilse's tells.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **13** + 7 = **20**. The Steward: **11** + 5 = **16**. Not caught.
- Orrin captures at +7 instead of +5. Against Ilse's Insight +4, his edge goes from +1 to +3, so each attempt now works about 62% of the time instead of 52%.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Magistra Ilse Varn** has never lost at Giant's Chess. She's started asking the House whether real soldiers could stand in for the constructs.
- **One black construct** moves by itself at night, one square at a time. The cleaners have mapped its path.
