# Phoenix Egg Keepaway

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A glowing golden egg, warm as a coal and unbreakable, flies across the pit. Nobody can hold it long; it's too hot. Pass, pass, pass. Keep it three times in a row and you score. Drop it and the other team pounces.*

**Description:** A weekly keepaway game. Two teams of three pass a warm, unbreakable **phoenix egg**. It's too hot to hold, so whoever has it **must pass it**. **Three good passes in a row scores.** A bad pass hands it to the other team, and a **natural 1** makes the egg **flare** and burn. **First to 3 scores wins.**

## How to Play

### A pass

- **The holder must pass** to a teammate. It's an attack roll: **Dex modifier + proficiency bonus**, against **AC 15** (the other team crowds every pass).
  - **Hit:** a teammate catches it. **+1 to your streak.**
  - **Miss:** intercepted. **The other team has the egg**, and your streak goes back to 0.
  - **Natural 1:** **the egg flares.** The passer takes **1d4 fire damage**, and the other team gets the egg.

### A game, step by step

1. **Toss a coin.** The winner starts with the egg.
2. **The team with the egg passes**, again and again, until it scores or loses the egg.
3. **Three good passes in a row: SCORE.** The other team gets the egg.
4. **First to 3 scores wins.**

**The passer chooses who to throw to.** Whoever catches it makes the next pass, so keep the egg away from your weakest thrower.

No spells and no magic items.

**Red:** Quill (PC, pass +5), Mirela (PC, pass +4) and Thokk (PC, pass +1).
**Blue:** Sela (NPC, pass +6), Grit (NPC, pass +2) and a dockhand (NPC, pass +3).

Red wins the coin toss.

Red learned its lesson after pass 3: **don't throw it to Thokk.**

|---|---|---|---|
| One pass works | 45% | 55% | 65% |

Two equal teams are 50/50. The team with the better two throwers has a big edge.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Choosing who to throw to every time.
- Thick gloves (they don't change the rolls, but they help with the heat).

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic** (no *mage hand*, no *protection from energy*).
- A cooled egg swapped in for your team's possession, or a bribed referee.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frost-salt on your gloves | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Your flares do no damage |
| Bribe the referee to miss a fumble (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | One missed pass by your team counts as caught |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Phoenix Egg Keepaway is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A team that's in on it forfeits the game.

### Cheating dice example

Mirela bribes the referee to miss Thokk's bad pass (pass 3).
- Mirela: Persuasion **11** + 5 = **16**. The Steward: **7** + 5 = **12**. Not caught.
- Thokk's pass counts as caught. Red's streak reaches 3: **Red scores early.**

- **A collector of rare eggs** has offered the House a fortune for it. The House said no. The collector didn't seem to accept that answer.
