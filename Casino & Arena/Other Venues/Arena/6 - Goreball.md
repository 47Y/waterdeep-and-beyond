# Goreball

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A heavy leather ball, two goals and eight bruisers in the pit. You can run it, you can throw it, and you can try to put it past the goalie. Get tackled and it hurts. The league table hangs in the hall, and the whole city bets on it.*

**Description:** A weekly league ball game. Two teams of four fight to get a heavy ball down a field of **five zones** and **shoot it past the goalie**. Each play, the carrier **runs**, **passes** or **shoots**. Lose a run and you get flattened. **First to 3 goals wins.**

## How to Play

### The field

- **Five zones** across the pit floor. Each team defends the goal at its own end.
- **Red attacks from zone 1 toward zone 5.** Blue attacks the other way.
- **Each team has a goalie.** Their **goal AC** is 10 + their Dex modifier + their proficiency bonus.

### A match, step by step

1. **Tip-off:** one player from each team makes a **Strength (Athletics)** contest. **The winner's team gets the ball in zone 3** (the middle).
2. **Each play, the carrier picks one:**
   - **Run:** a **Strength (Athletics)** contest against one defender (the defending team chooses who; the defender may use Athletics or Acrobatics). **Ties go to the defender.**
     - **Win:** move the ball **1 zone** forward.
     - **Lose:** you're tackled. **Take 1d4 bludgeoning damage**, and the other team gets the ball in this zone.
   - **Pass:** an attack roll (**Dex modifier + proficiency bonus**) to a teammate.
     - **AC 12** to a teammate in the same zone or 1 zone ahead. **AC 16** to a teammate 2 zones ahead.
     - **Miss:** intercepted. The other team gets the ball in the target zone.
   - **Shoot:** only from the zone right in front of the enemy goal. An attack roll (Dex + proficiency) against the **goalie's goal AC**.
     - **Hit:** **GOAL!** The other team restarts in zone 3.
     - **Miss:** the goalie's team gets the ball in their own goal zone.
3. **First to 3 goals wins.**

**Spells:** only **show cantrips** for flair (*light*, *dancing lights*, *prestidigitation*, *thaumaturgy*). They have no effect on the rolls. No other spells and no magic items.

**Netted curtain:** up.

### NPC matches

When no PC is playing, settle it with **Clash**: each exchange is one goal, and the first team to 3 wins. See [[Arena Combat Rules#Clash]].

## Dice Examples

**Red:** Thokk (PC, Athletics +5, pass +1), Quill (PC, pass and shoot +5), Mirela (PC) and Oswin (NPC).
**Blue:** Kord (NPC, Athletics +5), Grit (NPC), a dockhand (NPC) and Sela (NPC goalie, Dex +4, proficiency +2: **goal AC 16**).

- **Tip-off:** Thokk **14** + 5 = **19** against Kord **11** + 5 = **16**. **Red's ball, zone 3.**
- **Thokk runs:** **12** + 5 = **17** against Kord **8** + 5 = **13**. **Forward to zone 4.**
- **Thokk passes** to Quill in zone 5 (1 zone ahead, AC 12): **13** + 1 = **14**. **Caught.**
- **Quill shoots** against goal AC 16: **12** + 5 = **17**. **GOAL! Red leads 1–0.**
- **Blue restarts in zone 3. Kord runs:** **8** + 5 = **13** against Thokk **10** + 5 = **15**. **Tackled!** Kord takes **3** damage, and Red has the ball in zone 3.

**Edge case:** Quill can't shoot from zone 4. He has to be in zone 5, right in front of Blue's goal.

## Odds

**For a player at +5:**

| Play | Works |
|---|---|
| Pass to AC 12 | 70% |
| Pass to AC 16 | 50% |
| Shot at goal AC 15 | 55% |
| Shot at goal AC 17 | 45% |
| A run against an equal defender | 47.5% |

Running is risky. Short passes are the safest way up the field.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Tackling only the ball carrier (that's what a lost run is).
- Show cantrips for the crowd.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell other than a show cantrip, and any magic item.**
- A weighted ball, or a goalie who's been paid off.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bribe the rival goalie (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The goalie's goal AC drops by 3 for the match |
| Hide a "show" spell that isn't one (*guidance*, *bless*) | A caster on the team | Cha (Deception) to pass it off as a show cantrip | The spell works normally |
| Sand the ball before your team's possession | Anyone on the bench | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | +2 to your team's passes until the next goal |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Goreball is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A team that's in on it forfeits the match.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 5 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Mirela casts *guidance* on Quill before his shot, dressed up as a sparkly show cantrip.
- Mirela: Deception **16** + 7 = **23**. The Steward: **18** + 5 = **23**. **A tie goes to the Steward: caught.**
- Red forfeits the match. Mirela is banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear her name: 5 gp, 1 day serving (stitching balls), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The Dockside Gorers** have topped the league three seasons running. Their captain, Kord, has started losing on purpose, and his teammates have noticed.
- **The ball** is stuffed with something heavier than sand. Nobody has ever cut one open.
