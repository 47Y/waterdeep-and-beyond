# Rolling Gauntlet

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *"Roll, roll, roll... bank!" The halfling slams her hand on the table as the crowd groans. One more roll and a single pip could have wiped out everything she'd built. First to fifty wins the gauntlet.*

**Description:** A push-your-luck race between players. On your turn, you roll a d6 again and again, adding up the numbers. Stop whenever you like and **bank** the total. But roll a **1** and you lose everything from that turn. The first player to bank **50 points** wins the pot.

## How to Play

### Setting up

1. **Buy in.** Every player pays the same buy-in into the pot. The House takes **5%** as its rake.
2. **Turn order.** Players roll a d6 each; highest goes first, then clockwise. The first player has a small edge, so in a series of games the starting seat rotates.

### A turn, step by step

1. **Roll a d6.**
   - **2–6:** add it to your **turn total**. Then choose: **roll again** or **bank**.
   - **1:** your turn total drops to **0** and your turn ends. Points you banked on earlier turns are safe.
2. **Bank:** add your turn total to your **score**. Your turn ends.

### Winning

The first player whose score reaches **50 or more** wins the pot.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Mirela (PC), Thokk (NPC) and Quill (PC) each buy in for **10 gp**. The pot is 30 gp; after the 5% rake the winner gets **28 gp 5 sp**.

**Mirela's first turn**
- Rolls **4** (turn total 4), **6** (10), **3** (13), **5** (18). She banks. **Score: 18.**

**Thokk's first turn**
- Rolls **5** (5), **5** (10), **2** (12), **6** (18), **4** (22). Greedy, he keeps going: **1**. **Turn total gone.** **Score: 0.**

**Quill's first turn**
- Rolls **3** (3), **2** (5), **6** (11). He banks. **Score: 11.**

**Later in the game: the finish**
- Scores: Mirela **44**, Thokk **38**, Quill **47**. It's Mirela's turn.
- She rolls **2** (2), **4** (6). That's 50. She banks. **Mirela reaches 50 and wins** 28 gp 5 sp.

**Edge case: a 1 on the first roll**
- Thokk's next turn starts with a **1**. His turn ends at once with nothing added. His banked score doesn't change.

## Odds

**Chance to bank at least this much in a single turn** (if you keep rolling until you reach it):

| Target | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 | 30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chance | 59% | 47% | 38% | 30% | 24% |

- **A good habit:** bank once your turn total reaches about **20**, unless you need fewer points to win.
- **Equal players** each win about 1 game in N. The House's 5% rake means each player loses 5% of their buy-in on average.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Rolling or banking whenever you like.
- Taunting, cheering, and begging the die not to roll a 1.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the die.
- A loaded die, or calling a 1 something else.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** Other players can also try to spot you.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catch the die | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Reroll one 1 |
| Loaded die | Player or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) to swap it in | Reroll every 1 this turn |
| Miscount your score | Player | Cha (Deception) | Add 5 to your banked score |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand* nudge) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Turn one 1 into a 6 |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Rolling Gauntlet is played at a House table, so cheating at it is cheating the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. The cheater's buy-in is shared among the other players.

![[Cheating Penalties#Casino]]

### Cheating dice example

Thokk has a turn total of 24 and rolls a **1**. He snatches the die before it settles.
- Thokk: Sleight of Hand **14** − 1 = **13**. The Watcher: **10** + 5 = **15**. **Caught.**
- He's confiscated, filed and banned for a month, and owes **50 gp** (five times his 10 gp buy-in) or a favour.
