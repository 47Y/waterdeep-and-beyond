# Thimbles of Mask

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Three silver thimbles, one pea, and a grinning halfling whose hands move faster than your eyes. "Follow the pea, friend. Where's the pea?" The thimbles dance and stop.*

**Description:** A House operator hides a pea under one of three thimbles and shuffles them. The player watches closely and points to one. The operator's quick hands are up against the player's sharp eyes. A sharp-eyed adventurer can beat a slow operator, so the House keeps its best operators for its best players.

## How to Play

### A round, step by step

1. **Bet.** Place your bet.
2. **The shuffle.** The operator rolls **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)**. The player rolls **Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Follow the pea.**
   - **The player's total is higher:** they followed the pea and **win**.
   - **The operator's total is equal or higher:** the player lost track. They **guess** one of the three thimbles. Roll a d6: **1–2** finds the pea (a lucky guess wins); **3–6** loses.
4. **Pay out.** A win pays **9:10**.

### Operators and seating

Every table has its own operator:

| Table | Operator | Sleight of Hand |
|---|---|---|
| Silver Table | A grinning halfling | +5 |
| Gold Table | A trained House operator | +8 |
| Platinum Salon | A skilled House operator | +11 |
| By invitation only, at Platinum limits | A master of the craft | +14 |
| By invitation only, at Platinum limits | **Mask's Hand**, the House's best | +17 |

**The House seats players by their eyes.** A player's Perception must be at least 5 below the operator's Sleight of Hand. Anyone closer than that is politely moved to the next operator up. A player too sharp even for Mask's Hand (Perception +13 or higher) is politely asked to play something else.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Perception +0) plays at the Silver Table (operator +5), **4 gp** per round. His +0 is 5 below the operator, so the seating rule lets him stay.

**Round 1**
- Operator: **9** + 5 = **14**. Oswin: **17** + 0 = **17**. Oswin followed the pea.
- He **wins 9:10**: **3 gp 6 sp**.

**Round 2**
- Operator: **12** + 5 = **17**. Oswin: **11** + 0 = **11**. Oswin lost track and guesses.
- d6: **5**. Wrong thimble. He **loses** 4 gp.

**Round 3**
- Operator: **6** + 5 = **11**. Oswin: **11** + 0 = **11**. **A tie goes to the operator.** Oswin guesses.
- d6: **2**. **Lucky guess!** He wins **3 gp 6 sp**.

**Edge case: a sharp player is reseated**
- Quill (PC rogue, Perception +6) sits at the Silver Table. His +6 is higher than the operator's +5, so the floor staff move him up.
- At the Gold Table (operator +8) he's only 2 below, which is too close. He's moved up again.
- At the Platinum Salon (operator +11) he's exactly 5 below. He can play there, at Platinum limits.

## Odds

**Player's chance to win a round, and the House edge, by the gap between the player's Perception and the operator's Sleight of Hand:**

| Player's Perception vs the operator | Chance to win | House edge |
|---|---|---|
| Equal | 65.0% | **−23.5% (the player has the edge)** |
| 4 lower | 53.3% | −1.3% (the player has the edge) |
| 5 lower (the closest seat allowed) | 50.8% | 3.4% |
| 7 lower (the furthest seat the House can make you take) | 46.3% | 12.0% |
| 8 lower | 44.3% | 15.8% |
| 12 lower | 38.0% | 27.8% |

**This is why the House seats players.** At an equal table the player wins two times in three. A player can always choose a higher table than they need, and the last two rows show what that costs.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any natural sense or skill (Perception proficiency, Expertise, Keen Senses).
- Asking the operator to slow down. They never will.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that finds the pea, such as *detect thoughts* on the operator, or a familiar watching from above.
- Bumping the table, or a partner watching from behind the operator.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| While every eye is on the operator's hands, knee the table so that the thimbles wobble | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The operator rolls with disadvantage this round |
| Stand behind the operator, where you can see the pea slip under a thimble's back edge, and signal the player | A helper in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to look casual | The player wins this round |
| Send a familiar to watch from above and tell you how the thimbles moved | A caster with a familiar | Dex (Stealth) for the familiar. Roll again every round. | Advantage on your Perception this round |
| Hidden spell (*detect thoughts* on the operator, who knows where the pea is) | A caster within 30 ft | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The player wins this round |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Thimbles of Mask is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

![[Cheating Penalties#Casino]]

### Cheating dice example

Quill (PC) bets 10 pp (100 gp) at the Platinum Salon while his partner Vessa (NPC, Deception +4) signals from behind the operator.
- Vessa: Deception **13** + 4 = **17**. The Watcher: **12** + 5 = **17**. **A tie goes to the Watcher. Caught.**
- Both lose their chips and the night's winnings, go in the House's file, and are banned for a month. Quill owes **500 gp** (five times his 100 gp bet, under the Platinum cap of 1,000 gp); Vessa the same, or a favour from each.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Mask's Hand** has never had a losing night. Rumor says the operator is a priest of Mask, god of thieves, and the thimbles are holy relics.
- **A sore loser** is telling everyone that the Silver Table's operator palms the pea. The House asks the party to watch a few rounds and settle it in front of the crowd.
