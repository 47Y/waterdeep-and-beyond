# Dragon Chess

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Three boards stacked on brass rods: the sky, the land, and the caverns below. Two players lean over the tiers, moving dragons, griffons and dwarves between worlds. Around them, a quiet crowd holds its breath and its wagers.*

**Description:** A strategy game played on three stacked boards, and the casino's quiet table for thinkers. Two players bet the same amount and play a **match** of three games. Each game is an **Intelligence check**, adding proficiency if the player is proficient with a **dragonchess set**. The first to win two games takes the stakes. Spectators can bet on the match at the bookie's desk.

## How to Play

### A match, step by step

1. **Stake.** Both players put the same stake on the table. The House takes **5%** of the total as its rake.
2. **Play a game.** Both players make an **Intelligence check**, adding their proficiency bonus if they're proficient with a dragonchess set.
   - **Higher total** wins the game.
   - **Tie:** the game is drawn. Both roll again.
3. **First to win two games** wins the match and takes the stakes.

### Spectators

Spectators bet on matches at the bookie's desk. See [[Proposition Betting]].

## Dice Examples

**At the board:** Mirela (PC, Int +1, proficient +2 = **+3**) plays Thokk (NPC, Int +0, not proficient, **+0**). Each stakes **20 gp**. The pot is 40 gp; the rake is 2 gp, so the winner takes **38 gp**.

**Game 1**
- Mirela **8** + 3 = **11**. Thokk **14** + 0 = **14**. **Thokk wins.** Thokk 1, Mirela 0.

**Game 2**
- Mirela **15** + 3 = **18**. Thokk **9** + 0 = **9**. **Mirela wins.** 1 game each.

**Game 3**
- Mirela **10** + 3 = **13**. Thokk **13** + 0 = **13**. **Tie: a drawn game.** Both roll again.
- Mirela **16** + 3 = **19**. Thokk **12** + 0 = **12**. **Mirela wins the match** 2–1 and takes **38 gp**.

**Edge case: a quick match**
- Next match, Mirela wins Game 1 (**17** + 3 = 20 vs **5**) and Game 2 (**12** + 3 = 15 vs **11**). Two wins in two games: the match is over, and no third game is played.

## Odds

**Chance the better player wins, by how much higher their modifier is:**

| Modifier difference | Wins one game | Wins the match |
|---|---|---|
| Equal | 50% | 50% |
| +1 | 55% | 58% |
| +2 | 60% | 65% |
| +3 | 64% | 71% |
| +5 | 73% | 82% |

**A match rewards the better player** more than a single game does. The House's 5% rake means equal players each lose 5% of their stake on average.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Proficiency with a dragonchess set.
- Taking your time. There's a sand-glass, but nobody's ever turned it.
- Spectators murmuring, as long as they don't advise.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell**, Bardic Inspiration or *guidance* on the check.
- A spectator signaling moves, or moving a piece when your opponent looks away.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** Your opponent can also try to spot you.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move a piece unseen | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | +5 to this game's check |
| Spectator signals moves | A helper in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to look casual | Advantage on this game's check |
| Hidden *guidance* | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | +1d4 to this game's check |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Dragon Chess is played at a House table, so cheating at it is cheating the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. The opponent gets both stakes back in full.

![[Cheating Penalties#Casino]]

### Cheating dice example

In Game 3, Thokk slides his dwarf one square while Mirela studies the sky board.
- Thokk: Sleight of Hand **18** − 1 = **17**. The Watcher: **9** + 5 = **14**. Thokk beats the Watcher.
- Mirela rolls to spot it: Perception **11** + 3 = **14**. Not enough. Thokk gets **+5** and wins the game.
