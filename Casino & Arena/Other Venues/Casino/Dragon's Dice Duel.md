# Dragon's Dice Duel

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Three little knights against one great dragon. You roll three small dice; the dealer rolls one big red d20 carved like a dragon's egg. Your knights must beat the dragon. And if all three knights match, they slay it outright.*

**Description:** A head-to-head dice duel against the dealer. The player rolls **3d6** (the knights) and the dealer rolls **1d20** (the dragon). The higher total wins. Three dice gather around the middle, while a d20 can land anywhere, so it feels close every time. A triple is an automatic win.

## How to Play

1. **Bet.** Place your bet.
2. **Roll.** The player rolls **3d6** and adds them. The dealer rolls **1d20**.
3. **Compare.**
   - **The knights' total is higher:** the player **wins 1:1**.
   - **The dragon's roll is equal or higher:** the player **loses**. **Ties go to the dragon.**
   - **A triple** (all three d6 show the same number): **the knights slay the dragon.** The player **wins 1:1**, whatever the d20 shows.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Thokk (NPC) bets **5 gp** a duel.

**Duel 1**
- Knights: **4 + 5 + 6 = 15**. Dragon: **11**.
- 15 beats 11. Thokk **wins 5 gp**.

**Duel 2**
- Knights: **2 + 3 + 1 = 6**. Dragon: **14**.
- Thokk **loses** 5 gp.

**Duel 3**
- Knights: **5 + 3 + 4 = 12**. Dragon: **12**.
- **A tie goes to the dragon.** Thokk **loses** 5 gp.

**Edge case: a triple**
- Knights: **2 + 2 + 2 = 6**. Dragon: **18**.
- 6 would lose to 18, but **three 2s is a triple**: the knights slay the dragon. Thokk **wins 5 gp**.

## Odds

| Result | Chance |
|---|---|
| Player wins (including triples) | 49.0% |
| Of which a triple | 2.8% |
| Dragon wins (including ties) | 51.0% |

**House edge: 2.1%.** Without the triple rule, the player would win only 47.5%.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any bet within the table limits.
- Naming your three knights. Regulars swear it helps.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the dice.
- Loaded d6s, or swapping the dealer's d20.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loaded d6s | Player or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | +1 to each of your d6s for the rest of the night (maximum 6) |
| Swap the dragon's egg | A helper near the dealer | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The dealer's d20 rolls with disadvantage for the rest of the night |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand* nudge) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Turn one d6 to a face of your choice |

### Cheating dice example

Quill (PC) rolls **4, 4, 2** with a 10 gp bet and tries a *mage hand* (cast by Mirela, hidden) to turn the 2 into a 4.
- Mirela: Deception **16** + 7 = **23**. The Watcher: **11** + 5 = **16**. **Not caught.**
- The 2 becomes a 4: **4, 4, 4**, a triple. Quill wins **10 gp** no matter what the dragon rolls.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The dragon's egg d20** is carved from a real red dragon's eggshell. A red dragon cultist wants it back.
- **A paladin** plays every night and only ever bets on "slaying the dragon". He's on a losing streak and his order is getting worried.
