# Champion (Sharpshooter)

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A painted wooden bird perches on a pole in the middle of the pit. With a clank, the pole starts to slide along its rail, gliding in and out behind painted screens. The shooters line the second-floor rail, bows and crossbows pointed down. One piece at a time, the bird is shot apart. Whoever knocks off the last piece is crowned Shooting King for a year.*

**Description:** A monthly shooting contest, like the old popinjay shoots. Shooters take turns firing **from the second-floor rail** down at a **wooden bird** that **slides back and forth** behind a row of screens. The bird has **10 pieces**, and each hit knocks one off. **Whoever knocks off the last piece** wins the shoot. Every shoot earns **season points**, and at the end of the year the top 8 shoot one final bird. The winner becomes the **Shooting King** (or Queen) for a year.

## How to Play

### The bird

- **A wooden bird** on top of a **15-ft pole**, mounted on a **sliding carriage** that runs on a rail across the pit.
- **The rail runs behind a row of painted wooden screens**, so the bird keeps sliding in and out of cover.
- **10 pieces:** wings, tail feathers, head, and finally the body.
- **The pit is brightly lit** with lanterns for the whole shoot.

### Your gear

**You shoot as yourself:** your own weapon, magic items, spells, class features and feats, all under the normal rules. Two limits keep it a shooting contest:
- **Your shot must be a ranged weapon attack** (bow, crossbow, sling or thrown weapon). A spell can help you, like *bless*, but it can't be the shot itself: no *magic missile*, no *fire bolt*.
- **No spell may touch, move or hold the bird**, the pole or the carriage.

### A shoot, step by step

1. **Draw lots** for the shooting order. There's no initiative.
2. **On your turn, take one shot.** Only one, even if you have Extra Attack or another feature that gives more attacks.
3. **Timing (optional):** before your shot, you can watch the slide and pick your moment. Make a **Wisdom (Perception)** check, **DC 13**. On a success, roll the slide die **twice** and use the result you prefer.
4. **Roll the slide die (d6)** to see where the bird is when you fire:

| d6 | Where the bird is | Bird's AC |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | In the open | 16 |
| 3–4 | Half behind a screen (half cover) | 18 |
| 5–6 | Mostly behind a screen (three-quarters cover) | 21 |

   - **The Sharpshooter feat ignores half and three-quarters cover**, so a Sharpshooter always shoots at **AC 16**.
5. **Make your ranged attack** against that AC. **A hit knocks off one piece**, whatever the damage.
6. **Whoever knocks off the 10th piece wins.**

### Year-end championship

Every monthly shoot scores season points. The top 8 qualify for the year-end final: **one final bird**, shot with the same rules. Whoever knocks off its last piece is the **Shooting King** (or Queen) for a year.

## Dice Examples

**Late in the shoot. 2 pieces left.** The order is Vess, Quill, Hennick.
- **Vess** (NPC gloom stalker ranger 5, longbow +9, Perception +5, no Sharpshooter).
- **Quill** (PC rogue 3, shortbow +5, Perception +6).
- **Old Hennick** (NPC retired archer, longbow +7, **Sharpshooter**).

**Vess**
- **Timing:** Perception **9** + 5 = **14**, beating DC 13. She rolls the slide die twice: **5** and **3**. She takes the 3: **half cover, AC 18**.
- **Shot:** **8** + 9 = 17. **A miss.** She has Extra Attack, but it's still one shot per turn.

**Quill**
- **Timing:** Perception **7** + 6 = **13**, a success. Slide die: **6** and **2**. He takes the 2: **in the open, AC 16**.
- **Shot:** **12** + 5 = 17. **A hit! 1 piece left.**

**Old Hennick**
- He skips the timing check. Slide die: **5**, three-quarters cover. **Sharpshooter ignores it: AC 16.**
- **Shot:** **11** + 7 = 18. **A hit! The body falls. Old Hennick is the Shooting King!**

**Edge case: no timing, bad slide.** If Vess had skipped the timing check and rolled a **6** on the slide die, she'd face **AC 21**, and would need a 12 or better on the d20 (45%). With Sharpshooter, she'd face AC 16 and need a 7 (70%).

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 2 gp per shooter, each monthly shoot.
- **Monthly purse:** 10 gp to the shoot's winner.
- **The year-end final:** no entry for qualifiers. The Shooting King or Queen gets:
  - a sash,
  - a free table at every arena event for a year,
  - and 20 gp.
- **The House keeps:** the entries, the betting cut and the seats.

## Odds

**One shot, by attack bonus:**

| Attack bonus | +5 | +7 | +9 | +11 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No timing check | 38% | 48% | 58% | 68% |
| Timing check succeeds | 44% | 54% | 64% | 74% |
| Sharpshooter (always AC 16) | 50% | 60% | 70% | 80% |

**Who wins:**
- **Six equal shooters:** about 17% each.
- **A mixed field** (Sharpshooter +7, ranger +9, two at +5, two at +3, no timing checks): the Sharpshooter wins **24%**, the ranger **23%**, each +5 shooter **15%**, and each +3 shooter **11%**.

Skill helps, but the slide and turn order still decide a lot. That's why the crowd loves to bet on it.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Your own weapon, magic items, spells, class features and feats (one shot per turn).
- Taking your time to time the slide.
- Shouting at the shooter before you. (Tradition.)

### What counts as cheating

- **Any help from the crowd**: spells or tricks from anyone who isn't the shooter.
- A spell or trick that touches, moves or holds the bird.
- A loosened piece on the bird, or a bribed slide operator.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bribe the slide operator to stop the bird in the open (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Your slide die counts as a 1 for one shot |
| Loosen the bird's last piece | Anyone before the shoot | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The last piece falls on any shot within 2 of the bird's AC |
| Hidden spell from the crowd: *bless* from within 30 ft, or *guidance* with a pat on the shoulder | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | *Bless*: +1d4 on attack rolls for up to 1 minute. *Guidance*: +1d4 on one Perception check to time the shot. |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The monthly shoots are a **public event**. The year-end final is a **special public event** (see the Championship Season rules above). Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

| Fine | Or days in the stocks / serving the event |

| 20 gp | 2 days |

### Cheating dice example

At the year-end final, Quill slips the slide operator 5 gp to stop the bird in the open on his shot.
- Quill: Persuasion **9** + 1 = **10**. The Steward: **13** + 5 = **18**. **Caught.**
- Quill is disqualified and banned from next year's shoot. His portrait goes on the Cheater's Wall, the town crier reads his name, and he pays double for drinks citywide.
- To clear his name: 20 gp, 2 days in the stocks or serving at next year's shoot, or wait a year.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Last year's Shooting King** never collected his sash. His free table has sat empty all year, with a full mug on it every night.
- **The bird** is carved fresh each year by the same old woodcarver. This year she's carved something into the body that only shows when it splits.
