# Champion (Spell Caster)

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A shimmering sphere of force hangs over the pit, big enough for two. The duelists step through its skin and it seals behind them. Inside, fire and lightning crack against the walls. Outside, the crowd feels only the hum.*

**Description:** A monthly duel between two spellcasters inside a **warding globe**. No spell can get in or out, so the crowd is safe and no one can help from outside. The **first duelist to drop below half their HP loses.** The spell levels allowed depend on the tier. Every duel earns **season points**, and at the end of the year the top duelists of each tier battle for the **Spell Caster** title.

## How to Play

### The globe

- A **sphere 40 ft across**, sitting on the pit floor. The duelists start **30 ft apart**.
- **Nothing magical crosses its skin**, in either direction. Spells cast inside stay inside; spells from outside can't reach in.
- **No teleporting** out of the globe, and **no flying** (the city's rule).

### Spell levels by tier

| Tier | Highest spell level allowed |
|---|---|
| 1 (levels 1–4) | 2nd |
| 2 (levels 5–10) | 4th |
| 3 (levels 11–16) | 6th |
| 4 (levels 17–20) | Any |

**Counterspell is allowed** where the tier permits it (3rd level, so Tier 2 and up).

### Magic items

Duelists may use **their own magic items**: wands, staffs, rings, cloaks, potions. An NPC duelist facing a PC carries one from the House vault.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Magic Items]]

### A duel, step by step

1. **Roll initiative.** Fight with normal combat rules. **Spells, cantrips and magic items** only: no weapon attacks.
2. **The duel ends** when a duelist drops **below half their HP maximum** (or yields). The other duelist wins.
   - **Exactly half is not below half.** The duel goes on.
3. **Healers** step in as soon as the globe opens.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Arena Ward]]

**Netted curtain:** down. The globe holds every spell inside.

### NPC duels

When no PC is dueling, settle it with **Clash**: see [[Arena Combat Rules#Clash]].

### Year-end championship

Every monthly duel scores season points, with **separate standings for each tier**. The top 8 of each tier qualify for that tier's year-end bracket. Each bracket winner is the **Spell Caster champion** of their tier for a year.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Championship Season]]

## Dice Examples

**Mirela** (PC bard 3, HP 18, so she loses below 9; AC 13; spell attack +6; save DC 14).
**Orrin Vex** (NPC wizard 3, HP 16, so he loses below 8; spell attack +5; save DC 13). He wears the House's *cloak of protection*: **AC 13** (12 + 1) and **Wis save +2** (+1 + 1).
Tier 1: spells of 2nd level and below.

**Round 1**
- **Orrin** casts *scorching ray* (2nd level): three rays, each +5 against AC 13, each 2d6 fire.
  - Ray 1: **12** + 5 = 17, hit: **3** + **4** = 7.
  - Ray 2: **6** + 5 = 11, a miss.
  - Ray 3: **8** + 5 = 13, hit (meeting the AC hits): **1** + **1** = 2.
  - Mirela takes 9 and is at **9**: **exactly half, so she's still in.**
- **Mirela** casts *dissonant whispers* (1st level) on Orrin. Wisdom save: **11** + 2 = 13, which fails against DC 14. Damage: **3** + **2** + **1** = **6**. Orrin is at **10**. He must use his reaction to move away from her, to the far side of the globe.

**Round 2**
- **Orrin** casts *fire bolt* (1d10): **4** + 5 = 9, a miss.
- **Mirela** casts *vicious mockery* (1d4). Wisdom save: **6** + 2 = 8, a fail. Damage: **3**. Orrin is at **7**, **below half**. **Mirela wins.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per duelist (Tier 1), 5 gp (Tier 2), 20 gp (Tier 3), 50 gp (Tier 4).
- **Purse:** 5× the entry to the winner.
- **The year-end final:** 25× the tier's entry to each bracket winner, and the champion's title for their tier.
- **The House keeps:** the entries, the betting cut and the seats.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any spell of the allowed level, including *shield*, *counterspell* and *misty step* (inside the globe).
- Your own magic items, including a potion drunk before the duel.
- Yielding at any time.

### What counts as cheating

- Tampering with the rival's magic item.
- Bribing the globe-keeper to call the duel early.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swap the rival's magic item for a fake | Anyone backstage | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival duels without their item |
| Bribe the globe-keeper (5× the entry) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The keeper calls the duel when the rival is at exactly half HP |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Champion (Spell Caster) is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. The fine depends on the tier.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Tier | Fine | Or days in the stocks / serving the event |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 gp | 1 day |
| 2 | 50 gp | 5 days |
| 3 | 200 gp | 20 days |
| 4 | 500 gp | 50 days |

### Cheating dice example

Before the duel, Quill tries to swap Orrin's House cloak for a fake.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **12** + 7 = **19**. The Steward: **16** + 5 = **21**. **Caught.**
- If Mirela was in on it, she forfeits the duel. Quill is banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 10 gp, 1 day in the stocks or serving, or wait 3 months.
