# Naumachia

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The pit is flooded to the brim, dark water slapping at the walls. Two rafts push off from opposite ends, crews heaving at the oars, pennants snapping at the stern. Somebody is going in the water. Probably everybody.*

**Description:** A monthly sea battle in the **flooded pit**. Two crews row their rafts at each other, **ram**, board and shove. **Sink the enemy raft** or put **every enemy sailor in the water** to win. Sinking pays more. Anyone in the water stays in the water, but they can still fight, drag sailors in and rock the enemy raft.

## How to Play

### The water and the rafts

- **The pit is flooded 10 ft deep.**
- **Each raft:** 15 ft square, **AC 11, 50 HP**, with **4 oars**. A pennant flies from a pole at its stern.
- **Weapons:** House wooden weapons (**club** 1d4, **staff** 1d6) and one **boarding axe** per crew (1d8 slashing, which only damages rafts). **No armour, no spells and no magic items.**

### A battle, step by step

1. **The rafts start at opposite walls**, **20 ft apart**.
2. **Roll initiative** for each sailor and fight with normal combat rules.
3. **Rowing:** at the **start of each round**, before anyone acts, each crew names its rowers. The raft moves **5 ft per rower** (up to 4 rowers, so 20 ft). Rowing uses the rower's **action** for that round.
4. **Ramming:** if a raft with **3 or more rowers** that round hits the other raft, it's a **ram**.
   - **Only one raft ramming:** the rammed raft takes **2d6** damage, and the rammer takes **1d6**.
   - **Both rafts ramming** (both have 3 or more rowers): **both** take **2d6**.
5. **Overboard:** a sailor who's shoved off a raft (see the shove rule in [[1 - Last Ring|Last Ring]]), or who's aboard when it sinks, lands in the water.
   - **No one climbs back aboard** any raft, or out of the pit, until the battle ends.
   - **Swimming** uses the normal rules: unless you have a swimming speed, each foot you swim costs 1 extra foot of movement.
6. **In the water**, on your turn you can swim and take **one action**:
   - **Attack** a sailor within reach, with **disadvantage**.
   - **Pull:** grab a sailor standing at the edge of a raft next to you. **Strength (Athletics) contest** against their Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics). If you win, they're in the water.
   - **Rock the raft:** **Strength (Athletics), DC 15.** On a success, every sailor on that raft makes a **Dexterity (Acrobatics) check, DC 10**, or falls in.
   - **The centre 5-ft square** of a raft is out of reach from the water. It's the safest spot aboard.
7. **The battle ends** when:
   - **The enemy raft sinks** (0 HP, from ramming or the boarding axe). **Your crew wins the big purse.**
   - **Every enemy sailor is in the water.** **Your crew wins.**
   - **Both rafts sink in the same ram:** a **draw**. Each sailor takes back their entry.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Arena Ward]]

**Healers in boats** fish out anyone who drops to 0 HP in the water. The Ward keeps them stable, so no one drowns.

**Netted curtain:** up (to keep the crowd dry and the splinters out).

### NPC battles

When no PC is aboard, settle it with **Clash**, using the **Groups** rule: see [[Arena Combat Rules#Clash]].

## Dice Examples

**The Red raft:** Thokk (PC, HP 28, AC 9, Athletics +5), Quill (PC, HP 21, AC 13, Athletics +0, Acrobatics +5) and Mirela (PC, HP 18, AC 12 without armour, Athletics +0, Acrobatics +2).
**The Blue raft:** Grit (NPC thug, HP 32, AC 10, club +4, 1d4 + 2, Athletics +2) and three dockhands (NPCs: HP 11, AC 10, club +4, 1d4 + 2, Athletics +2, Acrobatics +1).

**Round 1**
- **Rowing:** Blue rows with all three dockhands: **15 ft**. Red rows with Mirela: **5 ft**. The rafts were 20 ft apart, so they meet. Blue had 3 rowers and Red only 1, so **only Blue is ramming**. The Red raft takes 2d6: **4** + **3** = **7**, and is at 43 HP. The Blue raft takes 1d6: **3**, and is at 47 HP.
- **Thokk** shoves a dockhand: Athletics **16** + 5 = **21** against the dockhand's **8** + 2 = **10**. **Splash.**
- **Grit** shoves Quill: Athletics **14** + 2 = **16** against Quill's Acrobatics **9** + 5 = **14**. **Quill goes in.**
- **Quill**, in the water, swims to the Blue raft and pulls Grit: Athletics **15** + 0 = **15** against Grit's **8** + 2 = **10**. **Grit goes in too.**
- **The dockhand in the water** swings at Thokk with disadvantage: **12** and **7**, keeps 7: 7 + 4 = 11, which hits AC 9: **3** + 2 = **5**. Thokk is at 23.
- Mirela and the other two dockhands used their actions rowing.

**Round 2** (the rafts are touching, so no one rows)
- **Thokk** shoves a second dockhand: **17** + 5 = **22** against **5** + 2 = **7**. **Splash.**
- **Grit**, in the water, pulls Mirela: **11** + 2 = **13** against her Acrobatics **7** + 2 = **9**. **Mirela goes in.**
- **Quill** rocks the Blue raft: Athletics **16** + 0 = **16**, beating DC 15. The last dockhand aboard rolls Acrobatics: **6** + 1 = **7**, failing DC 10. **He falls in.**
- **Every Blue sailor is in the water. Red wins** the 20 gp purse. Thokk is the only one still dry.

**Edge case: a double sinking.** Say both rafts are down to **6 HP**, and both crews row with 3 or more, so **both are ramming**. Each raft takes 2d6: Red takes **4** + **3** = **7**, Blue takes **5** + **2** = **7**. **Both sink at once.** It's a **draw**, and every sailor takes back their entry.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Ramming, boarding and shoving.
- From the water: attacking (with disadvantage), pulling sailors in and rocking rafts.
- Using the boarding axe on the enemy raft (not on sailors).

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- Climbing back aboard a raft or out of the pit.
- A weakened enemy raft (loosened lashings), or bribing the healers' boats to get in the way.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loosen the enemy raft's lashings | Anyone in the boat-shed | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The enemy raft starts at 35 HP |
| Bribe a healer's boat to drift in the way (2 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | One enemy swimmer can't reach your raft for a round |
| Hidden spell (*gust of wind*, *grease*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Naumachia is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A crew that's in on it is disqualified.

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 10 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

The night before, Quill sneaks into the boat-shed to loosen the Blue raft's lashings.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **16** + 7 = **23**. The Steward: **19** + 5 = **24**. **Caught.**
- The Red crew is disqualified. Quill is banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 10 gp, 1 day serving (bailing out the pit), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The sluice-master** controls the flooding. He says something big lives in the cistern that feeds the pit, and it gets in when the gates are open too long.
- **A retired admiral** pays for front-row seats every month and takes notes on the crews.
