# Tug over the Pit

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A thick rope stretches across the flooded pit, one team on each rim. Heels dig in, the rope creaks, and one by one, the losers are going over the edge into the water.*

**Description:** A weekly tug-of-war across the **flooded pit**. Two teams of four pull from **opposite rims** of the first floor. Every pull, each team picks a tactic in secret, then **every puller rolls**. The rope moves toward the stronger pull and **never resets**. When the rope drags a puller to the rim, **they go in the water** and their team pulls on with one fewer. **The first team with all four in the water loses.**

## How to Play

### The setup

- **The pit is flooded.** The water sits 5 ft below the first floor.
- **The teams stand on opposite rims.** Each team sets its **line-up** before the match: the 4 pullers stand in a line, **5 ft apart**.
- **The rope has a centre ribbon.** It starts over the middle of the pit. Each **mark** is 5 ft.
- **Where each puller falls:** the front puller stands **3 marks** from the rim, the second **4 marks**, the third **5 marks** and the last **6 marks**.

### A pull, step by step

1. **Pick a tactic in secret.** Both teams reveal at once.
   - **Heave** beats **Jerk** (you soak up their yank).
   - **Jerk** beats **Anchor** (you catch them flat-footed).
   - **Anchor** beats **Heave** (they can't shift you).
   - **The team whose tactic wins has advantage on this pull.** If both pick the same tactic, nobody gets advantage.
2. **Every puller still on the rope** makes a **Strength (Athletics)** check, **DC 12**. **Each success is 1 point.**
3. **The rope moves 1 mark for each point of difference**, toward the team with more points. *Example:* Red scores 3 and Blue scores 1, so the rope moves **2 marks** toward Red. **A tie: the rope doesn't move.**
4. **The rope stays where it is.** It never goes back to the centre. The next pull starts from there.
5. **Into the water.** When the rope moves toward Blue, Red is dragged toward the pit (and the other way round). After each pull, check how far the rope has dragged the losing team. **Every puller whose spot the rope has reached goes in:**

| Marks your team has been dragged | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| In the water | Front puller | Front and second | Front, second and third | All four: **you lose** |

   - **Two can go in on one pull.** The rope is at 3 marks toward Blue (Red's front puller is in) and Blue wins the pull by 2. The rope moves to 5 marks, so Red's pullers at **4 and 5** both go in.
   - **A puller in the water is out for good**, even if the rope swings back. Their team rolls **one die fewer** for each puller in the water.
6. **The first team with all four in the water loses.**

No spells and no magic items.

## Dice Examples

**Red** (front to back): Oswin (NPC, Athletics +2), Quill (PC, +0), Mirela (PC, +0), Thokk (PC, +5).
**Blue** (front to back): three dockhands (NPCs, +2 each), then Kord (NPC, +5).

**Pull 1**
- **Tactics:** Red Heave, Blue Anchor. **Anchor beats Heave: Blue has advantage.**
- **Red:** Oswin **11** + 2 = 13 ✓. Quill **9** ✗. Mirela **14** ✓. Thokk **6** + 5 = 11 ✗. **2 points.**
- **Blue:** dockhand **4** and **13**, keeps 13: 15 ✓. Dockhand **12** and **3**, keeps 12: 14 ✓. Dockhand **7** and **5**, keeps 7: 9 ✗. Kord **2** and **15**, keeps 15: 20 ✓. **3 points.**
- **The rope:** Blue by 1. It's **1 mark toward Blue.**

**Pull 2**
- **Tactics:** Red Jerk, Blue Heave. **Heave beats Jerk: Blue has advantage.**
- **Red:** Oswin **5** + 2 = 7 ✗. Quill **3** ✗. Mirela **10** ✗. Thokk **13** + 5 = 18 ✓. **1 point.**
- **Blue:** dockhand **15** and **9**, keeps 15: 17 ✓. Dockhand **8** and **11**, keeps 11: 13 ✓. Dockhand **6** and **4**, keeps 6: 8 ✗. Kord **3** and **9**, keeps 9: 14 ✓. **3 points.**
- **The rope:** Blue by 2. It's **3 marks toward Blue.** **Oswin is dragged over the rim. Splash!** Red has **3 pullers** left.

**Pull 3**
- **Tactics:** Red Anchor, Blue Heave. **Anchor beats Heave: Red has advantage.**
- **Red** (3 dice): Quill **8** and **14**, keeps 14 ✓. Mirela **17** and **2**, keeps 17 ✓. Thokk **11** and **4**, keeps 11: 16 ✓. **3 points.**
- **Blue:** **9** + 2 = 11 ✗. **13** + 2 = 15 ✓. **5** + 2 = 7 ✗. Kord **10** + 5 = 15 ✓. **2 points.**
- **The rope:** Red by 1. It's back to **2 marks toward Blue.** Oswin stays in the water.

**Pull 4**
- **Tactics:** both Jerk. **No advantage.**
- **Red** (3 dice): Quill **6** ✗. Mirela **12** ✓. Thokk **4** + 5 = 9 ✗. **1 point.**
- **Blue:** **14** + 2 = 16 ✓. **11** + 2 = 13 ✓. **8** + 2 = 10 ✗. Kord **1** + 5 = 6 ✗. **2 points.**
- **The rope:** Blue by 1. It's **3 marks toward Blue** again. Oswin's spot is already empty, and Quill stands at 4, so **nobody falls.**

**Pull 5 (edge case: two go in at once)**
- **Tactics:** Red Anchor, Blue Jerk. **Jerk beats Anchor: Blue has advantage.**
- **Red** (3 dice): Quill **10** ✗. Mirela **7** ✗. Thokk **9** + 5 = 14 ✓. **1 point.**
- **Blue:** dockhand **3** and **16**, keeps 16: 18 ✓. Dockhand **12** and **8**, keeps 12: 14 ✓. Dockhand **2** and **10**, keeps 10: 12 ✓. Kord **6** and **4**, keeps 6: 11 ✗. **3 points.**
- **The rope:** Blue by 2. It moves from 3 to **5 marks toward Blue**. **Quill (4) and Mirela (5) both go in.** Red has **1 puller** left.

**Pull 6**
- **Tactics:** both Heave. **No advantage.**
- **Red** (1 die): Thokk **14** + 5 = 19 ✓. **1 point.**
- **Blue:** **13** + 2 = 15 ✓. **5** + 2 = 7 ✗. **9** + 2 = 11 ✗. Kord **8** + 5 = 13 ✓. **2 points.**
- **The rope:** Blue by 1. It's **6 marks toward Blue**. **Thokk goes in. All four of Red are in the water: Blue wins.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **The House keeps:** the entries, the betting cut and the seats.

## Odds

**One pull**, two teams of equal pullers (+2), no tactic advantage:

| Pullers on the rope | Win the pull | Lose the pull | Tie |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 against 4 | 36% | 36% | 28% |
| 3 against 4 | 21% | 52% | 27% |
| 2 against 4 | 9% | 69% | 22% |
| 1 against 4 | 2% | 85% | 13% |

**The whole match** (every puller on a team has the same bonus):

| Difference in bonus | 0 | +1 | +2 | +3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The stronger team wins | 50% | 64% | 76% | 86% |
| Average pulls | 8 | 8 | 8 | 7 |

**The first splash decides almost everything.** A team that loses its front puller first wins less than 1% of the time.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any tactic, and any line-up (set before the match).
- Shouting to throw the other team's rhythm off.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- Greasing the other team's end, or hidden cleats in your boots.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hidden cleats (spikes nailed into the soles of your boots) | Any puller, when the boots are checked at the rim | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | You have advantage on your own checks all match |
| Grease the other team's end | Anyone before the match | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The other team has −2 on its checks all match |
| Hidden spell (*enhance ability*, Bull's Strength) | A caster in the crowd, touching a puller before the match | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | That puller has advantage on their checks all match |

Tug over the Pit is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A team that's in on it is disqualified.

### Cheating dice example

Quill hides cleats in his boots before the match.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **17** + 7 = **24**. The Steward: **18** + 5 = **23**. Not caught.
- Quill has advantage on every check this match. **Pull 1 again:** Quill rolls **9** and **15**, keeps 15 ✓. Red scores **3**, the same as Blue, so **the rope doesn't move.**

## Hooks / NPCs
