# Capture the Crystal

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A glowing crystal sits on a plinth in the middle of the pit, humming. Two teams of three crouch at opposite walls. It's heavy, it's slow to carry, and the moment you pick it up, everybody wants to hit you.*

**Description:** A weekly team game. Two teams of three fight over **one crystal**. Carry it into the **enemy's goal** to score a **capture**. The carrier is slow, and **a carrier who is hit drops it**. The first team to **3 captures** wins.

## How to Play

### The field

- **The pit floor:** 50 ft by 50 ft.
- **The crystal** starts on a plinth in the middle.
- **Each team's goal:** a 10-ft circle painted against its own back wall. **You score in the enemy's goal.**

### The rules

- **Weapons:** House wooden weapons (**club** 1d4, **staff** 1d6) and **cantrips**. **No armour, no levelled spells and no magic items.**
- **No teleporting and no flying.**

### A game, step by step

1. **Roll initiative** and fight with normal combat rules.
2. **Picking up the crystal takes an action.** It needs both hands.
3. **The carrier:**
   - Moves at **half speed** and **can't Dash**.
   - Can't attack or cast spells while holding it.
   - Can **hand it to a teammate** next to them (a free object interaction).
   - **Drops it in their space if hit** by any attack, or if they fail a saving throw against a damaging spell.
4. **Capture:** the carrier steps into the enemy's goal. Score 1. **The crystal goes back to the plinth.** Anyone who's down gets up at 1 HP (the healers), and both teams go back to their walls.
5. **The first team to 3 captures wins.**

![[Arena Combat Rules#Arena Ward]]

**Netted curtain:** up.

### NPC games

When no PC is playing, settle it with **Clash**: each exchange is one capture, and the first team to 3 wins. Use the **Groups** rule if the teams are different sizes: see [[Arena Combat Rules#Clash]].

## Dice Examples

**The Red team:** Thokk (PC, speed 30, staff +5, 1d6 + 3), Quill (PC, speed 30, AC 13) and Mirela (PC, *vicious mockery*, DC 14).
**The Blue team:** Sela (NPC, AC 14), Grit (NPC, club +4, 1d4 + 2) and Oswin (NPC, club +4, 1d4 + 2).

The crystal is **15 ft** from the edge of each goal.

**Round 1**
- **Quill** runs 25 ft to the plinth and grabs the crystal (his action). The carrier can't Dash, so Cunning Action doesn't help.
- **Grit** charges Quill: **15** + 4 = 19 against AC 13, a hit: **2** + 2 = 4. **Quill drops the crystal.** He's at 17.
- **Thokk** moves next to the crystal and attacks Grit to clear the way: **13** + 5 = 18, a hit: **4** + 3 = 7.
- **Sela** grabs the crystal for Blue (her action).
- **Mirela** casts *vicious mockery* on Sela. Wisdom save: **8** + 1 = 9, a fail against DC 14: **2** damage. **Sela failed a save against a damaging spell, so she drops the crystal.**
- **Oswin** moves in and swings at Thokk: **6** + 4 = 10, a miss.

**Round 2**
- **Quill** picks it up again (his action). He uses **Cunning Action to Disengage** (allowed; only Dash is banned), so leaving Grit's reach provokes no opportunity attack. He moves **15 ft** at half speed, to the edge of Blue's goal.
- **Grit** follows and attacks Quill: **5** + 4 = 9, a miss.
- **Thokk** steps between Quill and Sela.
- **Sela** can't reach Quill past Thokk, so she attacks Thokk instead.
- **Oswin** is busy with Mirela: **12** + 4 = 16 against AC 13, a hit for **3** + 2 = 5.

**Round 3**
- **Quill** Disengages again and steps into Blue's goal. **Red scores: 1–0.**

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Body-blocking, shoving and grappling the carrier.
- Picking up the crystal where it was dropped.

### What counts as cheating

- **Levelled spells and magic items.** Teleporting or flying.
- A weighted crystal swapped in, or a rival team's water drugged.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sticky gloves (tar on the palms) | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | You don't drop the crystal on the first hit |
| Drug a rival's water | Anyone on the bench | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | That rival is **poisoned** for the game |
| Hidden spell: *longstrider* by touch before the game, or *grease* from within 60 ft | A player or a friend (*longstrider*), or a caster in the crowd (*grease*) | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | *Longstrider*: +10 ft speed, so a speed 30 carrier moves 20 ft instead of 15. *Grease*: a 10-ft square turns slick, and anyone in it makes a Dexterity save or falls prone. |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Capture the Crystal is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A team that's in on it is disqualified.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 10 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Quill tars his palms before the game.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **10** + 7 = **17**. The Steward: **9** + 5 = **14**. Not caught.
- In round 1, Grit's hit doesn't make Quill drop the crystal. Next turn, Quill walks it 15 ft to the edge of Blue's goal.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The crystal** is real: a lump of raw arcane quartz the House bought cheap. Lately it hums louder when one particular spectator is in the stands.
- **Team Blue** has won six weeks running. Sela insists it's teamwork. Grit won't meet anyone's eye.
