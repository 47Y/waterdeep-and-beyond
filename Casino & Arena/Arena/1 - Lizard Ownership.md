# Lizard Ownership

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Every Derby Champion started as a hatchling chasing crickets in a stable stall. Buy one, name it, train it, and maybe one day its name goes up on the arena wall.*

**Description:** Anyone can buy a racing lizard from the arena stables, train it during downtime, enter it in [[1 - Lizard Races|Lizard Races]], and sell it on. Training earns **Training Levels**. Each level gives the lizard a **trait**, such as a faster sprint or a calmer temper, and moves it up to tougher race classes with bigger purses.

## How It Works

1. **Buy a lizard** from the arena stables (see Breeds below) and give it a name.
2. **Stable it.** Stalls at the arena cost **7 sp per week** for feed and bedding. If you don't pay, the stablemaster holds the lizard until you settle up.
3. **Train it** once per week (see Training below).
4. **Race it** in [[1 - Lizard Races|Lizard Races]]. Its Training Level decides which class it races in.
5. **Sell it** back to the stables whenever you like (see Selling below).

### Breeds

| Breed | Price | Speed | Temper DC | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mud Skink | 5 gp | +0 | 8 | Slow, but will do anything for a cricket |
| Racing Gecko | 25 gp | +1 | 10 | The common track lizard |
| Sand Runner | 100 gp | +2 | 13 | Fast and moody |
| Frilled Dasher | 500 gp | +3 | 16 | Fastest on the sand, and nearly impossible to handle |

**Speed** is added to every race roll. **Temper DC** is what a handler must beat to urge or train the lizard. Faster lizards are harder to handle, so a skilled handler with a Gecko can beat a clumsy one with a Dasher.

### Training

Training takes **one week of downtime** per session.

1. **Pay for the week:** 5 gp × (current Training Level + 1). That's 5 gp at Level 0, 10 gp at Level 1, and so on up to 25 gp at Level 4.
2. **Choose who trains it.**
   - **The owner trains it:** the owner rolls.
   - **Hire a trainer:** costs **+5 gp per week**. The stable trainer has Animal Handling **+4**.
3. **Roll Wisdom (Animal Handling)** against the lizard's **Temper DC**.
   - **Success:** the lizard earns **1 Training Mark**.
   - **Natural 20:** the lizard earns **2 Training Marks**.
   - **Failure:** no mark this week. The lizard just wasn't in the mood, and nothing bad happens.
4. **Every 3 Training Marks** raise the lizard by **1 Training Level**, up to a maximum of **Level 5**. Marks reset to 0 when the level goes up.
5. **Each new level, choose one trait** from the table below.

### Traits

| Trait | Effect | Max |
|---|---|---|
| Swift | +1 Speed | Twice |
| Calm | Temper DC −2 | Twice |
| Fast Starter | +2 on Round 1 (The Gate) | Once |
| Strong Finisher | +2 on the final round (The Home Stretch) | Once |
| Sand Swimmer | +2 on The Sand Pit round | Once |
| Steady Nerves | Can't be spooked. A "spook a rival" cheat against it fails automatically. | Once |
| Bonded | Once per race, the handler may reroll a failed urge | Once |

### Training Level and Race Class

| Training Level | Race Class |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | Novice |
| 2–3 | Veteran |
| 4–5 | Champion |

Entry fees for each class are in [[1 - Lizard Races#Race Classes|Lizard Races]].

### Selling

The stables pay **half the breed price + 20 gp per Training Level**. A **Derby Champion** sells for **double** that.

*Example:* Sir Scuttles is a Racing Gecko at Level 2. He sells for 12 gp 5 sp + 40 gp = **52 gp 5 sp**, or **105 gp** if he's a Derby Champion.

## Dice Examples

**Mirela** (PC, Animal Handling **+3**) buys **Sir Scuttles**, a Racing Gecko (Speed +1, Temper DC 10), for 25 gp and trains him herself.

**Week 1**
- Pays 5 gp (Level 0).
- Rolls **12** + 3 = **15** against DC 10. Success, so 1 mark. *(1 of 3)*

**Week 2**
- Pays 5 gp.
- Rolls **4** + 3 = **7**. Failure, so no mark. *(1 of 3)*

**Week 3**
- Pays 5 gp.
- Rolls a **natural 20**, which earns 2 marks. *(3 of 3)*
- **Sir Scuttles reaches Level 1.** Mirela picks **Calm**, so his Temper DC drops from 10 to **8**. Marks reset to 0.

**Week 4** (training now costs 10 gp a week)
- Mirela is off adventuring, so she pays 10 gp plus 5 gp for the stable trainer.
- The trainer rolls **9** + 4 = **13** against DC 8. Success, so 1 mark. *(1 of 3)*

**Total spent so far:** 25 gp (purchase) + 15 gp (weeks 1–3) + 15 gp (week 4) + 2 gp 8 sp (4 weeks of stabling) = **57 gp 8 sp**.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- **Training is private.** Nobody polices your stable, so magic is allowed during training. For example, *speak with animals* gives advantage on that week's training check.
- **Traits are legal on race day.** They are natural ability the lizard has earned.

### What counts as cheating

All race-day rules apply. See [[1 - Lizard Races#Rulings & Cheating|Lizard Races]]. Magic used on a lizard on race day is still cheating, even if it was allowed in training.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Hesta the stablemaster** knows every lizard's bloodline and will buy any lizard, no questions asked. That is exactly why the city watch keeps an eye on her.
- **After Sir Scuttles' first win,** a rival owner offers 3× his sale value for him. Then the offer turns into a threat.
