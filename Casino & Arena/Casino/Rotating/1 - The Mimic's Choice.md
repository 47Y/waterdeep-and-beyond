# The Mimic's Choice

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Three iron-bound chests sit on a velvet stage. One holds a golden crown. The other two hold clockwork mimics, all brass teeth and springs, built to snap shut on greedy fingers. Pick a chest. Then the host opens one of the others... and offers you a chance to change your mind.*

**Description:** A guessing game with a twist. The player picks one of three chests. The host, who knows where the crown is, opens one of the **other** chests to reveal a mimic, then offers the player the chance to **switch** to the last unopened chest. Most players stick with their first choice. They shouldn't.

## How to Play

### A round, step by step

1. **Bet.** Place your bet.
2. **Hide the crown.** The host shows the crown to the crowd, draws a curtain and rolls a die behind it to choose a chest. At the table, the DM secretly rolls a **d6**: **1–2** Chest A, **3–4** Chest B, **5–6** Chest C.
3. **Pick a chest.** The curtain opens. The player names one chest and stands beside it.
4. **The host opens a mimic.** The host opens one of the **two chests the player didn't pick**, always one with a mimic. (If both have mimics, the host picks at random.) The mimic snaps at the air and the crowd shrieks.
5. **Stay or switch.** The player keeps their chest or switches to the other closed chest.
6. **Open it.** The host opens the player's chest.
   - **The crown:** the player wins **2:5** (2 for every 5 bet).
   - **A mimic:** it snaps shut on the host's padded glove, and the player **loses**.

**The mimics are clockwork.** Pibble Brasswhistle, the gnome who builds the [[1 - The Enchanted Triad|Enchanted Triad]] machines, built them. A spring snaps the jaws shut on the host's padded glove, never on players.

## Dice Examples

**On stage:** Mirela (PC) bets **5 gp** each round.

**Round 1: switching wins**
- The DM rolls **4**: the crown is in **Chest B**.
- Mirela picks **Chest A**. The host opens **Chest C**: a mimic.
- Mirela **switches** to Chest B. **The crown!** She wins 2:5: **2 gp**.

**Round 2: switching loses**
- The DM rolls **1**: the crown is in **Chest A**.
- Mirela picks **Chest A**. The host opens **Chest B**: a mimic.
- She **switches** to Chest C: a mimic. She **loses** 5 gp. (Switching only loses when the first pick was right.)

**Round 3: staying loses**
- The DM rolls **6**: the crown is in **Chest C**.
- Thokk (NPC) bets 5 gp, picks **Chest B**, and the host opens **Chest A**: a mimic.
- Thokk **stays** with Chest B: a mimic. He **loses** 5 gp. Switching would have won.

**Edge case: the host's choice**
- The crown is in **Chest A**, and Thokk bets 5 gp and picks **Chest A**. Both other chests have mimics, so the host opens either one. Thokk stays and **wins 2 gp**.

## Odds

| Choice | Chance to win | Pays | House edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Always switch** | 67% (2 in 3) | 2:5 | 6.7% |
| **Always stay** | 33% (1 in 3) | 2:5 | 53.3% |

**Why switching works:** your first pick is right only 1 time in 3. The other 2 times, the crown is in one of the other chests and the host has just opened the one without it, so switching wins.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Staying or switching.
- Asking the crowd, praying, or talking to the chests.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that reveals the crown, such as *locate object*.
- Tipping a chest to feel its weight, or bribing the host.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| As the mimic snaps and every eye is on it, tip the chest beside you a finger's width. A mimic is heavy and a crown isn't | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Know whether your chest holds the crown, so you know whether to stay or switch |
| Bribe the host (Silver Table 1 gp, Gold Table 10 gp, Platinum Salon 100 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | For your next 5 rounds, the host rests a hand on the chest that holds the crown as the curtain opens |
| Hidden spell (*locate object* on the crown, which you've seen from within 30 feet) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | For up to 10 minutes, you know which chest holds the crown |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Mimic's Choice is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Quill (PC) bets 20 gp at the Gold Table and stands beside Chest B. The host opens Chest C, and as the mimic snaps, Quill tips Chest B a finger's width.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **14** + 7 = **21**. The Watcher: **10** + 5 = **15**. **Not caught.**
- The chest is heavy, so it holds a mimic. He switches to Chest A: the crown. He wins 2:5: **8 gp**.
- If the Watcher had rolled 16 or higher, Quill would have been caught. His chips and the night's winnings are taken, his portrait goes in the House's file, he's banned for a month, and he owes **100 gp** (five times his 20 gp bet) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Gnasher**, the oldest of the clockwork mimics, has started refusing to open for one particular host. Pibble swears the springs are fine, and says none of the mimics were built to blink.
- **A retired teacher** stands by the stage explaining why switching wins, at 1 sp to hear it. A mule driver who lost 40 gp by switching last night wants his money back from her, loudly, and half the crowd agrees. She offers to prove it with three cups and thirty rounds, right there, and to pay him the 40 gp if switching loses. She doesn't have 40 gp. He won't trust her hands, so she asks a party member to run the cups.
