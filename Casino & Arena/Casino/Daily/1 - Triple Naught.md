# Triple Naught

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A coin drops, a lever falls, and three brass wheels spin in their frame. They click to a stop one at a time. Zero. Zero. The whole floor turns to look.*

**Description:** A coin machine. Each player pays one coin and pulls a lever, and three wheels numbered 0 to 9 spin and stop. Matching numbers and runs pay small prizes. Three zeroes, a 1 in 1,000 chance, pays the **jackpot**. It needs no skill and no decisions, and it's one of the House's best earners.

## How to Play

### The machine

Each machine has **three brass wheels** in an open frame, each numbered 0 to 9. When you play it, **each wheel is one d10**, read as 0 to 9. One player uses a machine at a time. Every machine takes **silver, gold or platinum** coins.

### A pull, step by step

1. **Pay.** Put one coin in the machine: **1 sp**, **1 gp** or **1 pp**. You choose for each pull, and a bigger coin pays bigger prizes.
2. **Pull the lever.** Roll **three d10**, one at a time, from the left wheel to the right wheel.
3. **Read the wheels** from left to right, using the table below. A pull counts as the one result it matches.
4. **Collect.** An attendant pays any winnings. Then you can pull again.

| Result | What the wheels show | Example | Pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jackpot** | Three zeroes | 0, 0, 0 | 250:1 |
| **Triple** | Three of the same number, other than zero | 7, 7, 7 | 10:1 |
| **Run** | Three numbers in a row, going up or down | 3, 4, 5 or 5, 4, 3 | 5:1 |
| **Pair** | Two of the same number, side by side | 4, 4, 9 or 9, 4, 4 | 1:1 |
| Nothing | Anything else | 4, 9, 2 | Lose |

Two things can look like a win and aren't:
- **Matching numbers must be side by side.** 4, 9, 4 pays nothing, because the wheel in the middle splits them.
- **A run doesn't go past 9.** 0, 1, 2 is a run, but 8, 9, 0 and 9, 0, 1 pay nothing.

## Dice Examples

**At the machine:** Mirela (PC) plays with gold pieces, so each pull costs her **1 gp**.

**Pull 1**
- Wheels: **4, 9, 2**. Nothing. She **loses** 1 gp.

**Pull 2**
- Wheels: **4, 4, 9**. A pair, side by side. She keeps her coin and wins **1 gp**.

**Pull 3**
- Wheels: **3, 4, 5**. A run. She keeps her coin and wins **5 gp**.

**Edge case: two zeroes**
- Wheels: **0, 0, 7**. This isn't the jackpot. It's a pair, so she keeps her coin and wins **1 gp**.

**Edge case: the jackpot**
- Wheels: **0, 0, 0**. She keeps her coin and wins **250 gp**, and the attendant rings the bell.

## Prizes & Stakes

| Coin | Pair | Run | Triple | Jackpot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 sp | 1 sp | 5 sp | 1 gp | **25 gp** |
| 1 gp | 1 gp | 5 gp | 10 gp | **250 gp** |
| 1 pp | 1 pp | 5 pp | 10 pp | **250 pp (2,500 gp)** |

These are the winnings. You also keep the coin you paid for that pull.

## Odds

| Result | Chance |
|---|---|
| Jackpot | 0.1% (1 pull in 1,000) |
| Triple | 0.9% |
| Run | 1.6% |
| Pair | 18% |
| Nothing | 79.4% |

**Chance to win anything: 20.6%**, about 1 pull in 5.

**House edge: 19.4%.** Over 1,000 pulls with gold pieces, players pay in 1,000 gp and get about 806 gp back.

**The House's biggest risk:** one jackpot on a platinum coin, which pays 2,500 gp. Every pull has the same chance, so a machine is never "due" to pay.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- As many pulls as you like, and moving to whichever machine feels lucky.
- Kissing your coin and talking to the machine.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the wheels, such as *unseen servant*.
- Fake coins, working the lever to control a wheel, or bribing the mechanic.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drop a lead slug in place of a real coin | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | This pull is free |
| Ease the lever with a practised hand, so one wheel barely turns | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Choose the number on one wheel this pull |
| Bribe the mechanic to file notches into a machine's wheels (10 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | For your next 10 pulls on that machine, until the next inspection, a 9 on any wheel counts as a 0 |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant* nudge) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | As a wheel slows, the invisible servant stops it on the number of your choice |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Triple Naught is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Quill (PC) puts in a gold piece. He eases the lever, trying to hold the left wheel on 0.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **11** + 7 = **18**. The Watcher: **14** + 5 = **19**. **Caught.**
- His chips and the night's winnings are taken, his portrait goes in the House's file, he's banned for a month, and he owes **5 gp** (five times his 1 gp pull) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **A clerk with a ledger** has written down every pull for a tenday and swears one machine is due for the jackpot. He's selling shares to anyone who'll fund his pulls.
- **A stranger** hits the jackpot on a platinum coin, then offers the party a share of it to see them safely home with 2,500 gp in their bags.
