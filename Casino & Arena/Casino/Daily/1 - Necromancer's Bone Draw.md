# Necromancer's Bone Draw

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A pale dealer in a raven-feather cloak shakes a velvet bag of bones. Three white, two black, one gilded. "Two bones," she whispers. "Let the dead decide your fortune."*

**Description:** A spooky bag-draw game. A velvet bag holds **six bones**: three white, two black and one gold. The dealer draws **two** without looking. Players bet that both bones will match in color, or that the gold bone will be drawn.

## How to Play

### The bag

At the table, each bone is one number on a d6:

| d6 | Bone |
|---|---|
| 1, 2, 3 | White |
| 4, 5 | Black |
| 6 | **Gold** |

### A round, step by step

1. **Bet.** Each player bets on one or both:

   | Bet | Wins when… | Pays |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Same Color** | Both bones are the same color (two white or two black) | 5:2 |
   | **Gold Bone** | The gold bone is one of the two drawn | 9:5 |

2. **Draw.** The dealer reaches in without looking and draws two bones. Roll a **d6** for the first bone. Roll a **d6** again for the second bone; **if it shows the same number as the first, reroll it** (that bone is already out of the bag).
3. **Pay out.** The bones go back in the bag for the next round.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Thokk (NPC) bets **2 gp on Same Color**. Mirela (PC) bets **5 gp on Gold Bone**.

**Round 1**
- First bone: **2** (white). Second bone: **3** (white).
- Two whites: Thokk wins 5:2: **5 gp**. No gold: Mirela **loses**.

**Round 2**
- First bone: **6** (gold). Second bone: **4** (black).
- Not the same color: Thokk **loses**. Gold was drawn: Mirela wins 9:5: **9 gp**.

**Round 3**
- First bone: **1** (white). Second bone: **5** (black). Both **lose**.

**Edge case: a repeated number**
- First bone: **4** (black). Second roll: **4**, but bone 4 is already out of the bag, so **reroll**: **5** (black).
- Two blacks: Thokk wins **5 gp**.

## Odds

| Bet | Chance to win | Pays | House edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Same Color | 26.7% (two white 20%, two black 6.7%) | 5:2 | 6.7% |
| Gold Bone | 33.3% | 9:5 | 6.7% |

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any bet within the table limits.
- Whispering to the bones. The dealer encourages it.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the draw, such as *unseen servant* in the bag or *speak with dead* on the bones (they don't answer anyway).
- Slipping an extra bone into the bag, or bribing the dealer.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slip an extra white bone in, while the dealer holds out the bag for you to whisper to | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | It sits on top, so on the next draw the first bone is white |
| Bribe the dealer (Silver Table 1 gp, Gold Table 10 gp, Platinum Salon 100 gp) | Player | Cha (Persuasion) against the dealer's Wisdom (Insight) +3 first, then the dealer's Dex (Sleight of Hand) +5 against the Watcher | The dealer draws the gold bone |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant* in the bag) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The invisible servant finds the heavy gilded bone and pushes it up under the dealer's fingers: the first bone is gold |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Necromancer's Bone Draw is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

![[Cheating Penalties#Casino]]

### Cheating dice example

Quill (PC) bets 10 gp on Same Color and slips an extra white bone into the bag.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **17** + 7 = **24**. The Watcher: **20** + 5 = **25**. **Caught.**
- His chips and the night's winnings are taken, his portrait goes in the House's file, he's banned for a month, and he owes **50 gp** (five times his 10 gp bet) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Mistress Ossilia**, the dealer, is rumored to be a real necromancer, and the bones in her bag are said to be her former husbands. She laughs and says the gold one was her favorite. A nervous baker proposed to her last night, and she said yes. His mother offers the party 30 gp to find out if there's anything in the rumor before the wedding.
- **An old goldsmith** swears the gold bone was cast from his late wife's wedding ring, which he sold in a hard winter. Every night he bets on Gold Bone just to watch it come out of the bag, about 1 round in 3, and he's down to his last 5 gp. His son offers the party 20 gp to get him home. The goldsmith offers them his best tools to keep betting for him, so that he can sit and watch a little longer.
