# Shadow Hand

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Six players, six leather screens, six hidden dice. Nobody speaks. A gnome slides a stack of gold into the pot and smiles. Is that the smile of a twelve, or the smile of a liar?*

**Description:** A bluffing game played with one die each. Each player secretly rolls a **d12** as their "hand", may reroll it once, then bets on whether it's the highest at the table. Players can bluff, read each other, fold or raise. The highest hand at the showdown takes the pot, unless everyone else has folded first.

## How to Play

### A hand, step by step

The House gives each player a d12 and a leather screen. The player on the dealer's left goes first in every step, and that seat moves one place clockwise each hand.

1. **Ante.** Every player puts the same starting bet (the **ante**) into the middle of the table (the **pot**).
2. **Roll your hand.** Each player rolls **1d12** behind their screen and keeps it secret.
3. **Reroll (optional).** Going clockwise, each player may reroll their d12 **once**. They must keep the new result. After this, nobody touches their die until the showdown.
4. **Betting round.** Going clockwise, each player chooses one:
   - **Check:** bet nothing (only if nobody has bet yet).
   - **Bet:** put in one ante's worth.
   - **Call:** match the current bet.
   - **Raise:** match the current bet and add one more ante's worth. A hand allows **three raises** in total.
   - **Fold:** give up the hand and lose what you've already put in.

   Betting ends when every player still in has matched the highest bet. If everyone checks, go straight to the showdown. You can't bet more than you have, so if you can't match a bet, you must fold.
5. **Showdown.** Remaining players reveal their d12. The **highest number wins the pot**.
   - **Tied highest:** the tied players each roll a d12; highest wins. Roll again if they tie again.
   - **Everyone else folded:** the last player wins without showing their hand.
6. **The rake.** The House keeps **5%** of every pot, however it was won. The winner takes the rest.

### Reading a player

**Once per hand**, on your turn after someone has bet, you may roll **Wisdom (Insight)** against that player's **Charisma (Deception)** before you choose. If you win, the DM tells you truthfully whether their hand is **High (7–12)** or **Low (1–6)**. If you don't, you learn nothing. Ties go to the bettor.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Mirela (PC, Insight +2, Deception +7), Thokk (NPC, Insight +1, Deception −1) and Quill (PC, Insight +2, Deception +5), seated clockwise in that order. The ante is **2 gp**, so the pot starts at **6 gp**. Mirela goes first in Hand 1, Thokk in Hand 2 and Quill in Hand 3.

**Hand 1**
- Hands: Mirela rolls **4** and rerolls: **10**. Thokk rolls **8** and keeps it. Quill rolls **3** and rerolls: **5**.
- Betting: Mirela **bets 2 gp**.
- Thokk reads her: Insight **9** + 1 = **10** against Mirela's Deception **12** + 7 = **19**. He learns nothing, and **calls** 2 gp.
- Quill reads her: Insight **16** + 2 = **18** against Deception **4** + 7 = **11**. The DM says **"High."** Quill **folds**.
- Showdown: Mirela **10**, Thokk **8**. **Mirela wins** the 10 gp pot, minus 5 sp rake: **9 gp 5 sp**.

**Hand 2: a bluff**
- Hands: Quill rolls **1**, rerolls, and gets **2**. Mirela has **9**. Thokk has **6**.
- Thokk **checks**. Quill **bets 2 gp**.
- Mirela reads him: Insight **7** + 2 = **9** against Quill's Deception **15** + 5 = **20**. Nothing. She **folds**. Thokk **folds**.
- **Quill wins** the 8 gp pot without showing his 2, minus 4 sp rake: **7 gp 6 sp**.

**Hand 3**
- Hands: Mirela **7**, Thokk **12** (no reroll needed), Quill **6**.
- Quill and Mirela **check**. Thokk **bets 2 gp**. Quill **folds** and Mirela **calls**.
- Showdown: **Thokk wins** with 12. The 10 gp pot, minus 5 sp rake: **9 gp 5 sp**.

**Edge case: a tie at the showdown**
- Mirela and Thokk both show **11**. Each rolls a d12: Mirela **4**, Thokk **9**. **Thokk wins the pot.**

## Odds

**Chance your final hand wins the showdown**, if everyone has rerolled sensibly and nobody folds:

| Your hand | vs 1 opponent | vs 2 opponents | vs 3 opponents |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 12% | 2% | Under 1% |
| 6 | 27% | 9% | 4% |
| 9 | 54% | 27% | 15% |
| 10 | 67% | 43% | 26% |
| 11 | 80% | 63% | 48% |
| 12 | 93% | 87% | 80% |

- **When to reroll:** against one opponent, reroll **7 or lower**; against two, **8 or lower**; against three or more, **9 or lower**.
- **Equal players** win equally often: with 4 players, each wins about 1 hand in 4. The House's 5% rake means each player loses 5% of what they put in, on average. Good readers and good liars beat that.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Bluffing, lying about your hand and dramatic sighs.
- Reading players once per hand.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that reads hands or minds, such as *detect thoughts*.
- Peeking at another player's die, signaling a partner, or touching your die after the reroll step.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** Other players can also try to spot you.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| As you push in your chips and every eye follows them, turn your die with your other hand | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Set your hand to any number |
| As your neighbour tips their screen to check their die, catch it in a polished ring | Player next to the target | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Learn that player's exact hand |
| Stand in the crowd behind an opponent, read their die and signal your partner | A helper | Cha (Deception) to look casual | Your partner learns that opponent's exact hand |
| Hidden spell (*detect thoughts*) | A caster within 30 feet | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Learn one opponent's exact hand for the spell's 1 minute, about two hands |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Shadow Hand is played at a House table, so cheating at it is cheating the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. The pot is split evenly among the other players, including any who folded, and the House takes no rake on it. The debt is five times the ante.

![[Cheating Penalties#Casino]]

### Cheating dice example

At the Gold Table with a 20 gp ante, Thokk has a **3**. As he pushes in his chips, he tries to turn his die to a 12.
- Thokk: Sleight of Hand **19** − 1 = **18**. The Watcher: **8** + 5 = **13**. Thokk beats the Watcher.
- Quill is next to him: Perception **15** + 6 = **21**, beating 18. **Quill saw it.**
- Quill says nothing for now and can use it later. If he reports it, Thokk's chips and the night's winnings are taken, his portrait goes in the House's file, he's banned for a month, and he owes **100 gp** (five times the 20 gp ante) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **"The Masked Tell"** plays in a featureless silver mask, and nobody has ever read them. They've made a public promise: whoever makes them fold, then shows a hand of 3 or less, will see the face behind the mask. A gossip-sheet writer offers the party 100 gp for the name. A quiet stranger at the next table offers 200 gp to forget it.
- **A noble** put up her family's estate deed as her stake at the Platinum Salon, and lost it. She wants it won back, and she doesn't care how.
