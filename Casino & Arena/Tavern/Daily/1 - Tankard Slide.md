# Tankard Slide

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A full tankard, a long polished bar, and three painted rings, the last one right at the far end. Give it a shove. Too soft and it stops short. Go for the far ring and miss, and it sails off the end and clangs across the floor, and you pay for the mess. Just right, and it stops dead in the ring without spilling a drop.*

**Description:** A daily bar game. Players take turns sliding a **full metal tankard** down the bar, trying to stop it in one of **three painted rings**. The further the ring, the harder the slide and the more points it scores. The far ring sits right at the end of the bar, so a bad slide at it **flies off the end**, and you pay for the spilled ale and the mop-up. **Once someone reaches 5 points, the highest score wins**, and the losers buy everyone a mug of ale.

## How to Play

### Setting up

- **One metal House tankard**, full of ale. The barkeep sends it back up the bar after each slide, and refills it if it goes off the end.
- **Three rings** are painted on the bar: **Near** (halfway down), **Middle** (three-quarters of the way) and **Far** (right at the end).
- Sliders go in the order they sign up.

### Your slide, step by step

1. **Choose a ring** and say it aloud.
2. **Slide:** make a **Strength (Athletics)** or **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check.

   | Ring | DC | Points |
   |---|---|---|
   | Near | 10 | 1 |
   | Middle | 14 | 2 |
   | Far | 18 | 3 |

   - **Success:** it stops in the ring. Score the points.
   - **Failure:** it stops in the wrong place. No points.
   - **Failure at the Far ring by 5 or more:** it goes **off the end!** No points, and you pay 2 sp for the spilled ale and the mop-up.

### Winning

- When a slider reaches **5 or more points**, the sliders still to go this time around take their slides, so everyone has had the same number. The highest score wins the pot.
- **Ties:** the tied sliders each make one more slide at the Far ring. The higher total wins, and a slide that goes off the end still costs 2 sp. If the totals tie again, they slide again.
- **The losers buy everyone who played a mug of ale**, sharing the cost. It's just a drink, with no save.

## Dice Examples

**Sliders:** Thokk (NPC, Athletics +5), Quill (PC, Sleight of Hand +7) and Mirela (PC, Sleight of Hand +2).

**First time around**
- **Thokk** picks Middle (DC 14): **11** + 5 = **16**. **2 points.**
- **Quill** picks Far (DC 18): **13** + 7 = **20**. **3 points.**
- **Mirela** picks Near (DC 10): **12** + 2 = **14**. **1 point.**

**Second time around**
- **Thokk** picks Middle: **6** + 5 = **11**. It stops short. Still 2 points.
- **Quill** picks Far: **4** + 7 = **11**. That's 7 short of DC 18, so it goes **off the end!** Clang. Quill pays 2 sp. Still 3 points.
- **Mirela** picks Middle: **14** + 2 = **16**. **3 points.**

**Third time around**
- **Thokk** picks Middle: **15** + 5 = **20**. **4 points.**
- **Quill** only needs 2, so he picks Middle: **9** + 7 = **16**. **5 points.**
- **Mirela** also picks Middle: **13** + 2 = **15**. **5 points.**

**Edge case: a tie**
- Quill and Mirela both have 5, so each slides at the Far ring. Quill: **9** + 7 = **16**. Mirela: **8** + 2 = **10**, which is 8 short, so it goes off the end and she pays 2 sp. **Quill wins the pot.** Thokk and Mirela split the cost of three mugs of ale (1 sp 2 cp), so 6 cp each.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Pot:** 1 sp per player. The winner takes it.
- **The losers** share the cost of a mug of ale (4 cp) for everyone who played.
- **Off the end** costs 2 sp, paid to the House.
- **The House keeps:** the off-the-end money and the price of the ale.

## Odds

| Your bonus | Near | Middle | Far | Far goes off the end | Slides to reach 5 (Near / Middle / Far) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +0 | 55% | 35% | 15% | 65% | 9.1 / 8.6 / 13.3 |
| +2 | 65% | 45% | 25% | 55% | 7.7 / 6.7 / 8.0 |
| +5 | 80% | 60% | 40% | 40% | 6.3 / 5.0 / 5.0 |
| +7 | 90% | 70% | 50% | 30% | 5.6 / 4.3 / 4.0 |

**The Middle ring is the steady choice.** The Far ring only saves time at +7, and even then you can expect to lose about 2 sp a game. At +5 it takes the same number of slides as Middle and costs about 4 sp a game. It's still the right call when you're behind and need 3 points at once. The Near ring is for grabbing the last point.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Choosing Athletics or Sleight of Hand for each slide.
- Choosing any ring on any slide.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *unseen servant*, *guidance* or *prestidigitation*.
- Bribing the barkeep, or tampering with the bar or the tankard yourself.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bribe the barkeep (1 sp) to wipe the bar with a wet rag just before a rival's slide, as if tidying up | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The rival's slide has disadvantage |
| Bribe the barkeep (1 sp) to tip a mouthful out of the tankard as it comes back for your slide, so that it's lighter and easier to stop | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | That slide has advantage |
| Hidden spell: *guidance* by touch before your slide: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the back | A caster at the bar | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | +1d4 on one slide |
| Hidden spell: *prestidigitation* to soil the polished bar right before a rival's slide | A caster at the bar | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The rival's slide has disadvantage |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant*) | A caster at the bar or in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The invisible servant nudges the tankard to a stop in the ring: one failed slide that didn't go off the end counts as a success |

### Cheating dice example

Between slides, Quill offers the barkeep 1 sp to wipe the bar with a wet rag just before Thokk's next slide.
- Quill: Persuasion **14** + 1 = **15**. The Watcher: **15** + 5 = **20**. **Caught.**
- Quill is banned from the game for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. He still owes 2 sp for his off-the-end slide. To clear his name: 1 gp, 1 day serving (polishing the bar), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **A regular** has landed the Far ring three nights running. Somebody has started to wonder what's on the bar.
- **A sailor** bets a stranger a month's pay that he can land three Far rings in a row. He has already missed twice tonight, and he doesn't have a month's pay.
