# Axe at the Barrel

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Behind the tavern, a row of old barrels stands against the wall, their lids painted with targets. Step up to the line, throw a handaxe, and hear it thunk into the wood. Hit it hard enough and the stave splits with a crack, and the crowd cheers twice as loud.*

**Description:** A daily axe-throwing game in the yard. Players throw **handaxes** at a barrelhead. **A hit scores 1 point**, and a hit hard enough to **split a stave** (6 or more damage) scores **2**. The **first to 10 points** wins.

## How to Play

### A throw, step by step

1. **Make a ranged attack roll** with a handaxe (thrown: Strength modifier + proficiency). The barrelhead has **AC 13**.
   - **Miss:** no score.
   - **Hit:** roll the handaxe's damage (1d6 + Strength modifier).
2. **Score the hit:**

   | Damage | Points |
   |---|---|
   | 5 or less | 1 |
   | 6 or more (the stave splits) | 2 |

   **Critical hit (natural 20):** roll the damage dice twice, as normal for a critical hit.
3. Players take turns, **one throw each**.

### Winning

The **first to 10 points** wins, right away.

## Dice Examples

**Throwers:** Thokk (NPC, handaxe +5, damage 1d6 + 3) and Quill (PC, handaxe +2, damage 1d6 + 0).

| Throw | Thokk | Quill |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **12** + 5 = 17. Hit. Damage **2** + 3 = **5**: 1 point (1) | **15** + 2 = 17. Hit. Damage **6**: **2 points** (2) |
| 2 | **6** + 5 = 11. Miss (1) | **9** + 2 = 11. Miss (2) |
| 3 | **natural 20**. Damage **3** + **4** + 3 = **10**: 2 points (3) | **11** + 2 = 13. Hit. Damage **4**: 1 point (3) |

**And so on, until someone reaches 10.**

**Edge case: the finish**
- Thokk has 9 and Quill has 8. Thokk throws: **14** + 5 = **19**. Hit: 1 point gets him to 10. **Thokk wins.** (He didn't need the stave to split.)

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Lane fee:** 1 cp per throw.
- **Stake:** 1 sp per player. The winner takes it all, minus 5% for the House.
- **The House keeps:** the lane fees, the 5% and the new barrels it buys every week.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- The House's handaxes only (balanced and dull-edged).
- Features that add to thrown weapon attacks or damage, such as the Thrown Weapon Fighting Style.
- Rage (it adds to Strength damage).

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *magic weapon*, *shillelagh*, *bless*, *guidance*, *hunter's mark* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Your own sharpened axe, a pre-cracked stave, or stepping over the line.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Step over the line | Thrower | Dex (Stealth) | +2 to your attack this throw |
| Pre-crack your barrel's stave | Anyone, before the game | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Your hits need only 4 damage to split |
| Hidden spell: *bless* from within 30 ft, or *magic weapon* by touch on your axe before the game | A caster in the crowd (*bless*), or the thrower or a friend (*magic weapon*) | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | *Bless*: +1d4 on attack rolls for up to 1 minute. *Magic weapon*: +1 to attack and damage with that axe. |

Axe at the Barrel is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Quill creeps over the line on his 4th throw.
- Quill: Stealth **5** + 7 = **12**. The Watcher: **8** + 5 = **13**. **Caught.**
- Quill forfeits the game and his stake, is banned from the yard for 3 months, and is pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 1 gp, 1 day serving (stacking new barrels), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The yard's best thrower** is a retired executioner. He's very good, and very quiet, and nobody wants to beat him.
- **One barrel** at the end of the row is never replaced, no matter how split it gets. Something inside it knocks back.
