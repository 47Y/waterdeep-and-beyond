# Fey-Ring Chairs

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A fey fiddler with antlers in her hair plays a wild reel, faster and faster, as the contestants dance in a ring around too few chairs. Then the music stops dead. Everyone dives.*

**Description:** Musical chairs, tavern style. There's always **one chair fewer than the players**. When the fey fiddler stops playing, everyone scrambles for a seat. The slowest player can try to steal a chair at the last second. Whoever is left standing is out, a chair is taken away, and the music starts again.

## How to Play

### Setting up

Set out chairs in a ring: **one fewer chair than there are players**.

### A round, step by step

1. **The music plays.** Everyone dances around the ring.
2. **The music stops.** Everyone rolls **initiative** (d20 + Dexterity modifier). Highest totals grab chairs first. Ties for lowest roll again.
3. **The scramble.** The player with the **lowest** total has no chair. They can try to steal the chair of the player with the **second-lowest** total: a **DC 12 Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check.
   - **Success:** they slide in first. The **second-lowest** player is out.
   - **Failure:** the **lowest** player is out.
4. **Remove one chair**, and the music starts again.

### Winning

When two players are left with one chair, play one last round. The player who ends up in the last chair wins.

## Dice Examples

**Players:** Mirela (PC, Dex +2, Acrobatics +2), Quill (PC, Dex +3, Acrobatics +5), Thokk (NPC, Dex −1, Acrobatics −1) and "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Dex +0, Acrobatics +0). Three chairs.

**Round 1**
- Initiative: Mirela **14** + 2 = 16, Quill **9** + 3 = 12, Thokk **5** − 1 = **4**, Oswin **8** + 0 = **8**.
- Thokk is lowest (4) and tries to steal Oswin's chair (second-lowest, 8): Acrobatics **15** − 1 = **14**. Success! **Oswin is out.**
- One chair is removed. Two chairs left.

**Round 2**
- Initiative: Mirela **3** + 2 = **5**, Quill **17** + 3 = 20, Thokk **11** − 1 = **10**.
- Mirela is lowest and tries to steal Thokk's chair: Acrobatics **7** + 2 = **9**. Fails. **Mirela is out.**
- One chair left.

**Round 3: the final**
- Initiative: Quill **6** + 3 = **9**, Thokk **6** − 1 = **5**.
- Thokk is lowest and tries to steal Quill's chair: Acrobatics **10** − 1 = **9**. Fails. **Quill wins.**

**Edge case: a tie for lowest**
- If two players both had the lowest total, they'd roll initiative again between themselves to see who is lowest.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 5 sp per player.
- **Winner:** 70% of all entries and the fiddler's ribbon to wear for the month.
- **The House keeps:** 30% of entries and the cover charge for the music night.

## Odds

- **Equal players** each have the same chance: with 8 players, about 1 in 8.
- **High Dexterity helps twice:** it wins the scramble and the steal.

**Chance to steal a chair (DC 12 Acrobatics):**

| Acrobatics | +0 | +2 | +4 | +6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chance | 45% | 55% | 65% | 75% |

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Alert feat, advantage on initiative and other natural features.
- Dancing as wildly as you like.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *haste*, *guidance*, Bardic Inspiration or *grease* under a rival's feet.
- Tripping, shoving, or a partner "saving" a chair.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trip a rival in the scramble, made to look like an accident | Contestant | Cha (Deception) | That rival's initiative has disadvantage this round |
| Bribe the fiddler with a jar of rare honey (worth 5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The fiddler stops when you're right next to a chair: advantage on your initiative this round |
| Hidden spell | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Quill tries to trip Mirela in round 2, making it look like they collided in the scramble.
- Quill: Deception **4** + 2 = **6**. The Watcher: **15** + 5 = **20**. **Caught.**
- Quill is disqualified (his 5 sp entry is lost), banned for 3 months, and his portrait goes on the notice board. To come off the board: 5 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Lirael Thornsong**, the fey fiddler, is paid in honey, not coin. If she's ever paid in coin, she'll stop playing and leave.
- **Every month,** one chair more than should be there appears in the ring. Nobody sees who brings it.
