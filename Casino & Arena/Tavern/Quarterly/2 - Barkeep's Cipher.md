# Barkeep's Cipher

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Every season, the barkeep locks next season's secret recipe in an iron box with three locks: a word lock, a symbol lock and a clockwork mechanism. Teams race to open it. The fastest team gets to name the new drink.*

**Description:** A quarterly puzzle race for teams. Each team gets an identical locked recipe box with **three locks** that must be opened in order. Every attempt at a lock takes **5 minutes**, and a failed attempt means trying again. The team with the **lowest total time** wins and names next season's drink.

## How to Play

### The locks (in order)

| Lock | Check | DC |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Word lock | Intelligence (Investigation) | 12 |
| 2. Symbol lock | Intelligence (History) | 15 |
| 3. Clockwork mechanism | Dexterity, with thieves' tools proficiency if you have it | 18 |

### Each attempt, step by step

1. **One team member** makes the check for the current lock.
2. **One other team member** can **Help**, giving **advantage**.
3. **Success:** the lock opens. Move on to the next lock.
4. **Failure:** try again.
5. **Every attempt takes 5 minutes**, whether it works or not.

### Solving it for real

If the DM has a real puzzle for a lock (a word puzzle or a symbol code) and the players solve it out of character, that lock opens in **one attempt** (5 minutes) without a roll. No time to prepare? Use the [[#Ready-Made Puzzles]] at the bottom of this note.

### Winning

- The team with the **lowest total time** wins. The fastest possible time is **15 minutes** (three attempts, three successes).
- **Time limit: 1 hour.** Teams that haven't opened the box by then rank by how many locks they opened.
- **Ties:** the tied teams share the prize and name the drink together.

## Dice Examples

**Team Quill:** Quill (PC, Investigation +4, History +2, thieves' tools +7) and Mirela (PC, Investigation +1, History +1). Quill makes every check, and Mirela helps.

**Lock 1: Word lock (DC 12)**
- Attempt 1: **7** and **13**, keeps 13: 13 + 4 = **17**. Open. (**5 minutes**)

**Lock 2: Symbol lock (DC 15)**
- Attempt 2: **9** and **11**, keeps 11: 11 + 2 = **13**. Fail. (**10 minutes**)
- Attempt 3: **16** and **4**, keeps 16: 16 + 2 = **18**. Open. (**15 minutes**)

**Lock 3: Clockwork mechanism (DC 18)**
- Attempt 4: **5** and **12**, keeps 12: 12 + 7 = **19**. Open. (**20 minutes**)

**Team Quill finishes in 20 minutes.** The Lantern Guild (NPC team) finishes in 25. **Team Quill wins.**

**Edge case: the time limit**
- A team stuck on the clockwork lock at the 1-hour mark is ranked by locks opened (2), behind every team that finished.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 2 gp per team.
- **Winning team:** 10 gp and the right to **name next season's drink**.
- **The House keeps:** the rest of the entries, plus the bookings and bar sales.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**The clockwork lock decides most races.** A team without a proficient lockpick will spend a long time on it.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any team member can make any check, and anyone can help.
- Bringing your own thieves' tools.
- Solving a real puzzle at the table.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *knock*, *guidance*, *comprehend languages*, *identify* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A fifth team member, peeking at another team's box, or bribing the barkeep for the codes.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bribe the barkeep for the codes (10 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Locks 1 and 2 open on the first attempt |
| Peek at a faster team's box | Team member | Dex (Stealth) | +2 to every check on your current lock |
| Hidden *guidance*, by touch before the check | A teammate, or a friend with a pat on the back | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | +1d4 on one lock check |
| *Knock* | A caster | No roll: it's too loud. Automatically caught | — |

The Barkeep's Cipher is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A cheating team is disqualified together. The 3-month ban means missing the next season's Cipher.

### Cheating dice example

Stuck on lock 2, Mirela tries to bribe the barkeep for the symbol code.
- Mirela: Persuasion **13** + 5 = **18**. The Watcher: **9** + 5 = **14**. Not caught.
- Lock 2 opens on the next attempt. But the Lantern Guild's captain was watching: Perception **17** + 2 = **19**. She beats 18 and **saw it**. She demands 5 gp to keep quiet.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Inside one box** this season, a team finds a second recipe that isn't for a drink. It's a cipher, and it isn't the barkeep's.
- **The Lantern Guild** are professional locksmiths who've won four seasons running. Their captain is running out of drink names.

## Ready-Made Puzzles

One set for each season. Read the puzzle aloud or show it. The **clockwork mechanism** (lock 3) is always a roll.

### Spring

**Lock 1: Word lock (5 letters).** Engraved on the lid:
> *"I have keys, but I can't open a single lock."*

**Answer: PIANO.**

**Lock 2: Symbol lock (4 letters).** The dials show **K R S V**. Scratched underneath:
> *"Every letter has stepped three places forward."*

**Answer: HOPS.** Step each letter three places back: K → H, R → O, S → P, V → S.

### Summer

**Lock 1: Word lock (4 letters).** A verse is painted on the lid:
> *"**M**alt in the kettle,*
> ***E**mbers below,*
> ***A**le for the thirsty,*
> ***D**rink as you go."*

**Answer: MEAD.** The first letter of each line.

**Lock 2: Symbol lock (3 numbers).** Three symbols are carved on the box: a **tankard**, a **barrel** and a **coin**.
> *"Two tankards fill a barrel. A barrel and a tankard make nine. The coin is the barrel times the tankard, halved. Set them in that order."*

**Answer: 3, 6, 9.** Tankard = 3, barrel = 6 (3 + 3), and 6 + 3 = 9. Coin = 6 × 3 ÷ 2 = 9.

### Autumn

**Lock 1: Word lock (5 letters).** Engraved on the lid:
> *"The more I dry, the wetter I get."*

**Answer: TOWEL.**

**Lock 2: Symbol lock (3 numbers).** Three dials, and a verse:
> *"The faces of the die that rules every fate. The legs of the spider. The heads of the Dragon Queen."*

**Answer: 20, 8, 5.** A d20, a spider's eight legs, and Tiamat's five heads.

### Winter

**Lock 1: Word lock (3 letters).** Engraved on the lid:
> *"Forward I am heavy. Backward I am not."*

**Answer: TON.** Backward, it spells "not".

**Lock 2: Symbol lock (5 letters).** The dials show **19 · 20 · 15 · 21 · 20**. Scratched underneath:
> *"A is one."*

**Answer: STOUT.** Each number is a letter's place in the alphabet: S = 19, T = 20, O = 15, U = 21, T = 20.
