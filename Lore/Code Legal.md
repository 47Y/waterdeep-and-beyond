# Code Legal

The public outline of [[Waterdeep]]'s laws and typical sentences. Magisters have broad discretion; it is not an exhaustive account of legal precedent.

## Courts and Copies

- Copies are available in several languages at the [[Palace of Waterdeep]], or in Common from gate and harbor magisters.
- Magisters wear black robes and can sentence without a trial. A member of the [[Lords of Waterdeep|Masked Lords]] can overturn their rulings.
- Report crimes to the [[City Watch]] or a watch post.

## Practical Rules

- Carrying arms and armor is legal; assault and dueling are not.
- Participants in brawls can all be arrested regardless of who started the fight. Volo says minor tavern brawls are sometimes overlooked if nobody is seriously hurt and the proprietor seeks no damages.
- Boxing and wrestling require a venue registered for sport fighting.
- Large caravans and ships register on arrival. Small overland parties and individuals must register when staying beyond a tenday; night or winter arrivals should expect registration.
- Resident arcane spellcasters must register with the city; see [[Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors]].
- Flight over the city requires special licensing; see [[City Guard#Griffon Cavalry|Griffon Cavalry]].
