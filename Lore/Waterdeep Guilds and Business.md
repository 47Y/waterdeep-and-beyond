# Waterdeep Guilds and Business

More than forty guilds shape business in [[Waterdeep]]. Their customs are influential even though Guild Law is not itself the city's legal code.

- Practicing a trade without joining its guild is legal. Guild members cannot lawfully interfere with an unaffiliated business, but may refuse to sell it goods or services.
- Volo recommends hiring a trusted local solicitor and consulting senior guild members about specific requirements.
- Construction involves several trades: surveyors approve plans, cellarers and plumbers prepare the site, carpenters erect the structure, and specialist guilds handle stonework, fittings, and glazing.
- Related guilds already known to the party: [[Carpenter's Guild]] and [[Mason's Guild]].
