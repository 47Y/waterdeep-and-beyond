# Hadar

[[Twizzle]] identifies Hadar as her faith. During [[Session 7]], she used a drop of blood and a prayer to summon or contact Hadar while seeking protection for the [[Cassalanter Children|Cassalanter twins]]. She experienced a sensation of being underwater, then amusement and indifference. Hadar did not help.
