# Tyr

[[Lilith D Gluv]] appealed to Tyr for help protecting the [[Cassalanter Children|Cassalanter twins]]. Her prayer was followed by darkness and unnatural cold. Lilith concluded that she had angered Tyr and that he would not help, at least for now.

The cause of Tyr's apparent anger is unknown.
