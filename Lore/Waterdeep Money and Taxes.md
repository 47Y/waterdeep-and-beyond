# Waterdeep Money and Taxes

Public currency and routine charges in [[Waterdeep]], as described by Volo.

## Local Coins

| Coin | Value in Waterdeep |
| --- | --- |
| Nib | 1 cp |
| Shard | 1 sp |
| Dragon | 1 gp |
| Sun | 1 pp |
| Taol | 2 gp |
| Harbor moon | 50 gp |

- Taols and harbor moons lose much of their value outside the city.
- Taxes, fines, and guild fees require Waterdavian or Lords' Alliance currency. Many businesses accept only local coins for convenience.
- The [[Palace of Waterdeep]] exchanges currency without a fee, but an appointment may be a day or more away.

## Routine Charges

- **Resident tax:** 1 sp per person over ten, collected by the [[City Guard]] on the last day of each month within official wards.
- **Annual exemption:** 1 gp buys a twelve-month writ, which must be shown at every monthly collection.
- An unanswered door receives an estimated tax lien, payable to a magister before the following month ends.
- **Market stall:** 1 cp per day.
- **Conviction fee:** 1 sp per conviction, in addition to fines.
- **Conveyance leaving the city:** 1 gp, empty or full.
- **Ship docking:** 5 gp for up to fourteen days, excluding city and diplomatic vessels; leaving and returning incurs the charge again.
- Extra fire, wall, harbor, or mercenary taxes can be imposed in times of need. The guide does not establish that any such special tax is currently due in the campaign.
