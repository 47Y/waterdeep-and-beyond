# Waukeen

Waukeen is the deity served by [[Obaya Uday]] and is associated with [[Archbishop Francis]]. During [[Session 7]], Francis relayed that Waukeen had no interest in intervening for the [[Cassalanter Children|Cassalanter twins]] because money was not involved and had little power in the situation.
