# Starless Convergence

The Starless Convergence is a rare celestial phenomenon witnessed by [[Cordan]] in the Feywild.

## Known Details

- Occurs only once every several thousand years.
- The constellations of the Feywild began drifting out of alignment.
- Stars disappeared one by one, as though something was passing between them and the Feywild.
- The sky formed a vast dark shape resembling an open eye surrounded by broken antlers.
- Cordan believes it was an astronomical omen rather than a natural celestial event.

## Cordan's Investigation

- Cordan left the Feywild to understand what he saw.
- He believes the Material Plane sky may contain information unavailable in [[Astraelith]].
- He compares Feywild stars with Material Plane stars.
- He keeps most of this research private.

## Open Questions

- What did the eye and broken antlers represent?
- Why might [[Waterdeep]] matter to the omen?
