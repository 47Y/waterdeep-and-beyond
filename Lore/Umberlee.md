# Umberlee

Umberlee is the goddess of the sea. The official D&D 5e Basic Rules list her as chaotic evil, associate her clerics with the Tempest domain, and show her symbol as a wave curling left and right.

During [[Session 9]], [[Lord Zulpair]] said his family worships Umberlee—called “the Sea Bitch” in the session notes—primarily for ancestral reasons. This does not by itself establish a connection between the family's worship and the Dawnveil murders or [[Skyhorn]].
