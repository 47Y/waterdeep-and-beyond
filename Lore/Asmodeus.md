# Asmodeus

While infiltrating [[Cassalanter Villa]], [[Cordan]] discovered an underground chamber containing a 30-foot statue of Asmodeus. He observed roughly a dozen masked and robed figures performing a ritual there, including [[Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[Ammalia Cassalanter]].

Cordan suspected the ritual language was Infernal because of its apparent connection to Asmodeus, but this was not verified.
