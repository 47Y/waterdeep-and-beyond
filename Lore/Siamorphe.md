# Siamorphe

[[Cordan]] saw a painting of Siamorphe's holy symbol in the cellar of [[Cassalanter Villa]] but did not recognize it at the time. He later drew it for the party, and [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]], [[Dick Mixon]], and [[Twizzle]] identified it.

The sun portion of the painted symbol operated a secret door.
