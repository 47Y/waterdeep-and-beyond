# Founder's Day

At the conclusion of a ritual beneath [[Cassalanter Villa]], [[Victoro Cassalanter]] declared, "Founder's Day approaches," prompting cheers from roughly a dozen masked and robed participants.

The significance of the statement and any associated plan are unknown.

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “Flamerule 1: Founders' Day”; related sections where noted).

- Called **Founders' Day** in the guide and celebrated on **Flamerule 1**, commemorating [[Waterdeep]]'s founding.
- The Field of Triumph hosts illusory presentations of city history and martial exhibitions. Festhalls hold historical costume contests.
- Mages traditionally disguise Castle Waterdeep as Nimoar's old wooden fortress with an illusion lasting from midday to sunset.
- These public celebrations do not establish what [[Victoro Cassalanter]] intended by his statement at the ritual.
