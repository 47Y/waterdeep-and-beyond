# Eilistraee

Eilistraee is [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]]'s deity. During [[Session 7]], Tathlynd sought her protection for [[Elzerina Cassalanter]] and [[Terenzio Cassalanter]]. Her message, understood through [[Dick Mixon]]'s Comprehend Languages spell, was: “I will try to take these souls.”

On [[Founders' Day]], Eilistraee prevailed in a perceived divine struggle over the twins and saved their souls from [[Asmodeus]].
