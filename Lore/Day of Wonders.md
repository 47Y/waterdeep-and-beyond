# Day of Wonders

The party recognized the figure in the [[Rooftop Puppet Sketch]] as most closely resembling an automaton supplied by the [[Temple of Gond]] for the Day of Wonders parade.

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “Marpenoth 3: Day of Wonders”; related sections where noted).

- Celebrated on **Marpenoth 3** in [[Waterdeep]].
- The Gondar parade new inventions, ranging from practical improvements to large walking or rolling mechanical constructs (see [[Temple of Gond]]).
- Malfunctions sometimes disrupt the celebration. Volo recounts a flying chair that could ascend but not descend; the Griffon Cavalry rescued its inventor.
