# Trap Gauntlet

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Logs swing, trapdoors snap, darts hiss out of the walls and fire roars up through the grates. At the far end, a ramp of rolling boulders. Runners go one at a time against the clock, and the crowd counts every second out loud.*

**Description:** A weekly obstacle race through **eight real traps** built in the pit. Every runner goes alone. Each trap you **fail** hits you for **real damage** and adds **10 seconds** to your time. **Drop to 0 HP and your run is over.** **Fastest time wins.** On the last trap, the **avalanche ramp**, strong runners can shove a boulder aside instead of dodging.

## How to Play

### The traps

Each trap is a **Dexterity saving throw**.

| Trap | DC | Damage type |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Swinging logs | 10 | Bludgeoning |
| 2. Trapdoor (a 10-ft drop) | 11 | Bludgeoning |
| 3. Dart wall | 12 | Piercing |
| 4. Scything blade (sweeps at waist height) | 13 | Slashing |
| 5. Spinning spiked paddles | 14 | Piercing |
| 6. Fire jets (bursting from the floor grates) | 16 | Fire |
| 7. Collapsing plank bridge (over a flooded trench) | 18 | Bludgeoning |
| 8. The avalanche ramp (rolling boulders) | 20. **Or** make a **Strength (Athletics)** check, DC 20, to shove a boulder aside. | Bludgeoning |

### A run, step by step

1. **Every run starts at 60 seconds.**
2. **Make the save for each trap**, in order.
   - **Success:** you're through clean.
   - **Failure:** you take damage (below) and **add 10 seconds**. You still get through and keep running.
3. **Drop to 0 HP and your run is over.** No time is recorded. You make no death saving throws: you're stable, and the healers bring you back to 1 HP (see the Arena Ward below).
4. **Fastest total time wins.** If times are tied, the runner who took less damage wins. If that's tied too, they share the purse.

**Damage on a failure, by tier:**

| Tier | Damage |
|---|---|
| 1 (levels 1–4) | 1d8 |
| 2 (levels 5–10) | 3d6 |
| 3 and up (levels 11+) | 6d6 |

The damage type depends on the trap (see the table above).

**No spells and no magic items.**

## Dice Examples

**Quill** (PC, Tier 1, Dex save +5, HP 21).

| Trap | DC | Roll | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | **8** + 5 = 13 | Clean |
| 2 | 11 | **4** + 5 = 9 | **Fail.** **6** bludgeoning, +10 s. Quill is at 15. |
| 3 | 12 | **15** + 5 = 20 | Clean |
| 4 | 13 | **7** + 5 = 12 | **Fail.** **5** slashing, +10 s. Quill is at 10. |
| 5 | 14 | **12** + 5 = 17 | Clean |
| 6 | 16 | **9** + 5 = 14 | **Fail.** **3** fire, +10 s. Quill is at 7. |
| 7 | 18 | **14** + 5 = 19 | Clean |
| 8 | 20 | **17** + 5 = 22 | Clean |

**Quill's time:** 60 + 10 + 10 + 10 = **90 seconds**, with 14 damage taken.

**Edge case: shoving on the ramp.** Thokk (Dex save −1, Athletics +5) reaches trap 8. He'd need a 21 on the save, so he shoves instead: Athletics **15** + 5 = **20**. That meets the DC: **clean.**

**Edge case: dropping to 0 HP.** Mirela (PC, Tier 1, Dex save +2, HP 18) reaches trap 7 with **4 HP** left. Save: **10** + 2 = 12, a fail. The bridge gives way: **7** bludgeoning. She drops to **0 HP**. **Her run is over** and no time is recorded. She's stable, and the healers fish her out of the trench at 1 HP.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Walking the course before the event (but not touching the traps).
- Choosing to shove or save on the ramp.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- Jamming a trap before your run, or bribing the trap crew.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wedge a trap so it fires slow | Anyone near the course | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on that trap's save |
| Bribe the trap crew to fire early on a rival (2 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The rival has disadvantage on one trap |
| Hidden spell (*haste*, *bless*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Before Quill's run, Mirela wedges trap 6 (the fire jets).
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **16** + 2 = **18**. The Steward: **12** + 5 = **17**. Not caught.
- Quill has advantage on trap 6: **9** and **14**, keeps 14: 14 + 5 = **19**. Clean. His time drops to **80 seconds**, and he takes only 11 damage.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The trap-wright, Dunn Holloway,** builds a new trap every season. This season's was built to someone else's plans, and nobody will say whose.
- **The course record** (60 seconds flat, a clean run) was set by someone who never came back to defend it.
