# Log Joust

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The pit is flooded. A single log, fixed at both ends, spans the water. Two fighters edge out along it with padded staves, arms out, knees bent. One good whack and somebody's swimming.*

**Description:** A weekly water duel. Two fighters stand on a **fixed log** over the **flooded pit** and hit each other with **padded staves**. Every time you're hit, you must keep your balance or **fall in**. **Whoever falls in loses.**

## How to Play

### The rules of the log

- **The pit is flooded**, and a fixed log spans it, a few feet above the water.
- **Weapons:** a padded staff (**1d6 + Str**, bludgeoning). It's a Strength weapon, even for a rogue.
- **Light armour only. No shields, no spells and no magic items.**

### A bout, step by step

1. **The fighters start 10 ft apart** on the log. Roll initiative.
2. **On your turn, make one staff attack.** (Extra Attack and Action Surge don't apply: one strike per turn.)
3. **On a hit**, deal damage. Then the target makes a **Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check (their choice), **DC 10 + the damage**.
   - **Success:** they keep their balance.
   - **Failure:** **they fall in and lose.**
4. **You also lose if:**
   - You **step off** the log or touch the water.
   - You **grab your opponent's staff.**
   - You drop to **0 HP**.

**Netted curtain:** up (to keep the splash off the crowd).

## Dice Examples

**Thokk** (PC, leather armour AC 10, staff +5, 1d6 + 3, Athletics +5).
**Quill** (PC, leather armour AC 14, staff +2, 1d6 + 0, Acrobatics +5).

Thokk wins initiative.

**Round 1**
- **Thokk:** **11** + 5 = 16 against AC 14, a hit: **2** + 3 = **5**. Quill's balance: DC 10 + 5 = 15. Acrobatics **12** + 5 = **17**. He stays on.
- **Quill:** **13** + 2 = 15 against AC 10, a hit: **4** + 0 = **4**. Thokk's balance: DC 14. Athletics **10** + 5 = **15**. He stays on.

**Round 2**
- **Thokk:** **17** + 5 = 22, a hit: **5** + 3 = **8**. Quill's balance: DC 18. Acrobatics **9** + 5 = **14**. **Splash! Thokk wins.**

**Edge case: a lucky grab.** If Quill had grabbed Thokk's staff to steady himself, he'd have lost on the spot, even without falling.

## Odds

**Chance a single strike drops the target** (hit, then a failed balance check):

| Attacker | Target | Drops them |
|---|---|---|
| +5, 1d6 + 3 | AC 13, balance +5 | 34% |
| +5, 1d6 + 3 | AC 13, balance +2 | 44% |
| +5, 1d6 + 3 | AC 15, balance +5 | 29% |
| +3, 1d6 + 1 | AC 13, balance +5 | 23% |

- **Between equal fighters, whoever strikes first wins 60%.**
- **Thokk against Quill:** Thokk wins 65% if he strikes first and 49% if Quill does. That's **57% overall**.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Feinting, crouching and backing off along the log.
- Any non-magical class feature that works with one strike per turn.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- Grease on your rival's end of the log, or a weighted staff.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grease the rival's end of the log | Anyone with access before the bout | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival has disadvantage on their first balance check |
| Weighted staff (lead in the padding) | Fighter | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | +2 damage, so the balance DC goes up too |
| Hidden spell (*gust*) | A caster in the crowd, within 30 ft of the rival | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The rival makes a Strength save against the caster's spell DC. On a failure, they're pushed off the log and fall in. |

### Cheating dice example

Thokk tries to slip a lead-weighted staff out of the rack.
- Thokk: Sleight of Hand **14** − 1 = **13**. The Steward: **7** + 5 = **12**. Not caught.
- Every hit of his now does +2 damage. His 5-damage hit in round 1 becomes 7, so Quill's balance DC rises from 15 to 17.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The log** has been replaced twice this year after "accidents." The carpenter swears both were sawn.
- **A fishmonger's guild** sponsors the champion and pays in salted herring as well as coin.
