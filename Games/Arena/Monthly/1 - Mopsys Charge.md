# Mopsy's Charge

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The beast-gate opens and Mopsy the owlbear bursts onto the sand, feathers up, hooting. No chains. No handlers. Just her and one leaper. She charges. At the last moment the leaper springs aside, or vaults clean over her back. The crowd screams. Mopsy skids, wheels round, and comes again.*

**Description:** A monthly daredevil event. Each leaper steps into the pit with the House owlbear, **Mopsy**, running **loose**. She charges again and again, and each charge is harder to dodge. Every clean dodge scores **style points**. Get caught and she claws you, but your run goes on. Stay in as long as you dare, then **climb out**. No one comes in to save you unless you drop. Most style points wins.

## How to Play

### A run, step by step

1. **Mopsy charges.** Make a **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check to leap or sidestep out of the way. The DC starts at **13** and goes up by **1** with every charge.

| Charge | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | and so on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DC | 13 | 14 | 15 | 16 | 17 | +1 each |

2. **Success:** you're clear. Score **style points equal to how much you beat the DC by, + 1.**
   - Example: DC 13, a total of 17, beats it by 4: **5 style points**.
3. **Failure:** you score nothing for that charge, and Mopsy makes **one claw attack** against you (**+7 to hit, 2d8 + 5 slashing**). **That's at most one hit per charge.** Your run goes on.
4. **Between charges**, you can call it and run for the **net on the pit wall**. Make a **Strength (Athletics)** check, **DC 10**, to scramble up it.
   - **Success:** you're out, and you keep every point you've scored.
   - **Failure:** Mopsy reaches you first and makes **one claw attack**. Then you're up and out.
5. **Most style points wins.** If it's tied, the leaper who cleared more charges wins. If it's still tied, they share the purse.

### What you can bring

- **Any armour and gear you own**, including magic items.
- **Your normal stats:** class features, feats and skills (Uncanny Dodge, for example).
- **No spells.**

### Hands off Mopsy

Mopsy charges, but this is a dodging event, not a fight. **The House will not tolerate harm to her.**
- **First attack** on Mopsy: your style points drop to **0**.
- **Second attack:** you're **disqualified** and must leave the pit.

### No rescue

**No one comes into the pit to save you.** Only when you **drop to 0 HP** does the Arena Ward step in:

![[Arena Combat Rules#Arena Ward]]

**Clearing the pit:** when a leaper climbs out or drops, the **keeper** throws meat through the beast-gate. Mopsy trots back to her pen for it, and the gate shuts behind her. Only then do the healers come onto the sand.

**Netted curtain:** up.

## Dice Examples

**Quill** (PC rogue 3, leather armour AC 14, HP 21, Acrobatics +5, Athletics +0).

| Charge | DC | Roll | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 13 | **14** + 5 = 19 | Beats it by 6: **7 points** |
| 2 | 14 | **11** + 5 = 16 | Beats it by 2: **3 points** |
| 3 | 15 | **8** + 5 = 13 | **Fails.** Mopsy claws: **9** + 7 = 16 against AC 14, a hit: **6** + **3** + 5 = **14** damage. Quill is at 7. |

Quill is at 7 HP and decides to call it. He runs for the net: Athletics **12** + 0 = **12**, beating DC 10. **He's out with 10 style points.**

**Edge case: a failed climb.** If Quill had rolled **7** + 0 = 7, Mopsy would have reached him first. Her claw: **15** + 7 = 22, a hit: **4** + **2** + 5 = **11**. Quill drops to 0 HP and is stable. The keeper throws the meat, Mopsy trots back to her pen, and the healers bring Quill back to 1 HP. **He keeps his 10 style points.**

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any armour and gear you own, including magic items.
- Your class features, feats and skills.
- Climbing out between charges, whenever you like.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell**, from the leaper or from the crowd.
- Drugging Mopsy's feed, or bribing the keeper to overfeed her.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bribe the keeper to overfeed Mopsy before your run (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Mopsy is sluggish: −2 to the DC on all your charges |
| Drug Mopsy's feed | Anyone with access to the beast-pens | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Mopsy is **poisoned**: her claw has disadvantage |
| Hidden spell (*jump*), by touch before you step in: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the back | The leaper or a friend | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on one charge |

### Cheating dice example

Mirela bribes the keeper 5 gp to overfeed Mopsy before Quill's run.
- Mirela: Persuasion **12** + 5 = **17**. The Steward: **11** + 5 = **16**. Not caught.
- Quill's DCs become 11, 12 and 13. Every dodge scores more, and his charge 3 roll of 13 now **just clears it**: (19 − 11 + 1) + (16 − 12 + 1) + (13 − 13 + 1) = 9 + 5 + 1 = **15 style points**. No claw.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Mopsy** was raised from an egg by the head keeper. She's never once hurt him. Everyone else is fair game.
- **A leaper from far away** does a full twist over Mopsy every month and never gets caught. People are starting to wonder about her.
