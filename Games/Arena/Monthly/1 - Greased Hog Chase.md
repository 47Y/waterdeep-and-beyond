# Greased Hog Chase

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *One hog. One bucket of lard. Eight grown adults in the mud. The hog squeals, the crowd howls, and somebody belly-flops for the third time running.*

**Description:** A monthly comedy event. A **greased hog** is let loose in the muddy pit with a crowd of chasers. **Grab it and hold on long enough to carry it to the pen.** The first chaser who gets it there wins.

## How to Play

### The hog

- **Acrobatics +3**, and it rolls with **advantage** (it's greased).
- **It never attacks.** It just squirms.

### A chase, step by step

1. **Roll initiative** for every chaser.
2. **On your turn, if the hog is free:** try to **grab** it. It's a **Strength (Athletics)** contest against the hog's Acrobatics. **Ties go to the hog.**
   - **Win:** you're holding the hog.
   - **Lose:** it slips away.
3. **On your turn, if you're holding the hog:** it squirms. Repeat the same contest.
   - **Win:** you carry it one stage toward the pen.
   - **Lose:** it escapes, and it's free again.
4. **You win** when you've **grabbed it and held it through two more of your turns** (three wins in a row).

No spells, no magic items and no attacking other chasers. Tripping over each other is allowed and expected.

**Netted curtain:** down. (The mud reaches the front row anyway.)

## Dice Examples

**Chasers:** Thokk (PC, Athletics +5), Oswin (NPC, Athletics +2) and Quill (PC, Athletics +0).

**Round 1**
- **Thokk grabs:** **14** + 5 = **19**. The hog: **12** and **17**, keeps 17: 17 + 3 = **20**. **It slips away.**
- **Oswin grabs:** **18** + 2 = **20**. The hog: **9** and **6**, keeps 9: 9 + 3 = **12**. **Oswin has it!**
- **Quill** can't grab a hog that's already held. He circles, waiting.

**Round 2**
- **Thokk** waits.
- **Oswin holds:** **11** + 2 = **13**. The hog: **15** and **4**, keeps 15: 15 + 3 = **18**. **It escapes.**
- **Quill grabs:** **16** + 0 = **16**. The hog: **3** and **10**, keeps 10: 10 + 3 = **13**. **Quill has it!**

**Round 3**
- **Quill holds:** **13** + 0 = **13**. The hog: **8** and **10**, keeps 10: 10 + 3 = **13**. **A tie goes to the hog. It escapes.**

**Rounds 4–6:** Thokk grabs it (**19** vs 16), holds (**21** vs 18) and holds again (**15** vs 14). **Thokk carries the hog into the pen and wins!**

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Diving, sliding and tripping over other chasers. (Nobody gets hurt in the mud.)
- Teaming up to herd the hog. (Only one person can win.)

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- Rosin or sand on your hands, or bribing the swineherd to under-grease the hog.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rosin on your palms | Chaser | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | +2 to your grab and hold checks |
| Bribe the swineherd (2 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The hog is lightly greased: no advantage for the hog |
| Hidden spell (*enhance ability*), by touch before the chase: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the back | The chaser or a friend | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on your grab and hold checks |

Greased Hog Chase is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Quill rubs rosin on his palms before the chase.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **5** + 7 = **12**. The Steward: **10** + 5 = **15**. **Caught.**
- Quill is pulled out of the mud before the chase. He's banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 5 gp, 1 day serving (washing the hog), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The hog, Duchess,** has escaped every chase for three months. The swineherd is very proud and very suspicious of anyone who wants to buy her.
- **A butcher** offers the winner double for the hog, but only if it's delivered alive, tonight, to an address in the warehouse district.
