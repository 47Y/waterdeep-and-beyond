# Ring Tilt

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Three carved horses wait at the ends of three arms, long, middling and short, all wound tight on one great spring. Iron rings dangle from posts all the way round. The operator pulls the lever and the horses whirl away together, the same way round. The riders lean out, lances level, and try to thread every ring.*

**Description:** A monthly ring-spearing contest on the House's **jousting carousel**, held on the same day as the [[1 - Mounted Joust|Mounted Joust]]. Up to **three riders** ride at once, one on each arm. All the arms spin **the same way**, and each rider tries to **spear the rings** hanging along their path. Ride **three runs**; the rider with the **most rings** of the day wins.

## How to Play

### The carousel

- **Three arms** on one pivot: **20 ft**, **15 ft** and **10 ft** long, each carrying a wooden horse. A heat can use one, two or all three.
- **All three arms are sprung the same way round**, so every rider takes their rings on the **same side**: all on the right, or all on the left. Never mixed.
- **Each run, three rings** hang along each rider's path. The rings on the slower inner arms hang smaller, so **every ring is AC 15**, whichever arm you ride.
- **Clothes on your back:** the House's practice lance and horse. No armour, no spells and no magic items.
- The same carousel runs the [[1 - Mounted Joust|Mounted Joust]]. Each player picks which of the two games to enter.

### A heat, step by step

1. **Each run:** every rider makes **three lance attacks**, one against each ring on their path, **AC 15**. **Each hit spears one ring.**
2. **Ride three runs.** That's **nine rings** at most.
3. **The rider with the most rings** across the whole day wins.
   - **A tie:** the tied riders ride one more run together. Most rings wins. Still tied: ride again.

## Dice Examples

**One heat of three riders:**
- **Thokk** (PC fighter 3, lance +5) on the 20-ft arm.
- **Quill** (PC rogue 3, not trained with the lance, +0) on the 15-ft arm.
- **Kord** (NPC berserker, lance +5) on the 10-ft arm.

Every ring is AC 15.

| Run | Thokk (+5) | Quill (+0) | Kord (+5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **12** = 17 hit, **8** = 13 miss, **15** = 20 hit: **2** | **16** hit, **9** miss, **18** hit: **2** | **10** = 15 hit, **13** = 18 hit, **4** = 9 miss: **2** |
| 2 | **11** = 16 hit, **17** = 22 hit, **6** = 11 miss: **2** | **7** miss, **15** hit, **3** miss: **1** | **9** = 14 miss, **2** = 7 miss, **14** = 19 hit: **1** |
| 3 | **10** = 15 hit, **5** = 10 miss, **13** = 18 hit: **2** | **20** hit, **11** miss, **19** hit: **2** | **16** = 21 hit, **12** = 17 hit, **18** = 23 hit: **3** |
| **Total** | **6** | **5** | **6** |

**Thokk and Kord are tied on 6**, so they ride one more run together.
- Thokk: **14** + 5 = 19 hit, **9** + 5 = 14 miss, **11** + 5 = 16 hit: **2**.
- Kord: **7** + 5 = 12 miss, **15** + 5 = 20 hit, **3** + 5 = 8 miss: **1**.
- **Thokk wins.**

- **The House keeps:** the entries, the betting cut and the seats.

## Odds

**Spearing one ring (AC 15):**

| Lance attack | +0 | +2 | +4 | +5 | +7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spears the ring | 30% | 40% | 50% | 55% | 65% |

**All nine rings** at +5 happens about **once in 200 heats**.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any class feature that isn't a spell.
- Choosing which arm to ride, if it's free.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic item.**
- Tampering with the rings, the lances or the saddles, or bribing the ring-hanger.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bribe the ring-hanger to hang your rings loose (3 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Your rings are AC 13 |
| Grease a rival's saddle | Anyone in the stables | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival has disadvantage on their ring attacks in the first run |
| Hidden spell from the rim (*bless*) | A caster in the crowd, within 30 ft of the rider | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | +1d4 on the rider's lance attacks, for up to 1 minute |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Ring Tilt is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

|---|---|
| 10 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Before his heat, Quill slips the ring-hanger 3 gp to hang his rings loose.
- Quill: Persuasion **15** + 1 = **16**. The Steward: **12** + 5 = **17**. **Caught.**
- Quill is disqualified and banned from the arena for 3 months, and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 10 gp, 1 day serving (hanging the rings), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The ring-hanger's daughter** rides every month and has never missed a ring. The other riders swear her rings hang lower.
- **One ring each month is silver.** Whoever spears it keeps it. This month's is engraved with a name the party knows.
