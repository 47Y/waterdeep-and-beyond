# Mirror Duel

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A tall silver mirror stands in the pit. The challenger steps up to it, and their reflection steps out: same weapon, same armour, same smirk. To beat yourself, you have to know yourself.*

**Description:** A monthly duel against an **illusory copy of yourself**. The copy has **your attack bonus and your AC**. Over **three rounds**, you score points for **reading its tactics** and for **landing hits**, and lose points when it hits you. **Highest score of the night wins.**

## How to Play

### A duel, step by step

The duel lasts **three rounds**. Each round:

1. **The copy picks a tactic in secret.** The DM rolls a d3: **1 Press, 2 Guard, 3 Feint.**
2. **Read it:** make a **Wisdom (Insight)** check, **DC 15**.
   - **Success:** **+1 point**, and you have **advantage** on your attack this round.
3. **You attack the copy** (your attack bonus against your own AC). **A hit: +1 point.**
4. **The copy attacks you** (the same bonus against the same AC). **A hit: −1 point.**

**The copy is an illusion.** The mirror is a stage prop. The **mirror-keeper**, hidden behind it, casts *silent image* (1st level) of the challenger and moves it. Its hits score against you but **deal no damage**.

**Highest score of the night wins.** If it's tied, the duelist with more successful reads wins. If it's still tied, they share.

No spells and no magic items.

## Dice Examples

**Thokk** (PC, attack +5, AC 16, Insight +1).

| Round | Read (DC 15) | Thokk attacks AC 16 | The copy attacks AC 16 | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **15** + 1 = 16 ✓ **+1** | Advantage: **8** and **17**, keeps 17: 17 + 5 = 22. Hit **+1** | **12** + 5 = 17. Hit **−1** | **1** |
| 2 | **6** + 1 = 7 ✗ | **14** + 5 = 19. Hit **+1** | **9** + 5 = 14. Miss | **2** |
| 3 | **13** + 1 = 14 ✗ | **10** + 5 = 15. Miss | **16** + 5 = 21. Hit **−1** | **1** |

**Thokk's score: 1.**

**Edge case: below zero.** If the copy had hit every round and Thokk had missed everything, his score would be **−3**. Scores can go negative.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Purse:** 8 gp to the night's highest score.
- **The House keeps:** the entries, the betting cut and the seats.

## Odds

**Average score over three rounds** (attack +5 against AC 16):

| Insight | +0 | +1 | +3 | +5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Average score | 1.1 | 1.3 | 1.7 | 2.1 |

**Your attack bonus barely matters**, because the copy always has the same one. Insight wins this game.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any weapon you own (the copy gets the same one).
- Talking to the copy. (The mirror-keeper answers in your voice from behind the glass.)

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- A bribed mirror-keeper who tips you off about the copy's tactic.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bribe the mirror-keeper to signal the tactic (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Advantage on every read |
| A hidden mirror shard to "see" the copy's next move | Duelist | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | +2 to every read |
| Hidden *guidance*, by touch before you step in: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the shoulder | The duelist or a friend | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | +1d4 on one read |

### Cheating dice example

Mirela bribes the mirror-keeper to signal Thokk.
- Mirela: Persuasion **17** + 5 = **22**. The Steward: **14** + 5 = **19**. Not caught.
- Thokk reads with advantage. In round 2: **6** and **18**, keeps 18: 18 + 1 = 19. ✓ That's +1 point and advantage on his attack.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The mirror** came from a wizard's estate sale. The mirror-keeper swears it's only a prop, but sometimes the copy stays out after the spell has ended.
- **A duelist** scored −3 last month and swears the copy was a better version of her, not a copy.
