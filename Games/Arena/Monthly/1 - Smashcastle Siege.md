# Smashcastle Siege

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Two oak gates face each other from opposite ends of the pit, flags flying from their towers. In front of each, four sweating fighters grip the rope handles of an iron-capped battering ram. The horn blows. BOOM. BOOM. Splinters fly. Whose gate breaks first?*

**Description:** A monthly team race. Two teams of four each pound their own **real gate** with a **real battering ram**. There are **no defenders**: it's a straight race. Each good swing puts a **crack** in the gate. **The first gate to take 10 cracks bursts, and that team wins.**

## How to Play

### The gates

- **Two real oak gates**, one at **each end of the pit**, facing each other. Each team has its own gate and its own ram.
- **The ram** is a real battering ram: an iron-capped log with rope handles, carried by all four crew.
- **Clothes on your back:** no armour, no weapons, no magic items and no spells.

### A round, step by step

1. **Every crew member** makes a **Strength (Athletics)** check, **DC 15**.
2. **Each success is 1 crack** in your gate.
3. **The gate bursts at 10 cracks.** That team wins.
4. **Dropped ram:** if **the whole crew fails** in a round, they drop the ram. They spend the **next round** lifting it and get no swing.
5. **Both gates burst in the same round:** the team with **more cracks** wins. Still tied: each team picks one crew member for a **Strength (Athletics) roll-off**.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Arena Ward]]

**Netted curtain:** down. Nothing leaves the pit.

## Dice Examples

**Red:** Thokk (PC, Athletics +5), Kord (NPC, Athletics +5), Oswin (NPC, Athletics +2) and Grit (NPC, Athletics +2).
**Blue:** the Dockside Four (NPC dockhands, Athletics +3 each).

**Round 1**
- **Red:** Thokk **12** + 5 = 17 ✓. Kord **8** + 5 = 13 ✗. Oswin **15** + 2 = 17 ✓. Grit **3** + 2 = 5 ✗. **2 cracks.** Red is at **2**.
- **Blue:** **14** + 3 = 17 ✓, **9** + 3 = 12 ✗, **16** + 3 = 19 ✓, **5** + 3 = 8 ✗. **2 cracks.** Blue is at **2**.

**Round 2**
- **Red:** **18** + 5 = 23 ✓, **11** + 5 = 16 ✓, **13** + 2 = 15 ✓ (meeting the DC succeeds), **7** + 2 = 9 ✗. **3 cracks.** Red is at **5**.
- **Blue:** **4** + 3 = 7 ✗, **7** + 3 = 10 ✗, **2** + 3 = 5 ✗, **10** + 3 = 13 ✗. **Nobody succeeds. Blue drops the ram!**

**Round 3**
- **Red:** **9** + 5 = 14 ✗, **14** + 5 = 19 ✓, **6** + 2 = 8 ✗, **17** + 2 = 19 ✓. **2 cracks.** Red is at **7**.
- **Blue:** lifting the ram. No swing. Blue stays at **2**.

**Round 4**
- **Red:** **10** + 5 = 15 ✓, **5** + 5 = 10 ✗, **12** + 2 = 14 ✗, **19** + 2 = 21 ✓. **2 cracks.** Red is at **9**.
- **Blue:** **15** + 3 = 18 ✓, **12** + 3 = 15 ✓, **18** + 3 = 21 ✓, **11** + 3 = 14 ✗. **3 cracks.** Blue is at **5**.

**Round 5**
- **Red:** **16** + 5 = 21 ✓, **3** + 5 = 8 ✗, **9** + 2 = 11 ✗, **8** + 2 = 10 ✗. **1 crack.** Red is at **10**. **The gate bursts!**
- **Blue:** **13** + 3 = 16 ✓, **17** + 3 = 20 ✓, **6** + 3 = 9 ✗, **12** + 3 = 15 ✓. **3 cracks.** Blue is at **8**.

**Red wins.**

**Edge case: both burst in the same round.** Say Blue had reached 11 cracks in round 5. Red has 10, so **Blue wins** on more cracks. If both had exactly 10, Thokk and a dockhand would roll off with Athletics.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per player.
- **Purse:** 15 gp to the winning team, shared.
- **Spectators bet** on **Red** or **Blue**.
- **The House keeps:** the entries, the betting cut and the seats.

## Odds

**Average rounds to burst the gate**, if every crew member has the same bonus:

| Crew bonus | +2 | +4 | +5 | +6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Average rounds | 7.5 | 5.7 | 5.1 | 4.7 |

**Who wins:**

| Matchup | +4 vs +4 | +5 vs +4 | +6 vs +4 | +4 vs +2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The stronger team wins | 50% | 62% | 72% | 74% |

A crew with mixed bonuses is close to a crew of its average bonus.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any order of crew on the ram.
- Chanting, drums and calling the swing.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- A weakened gate, or tampering with the rival's ram.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crack your own gate's bar the night before | Anyone before the race | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Your gate bursts at 8 cracks |
| Grease the rival ram's rope handles | Anyone before the race | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival crew has −2 on its checks |
| Hidden spell (*enhance ability*, Bull's Strength), by touch before the race: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the back | A crew member or a friend | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | That crew member has advantage on their checks |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Smashcastle Siege is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A team that's in on it is disqualified.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 10 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Kord sneaks into the pit the night before and cracks Red's gate bar.
- Kord: Sleight of Hand **9** + 1 = **10**. The Steward: **4** + 5 = **9**. Not caught.
- Red's gate now bursts at 8 cracks. In the example above, Red reached 9 in round 4, so **Red wins a round earlier**.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The gates** are rebuilt every month to a new design by a local engineer. She's trying to get a city wall contract.
- **A mercenary captain** pays a team's entry each month and watches how they work together.
