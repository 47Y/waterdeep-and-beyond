# Mounted Joust

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *In the middle of the pit, two carved war-horses stand side by side at the ends of two long arms, one longer than the other. The operator winds the great spring until it groans, then pulls the lever. The arms whip apart in opposite directions. Halfway round, the riders meet, lances lowered.*

**Description:** A monthly joust on the House's **jousting carousel**. Each rider sits on a wooden horse at the end of an arm. The arms are sprung in **opposite directions** and meet **once**, halfway round. Each run is one **tilt**. Score points over **three runs**, or **unhorse** your rival to win outright.

## How to Play

### The carousel

- **A pivot** in the middle of the pit with two arms: one **20 ft** long and one **15 ft** long, each carrying a wooden horse.
- **A run:** both riders start side by side at the start line (0°). The operator winds the spring and releases it, and the arms swing out in **opposite directions**.
- **The tilt:** the riders meet **180° away**, halfway round. The two arms pass **5 ft apart**, well inside a lance's reach.
- **One meeting per run.** By the time the arms would come back round to the start, the spring is spent. The operator winds it again for the next run.
- **Weapon:** the House's practice lance (**1d8 + Str**, bludgeoning). No spells.
- The same carousel runs [[1 - Ring Tilt|Ring Tilt]] on the same day. Each player picks which of the two games to enter.

### Magic items

Riders may use **their own armour and magic items**. An NPC rider facing a PC carries one item from the House vault.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Magic Items]]

### A bout, step by step

Each run is one **tilt**. Both riders attack each other **at the same time**.

1. **A hit scores 1 point**, or **2 points if the damage is 8 or more** (the lance shatters).
2. **On a hit**, the target makes a **Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check (their choice), **DC 10 + the damage**. **A failure unhorses them, and they lose at once.**
3. **If both riders are unhorsed in the same tilt**, the bout goes to points.

**After three runs**, the rider with the most points wins. If it's tied, ride another run.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Arena Ward]]

**Netted curtain:** up (lance splinters fly).

## Dice Examples

**Thokk** (PC fighter 3, own armour AC 16, lance +5, 1d8 + 3, Athletics +5, HP 28).
**Dame Rowan** (NPC knight, lance +5, 1d8 + 3, seat +3, HP 52). She carries the House's *cloak of protection*: **AC 19** (18 + 1). The cloak's +1 to saving throws doesn't help her seat checks, which are ability checks.

**Run 1**
- Thokk: **14** + 5 = 19 against AC 19, a hit (meeting the AC hits): **6** + 3 = **9**. That's 8 or more: **2 points**. Rowan's seat: DC 19. **17** + 3 = **20**. She stays on.
- Rowan: **11** + 5 = 16 against AC 16, a hit: **3** + 3 = **6**. **1 point.** Thokk's seat: DC 16. **13** + 5 = **18**. He stays on.
- **Score:** Thokk 2, Rowan 1.

**Run 2**
- Thokk: **7** + 5 = 12, a miss.
- Rowan: **19** + 5 = 24, a hit: **8** + 3 = **11**. **2 points.** Thokk's seat: DC 21. **16** + 5 = **21**. He hangs on! Thokk is at 11 HP.
- **Score:** Thokk 2, Rowan 3.

**Run 3**
- Thokk: **18** + 5 = 23, a hit: **5** + 3 = **8**. **2 points.** Rowan's seat: DC 18. **4** + 3 = **7**. **Rowan is unhorsed. Thokk wins.**
- Rowan's lance misses: **5** + 5 = 10.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Your own armour and magic items, and any class feature that isn't a spell.
- Choosing Athletics or Acrobatics fresh for each seat check.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell**, from a rider or from the crowd.
- A strap to tie yourself to the saddle, a lance cracked in advance so it shatters on any hit, or a bribed operator.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| A hidden saddle strap | Rider | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on seat checks |
| Pre-crack your own lance | Rider, or anyone in the armoury | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The lance shatters on its first hit and scores **2 points**, whatever the damage. Damage and the seat check stay normal. |
| Bribe the operator to jolt the rival's arm (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The rival has disadvantage on their tilt in one run |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Mounted Joust is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|

### Cheating dice example

Quill sneaks into the armoury to crack Thokk's lance, so Thokk's first hit shatters it for 2 points.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **11** + 7 = **18**. The Steward: **15** + 5 = **20**. **Caught.**
- If Thokk was in on it, he's disqualified. Quill is banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 20 gp, 2 days serving (greasing the carousel), or wait 3 months.
