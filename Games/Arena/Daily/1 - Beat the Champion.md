# Beat the Champion

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *"Who will face the champion?" The herald points into the crowd. Last three rounds and get your money back. Bloody him and you'll be paid. Beat him, and the title's yours.*

**Description:** A daily open challenge. There are no challenges on Fight Night, on Flood Days or on championship final days (see [[Arena Schedule]]). Anyone can pay to fight a **House champion** of their chosen tier. You're paid for **surviving**, for **hurting** the champion and, above all, for **beating** them. Or put up a **high-stakes wager**: if it's big enough, a champion matches it, and the winner takes the pot. Fights use normal combat.

## How to Play

There are **two ways to challenge**:
- **The paid challenge:** pay a small entry and get paid for how well you do.
- **The high-stakes duel:** wager real money against a stronger champion. Winner takes the pot.

### The paid challenge: choose your champion

| Tier | Champion | Entry | Survive 3 rounds | Champion below half HP | Defeat the champion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thug (CR 1/2) | 1 gp | 1 gp | 3 gp | 10 gp |
| 2 | Gladiator (CR 5) | 5 gp | 5 gp | 15 gp | 50 gp |
| 3 | Champion (CR 9, *Volo's*) | 20 gp | 20 gp | 60 gp | 200 gp |
| 4 | A named legend (DM's choice) | 50 gp | 50 gp | 150 gp | 500 gp |

**You're paid the single highest reward you earn** (not all of them). A challenger who defeats the champion also gets their **name on the board** for that tier.

### Magic items

Challengers bring everything they've earned: weapons, armour, **magic items** and spells. **Every House champion carries one magic item** of the tier being challenged.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Magic Items]]

### A challenge, step by step

1. **Pay the entry** and choose a tier.
2. **Roll initiative** and fight with normal combat rules. Your own weapons, armour, magic items and spells are all allowed.
3. **The fight ends** when either fighter drops to **0 HP** or yields.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Arena Ward]]

**Netted curtain:** up.

### NPC challenges

When no PC is fighting, skip the items and settle it with **Clash**: see [[Arena Combat Rules#Clash]].

### The high-stakes duel

A champion won't risk their name for pocket change. **Make it worth their while.**

1. **Name your wager.** It must be at least the minimum for your tier. **The champion matches it.** Both stakes go into the **pot**.

| Your level | Minimum wager |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | 25 gp |
| 5–10 | 250 gp |
| 11–16 | 1,000 gp |
| 17–20 | 5,000 gp |

2. **The House picks the champion:** **1 or 2 grades above you** on the Clash grade table (by your level). The champion carries one House magic item of your tier.
3. **Fight 1 v 1** with normal combat rules and everything you own. **No milestone rewards**: it's win or lose.
4. **The fight ends** when either fighter drops to **0 HP** or yields. **Yielding loses the wager.**
5. **The winner takes the pot**, minus the House's **10%** cut.

## Dice Examples

**A paid challenge.** **Quill** (PC rogue 3, HP 21, AC 14, shortsword +5, 1d6 + 3, Sneak Attack 2d6) challenges the **Tier 1 Thug** (HP 32, two mace attacks +4, 1d6 + 2). The Thug wears a *cloak of protection*, so his **AC is 12** (11 + 1) and he gets +1 on saving throws.

**The bookie:** Quill (level 3, Pit regular, +4) against the Thug (Green, +2). Quill wins 68%.

There's no cover in the pit, so Quill can't hide. Instead he uses **Steady Aim** each turn (a bonus action: he doesn't move, and his attack has advantage, so Sneak Attack applies).

**Round 1**
- **Quill:** advantage, **7** and **15**, keeps 15: 15 + 5 = 20. Hit, with Sneak Attack: **4** + 3 + **6** + **4** = **17**. The Thug is at 15, **below half** (16). Quill has earned at least 3 gp.
- **Thug:** **12** + 4 = 16. Hit: **5** + 2 = 7. Quill is at 14. **9** + 4 = 13, a miss.

**Round 2**
- **Quill:** advantage, **17** and **5**, keeps 17: 17 + 5 = 22. Hit, with Sneak Attack: **2** + 3 + **3** + **1** = **9**. The Thug is at 6.
- **Thug:** **4** + 4 = 8, a miss. **14** + 4 = 18. Hit: **3** + 2 = 5. Quill is at 9.

**Round 3**
- **Quill:** advantage, **8** and **11**, keeps 11: 11 + 5 = 16. Hit, with Sneak Attack: **3** + 3 + **4** + **2** = **12**. **The Thug drops.** Quill wins **10 gp** (only the highest reward) and his name goes on the Tier 1 board.

**A high-stakes duel.** **Thokk** (PC fighter 3, HP 28, own armour AC 16, battleaxe +5, 1d8 + 3, Action Surge, Second Wind) wagers **25 gp**, the level 1–4 minimum.
- **The champion:** Thokk is level 3 (Pit regular). The House sends **Ser Aldric**, a **Veteran**, one grade up (HP 58, two longsword attacks +5, 1d8 + 3). He carries the House's *cloak of protection*: **AC 18** (splint 17 + 1).
- **The pot:** 25 + 25 = **50 gp**. The House's 10% is 5 gp, so the winner gets **45 gp**.
- **The bookie:** Thokk (+4) against Aldric (+6). Aldric wins 68%; Thokk 32%.

**Round 1**
- **Thokk:** **15** + 5 = 20, a hit: **6** + 3 = **9**. Action Surge: **13** + 5 = 18, a hit (meeting the AC hits): **4** + 3 = **7**. Aldric is at 42.
- **Aldric:** **14** + 5 = 19, a hit: **5** + 3 = **8**. Thokk is at 20. **9** + 5 = 14, a miss.

**Round 2**
- **Thokk:** **8** + 5 = 13, a miss.
- **Aldric:** **16** + 5 = 21, a hit: **7** + 3 = **10**. **12** + 5 = 17, a hit: **3** + 3 = **6**. Thokk is at 4.

**Round 3**
- **Thokk** uses **Second Wind**: **6** + 3 = **9** HP back. He's at 13. Then **17** + 5 = 22, a hit: **8** + 3 = **11**. Aldric is at 31.
- **Aldric:** **11** + 5 = 16, a hit: **4** + 3 = **7**. **18** + 5 = 23, a hit: **2** + 3 = **5**. Thokk is at 1.

**Round 4:** Thokk **yields**. **Aldric takes the 45 gp.** Thokk is out his 25 gp, and the House keeps 5 gp.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **The paid challenge:** entry and rewards are in the table above.
- **The high-stakes duel:** the winner takes the pot, minus 10%.
- **The House keeps:** the entries of everyone who loses a paid challenge, 10% of every duel pot, and the betting cut.

## Odds

The bookie prices each challenge with Clash, using the challenger's level. In a high-stakes duel, the champion is 1 grade up (the challenger wins **32%**) or 2 grades up (the challenger wins **18%**).

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Your own weapons, armour, magic items and spells.
- Yielding at any time. In a paid challenge, you keep any reward you've already earned. In a high-stakes duel, yielding loses the wager.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any help from outside the pit**: spells, thrown items or shouted warnings with magic in them.
- Tampering with the champion's magic item, drugging the champion, or bribing the champion to go easy.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swap the champion's magic item for a fake | Anyone backstage | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The champion fights without their item |
| Drug the champion's water | Anyone backstage | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The champion is **poisoned** for the fight |
| Bribe the champion to go easy (twice the entry) | Anyone backstage | Cha (Persuasion) | The champion holds back: disadvantage on their first attack each round |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Beat the Champion is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. The fine depends on the tier.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Tier | Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 gp | 1 day |
| 2 | 50 gp | 5 days |
| 3 | 200 gp | 20 days |
| 4 | 500 gp | 50 days |

### Cheating dice example

Before a Tier 2 challenge, Mirela tries to drug the Gladiator's water.
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **15** + 2 = **17**. The Steward: **13** + 5 = **18**. **Caught.**
- If the challenger was in on it, they're disqualified. Mirela is banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear her name: 50 gp, 5 days serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The Tier 4 champion** is a different legend every month. This month it's someone the party has met before, and they've been asking for the party by name.
- **The Tier 1 Thug, Bram,** loses on purpose to young challengers who look like they need the money.
