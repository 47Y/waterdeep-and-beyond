# Lizard Races

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Sand is raked smooth, the gates rattle, and a row of hissing lizards strain at their leashes. Three rounds, one finish line, and every coin in the stands riding on which scaly beast wants it most.*

**Description:** A weekly race on the arena sand. Each lizard has a handler who can **urge** it on during each round of the race. A successful urge makes the lizard faster, but a failed one makes it sulk. After three rounds, the lizard with the highest total wins. Anyone can enter a lizard, and everyone else can bet. Once a year the best lizards meet in the **Grand Derby**.

Lizards can be bought, trained and sold. See [[1 - Lizard Ownership|Lizard Ownership]].

## How to Play

1. **Line up.** Each lizard has a **Speed** bonus and a **Temper DC** from its breed (see Breeds below). Training can improve both.
2. **The race has three rounds.** Each round is one section of the track:
   - **Round 1, The Gate:** bursting out of the starting gate.
   - **Round 2, The Sand Pit:** churning through deep sand mid-track.
   - **Round 3, The Home Stretch:** the sprint to the finish line.
3. **Each round works the same way:**
   1. **Urge (optional).** Before rolling, the handler can urge the lizard with a **Wisdom (Animal Handling)** check against the lizard's **Temper DC**.
      - **Success:** the lizard rolls with **advantage** this round.
      - **Failure:** the lizard rolls with **disadvantage** this round because it sulks.
      - **Don't urge:** roll normally.
   2. **Race roll.** Every lizard rolls **d20 + Speed**.
   3. **Keep a running total.** Announce the standings after each round so the crowd can cheer.
4. **Finish.** After Round 3, the highest total wins, the next highest is second, and so on.
5. **Photo finish.** If lizards tie for a placing, each tied lizard rolls **d20 + Speed** with no urging. Highest wins. If they tie again, repeat.

### Race Classes

Each race day runs one race per class, so new lizards don't have to face champions. A lizard's class depends on its Training Level (see [[1 - Lizard Ownership|Lizard Ownership]]).

| Class | Training Level | Entry |
|---|---|---|
| Novice | 0–1 | 1 gp |
| Veteran | 2–3 | 5 gp |
| Champion | 4–5 | 20 gp |

## Dice Examples

**The field (Novice class):**
- **Sir Scuttles:** Racing Gecko (Speed +1, Temper DC 10). Handler: **Mirela** (PC), Animal Handling **+3**.
- **Old Brick:** Mud Skink (Speed +0, Temper DC 8). Handler: **Gus** (NPC), who never urges.
- **Red Rattle:** Sand Runner (Speed +2, Temper DC 13). Handler: **Vessa** (NPC), Animal Handling **+5**.

**Round 1: The Gate**
- Mirela urges: **12** + 3 = **15** against DC 10. Success, so advantage. Sir Scuttles rolls 7 and **14**, keeps the 14: 14 + 1 = **15**.
- Gus doesn't urge. Old Brick rolls **11** + 0 = **11**.
- Vessa urges: **4** + 5 = **9** against DC 13. Failure, so disadvantage. Red Rattle rolls 17 and **6** and must keep the 6: 6 + 2 = **8**.
- *Standings:* Scuttles 15, Brick 11, Rattle 8.

**Round 2: The Sand Pit**
- Mirela doesn't urge. Sir Scuttles rolls **9** + 1 = **10**. Total **25**.
- Old Brick rolls **13** + 0 = **13**. Total **24**.
- Vessa urges: **15** + 5 = **20** against DC 13. Success, so advantage. Red Rattle rolls **18** and 11: 18 + 2 = **20**. Total **28**.
- *Standings:* Rattle 28, Scuttles 25, Brick 24.

**Round 3: The Home Stretch**
- Mirela urges: **9** + 3 = **12** against DC 10. Success. Sir Scuttles rolls **16** and 5: 16 + 1 = **17**. Total **42**.
- Old Brick rolls **18** + 0 = **18**. Total **42**.
- Vessa doesn't urge. Red Rattle rolls **10** + 2 = **12**. Total **40**.
- *Final:* Scuttles 42, Brick 42, Rattle 40. **That's a tie for first.**

**Photo finish**
- Sir Scuttles: **13** + 1 = **14**.
- Old Brick: **11** + 0 = **11**.
- **Sir Scuttles wins.** Old Brick is second and Red Rattle third.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** set by class (see Race Classes above).
- **Purse:** first place takes 60% of that race's entry fees and second takes 20%. The House keeps 20%.
- **Winner's extras:** the lizard's name is chalked on the Winner's Board until next week's race.
- **Spectator betting:** everyone's bets go into one pool. The House takes 15% and the rest is split among people who backed the winner.
  - *Example:* the pool is 80 gp and 10 gp of it was bet on Sir Scuttles. After the House's 15%, 68 gp is left. 68 ÷ 10 = **6.8 gp paid back for every 1 gp bet on Sir Scuttles**.

## Grand Derby (Yearly Championship)

Once a year, at the end of the racing season, the best lizards race for the title.

- **Who can enter:** any lizard that won at least one weekly race this year, from any class. There are 8 places.
  - If more than 8 qualify, the lizards with the most wins get in. Ties are settled with a d20 roll-off.
  - If fewer than 8 qualify, the empty places are sold for 50 gp each, first come, first served.
- **Entry:** 20 gp per lizard.
- **The race:** the same rules as a weekly race, but with **five rounds**:
  1. The Gate
  2. The Sand Pit
  3. The Water Jump
  4. The Rock Garden
  5. The Home Stretch
- **Purse:** all entry fees plus 200 gp added by the House. First place takes 60%, second 25%, third 15%.
- **The Champion gets:**
  - A gold collar.
  - The title **Derby Champion**, which doubles the lizard's sale value (see [[1 - Lizard Ownership|Lizard Ownership]]).
  - Its name painted on the arena wall permanently.
- **Betting:** the same pool as weekly races, with the House taking 15%.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- The handler's own Animal Handling skill.
- The lizard's breed and its trained traits (see [[1 - Lizard Ownership|Lizard Ownership]]).
- Talking to, coaxing and cheering for your lizard.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** on a lizard or a rival on race day, such as *speak with animals*, *longstrider*, *haste* or *animal friendship*.
- **Doping** a lizard, spooking a rival lizard, or bribing track staff.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** Each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dope your lizard (pepper paste) | Handler | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on every round. No urging needed. |
| Spook a rival | Anyone at the rail | Dex (Sleight of Hand) to flick a pebble | That rival rolls with disadvantage for one round |
| Hidden spell (*longstrider*), by touch before the race | The handler, or anyone who can reach the lizard before the gate opens | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | +2 on every round |
| Bribe the gatekeeper (10 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | +5 on Round 1 (an early gate) |
| Pay a rival handler to lose (25 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | That lizard rolls with disadvantage on every round |

### Cheating dice example

Before Round 2, Vessa flicks a pebble at Sir Scuttles.
- Vessa: Sleight of Hand **15** + 4 = **19**. The Steward: **11** + 5 = **16**. The Steward misses it, so Sir Scuttles will roll with disadvantage this round.
- Mirela is watching, so she rolls Wisdom (Perception): **17** + 3 = **20**. That beats Vessa's 19, so she saw the cheat.
- Mirela chooses to **report it**. Vessa and Red Rattle are disqualified, Vessa's portrait is pinned to the track's notice board, and she is banned from the track for three months.
- To clear her name, Vessa must pay the Novice fine of 10 gp, spend 1 day in the stocks or scooping stalls, or wait 3 months.
- *If Mirela had stayed quiet,* she could have demanded a cut of Vessa's winnings, or kept the secret to use later.
