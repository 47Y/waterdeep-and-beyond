# Champion (Pit Fighter)

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Steel rings on steel. A gout of fire rolls across the sand and the front row ducks behind the net. Two against two, and only one rule: stay standing. The crowd on the rim roars every time someone goes down.*

**Description:** The arena's weekly fight card: good old-fashioned pit fighting. Bouts are **1 v 1, 2 v 2 or 3 v 3**, between any mix of **PCs, NPCs and creatures** from the beast-pens. Fighters bring **everything they've got**: weapons, armour, magic items and spells. Every bout is **normal combat, fought to the finish**. Bouts with no PC are settled quickly with **Clash**. Every bout earns **season points**, and at the end of the year the top fighters and teams battle for the **Pit Champion** title.

## How to Play

### The card

- Each week the House runs several bouts: **1 v 1, 2 v 2 or 3 v 3**.
- **Fighters can be PCs, NPCs or creatures** from the beast-pens, in any mix: PCs against NPCs, NPCs against a creature, creature against creature.
- **Full gear:** your own weapons, armour, magic items, **spells** and class features.

### Magic items

![[Arena Combat Rules#Magic Items]]

### A bout, step by step

1. **Roll initiative** and fight with **normal combat rules**.
2. **A fighter is out** when they drop to **0 HP** or **yield** (drop your weapon and raise a hand). A fighter who yields takes no more actions and leaves the pit. Attacking a fighter who has yielded counts the same as attacking a fallen one.
3. **A side loses** when **all its fighters are out**. The other side wins.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Arena Ward]]

**Netted curtain:** up (spells fly).

### Bouts with no PC

When no PC is fighting, settle the bout with **Clash**. For 2 v 2 and 3 v 3, use the **Groups** rule: see [[Arena Combat Rules#Clash]].

### Year-end championship

Every weekly bout scores season points. The top 8 in each format qualify for the year-end final: **three brackets**, for 1 v 1, 2 v 2 and 3 v 3. The 1 v 1 winner is the **Pit Champion** and wears the belt for a year.

## Dice Examples

**A 2 v 2 bout.**
- **Red:** Thokk (PC fighter 3, HP 28, own armour AC 16, battleaxe +5, 1d8 + 3, Action Surge, Second Wind) and Quill (PC rogue 3, HP 21, leather AC 14, shortsword +5, 1d6 + 3, Sneak Attack 2d6).
- **Blue:** Grit (NPC thug, HP 32, two mace attacks +4, 1d6 + 2) and a **dire wolf** (creature, HP 37, AC 14, bite +5, 2d6 + 3; on a hit the target makes a **DC 13 Strength save** or falls **prone**; **Pack Tactics**: advantage when an ally is next to the target).
- Grit faces PCs, so he carries the House's *cloak of protection*: **AC 12** (leather 11 + 1). The wolf can't use an item.

**Round 1**
- **Quill** stabs Grit. Thokk is next to Grit, so Sneak Attack applies: **13** + 5 = 18, a hit: **4** + 3 + **5** + **2** = **14**. Grit is at 18.
- **The wolf** bites Thokk. Grit is next to Thokk, so Pack Tactics gives advantage: **15** and **8**, keeps 15: 15 + 5 = 20, a hit: **5** + **4** + 3 = **12**. Thokk is at 16. His Strength save: **11** + 5 = 16. He stays on his feet.
- **Thokk** swings at Grit: **14** + 5 = 19, a hit: **6** + 3 = **9**. Grit is at 9.
- **Grit:** **12** + 4 = 16, a hit: **3** + 2 = **5**. Thokk is at 11. **7** + 4 = 11, a miss.

**Round 2**
- **Quill** stabs Grit again, with Sneak Attack: **10** + 5 = 15, a hit: **2** + 3 + **3** + **4** = **12**. **Grit drops.**
- **The wolf** bites Thokk. No ally beside him now, so no advantage: **9** + 5 = 14, a miss.
- **Thokk** uses Action Surge on the wolf: **16** + 5 = 21, a hit: **5** + 3 = **8**. **12** + 5 = 17, a hit: **7** + 3 = **10**. The wolf is at 19.

**Round 3**
- **Quill** stabs the wolf. Thokk is next to it, so Sneak Attack applies: **17** + 5 = 22, a hit: **3** + 3 + **6** + **2** = **14**. The wolf is at 5.
- **The wolf** bites Thokk: **18** + 5 = 23, a hit: **3** + **2** + 3 = **8**. Thokk is at 3. His Strength save: **4** + 5 = 9. **He's knocked prone.**
- **Thokk** stands up (half his movement) and uses **Second Wind** (a bonus action): **7** + 3 = **10** HP back. He's at 13. Then: **11** + 5 = 16, a hit: **4** + 3 = **7**. **The wolf drops. Red wins.**

**Edge case: a yield.** Say Quill had been at 4 HP after round 2. He drops his blade and raises a hand: **he's out**, and no one may attack him. Thokk fights on alone. If Thokk drops too, Blue wins.

**A bout with no PC, using Clash:** Grit (Green, +2) against Kord (a berserker, Pit regular, +4).
- The bookie: Kord (68%) pays **1 gp 2 sp** per 1 gp. Grit (32%) pays **2 gp 7 sp** per 1 gp. Mirela bets **2 gp on Grit**.

| Exchange | Grit | Kord | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **15** + 2 = 17 | **9** + 4 = 13 | Grit 1–0 |
| 2 | **8** + 2 = 10 | **14** + 4 = 18 | 1–1 |
| 3 | **12** + 2 = 14 | **6** + 4 = 10 | Grit 2–1 |
| 4 | **16** + 2 = 18 | **11** + 4 = 15 | **Grit wins, 3–1** |

**An upset!** Mirela collects **5 gp 4 sp**.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Everything you own: weapons, armour, magic items, spells and class features.
- Yielding at any time.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any help from outside the pit**: spells or items from the crowd.
- Drugging a rival's water, swapping a rival's House item for a fake, or bribing the pen-master to send out a weaker creature.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drug a rival's water before the bout | Anyone in the corner | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival is **poisoned** for the bout |
| Swap a rival's House item for a fake | Anyone backstage | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival fights without their item |
| Bribe the pen-master to send out a weaker creature (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The creature is one Clash grade lower |
| Hidden spell from the crowd (*healing word*, *bless*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Before the bout, Quill tries to drug Grit's water.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **13** + 7 = **20**. The Steward: **16** + 5 = **21**. **Caught.**
- If Thokk was in on it, Red is disqualified and Blue wins the bout. Quill is banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 10 gp, 1 day in the stocks or serving (raking the sand), or wait 3 months.

- **Grit** fights every week for the 1 gp loser's purse. He owes money to someone who keeps a seat by the rail.
