# Spectator Betting

Shared betting rules for tavern contests and arena events. Each game embeds the sections it needs, so a change here updates every game. For fixed-odds bets on anything in the building, see [[Proposition Betting]].

## Betting Pool

Spectators bet into a **pool** on who will win. The House doesn't bet against anyone; it just keeps a cut.

1. **Bet before the start.** Each spectator bets any amount on one contestant (or team). Betting closes when the event begins.
2. **The House takes 15%** of the whole pool.
3. **The rest goes to the winners**, split in proportion to how much each person bet on the winner.

**Payout for each 1 gp bet on the winner = (total pool × 0.85) ÷ (total bet on the winner)**

- **Popular favourites pay little.** Long shots pay a lot.
- **A tie for first:** the winners' share is split between bets on each tied winner, in proportion.
- **The event is cancelled:** every bet is refunded in full.
- **A contestant is disqualified for cheating:** bets on them lose.

## Pool Example

The pool for a race has 100 gp in total: **60 gp on Thokk**, **30 gp on Mirela** and **10 gp on Vessa**.
- The House takes 15 gp, leaving **85 gp** for the winners.
- **Mirela wins.** Each 1 gp bet on her pays 85 ÷ 30 = **2 gp 8 sp** (back, including the original 1 gp).
- A spectator who bet **5 gp on Mirela** collects **14 gp 1 sp** (rounded down to the silver).
- If **Vessa** had won instead, each 1 gp on her would have paid 85 ÷ 10 = **8 gp 5 sp**.
