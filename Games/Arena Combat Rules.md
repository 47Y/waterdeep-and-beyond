# Arena Combat Rules

The shared safety and fighting rules for every arena event. Each arena game embeds the sections it needs, so a change here updates every game.

## Arena Ward

Every arena event, whether it's a fight or not, runs under the Arena Ward.

- **Real damage.** Weapons, claws, falls and traps deal real hit-point damage.
- **0 HP means out, not dead.** A creature that drops to 0 HP falls unconscious and is **stable**.
- **Healers on standby.** As soon as the referee calls the fall, healers rush onto the sand and bring the fallen creature back to **1 HP**. Any further healing is up to the creature.
- **Spectator safety.** A **netted curtain** can be raised around the whole of the first floor to protect the crowd. Each game says whether it goes up.

### Attacking a fallen fighter

Attacking a creature at 0 HP is **attempted murder**, not cheating. The **Arena Master** (Wisdom (Insight) +5) decides whether it was on purpose.

| | On purpose | Accident |
|---|---|---|
| **Examples** | Keeps attacking after the referee calls the fall | A blow already swinging as the opponent dropped; an area effect started before the fall |
| **What happens** | Manacled and taken to the **lower cells**, out of public view, to await the city watch | The referee stops the bout. The attacker **loses the bout**. No arrest. |

**Claiming it was an accident:** the attacker rolls Charisma (Deception) against the Arena Master's Wisdom (Insight). With PCs, the DM already knows their intent; the roll is only for NPCs, or for a PC who lies.

## Magic Items

Players keep what they've earned. **PCs may use their own magic items** in the events that allow them (each game says so).

**Evening the odds:** every **NPC who faces a PC** carries **one magic item** from the House vault, matched to the PCs' tier. In a team event, each NPC on the other side carries one. The item goes back to the vault after the event.

| Tier | PC level | Item rarity | Examples (DM picks one) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–4 | Uncommon | *Cloak of protection*, a *+1 weapon*, a *+1 shield*, *wand of magic missiles* |
| 2 | 5–10 | Rare | *Ring of protection*, a *+2 weapon*, *cloak of displacement*, *wand of lightning bolts* |
| 3 | 11–16 | Very rare | A *+3 weapon*, *animated shield*, *dwarven thrower*, *staff of fire* |
| 4 | 17–20 | Legendary | *Defender*, *armor of invulnerability*, *staff of the magi* |

- **Beasts** can't use items. If the party is well equipped, the DM may send out a beast one CR higher instead.
- **PC against PC:** no House items. Each side uses their own.
- **No PC in the event:** skip the items and settle it with Clash.

## Clash

A quick way to settle a fight **without running combat**. Use it for:
- **Background fights:** NPC against NPC, NPC against creature, or creature against creature.
- **Quick betting:** the bookie's price for any fight, including one with a PC in it.

**When a PC is fighting, run real combat.** Clash only sets the betting price.

### 1. Pick each side's grade

You don't need a stat block. Pick the grade by **challenge rating (CR)**, or by feel. To price a fight with a PC in it, use the PC's **level**.

| Grade | Clash bonus | CR | PC level | NPC examples | Creature examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Green | +2 | 1/4–1/2 | 1–2 | Tavern brawler, thug | Wolf, giant goat, black bear |
| Pit regular | +4 | 1–2 | 3–4 | Berserker, bandit captain | Brown bear, dire wolf, lion, ogre |
| Veteran | +6 | 3–4 | 5–7 | Veteran, knight | Owlbear, manticore, ettin |
| Champion | +8 | 5–7 | 8–10 | Gladiator | Troll, hill giant, chimera |
| Legend | +10 | 8+ | 11+ | A famous champion, a warlord | Young green dragon, hydra |

**Groups:** a side made of several fighters uses its typical member's grade, **+1 for each fighter it has more than the other side**. *Example:* four level 3 hunters (+4) against one owlbear (+6): the hunters get +4 + 3 = **+7**.

### 2. Fight

1. **Each exchange:** both sides roll **d20 + Clash bonus**. The higher total wins the exchange. **Ties are rerolled.**
2. **The first side to win 3 exchanges wins the fight.** The loser drops to 0 HP and is stable (see Arena Ward).

### Clash odds

| Bonus difference | 0 | 1 | 2 (1 grade) | 3 | 4 (2 grades) | 5 | 6 (3 grades) | 8 (4 grades) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The stronger side wins | 50% | 59.5% | 68% | 76% | 82% | 87% | 91% | 96% |

The bookie prices bets from these chances. See [[Proposition Betting]].

### Clash example

**A pit regular (berserker, +4) against an owlbear (Veteran, +6).** The owlbear is one grade higher and wins 68% of the time.
- **The bookie's prices:** the owlbear (68%) pays **1 gp 2 sp** per 1 gp. The berserker (32%) pays **2 gp 7 sp** per 1 gp.

| Exchange | Berserker | Owlbear | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **14** + 4 = 18 | **9** + 6 = 15 | Berserker 1–0 |
| 2 | **7** + 4 = 11 | **12** + 6 = 18 | 1–1 |
| 3 | **10** + 4 = 14 | **8** + 6 = 14 | Tie: reroll |
| 3 (again) | **16** + 4 = 20 | **11** + 6 = 17 | Berserker 2–1 |
| 4 | **5** + 4 = 9 | **15** + 6 = 21 | 2–2 |
| 5 | **13** + 4 = 17 | **13** + 6 = 19 | **Owlbear wins, 3–2** |

The berserker drops, and the healers drag him clear.

## Championship Season

The shared season rules for the **Champion** events (Pit Fighter, Spell Caster, Sharpshooter). Each event keeps its own rules for regular bouts; this section covers how you qualify and how the year-end final works.

### Season points

The season runs for **one year**. Every regular event scores:

| Result | Season points |
|---|---|
| **Win** (a bout, a duel or a shoot) | **3** |
| **Take part and lose** | **1** |

- **Separate standings** for each format: 1 v 1, 2 v 2 and 3 v 3 are each ranked on their own.
- **Teams** that want to qualify register a **name and a fixed roster**. The points go to the team, not to each fighter.
- **Events with tiers** (like the Spell Caster's spell-level tiers) keep separate standings for each tier.

### Qualifying

- **The top 8** in each standing win a **slot** in the year-end final.
- **A tie for 8th place:** most wins takes the slot. Still tied: one extra bout (or shoot-off) decides it.
- **A qualifier who can't attend:** the next in the standings takes the slot.

### The year-end final

- **Bracket events** (bouts and duels): an **8-slot knockout**. Quarterfinals, semifinals and the final are held on **three separate days**, so every fighter starts each round fresh, after a long rest.
- **Shooting events:** the 8 qualifiers shoot **one final bird** together.
- **A final with no PC in it** is settled with **Clash**.
- **The winner** takes the title for a year. Each event sets its own prizes.
