# Mother Lode

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Three cut-gem dice rattle down a brass mine chute and spill into an ore cart. Diamond, onyx, emerald, sapphire, ruby, topaz: name your gem, and pray the vein runs true.*

**Description:** A fast dice table where everyone bets on the same roll. The dealer drops **three gem dice** down a chute, and players bet on which gems appear, whether the total is big or small, or on rare results like a triple. It's simple, quick and loud, and the busiest table on the casino floor.

## How to Play

### The dice

Each die is a d6 with a gem on each face:

| d6 | Gem |
|---|---|
| 1 | Diamond |
| 2 | Onyx |
| 3 | Emerald |
| 4 | Sapphire |
| 5 | Ruby |
| 6 | Topaz |

### A round, step by step

1. **Place bets.** Each player places one or more bets from the table below. You can make several different bets on the same roll. No more bets once the dealer drops the dice.
2. **Roll.** The dealer rolls **3d6** down the chute.
3. **Pay out.** Every bet is settled against that one roll. Then the next round begins.

### Bets

| Bet | You win when… | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| **Gem** | Your chosen gem shows on at least one die | 1:1 for one die, 2:1 for two dice, 3:1 for all three |
| **Small** | The total is 4–10, **and** it is not a triple | 1:1 |
| **Big** | The total is 11–17, **and** it is not a triple | 1:1 |
| **Devil's Thirteen** | The total is exactly 13 | 8:1 |
| **Any Lode** | All three dice show the same gem (any triple) | 30:1 |
| **Named Lode** | All three dice show the gem you named | 180:1 |

**Triples beat Small and Big.** If all three dice match, every Small and Big bet loses. This is where the House makes its money on those bets.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Mirela (PC) bets **2 gp on Ruby**. Thokk (NPC) bets **5 gp on Big**. Quill (PC) bets **1 gp on Devil's Thirteen**.

**Round 1**
- The dice show **5, 5, 3** (Ruby, Ruby, Emerald). Total 13.
- Mirela: Ruby shows on **two** dice, so she wins 2:1: **4 gp**.
- Thokk: 13 is Big (11–17) and not a triple, so he wins 1:1: **5 gp**.
- Quill: the total is exactly 13, so he wins 8:1: **8 gp**.

**Round 2**
- Same bets. The dice show **1, 2, 4** (Diamond, Onyx, Sapphire). Total 7.
- Mirela: no Ruby, so she **loses** 2 gp.
- Thokk: 7 is Small, so he **loses** 5 gp.
- Quill: not 13, so he **loses** 1 gp.

**Round 3**
- Same bets. The dice show **5, 6, 6** (Ruby, Topaz, Topaz). Total 17.
- Mirela: Ruby shows on **one** die, so she wins 1:1: **2 gp**.
- Thokk: 17 is Big, so he wins **5 gp**.
- Quill **loses** 1 gp.

**Edge case: a triple**
- Thokk bets 5 gp on Big, and Mirela bets 1 gp on Any Lode. The dice show **4, 4, 4** (three Sapphires). Total 12.
- 12 would normally be Big, but **triples beat Big**, so Thokk **loses** 5 gp.
- Mirela's Any Lode wins 30:1: **30 gp**.

## Odds

| Bet | Chance to win | House edge |
|---|---|---|
| Gem | 42.1% (one die 34.7%, two dice 6.9%, three dice 0.5%) | 7.9% |
| Small or Big | 48.6% | 2.8% |
| Devil's Thirteen | 9.7% | 12.5% |
| Any Lode | 2.8% | 13.9% |
| Named Lode | 0.5% | 16.2% |

**Best bets:** Small or Big. **Worst bet:** Named Lode, but it's the one that makes the crowd scream.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any bet, any amount within the table limits, on any roll.
- Hunches, lucky charms and shouting at the dice.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the dice, such as *unseen servant*.
- Bribing the dealer to use a loaded die, or slipping a bet onto the table after the dice have landed.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| As the dice land in the cart, while every eye is on them, slip a bet onto the felt | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Place a bet after the dice land |
| Bribe the dealer to drop your loaded die (Silver Table 1 gp, Gold Table 10 gp, Platinum Salon 100 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | For 10 rolls, until the House changes the dice, a 1 on that die counts as a 6 |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant* nudge) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | As the dice settle in the cart, the invisible servant turns one die to a face of your choice |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Mother Lode is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

![[Cheating Penalties#Casino]]

### Cheating dice example

At the Gold Table, Quill bets 20 gp on Ruby and tries to slide a Ruby Named Lode bet onto the felt after the dice show 5, 5, 5.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **14** + 7 = **21**. The Watcher: **16** + 5 = **21**. **A tie goes to the Watcher. Caught.**
- His chips and winnings for the night are confiscated, his portrait goes into the House's file, and he's banned for a month.
- His debt is 100 gp (five times his 20 gp bet, under the Gold Table's 250 gp cap), or he owes the House a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **A dwarf engineer** has built a full copy of the chute in her cellar, thrown it 10,000 times and decided that it favors Devil's Thirteen. Tonight she'll stake the 400 gp she raised on her workshop on that one bet. Her apprentice has checked her tally and found that she carried a number wrong: the chute is fair, and the bet loses an eighth of every stake on average. He's too scared to tell her, and he begs the party to do it.
- **A miners' crew** pools a month's wages, 20 gp, for one Named Lode bet on Topaz at 180:1. One of them, drunk, tells the party why: they've struck a topaz seam the mine's owner knows nothing about, and 3,000 gp would buy the mine before he finds out. The bet would pay 3,600 gp, at a chance of 1 in 216. The party could lend them the money for a share, let the dice decide, or sell the secret to the owner.
