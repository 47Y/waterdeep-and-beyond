# The Enchanted Triad

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A row of gnomish machines hums along the casino wall, brass and glass and blinking runes. Drop in a coin, pull the gnome-headed lever, and three enchanted reels whirl. Match them up and the machine sings.*

**Description:** A gnomish coin machine. Pay one coin and pull the lever, and three wheels of symbols (the **reels**) spin behind glass. You win if the symbols match or if Gnomes show. It needs no skill and no decisions.

## How to Play

### The reels

Each machine has three reels. One player uses a machine at a time, and every machine takes **silver, gold or platinum** coins. At the table, each reel is a d10. A 0 on the d10 counts as 10, the **Gnome**.

| d10 | Symbol |
|---|---|
| 1 | Copper |
| 2 | Silver |
| 3 | Gold |
| 4 | Gem |
| 5 | Crown |
| 6 | Key |
| 7 | Star |
| 8 | Moon |
| 9 | Sun |
| 10 (0) | **Gnome** |

### A pull, step by step

1. **Pay.** Put one coin in the machine: **1 sp**, **1 gp** or **1 pp**. You choose for each pull, and a bigger coin pays bigger prizes.
2. **Pull the lever.** Roll **three d10**, one at a time, from the left reel to the right reel.
3. **Check the result.** Look at the payout table, highest result first. Only the highest result pays.
4. **Collect.** An attendant pays any winnings. Then you can pull again.

### Payouts

| Result | Example | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| **Three Gnomes** | 10, 10, 10 | 200:1 |
| **Any other triple** | 6, 6, 6 | 30:1 |
| **Two Gnomes** | 10, 10, 4 | 5:1 |
| **One Gnome** | 10, 3, 7 | Your coin comes back (no win, no loss) |
| Anything else | 5, 5, 2 or 1, 4, 8 | Lose |

**A pair that isn't Gnomes loses.** Only Gnomes and triples pay.

## Dice Examples

**At the machine:** Vessa (NPC) feeds the machine **1 gp** per pull.

**Pull 1**
- Reels: **3, 0, 7** (Gold, **Gnome**, Star).
- One Gnome: her **1 gp comes back**.

**Pull 2**
- Reels: **5, 5, 2** (Crown, Crown, Silver).
- A pair of Crowns, but no Gnomes and no triple. She **loses** 1 gp.

**Pull 3**
- Reels: **0, 0, 4** (**Gnome**, **Gnome**, Gem).
- Two Gnomes pays 5:1: she wins **5 gp**.

**Edge case: a triple**
- Reels: **6, 6, 6** (Key, Key, Key).
- Any other triple pays 30:1: she wins **30 gp**. The machine plays a jingle and spits a brass token marked "LUCKY".

## Prizes & Stakes

| Coin | Two Gnomes | Any other triple | Three Gnomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 sp | 5 sp | 3 gp | **20 gp** |
| 1 gp | 5 gp | 30 gp | **200 gp** |
| 1 pp | 5 pp | 30 pp | **200 pp (2,000 gp)** |

These are the winnings. You also keep the coin you paid for that pull.

## Odds

| Result | Chance |
|---|---|
| Three Gnomes | 0.1% (1 in 1,000) |
| Any other triple | 0.9% |
| Two Gnomes | 2.7% |
| One Gnome (coin back) | 24.3% |
| Lose | 72.0% |

**House edge: 11.5%.** Players come back for the noise and the chance at 200:1.

**The House's biggest risk:** Three Gnomes on a platinum coin, which pays 2,000 gp.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Pulling as fast or as slowly as you like.
- Talking to the machine, kicking the stand (gently) and lucky charms.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the reels, such as *unseen servant*.
- Opening the cabinet, catching a reel with a wire, or feeding the machine fake coins.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| As the last reel slows, catch it with a thin wire through the coin slot | Player, proficient with thieves' tools | Dex (thieves' tools) | Move the last reel one symbol up or down. The symbols run in the order of the table above, and Gnome is next to Copper |
| Drop a lead slug in place of a real coin | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | This pull is free |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant*, reaching in through the coin slot) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | As a reel slows, the invisible servant stops it on the symbol of your choice |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Enchanted Triad is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Quill (PC) puts in a gold piece. The first two reels stop on **0, 0**, and the last is slowing towards **9**, one symbol short of a Gnome. He reaches in with a wire.
- Quill: thieves' tools **9** + 7 = **16**. The Watcher: **14** + 5 = **19**. **Caught.**
- His chips and the night's winnings are taken, his portrait goes in the House's file, he's banned for a month, and he owes **5 gp** (five times his 1 gp pull) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Pibble Brasswhistle**, the gnome who builds the machines, has noticed that one of them spins by itself after closing and always stops on Key, Moon, Crown. Pibble mentions it at the bar, and a locksmith nearby goes pale. Those three signs open a vault she built last year for a private bank. She offers the party 200 gp to find out who else knows, before she has to tell the bank.
- **An angry gnome** swears the grinning Gnome on the reels is a portrait of her husband, who walked out eleven years ago owing her everything. She feeds silver into a machine all night and shouts at it. She offers the party 50 gp to find out who sat for that picture. Pibble goes very quiet when she asks, and later asks the party to leave it alone.
