# Dwarven Hazard

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The shooter blows on the bones, the crowd leans in, and two dice clatter off the stone wall at the end of the table. "Point is eight!" roars the dwarf at the rail. Every eye waits for the next throw.*

**Description:** One player, the **Shooter**, throws two dice while everyone at the table bets on the outcome. A dwarf announcer calls every throw, which is where the bets get their names. The first roll can win or lose instantly, or it sets a **Point**. After that, the Shooter keeps rolling until they hit the Point again (win) or roll a 7 (lose). It has some of the best odds in the casino for players who know which bets to make.

## How to Play

### A round, step by step

1. **Choose a Shooter.** The player on the announcer's left throws first. The Shooter keeps the dice until they roll a 7 while chasing a Point (step 4), then the dice pass clockwise to the next player. A player who doesn't want to throw passes the dice on.
2. **Bet.** Before the first roll, each player, the Shooter included, bets **With the Dwarf** or **Against the Dwarf** (see Bets).
3. **The Come-Out roll.** The first roll of a round is called the Come-Out roll. The Shooter throws the table's **two d6** so that both bounce off the low stone wall at the far end of the table, then adds them up. A throw that misses the wall doesn't count and is thrown again.

   | Come-Out total | With the Dwarf | Against the Dwarf |
   |---|---|---|
   | 7 or 11 | **Wins** | Loses |
   | 2 or 3 | Loses | **Wins** |
   | 12 | Loses | Your bet comes back |
   | 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 or 10 | That number becomes the **Point** | That number becomes the **Point** |

4. **Chasing the Point.** If a Point was set, the Shooter keeps rolling 2d6 until either:
   - **The Point comes up again:** With the Dwarf **wins**, Against the Dwarf loses.
   - **A 7 comes up:** With the Dwarf loses, Against the Dwarf **wins**.
   - Any other number means nothing. Roll again.
5. **New round.** Once the bets are settled, the next round starts with new bets and a new Come-Out roll.

### Bets

| Bet | What it means | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| **With the Dwarf** | You're betting the Shooter wins | 1:1 |
| **Against the Dwarf** | You're betting the Shooter loses | 1:1 |
| **Ancestor's Odds** | An extra bet placed **after** a Point is set, up to the size of your main bet. It wins or loses when your main bet does. | See below |

**Ancestor's Odds payouts** (if you bet With the Dwarf):

| Point | Pays |
|---|---|
| 4 or 10 | 2:1 |
| 5 or 9 | 3:2 |
| 6 or 8 | 6:5 |

If you bet Against the Dwarf, Ancestor's Odds pays the reverse: 1:2 on 4 or 10, 2:3 on 5 or 9, and 5:6 on 6 or 8. Any fraction of a copper is rounded down.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Thokk (NPC) is the Shooter and bets **10 gp With the Dwarf**. Mirela (PC) bets **5 gp With the Dwarf**. Quill (PC) bets **5 gp Against the Dwarf**.

**Round 1: an instant win**
- Come-Out roll: **5** and **6** = **11**.
- 11 wins for With the Dwarf. Thokk wins **10 gp**, Mirela wins **5 gp**, and Quill **loses** 5 gp.

**Round 2: a Point is set and made**
- Same bets. Come-Out roll: **3** and **5** = **8**. **The Point is 8.**
- Mirela adds **5 gp Ancestor's Odds** behind her bet.
- Thokk rolls **2 + 4 = 6**. Nothing happens. Then **6 + 3 = 9**. Nothing. Then **4 + 4 = 8**. **The Point is made!**
- Thokk wins **10 gp**. Mirela wins **5 gp** on her main bet plus **6 gp** on her Odds (6:5 on 5 gp). Quill **loses** 5 gp.

**Round 3: a 7 before the Point**
- Same bets. Come-Out roll: **1** and **3** = **4**. **The Point is 4.**
- Quill adds **4 gp Ancestor's Odds** behind his bet.
- Thokk rolls **5 + 2 = 7**. The 7 came first.
- Thokk and Mirela **lose** their bets. Quill wins **5 gp** on his main bet plus **2 gp** on his Odds (1:2 on 4 gp). The dice pass to Mirela.

**Edge case: a 12 on the Come-Out**
- Mirela is now the Shooter. Come-Out roll: **6** and **6** = **12**.
- With the Dwarf **loses**, but Against the Dwarf doesn't: Quill gets his 5 gp back without winning or losing. Mirela keeps the dice.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any bet within the table limits. Ancestor's Odds can only be placed after a Point is set.
- Blowing on the dice, lucky phrases and dramatic throws, as long as both dice tumble and hit the far wall.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the dice.
- Skidding a die so it doesn't tumble, loaded dice, or changing a bet after a roll.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skid throw: inside a normal-looking throw, one die slides flat down the table and hits the wall without tumbling | Shooter | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | On this throw, that die lands on the face you set |
| Swap one of the House dice for your own loaded one | Shooter or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | For 10 throws, until the House changes the dice, a 1 on that die counts as a 6 |
| Slide an extra chip onto your bet as the dice land, while every eye is on them | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | A winning bet pays as if you'd bet double |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant* nudge) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | As the dice settle, the invisible servant turns one die to a face of your choice |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Dwarven Hazard is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

![[Cheating Penalties#Casino]]

### Cheating dice example

Mirela is the Shooter with the Point on 10 and 5 gp bet With the Dwarf. She tries to skid a 4 down the table, so that a 6 on the other die makes her 10.
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **12** + 2 = **14**. The Watcher: **10** + 5 = **15**. **Caught.**
- Her chips and the night's winnings are taken, her portrait goes in the House's file, she's banned for a month, and she owes **25 gp** (five times her 5 gp bet) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **A young dwarf** has been betting Against the Dwarf for weeks, because it's the better bet, and winning. His grandmother, who swore that no one in the clan would ever bet against a dwarf, has just sat down at his table. He slides his chips to the party and begs them to say they're theirs, before Grumbar Ironrail, the announcer, who knows every regular by name, calls out his bet.
- **A young sailor** on his first night ever has made eleven Points in a row, and he doesn't know what a Point is. His ship sails at dawn with all his pay aboard, and he wants to leave, but the packed table has hundreds of gold riding on him and nobody will let him pass the dice. He catches a party member's eye and asks how to get out.
