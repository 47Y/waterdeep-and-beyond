# Thabort

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *An old dice game: six throws to catch every face from one to six. Collect the full spread and the House pays. Miss a single number and the dice laugh at you.*

**Description:** An old gambling game. The player throws **two dice at a time, six times**, trying to see every number from 1 to 6 at least once. Seeing all six numbers is called a **spread**. Get the spread and you win; miss even one number and you lose. A side bet pays big if you get the spread fast.

## How to Play

The House gives you two dice and a slate with 1 2 3 4 5 6 chalked on it. At the table, use scrap paper.

1. **Bet.** Place your main bet. You may also place a **Quick Spread** side bet (see below).
2. **Throw.** Roll **2d6**. Cross off every number that shows on your slate. Doubles only cross off one number.
3. **Repeat** for up to **six throws** in total.
4. **Win or lose.** The dealer reads your slate when you claim a spread.
   - **All six numbers crossed off** within six throws: your main bet wins **6:5**.
   - **Any number still missing** after the sixth throw: you lose your main bet.
   - You can stop as soon as you have all six numbers; there's no need to finish the throws.
5. **Quick Spread side bet:** wins **7:1** if you cross off all six numbers within your **first four throws**.

## Dice Examples

**Mirela** (PC) bets **5 gp** with a **1 gp Quick Spread** side bet.

**Throw 1:** **3** and **5**. Marked: 3, 5.
**Throw 2:** **5** and **1**. The 5 is already marked. Marked: 1, 3, 5.
**Throw 3:** **6** and **2**. Marked: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6. Only **4** is missing.
**Throw 4:** **6** and **6**. Nothing new. That's four throws, so her Quick Spread side bet **loses** 1 gp.
**Throw 5:** **4** and **1**. **4 is marked. Spread complete!**
- Main bet wins 6:5: **6 gp** on her 5 gp bet.

**A losing game:** Thokk (NPC) bets **10 gp**.
- His six throws mark 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6, but no 4 ever shows.
- After throw 6 he **loses** 10 gp.

**Edge case: a lucky Quick Spread.** Quill (PC) bets **2 gp** and **2 gp Quick Spread**.
- Throw 1: **1, 2**. Throw 2: **3, 4**. Throw 3: **5, 1**. Throw 4: **6, 6**. **All six by throw 4!**
- Main bet wins 6:5: **2 gp 4 sp**. Quick Spread wins 7:1: **14 gp**.

**Thabort pays 6:5,** so main bets must be in multiples of 5 sp to pay out cleanly. Any bet in whole gold or platinum already is. The House never pays in copper.

## Odds

| Bet | Chance to win | Pays | House edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spread in six throws | 43.8% | 6:5 | 3.7% |
| Quick Spread (four throws) | 11.4% | 7:1 | 8.8% |

**The last number is the hardest.** Once you're missing only one number, each throw has about a 31% chance to find it.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Stopping as soon as the spread is complete.
- Any lucky ritual, as long as both dice leave your hand together.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the dice.
- Throwing your own dice, or crossing off a number that didn't show.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| As the dice land and every eye is on them, cross an extra number off your slate | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Cross off one number that didn't show |
| Swap the House dice for your own pair with repeated faces: one has only 1, 2, 3 and the other only 4, 5, 6. Opposite faces match, so from any one side each die looks normal | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | For 10 throws, until the House changes the dice, every throw shows one number from 1–3 and one from 4–6 |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant* nudge) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | As the dice settle, the invisible servant turns one die to a face of your choice |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Thabort is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Thokk bets 10 gp at the Gold Table. After four throws he's still missing the 4 and the 6. As his fifth throw lands, he tries to cross off the 4.
- Thokk: Deception **11** − 1 = **10**. The Watcher: **9** + 5 = **14**. **Caught.**
- His chips and the night's winnings are taken, his portrait goes in the House's file, he's banned for a month, and he owes **50 gp** (five times his 10 gp bet) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **An old halfling** says she invented Thabort, and carries a very old parchment to prove it. She'll only part with it to someone who throws a Quick Spread in front of her, at 20 gp a try, paid to her. That's about 1 chance in 9. A scholar at the bar offers 300 gp for the parchment if it's real, and nobody can say whether it is.
- **A merchant** has missed the same number, the 3, on his last throw nine nights running. He's sure it's a curse from his dead brother, after they fell out over the family business. He offers 30 gp to anyone who'll throw for him while he holds the slate, to see if the curse follows the slate or the man. If the 3 still doesn't come, he says he'll hand the business to his brother's widow, and his sons offer the party 50 gp to make sure he sees a 3.
