# Beholder's Eye Darts

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The dartboard is painted as a beholder: a ring of stalks on the outside, a wide toothy mouth inside that, and in the very middle a single glaring eye. Hit the eye and the whole tavern cheers.*

**Description:** A darts night. The board has three rings: the **Outer** ring of eyestalks, the **Inner** ring of the mouth, and the **Eye** in the centre. Before each throw, you **declare which ring you're aiming at**. Harder rings score more, and a near miss can still land in the Outer ring. Three darts per turn; the **first to 51 points** wins. The House rents and sells **bar darts and flights**, and the best of them give a small edge.

## How to Play

### Throwing a dart

1. **Declare a ring:** Outer, Inner or Eye.
2. **Make a ranged attack roll** with a dart: Dexterity modifier + proficiency (darts are simple weapons), **plus your darts' and flights' bonuses** (see House Darts below).

   | Ring | AC to hit | Points |
   |---|---|---|
   | Outer | 12 | 1 |
   | Inner | 16 | 3 |
   | Eye | 20 | 10 |

   - **Hit the ring you declared:** score its points.
   - **Missed the Inner or Eye, but the total is 12 or more:** the dart lands in the Outer ring. **1 point.**
   - **Natural 20:** the **Eye**, whatever you declared. **10 points.**
   - **Natural 1:** off the board. 0 points.

### A turn and a game

- **Three darts per turn.** Players take turns in order.
- **The first player to reach 51 or more** wins, right away.

### House darts

**Bar darts are game darts, not weapons.** They're too light to fight with, and their bonuses count only in darts games. Everyone throws loaners unless they rent or buy better.

**Darts**

| Set | Rent (per night) | Buy (set of 3) | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loaners | Free | — | None |
| Brass darts | 1 sp | 1 gp | None. The set is yours. |
| Weighted steel darts | 5 sp | 5 gp | **Reroll any natural 1** |
| Gnome-balanced darts | 1 gp | 25 gp | **+1** |
| Dragonsteel darts | Not for rent | 250 gp | **+2** |

**Flights**

Flights are **not for rent**. Rented darts always come with duck feathers.

| Flights | Buy (set of 3) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Duck feather | Included with every set | None |
| Hawk feather | 25 gp | **Reroll any natural 1** |
| Griffon feather | 150 gp | **+1** |

**Extras**

| Extra | Price |
|---|---|
| Dyed flights, any colour (hawk or griffon) | 1 sp per set |
| Engraving, any set of darts | 5 sp per set |

**Stacking:** a dart's bonus and a flight's bonus add together. Rerolls don't stack: weighted steel darts with hawk flights still reroll a natural 1 only once. **The best legal set-up is Dragonsteel darts with griffon flights: +3.**

**Enchantments:** the DM may allow an enchanter to put a magic bonus (+1 to +3) on bar darts. Throwing enchanted darts on darts night is **cheating** (see below).

## Dice Examples

**Throwers:** Quill (PC, dart +5, loaners) and Thokk (NPC, dart +1, weighted steel darts).

**Quill's turn 1** (he aims at the Eye every time)
- Dart 1: **16** + 5 = **21**. Beats AC 20. **Eye: 10.**
- Dart 2: **9** + 5 = **14**. Misses the Eye but 14 is 12 or more. **Outer: 1.**
- Dart 3: **4** + 5 = **9**. **Miss: 0.**
- Quill has **11**.

**Thokk's turn 1** (he aims at the Inner ring)
- Dart 1: **15** + 1 = **16**. **Inner: 3.**
- Dart 2: **natural 1**. His weighted steel darts let him **reroll**: **11** + 1 = **12**. Misses the Inner, but 12 lands in the **Outer: 1.**
- Dart 3: **natural 20**. **The Eye: 10!**
- Thokk has **14**.

**Edge case: the finish**
- A few turns later, Quill has 49 and needs only 2. He aims at the Inner ring (a hit gives him 3, and even a near miss gives him 1 for 50 and another try). Dart 1: **13** + 5 = **18**. **Inner: 3. Quill reaches 52 and wins.**

**Gear example:** Saelis (NPC, dart +6) throws Dragonsteel darts (+2) with griffon flights (+1): **+9** on every dart. Aiming at the Eye: **11** + 9 = **20**. **Eye: 10.** With loaners, that 11 would have scored only 1.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per thrower, per week.
- **Winner of each game:** 80% of that game's entries.
- **The House keeps:** 20% of entries, dart and flight sales and rentals, and the bar sales from a crowded darts night.

## Odds

**Average points per dart, by which ring you aim at:**

| Dart bonus | Aim Outer | Aim Inner | Aim Eye | Turns to reach 51 (aiming at the Eye) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| +1 | 0.95 | **1.45** | 1.4 | about 13 |
| +4 | 1.1 | 1.9 | **2.9** | about 7 |
| +5 | 1.15 | 2.05 | **3.4** | about 6 |
| +7 | 1.25 | 2.35 | **4.4** | about 4.5 |

**From +4 up, always aim at the Eye.** Weaker throwers do slightly better aiming at the Inner ring.

**What gear is worth:** the chance to beat an equal thrower who's using loaners. Each +1 adds about **0.5 points per dart**.

| Your dart bonus | +1 gear | +2 gear | +3 gear |
|---|---|---|---|
| +2 | 68% | 81% | 88% |
| +5 | 61% | 70% | 78% |
| +8 | 58% | 65% | 72% |

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- House darts, rented or bought, and House flights you've bought. Bar darts and flights of the same quality bought elsewhere are also allowed.
- Taking your time, talking trash and making the crowd laugh.
- Features that add to ranged attack rolls, such as the Archery Fighting Style (darts are ranged weapons).

### Checking the darts

At the start of darts night, the **Floor Watcher** casts *detect magic* as a ritual and checks every set of darts at the throw line. Only the caster sees a magic aura, so anyone who wants to throw enchanted darts has to swap them in after the check.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *guidance*, *bless*, *mage hand* or Bardic Inspiration.
- **Enchanted darts.**
- Stepping over the throw line, distracting a rival on purpose, bribing the chalker, or tampering with a rival's darts.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Toe over the line:** lean or step past the throw line | Thrower | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | +1 to every dart this turn |
| **Distract a rival** mid-throw: a cough, a dropped tankard, lantern light in their eyes | Anyone near the board | Cha (Deception) to make it look like an accident | The rival's dart has disadvantage |
| **Bribe the chalker**, the scorekeeper (2 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | +3 points chalked on your score this turn |
| **Tamper with a rival's darts:** bend the flights or loosen the tips while they sit on the table | Anyone near the table | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival takes −2 on every dart this game |
| **Enchanted darts**, swapped in after the *detect magic* check | Thrower | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The enchantment's bonus (+1 to +3) adds to your gear |
| **Hidden spell** (*bless*) | A caster in the crowd, within 30 ft | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The thrower adds 1d4 to their dart attack rolls, for up to 1 minute |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Beholder's Eye Darts is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. Enchanted darts are confiscated.

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 10 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Quill leans a full foot over the throw line on his first dart.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **8** + 7 = **15**. The Watcher: **15** + 5 = **20**. **Caught.**
- Quill forfeits his 1 gp entry, is banned from darts night for 3 months, and is pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 10 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The board itself** was painted from life. The artist insists the beholder posed. Some nights, the painted Eye seems to follow the darts.
- **The league champion**, a one-armed elf named Saelis, has never been beaten. She throws Dragonsteel darts with griffon flights, dyed black. She's looking for a partner for the doubles league.
