# Puzzle Jug Gauntlet

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Five clay jugs sit on a shelf, each riddled with holes. Tip one the wrong way and the ale pours down your front. Find the hidden spout and you drink in triumph. Nobody has cleared all five in two years, and the jackpot is getting fat.*

**Description:** A monthly puzzle challenge with a rolling jackpot. Each **puzzle jug** is a clay jug with holes around the neck and hidden tubes inside. Tip it and the ale pours out through the holes, unless you find the one way of drinking that works, such as covering the right holes and sipping from a hidden spout. Contestants try the five jugs in order, each harder than the last. Fail one and you're drenched, and you lose everything you've earned. After jug 3 and jug 4, you can **walk away** with your reward instead of risking it on the next jug. Clear all five and you win the jackpot.

## How to Play

### A run, step by step

Contestants go one at a time, in the order they sign up. The barkeep refills the jugs out of sight between runs.

1. **Pay the entry.** Half of every entry goes into the jackpot.
2. **Try jug 1.** Make an **Intelligence (Investigation)** or **Wisdom (Perception)** check (your choice) against the jug's DC in the table below.
   - **Success:** you find the trick and take a mouthful. It's not enough to count as a drink on the [[Drunk Track]], so there's no Constitution save. Move on to the next jug.
   - **Failure:** you're **drenched**. Your run ends and you get **nothing**.
3. **After jug 3 and after jug 4, choose:** **walk away** and collect the reward for the jug you just cleared, or **go on** to the next jug and risk it.
4. **Keep going** until you walk away, get drenched, or clear all five.

| Jug | DC | Clear it, and you can walk away with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 11 | — |
| 2 | 14 | — |
| 3 | 17 | Your entry back |
| 4 | 20 | 3 times your entry |
| 5 | 23 | **The jackpot** |

## Dice Examples

**Contestants:** Quill (PC, Investigation +4, Perception +6) and Mirela (PC, Investigation +1, Perception +3). Entry is **1 gp**. The jackpot is **38 gp**.

**Quill's run** (uses Perception, +6)
- Jug 1 (DC 11): **8** + 6 = **14**. Clear.
- Jug 2 (DC 14): **12** + 6 = **18**. Clear.
- Jug 3 (DC 17): **11** + 6 = **17**. Clear. He could walk away with **1 gp** (his entry back). With 38 gp in the jackpot, he goes on.
- Jug 4 (DC 20): **13** + 6 = **19**. **Drenched.** He gets **nothing**.

**Mirela's run** (uses Perception, +3)
- Jug 1 (DC 11): **5** + 3 = **8**. **Drenched on the first jug.** She gets nothing.

**Edge case: walking away**
- Thokk (NPC, Perception +1) somehow clears jug 4 with a **19** + 1 = **20**. Jug 5 is DC 23, and even a natural 20 only gets him to 21. He walks away with **3 gp**. Only contestants with +3 or better can clear jug 5 at all.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per run. Contestants may try again for another entry.
- **Jackpot:** starts at 20 gp (the House seeds it) and grows by 5 sp with every entry until someone wins it. Then it starts again at 20 gp.
- **The House keeps:** the other half of every entry, minus the rewards it pays out, plus drinks sold to the crowd.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Choosing Investigation or Perception for each jug.
- Taking your time to look the jug over.
- Walking away, or going on.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *guidance*, *enhance ability* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Watching the barkeep refill the jugs, or bribing someone who knows the trick.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Watch the refill | Contestant or a helper | Dex (Stealth) | Advantage on one jug |
| Bribe the barkeep (10 gp) to tell you the trick | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Clear one jug automatically |
| Hidden *guidance*, cast by touch with a pat on the back | A caster, or the contestant on themselves | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | +1d4 on one jug check |
| Bardic Inspiration, whistled or shouted as a cheer | A bard within 60 ft | Cha (Deception) to pass it off as crowd noise | The contestant adds the die to one jug check |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Puzzle Jug Gauntlet is a **public event**: the jackpot belongs to everyone who has paid in. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A bribed barkeep loses the job.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 10 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Before jug 4, Mirela slips Quill a Bardic Inspiration die, disguised as a good-luck whistle.
- Mirela: Deception **14** + 7 = **21**. The Watcher: **20** + 5 = **25**. **Caught.**
- Quill is disqualified, and both are banned for 3 months with portraits on the notice board. To clear their names: 10 gp each, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.
