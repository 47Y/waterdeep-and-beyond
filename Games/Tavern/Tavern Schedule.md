## How the Calendar Works

- **The year** has **12 months of 30 days**, plus **5 festival days** that fall between months (Calendar of Harptos).
- **Each month** has **3 tendays**: days 1–10, 11–20 and 21–30.
- **"Weekly" games** run **once a tenday**.
- **Tavern and arena take turns,** so they never clash: tavern, arena, tavern, arena, tavern.
- **Fight Night** is two arena events: two pit fights in one day.
- **Days with one arena event:** the second and third tavern games both follow it, one after the other.
- **Days with three arena events (10 and 14):** no tavern game runs between the second and third.
- **Brewfest** runs for two days: Tarsakh 30 and Greengrass.
