# Stein Hold

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A line of contestants along the bar, each holding a full one-litre glass stein straight out in front of them. Arm level with the shoulder. No bending, no switching hands, no spilling. The minutes crawl by. The arms start to shake.*

**Description:** A weekly endurance contest. Contestants hold a full stein at arm's length, and the last one still holding it properly wins. Each minute is harder than the last. Dip once and you get a warning; dip twice and you're out. Spill a drop and you're out at once.

## How to Play

### Setting up

- Contestants stand in a line along the bar. Each gets a **full one-litre glass stein**, filled at the bar.
- **The position:** arm straight out in front at shoulder height, elbow locked, the stein held by its handle in one hand.
- **The Floor Watcher judges every arm.** A **dip** is when your arm drops below shoulder level.
- Each contestant chooses **Strength (Athletics)** or **Constitution** checks for the whole contest.

### Each minute, step by step

1. **Hold.** Every contestant makes their chosen check at **DC 9 + the minute number** (minute 1 is DC 10, minute 2 is DC 11, and so on).
   - **Success:** they hold steady.
   - **Failure:** their arm dips. The **first dip is a warning**. The **second dip puts them out**.
   - **Natural 1:** they **spill** the stein and are **out** at once.
2. **Next minute.**

### Winning

- The last contestant still holding wins.
- **Sudden death:** if every remaining contestant goes out in the same minute:
  - **A spill is always out.** A contestant who spilled can't win.
  - Of the contestants who went out on a **second dip** that minute, the one with the **highest check total** wins.
  - If every one of them spilled, nobody wins, and **the House keeps the pot**.
- **The winner drinks the stein.** It's earned. That's one **Constitution saving throw at DC 10** on the [[Drunk Track]].

## Dice Examples

**Contestants:**
- Thokk (NPC, Strength (Athletics) +5).
- Mirela (PC, Constitution check +1).
- "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Constitution check +4, Constitution save +4).

**Minute 1** (DC 10)
- Thokk **8** + 5 = 13, holds. Mirela **13** + 1 = 14, holds. Oswin **4** + 4 = **8**, **warning**.

**Minute 2** (DC 11)
- Thokk **14** + 5 = 19, holds. Mirela **7** + 1 = **8**, **warning**. Oswin **12** + 4 = 16, holds.

**Minute 3** (DC 12)
- Thokk **natural 1**. **Spill! Thokk is out.**
- Mirela **6** + 1 = **7**. Second dip. **Mirela is out.**
- Oswin **9** + 4 = **13**, holds. **Oswin wins**, and drinks the stein to applause: Constitution save **14** + 4 = **18**. Pass.

**Edge case: sudden death**
- If Oswin had rolled **5** in minute 3 (5 + 4 = 9, a fail), it would be his second dip, and everyone would be out in the same minute.
- Thokk spilled, so he can't win. Mirela (7) and Oswin (9) both went out on a second dip. **Oswin's 9 beats Mirela's 7, and Oswin wins.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per contestant.
- **Winner:** 70% of all entries. Their stein hangs above the bar, with their name and how many minutes they held, until someone holds longer.
- **The House keeps:** 30% of entries, every stein drunk, and its cut of spectator bets. If everyone left spills in the same minute, the House keeps the whole pot.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Choosing Strength (Athletics) or Constitution checks.
- Natural traits and class features that don't use magic. (A barbarian's rage gives advantage on Strength checks.)
- Grimacing, swearing and singing through the pain.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *bless*, *guidance*, *enhance ability* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A short-poured stein, a hidden sling, or a partner propping your elbow.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Short pour:** bribe the barkeep to fill your stein below the line (2 gp). The stein is glass, so the Watcher might see the level. | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Advantage on every check |
| **Hidden sling** under your sleeve, from shoulder to wrist | Contestant | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Ignore your first warning |
| **Hidden spell** (*guidance*), by touch: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the shoulder | The contestant or a friend | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | +1d4 on one minute's check |
| **Jostle a rival**, made to look like an accident | Anyone in the crowd | Cha (Deception) | That rival's check has disadvantage this minute |

### Cheating dice example

Quill has money on Mirela. In minute 2, he stumbles into Oswin from the crowd, made to look like an accident.
- Quill: Deception **18** + 2 = **20**. The Watcher: **11** + 5 = **16**. Not caught.
- Oswin rolls with disadvantage: **12** and **3**, keeps 3: 3 + 4 = **7** against DC 11. It's his second dip (he had a warning in minute 1), so **Oswin is out.**
- In minute 3, Thokk spills and Mirela dips for the second time. Both go out in the same minute, so it's sudden death. Thokk spilled, so he can't win. **Mirela wins.**

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The champion's stein** above the bar has belonged to a half-orc smith named Varga for two years. She says she'll retire the day someone beats her.
- **A visiting cleric** keeps entering and losing on purpose. The House suspects she's scouting the crowd for something.
