# Wurst Wall

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The kitchen doors swing open and out comes a wall: a rack of sausages stacked to the rafters. Ten minutes on the hourglass. Eat as many as you can. Know when to stop, because if it all comes back up, your score goes with it. The crowd counts every bite.*

**Description:** A weekly sausage-eating contest over **10 one-minute rounds**. All the eaters sit at one long table and eat at the same time. A member of the House staff counts for each eater and calls their total aloud after every round, so everyone knows the scores. Each round, a **Constitution check** decides how many sausages you eat. Once you've eaten 10, your stomach starts to fight back: each round you must pass a **Constitution save** against your own total to keep eating. You can **stop** at any time and keep your score. Fail the save and you're done; fail it badly and you're sick, and you **lose your whole score**. The most sausages eaten wins.

## How to Play

### Each round, step by step

1. **Stop?** You may stop at the start of any round. You keep your score, and you're safe.
2. **Hit the wall?** If you've eaten **10 or more** sausages, make a **Constitution saving throw**. The DC is **the number of sausages you've eaten so far**.
   - **Success:** keep eating this round.
   - **Failure:** you're **full**. You stop eating for the rest of the contest, you keep your score, and you're **poisoned for 1 hour**.
   - **Failure by 5 or more:** you're **sick**, into the bucket under the table. Your score drops to **0**, and you're **poisoned for 1 hour**.
3. **Eat.** Make a **Constitution check**. Your total decides how many sausages you eat this round:

   | Constitution check total | Sausages |
   |---|---|
   | 4 or less | 0 |
   | 5–9 | 1 |
   | 10–14 | 2 |
   | 15–19 | 3 |
   | 20–24 | 4 |
   | 25 or more | 5 |

4. **Next round.** After round 10, the contest ends.

### Winning

- The most sausages eaten wins.
- **Ties:** the tied eaters play one extra round, called an eat-off. The higher Constitution check total wins. If the totals tie again, they go again. Nobody needs a save for the eat-off, but a full eater is still poisoned, so they roll with disadvantage.
- **Everyone on 0:** if every eater was sick, nobody wins and the House keeps the purse.

## Dice Examples

**Eaters:** Thokk (NPC, Con check +3, Con save +5) and "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Con check +4, Con save +4).

**Thokk**
- Round 1: **12** + 3 = 15. **3 sausages** (total 3).
- Round 2: **9** + 3 = 12. **2** (total 5).
- Round 3: **17** + 3 = 20. **4** (total 9).
- Round 4: **6** + 3 = 9. **1** (total 10). He's hit the wall.
- Round 5: Save DC 10: **13** + 5 = 18. Pass. Eat: **11** + 3 = 14. **2** (total 12).
- Round 6: Save DC 12: **4** + 5 = **9**. Fail by 3. **Thokk is full at 12, and poisoned for 1 hour.**

**Oswin**
- Round 1: **15** + 4 = 19. **3** (total 3).
- Round 2: **10** + 4 = 14. **2** (total 5).
- Round 3: **8** + 4 = 12. **2** (total 7).
- Round 4: **13** + 4 = 17. **3** (total 10).
- Round 5: Save DC 10: **9** + 4 = 13. Pass. Eat: **16** + 4 = 20. **4** (total 14).
- Round 6: Save DC 14: **12** + 4 = 16. Pass. Eat: **3** + 4 = 7. **1** (total 15).
- Round 7: Thokk is out on 12, so Oswin **stops** at 15.

**Oswin wins, 15 to 12.**

**Edge case: getting greedy**
- If Oswin had kept eating in round 7, his save would be DC 15. On a **5** + 4 = **9** he fails by 6: he's **sick**, his score drops to 0, and **Thokk wins with 12**.

**Edge case: an eat-off**
- If both had finished on 15, they'd play one extra round. Each makes a Constitution check, and the higher total wins.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per eater (the sausages are included).
- **Winner:** 5 gp and a **lifetime 10% discount on food** at the tavern.
- **The House keeps:** the rest of the entries, the bar sales, and its cut of spectator bets.

## Odds

| Con check / Con save | Stop at 12 | Stop at 14 | Stop at 16 | Never stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| +1 / +1 | 9.7 (20%) | 8.1 (37%) | 6.6 (49%) | 5.5 (56%) |
| +3 / +3 | 11.1 (10%) | 10.2 (24%) | 8.8 (37%) | 6.2 (53%) |
| +3 / +5 | 12.2 (2%) | 12.2 (11%) | 11.3 (21%) | 7.9 (43%) |
| +5 / +7 | 12.8 (0%) | 14.0 (2%) | 14.1 (9%) | 8.8 (42%) |

**Most eaters make their first save around round 5 to 7.** After that, every sausage makes the next save harder. Most eaters should stop at 12 to 14, unless they need more to beat the leader. A good Constitution save matters as much as a big appetite.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any eating technique: dunking, folding, swallowing whole.
- Water and sips of ale between bites. (Sips don't count as a drink on the Drunk Track.)
- Stopping whenever you like.
- Natural traits. (Dwarven Resilience doesn't help: sausage isn't poison, however it tastes.)

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *enhance ability* (Bear's Endurance), *bless*, *resistance* or *guidance*.
- Paying your counter to call extra sausages, or paying the kitchen for smaller sausages on your plate or salted ones on a rival's.
- Hiding sausages. (With a counter beside you and the crowd watching, it can't be done unseen, so it has no row below.)

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before the contest, bribe your counter to call extra sausages (1 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | +2 to your final score. You didn't eat them, so they don't count towards your save DC. |
| Before the contest, bribe the kitchen for slightly smaller sausages on your plate (1 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Advantage on every Constitution check |
| Before the contest, bribe the kitchen to oversalt a rival's sausages (1 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The rival has disadvantage on their first save |
| Hidden spell: *enhance ability* by touch before you sit: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the back | The eater, or a friend | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on Constitution checks for the whole contest |
| Hidden spell: *bless* from within 30 ft, while the crowd is watching the table | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Lasts 1 minute, so +1d4 on one round's Constitution save |

### Cheating dice example

Before the contest, Thokk slips his counter 1 gp to call two extra sausages.
- Thokk: Persuasion **11** − 1 = **10**. The Watcher: **7** + 5 = **12**. **Caught.**
- The Watcher sees the coin change hands and pulls them both aside. Thokk is disqualified, banned for 3 months, and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 10 gp, 1 day serving (washing every plate from the contest), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Mother Gretl** runs the kitchen and swears no one will ever finish the Wall. She's right: there's always more behind the rack.
- **A gaunt stranger** enters every tenday and eats impossibly much, impossibly calmly. He never seems to get full.
