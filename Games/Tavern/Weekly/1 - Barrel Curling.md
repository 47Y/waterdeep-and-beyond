# Barrel Curling

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A long waxed lane runs the length of the cellar, with a painted target at the far end. You set your little weighted barrel spinning, push, and let go. It slides, curls, and settles... right in the middle. Then your opponent's barrel comes skidding down and knocks it clean away.*

**Description:** A weekly team league, played in the tavern cellar with small weighted barrels. Teams of two take turns sliding barrels at a target. Each throw needs the right **weight** (Strength) and the right **line** (Dexterity). Get both right and you land in the **House** for 3 points; get one right and you land in the **Ring** for 1. Or you can try to knock an opponent's barrel out. The highest total after **three ends** wins.

## How to Play

### Setting up

- Two teams of two. Toss a coin to see which team throws first.
- A match is **three ends** (rounds). In each end, every player throws **one barrel**, alternating between the teams.

### A throw: choose one

**Draw** (try to score):
1. **Weight:** make a **DC 12 Strength (Athletics)** check.
2. **Line:** make a **DC 15 Dexterity** check.

| Result | Where it lands | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Both succeed | The House (centre) | 3 |
| One succeeds | The Ring | 1 |
| Both fail | Off the target | 0 |

**Takeout** (knock an opponent's barrel away):
- Pick one opposing barrel that's on the target this end. Make a **DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** check.
- **Success:** that barrel is knocked off and scores 0. Your barrel slides off too and scores 0.
- **Failure:** nothing happens.

### Scoring

- At the end of each end, add up the points of every barrel still on the target.
- The highest total after three ends wins.
- **Ties:** play an extra end.

## Dice Examples

**Team Stonejaw:** Thokk (NPC, Athletics +5, Dex −1) and "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Athletics +2, Dex +0).
**Team Quill:** Quill (PC, Athletics +0, Dex +3) and Mirela (PC, Athletics +0, Dex +2).
Team Quill throws first.

**End 1**
- **Quill** (draw): Weight **13** + 0 = **13** ✓. Line **14** + 3 = **17** ✓. **The House: 3.**
- **Thokk** (takeout on Quill's barrel): Athletics **11** + 5 = **16** ✓. **Quill's barrel is knocked off.** Thokk's barrel slides off too.
- **Mirela** (draw): Weight **9** + 0 = **9** ✗. Line **16** + 2 = **18** ✓. **The Ring: 1.**
- **Oswin** (draw): Weight **15** + 2 = **17** ✓. Line **8** + 0 = **8** ✗. **The Ring: 1.**
- **End 1 score:** Team Quill 1, Team Stonejaw 1.

After ends 2 and 3: **Team Stonejaw 5, Team Quill 4. Team Stonejaw wins.**

**Edge case: nothing to take out**
- If no opposing barrel is on the target, you can't choose a takeout. You draw.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per team, per match.
- **Winning team:** 80% of both entries.
- **League champions** (most wins over the season): their names on the cellar wall and a free keg.
- **The House keeps:** 20% of entries, the lane fees and the cellar bar.

## Odds

**A draw:**

| Athletics | Dex | House (3) | Ring (1) | Average points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| +2 | +2 | 22% | 51% | 1.2 |
| +5 | +2 | 28% | 54% | 1.4 |
| +5 | +5 | 38.5% | 48% | 1.6 |
| +7 | +7 | 52% | 41% | 2.0 |

**A takeout** (DC 15 Athletics) works 30% of the time at +0, 40% at +2 and 55% at +5. Strong throwers should save takeouts for barrels in the House.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Choosing draw or takeout on every throw.
- Sweeping the lane in front of your own barrel (it's tradition, but it has no effect on the dice).
- Rage and other features that don't use magic.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *mage hand*, *gust*, *guidance*, *enhance ability* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A barrel weighted with lead, or wax rubbed onto the lane.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead-weighted barrel | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) to swap it in | Advantage on every weight and takeout check |
| Wax the lane in front of a rival | Anyone | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's next line check has disadvantage |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The invisible servant shoves one barrel from the Ring to the House, or from the House to the Ring |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Barrel Curling is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A caught team forfeits the match.

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 10 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Oswin swaps in a lead-weighted barrel before end 2.
- Oswin: Sleight of Hand **17** + 0 = **17**. The Watcher: **9** + 5 = **14**. Not caught.
- Quill picks it up to move it off the lane between ends and feels the weight: Perception **13** + 6 = **19**. He beats 17 and **sees it**. He can report Team Stonejaw, demand a bribe, or say nothing.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The cellar lane** has a mystery: on quiet nights, the barrels curl towards the same spot, and nobody can say why.
- **The dwarven league** has challenged the tavern's best team to a match, with the losers buying the winners' ale for a year.
