# Hammerschlagen

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A great stump of oak stands by the bar, studded with old nails. Everyone gets a fresh nail tapped in at the tip. Players take turns with the hammer, using the narrow wedge end, one swing each. First nail flush with the wood wins. Miss, and you down a shot of schnapps.*

**Description:** A weekly nail-driving game. Each player has their own nail in a big stump. Players take turns, **one swing each**, using the thin wedge end of a cross-peen hammer. A good swing drives the nail deeper; a bad one drives nothing and costs you a shot of schnapps. **The first nail driven flush wins the pot**, and the player furthest behind buys the round.

## How to Play

### Setting up

- Each player pays **2 sp** to enter. Your nail is included.
- **Every player plays for themselves**, each with their own nail. The players stand around the stump in a ring.
- **The nails:** a House hand, the **nail-setter**, takes the nails from the House box and taps each one into the stump, just far enough to stand up. That's **0 depth**.
- **Depth** is how far your nail has gone in. At **4 depth**, it's flush with the wood.
- **There is one hammer**, a cross-peen: one side of its head is flat, the other is a thin wedge. **You must hit with the wedge.** The players share the hammer and pass it around the ring.

### Your swing, step by step

1. Make a **Strength (Athletics)** check, or a **Dexterity check with carpenter's tools** if you're proficient.

   | Total | Depth |
   |---|---|
   | 9 or less | 0. **Take a shot** (see Shots below). |
   | 10–14 | 1 |
   | 15–19 | 2 |
   | 20 or more | 3 |

2. **Natural 1:** you **bend the nail**. Take a shot, and your next turn is spent straightening it (no swing).
3. **Pass the hammer** to the player on your left. It's their turn.

### Shots

- **Every miss** (0 depth, or a bent nail) means **one shot of schnapps**. The drinker pays for it: **1 sp**.
- **Each shot is one drink:** one **Constitution saving throw at DC 10** on the Drunk Track.

### Winning

- The **first nail flush** (4 depth) wins the pot. The game ends right away.
- **The player with the least depth buys the next round.** If several are tied for least, they split it.

### Drunk Track

![[Drunk Track#Rules]]

**Being Drunk hurts here:** a Drunk player has disadvantage on their swings.

## Dice Examples

**Players** (in order): Thokk (NPC, Athletics +5, Con save +5 with advantage), Quill (PC, Athletics +0, Con save +1) and Mirela (PC, Athletics +0, Con save +1).

**Round 1**
- **Thokk:** **12** + 5 = **17**. 2 depth (2).
- **Quill:** **7** + 0 = **7**. Nothing. He takes a shot: Constitution save **13** + 1 = **14**. Pass.
- **Mirela:** **15** + 0 = **15**. 2 depth (2).

**Round 2**
- **Thokk:** **natural 1**. The nail bends! He takes a shot (Constitution save: **4** and **16**, keeps 16: 16 + 5 = **21**, pass), and loses his next turn straightening it.
- **Quill:** **18** + 0 = **18**. 2 depth (2).
- **Mirela:** **11** + 0 = **11**. 1 depth (3).

**Round 3**
- **Thokk** straightens his nail. No swing.
- **Quill:** **14** + 0 = **14**. 1 depth (3).
- **Mirela:** **10** + 0 = **10**. 1 depth (4). **Flush! Mirela wins the pot.**

Thokk has the least depth (2), so **Thokk buys the round**, to the crowd's delight.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 2 sp per player, nail included.
- **Winner:** 80% of the entries.
- **Last place** buys the next round for the players.
- **Shots:** 1 sp each, paid by the drinker.
- **The House keeps:** 20% of the entries (which pays for the nails), the shots and the round.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity with carpenter's tools, chosen each swing.
- One continuous swing per turn, wedge end only.
- Dwarven Resilience on the Constitution saves.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *enhance ability*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Bribing the nail-setter, using the hammer's flat face, or a second tap.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sneak a second tap | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | +1 depth |
| **Bribe the nail-setter** (1 sp) to tap your nail in deeper | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Your nail starts at 1 depth |
| Hidden spell (*enhance ability*), by touch before the game: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the back | The player or a friend | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on your Strength checks for the game |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Hammerschlagen is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

In round 3, Quill sneaks a second tap.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **16** + 7 = **23**. The Watcher: **10** + 5 = **15**. Not caught.
- Quill's round 3 swing (14: 1 depth) plus the extra tap takes him from 2 to 4 depth. He swings before Mirela, so **Quill wins instead**.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The stump** is from a lightning-struck oak. It's said that whoever drives the thousandth nail into it will be struck by lightning too. The count is at 994.
- **A dwarven smith** sells "lucky nails" at the door. They're noticeably shorter, and they only help if they end up in the House box. The nail-setter is his nephew.
