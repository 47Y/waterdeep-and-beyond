# Blindfold Tavern Maze

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Couples' night. The tavern is full and nobody has moved, except for a few tables nudged into awkward places. A bell hangs from the ceiling over a cleared table at the far end. One partner is blindfolded at the door. The other shouts directions across a room full of drinkers, who are shouting wrong ones.*

**Description:** A weekly pairs race across the tavern as it is. The **runner** is blindfolded at the door and has to cross the busy room, climb onto a cleared table and **ring the bell** hanging above it. The **guide** stays at the door and calls out directions. There's no straight line to the bell, so the runner has to turn through **five sections**. Every failed try costs **30 seconds**, and crashing into a patron earns a **boot to the backside**. The fastest pair wins.

## How to Play

### The pair

- **Any two people** can enter as a pair. Couples' night is just the crowd's favourite.
- **The runner** is blindfolded at the door.
- **The guide** stays at the door for the whole run and calls out directions.
- **One pair runs at a time.**

### The course

- **The room stays as it is.** Patrons stay at their tables and keep drinking. The House moves only a few tables, so there's no straight line from the door to the bell.
- **The bell** hangs from the ceiling above a **cleared table** at the far end. No patron sits at that table or stands near it.
- **The route** has five sections, in this order:

| Section | What it's like | Runner's check | DC |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The quiet tables | Wide gaps, and the guide is easy to hear | Wisdom (Perception) | 12 |
| 2. The busy tables | Tight gaps between chairs, elbows and serving staff | Dexterity (Acrobatics) | 15 |
| 3. The shouting crowd | Drinkers yelling wrong directions | Wisdom (Perception) | 18 |
| 4. The bell table | Climb up onto the table | Strength (Athletics) | 12 |
| 5. The bell | Stand up on the table and ring the bell | Dexterity (Acrobatics) | 15 |

### Each try at a section

1. **The guide calls it:** a **Charisma (Performance)** check, DC 12. **Success** gives the runner **advantage** on this try.
2. **The runner rolls** the section's check. Every try takes **30 seconds**, pass or fail.
   - **Success:** move on to the next section.
   - **Failure in sections 1–3:** you crash into a patron. They get a **free boot to your backside** (see below). Then try the section again.
   - **Failure in sections 4–5:** you slip off the table. It's too low to hurt. Try the section again.
3. **Ring the bell** (pass section 5) and the clock stops.

### The boot

The patron makes an attack roll at **+2** against the runner's **AC**, with **advantage**, because the runner is blindfolded. **On a hit, the runner takes 1d2 bludgeoning damage.**

**Drop to 0 HP** and your run is over. You're stable.

### Time limit

At **6 minutes**, the runner takes off the blindfold. The pair didn't finish and places behind every pair that did.

### Winning

- **The fastest pair wins.** A clean run (no failures) takes **2 minutes 30 seconds**.
- **Ties:** the tied runners each make one **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check. The highest total wins. If they tie again, they roll again.

## Dice Examples

**Pair 1:** Mirela guides (PC, Performance +5). Quill runs (PC, Perception +6, Acrobatics +5, Athletics +0, AC 14, HP 21).

| Section | Mirela's call (DC 12) | Quill's roll | Result | Clock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Quiet tables (DC 12) | **13** + 5 = 18 ✓ | Advantage: **7** and **15**, keeps 15: 15 + 6 = 21 | ✓ | 0:30 |
| 2. Busy tables (DC 15) | **4** + 5 = 9 ✗ | **8** + 5 = 13 | ✗ He crashes into a patron. The boot: **9** and **14**, keeps 14: 14 + 2 = 16, which hits AC 14. **2** damage (HP 19). | 1:00 |
| 2. Busy tables, again | **12** + 5 = 17 ✓ | Advantage: **11** and **16**, keeps 16: 16 + 5 = 21 | ✓ | 1:30 |
| 3. Shouting crowd (DC 18) | **15** + 5 = 20 ✓ | Advantage: **10** and **13**, keeps 13: 13 + 6 = 19 | ✓ | 2:00 |
| 4. Climb the table (DC 12) | **6** + 5 = 11 ✗ | **14** + 0 = 14 | ✓ | 2:30 |
| 5. Ring the bell (DC 15) | **16** + 5 = 21 ✓ | Advantage: **3** and **12**, keeps 12: 12 + 5 = 17 | ✓ **Ding!** | **3:00** |

**Pair 2:** Thokk guides (NPC, Performance −1). "Fat" Oswin runs (NPC, Perception +0, Acrobatics +0, Athletics +2, AC 11, HP 9).

| Section | Thokk's call (DC 12) | Oswin's roll | Result | Clock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Quiet tables (DC 12) | **15** − 1 = 14 ✓ | Advantage: **4** and **13**, keeps 13: 13 + 0 = 13 | ✓ | 0:30 |
| 2. Busy tables (DC 15) | **8** − 1 = 7 ✗ | **9** + 0 = 9 | ✗ The boot: **6** and **17**, keeps 17: 17 + 2 = 19, which hits. **1** damage (HP 8). | 1:00 |
| 2. Busy tables, again | **12** − 1 = 11 ✗ | **16** + 0 = 16 | ✓ | 1:30 |
| 3. Shouting crowd (DC 18) | **14** − 1 = 13 ✓ | Advantage: **11** and **7**, keeps 11: 11 + 0 = 11 | ✗ The boot: **3** and **5**, keeps 5: 5 + 2 = 7, a miss. | 2:00 |
| 3. Shouting crowd, again | **18** − 1 = 17 ✓ | Advantage: **19** and **2**, keeps 19: 19 + 0 = 19 | ✓ | 2:30 |
| 4. Climb the table (DC 12) | **10** − 1 = 9 ✗ | **11** + 2 = 13 | ✓ | 3:00 |
| 5. Ring the bell (DC 15) | **13** − 1 = 12 ✓ | Advantage: **15** and **9**, keeps 15: 15 + 0 = 15 | ✓ **Ding!** | **3:30** |

**Mirela and Quill win: 3:00 beats 3:30.**

**Edge case: the time limit.** If Oswin had still been trying the shouting crowd at 6:00, he'd take off the blindfold. Pair 2 would place behind every pair that rang the bell.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per pair.
- **Winning pair:** 70% of the entries and a **free dinner for two**.
- **The House keeps:** 30% of entries and a packed room on couples' night.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any way of giving directions: words, songs, or a code you agreed in advance.
- The crowd shouting wrong directions. That's the game.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *message*, *guidance*, *enhance ability* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Peeking past the blindfold, or paying someone in the crowd to imitate a rival's guide.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Peek:** tie the blindfold loose and look down past your nose | Runner | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on every runner check |
| **Message:** the guide whispers through the spell | A guide who can cast *message* | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Every call succeeds |
| **Paid mimic in the crowd** (2 gp): someone copies a rival guide's voice | Anyone | Cha (Deception) | The rival runner has disadvantage on section 3 |

The Blindfold Tavern Maze is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A caught pair is disqualified together.

### Cheating dice example

Mirela pays a mimic in the crowd to copy Thokk's gravelly voice.
- Mirela: Deception **10** + 7 = **17**. The Watcher: **8** + 5 = **13**. Not caught.
- In the shouting crowd, Thokk's call succeeds (advantage), but the mimic gives Oswin disadvantage. **Advantage and disadvantage cancel**, so Oswin rolls one die: **11** + 0 = **11**. ✗ Another boot and another 30 seconds.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **A pair of newlyweds** have won three weeks running. Their secret: she isn't really blindfolded. Or he isn't really guiding.
- **Old Harl** refuses to move his table on maze night, and it's always right in the route. He's never missed with his boot.
- **An old married couple** enter every week. He runs blindfolded, and she calls. Her calls are always wrong, straight into the busiest tables, and he takes boot after boot. Is she just bad at the game, or is this her way of getting even after forty years?
