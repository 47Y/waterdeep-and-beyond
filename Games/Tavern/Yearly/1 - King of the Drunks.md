# King of the Drunks

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Once a year the taps run free and the toughest drinkers in the city take their stools. One pint per round. Every round the ale gets stronger. Last one upright is crowned King of the Drunks.*

**Description:** The tavern's biggest night of the year. Contestants sit in a row on stage and drink one pint per round, and each round's ale is stronger than the last. Every pint is a fight to keep it together, and three failures put you off your stool and out of the contest. The last drinker still in wins free drinks for a year, a portrait and plaque in the hall, and a beer brewed and named in their honor.

## How to Play

1. **Seat everyone.** Each contestant starts at **0 failures (Sober)**.
2. **Drink.** At the start of each round, every contestant drinks one pint.
3. **Roll.** Every contestant makes a **Constitution saving throw**. The DC is **9 + the round number** (round 1 is DC 10, round 2 is DC 11, and so on).
4. **Check the result:**
   - **Total equals or beats the DC:** you hold it down. Nothing changes.
   - **Total is below the DC:** gain **1 failure**.
   - **Natural 1** (the d20 shows a 1): gain **2 failures**.
5. **Three failures and you're out.** You slump off your stool.
6. **Repeat** until one contestant is left. That contestant wins.
7. **Sudden death.** If every remaining contestant is knocked out in the same round, they all drink once more at the same DC. The highest total wins. If the top totals tie, repeat.

### Drunk Track

| Failures | State | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Sober | None |
| 1 | Tipsy | None. Roleplay only. |
| 2 | Drunk | **Poisoned** condition. This does *not* affect the contest, because poisoned only hurts attack rolls and ability checks, not saving throws. |
| 3 | Out | Slumps off the stool. Stays poisoned until a long rest. |

## Dice Examples

**Contestants:**
- **Thokk Stonejaw** (NPC, dwarf fighter): Con +3, proficient in Con saves (+2), so **+5 with advantage** (Dwarven Resilience).
- **Mirela** (PC, human bard): Con +1, not proficient, so **+1**.
- **"Fat" Oswin** (NPC, three-time champion): Con +4, so **+4**.

**Round 1, DC 10**
- Thokk rolls two d20s for advantage and gets **12** and 6. He keeps the 12: 12 + 5 = **17**. Pass.
- Mirela rolls **11**: 11 + 1 = **12**. Pass.
- Oswin rolls **4**: 4 + 4 = **8**. Fail, so 1 failure (Tipsy).

**Round 2, DC 11**
- Thokk: 3 and **15**. Keeps the 15: 15 + 5 = **20**. Pass.
- Mirela: **natural 1**. That's 2 failures, so she's Drunk.
- Oswin: **9** + 4 = **13**. Pass. He stays at 1 failure.

**Round 3, DC 12**
- Thokk: 2 and **5**. Even his better die is low: 5 + 5 = **10**. Fail, so 1 failure.
- Mirela: **10** + 1 = **11**. One short. That's her 3rd failure, so she's **out**.
- Oswin: **16** + 4 = **20**. Pass.

**Sudden death (round 9, DC 18):** Thokk and Oswin both sit at 2 failures, and both fail in round 9. Both would be out at once, so they drink one more pint at DC 18 and the highest total wins.
- Thokk: **14** and 9. Keeps the 14: 14 + 5 = **19**.
- Oswin: **17** + 4 = **21**.
- **Oswin wins.** He keeps his crown for a fourth year.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 5 gp per contestant.
- **Winner's prizes:**
  - Free drinks for one year at menu price in any tavern in the city. The House reimburses those taverns. Ale is 4 cp a mug, so the House budgets about 75 gp.
  - A portrait and plaque in the grand hall.
  - A beer brewed and named for the winner.
  - 50% of all entry fees.
- **The House keeps:** the other 50% of entry fees, all crowd bar sales, and its cut of spectator bets.
- **Spectator betting:** everyone's bets go into one pool. The House takes 15% and the rest is split among people who backed the winner.
  - *Example:* the pool is 100 gp and 20 gp of it was bet on Oswin. After the House's 15%, 85 gp is left. 85 ÷ 20 = **4.25 gp paid back for every 1 gp bet on Oswin**.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- **Natural toughness counts.** Your own Con modifier, proficiency in Con saves, and racial traits are all legal.
- **Alcohol counts as poison**, so **Dwarven Resilience** and similar traits give **advantage** on the save.
- **Being Drunk (poisoned) doesn't hurt the contest.** Poisoned only affects attack rolls and ability checks, not saving throws.
- **Natural 1 house rule:** a natural 1 counts as two failures. In standard 2014 rules it would just be a low roll.

### What counts as cheating

Any help from outside your own body, whether magical or not:
- **Spells** such as *bless*, *resistance*, *lesser restoration* or *protection from poison*, cast by you or anyone else.
- **Bardic Inspiration**, because the die comes from someone else.
- **Antitoxin** (50 gp in the PHB). It gives advantage on saves against poison for 1 hour, which covers the whole contest.
- **Tampering:** watering down your own pint, spiking a rival's, or bribing the pourer.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help a contestant, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water your pint | Contestant or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Automatically pass this round |
| Sneak an antitoxin | Contestant | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on every remaining save |
| Hidden spell | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally. A round lasts about 5 minutes, so a 1-minute spell like *bless* covers one round only. |
| Spike a rival's pint | Anyone | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival rolls with disadvantage this round |
| Bribe the pourer (10 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The contestant gets advantage for the next 2 rounds |

### Cheating dice example

In round 3, Mirela is one failure from elimination. Her rogue friend Quill (Sleight of Hand +7) tries to swap her pint for water.
- Quill rolls **8** + 7 = **15**.
- The Watcher rolls **13** + 5 = **18**. The Watcher wins.
- **Caught.** Mirela is disqualified and both of them are banned from next year's contest. Two new portraits go up on the Cheater's Wall, the town crier reads their names in the square, and their ale now costs 8 cp a mug instead of 4 cp.
- To clear their names, each must pay 50 gp, spend 5 days in the stocks or pouring at the tavern, or wait out the year.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **"Fat" Oswin** has won three years running. The rumour is that his brewer makes a special ale just for his stein.
- **A rival tavern** wants this year's champion to swear loyalty to *their* house.
