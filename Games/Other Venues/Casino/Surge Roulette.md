# Surge Roulette

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Behind a shimmering wall of warding glass, a wild-haired sorcerer cracks her knuckles. "Place your bets!" She snaps her fingers, and raw magic erupts. Will she turn blue? Grow a beard of feathers? Explode in a harmless shower of butterflies?*

**Description:** A magic show and a betting game in one. A House **wild magic sorcerer** stands in a warded glass booth and lets loose a surge of wild magic. Players bet on which effect happens. It's loud, strange, and the crowd loves it.

## How to Play

### The surge

The DM rolls **d100** on the **Wild Magic Surge** table in the *Player's Handbook* (page 104). That table has **50 effects**, and each effect covers **two numbers** (01–02, 03–04, and so on up to 99–00).

**The booth is warded.** Every effect happens inside the glass. Nothing reaches the crowd, and the sorcerer is protected by the House's wards.

### A round, step by step

1. **Bet.** Each player bets on one or more of:
   - **Single Surge:** one effect (two numbers, such as 07–08).
   - **Band:** one group of 20 numbers.

     | Band | Numbers |
     |---|---|
     | First | 01–20 |
     | Second | 21–40 |
     | Third | 41–60 |
     | Fourth | 61–80 |
     | Fifth | 81–00 |

2. **Surge.** The sorcerer snaps her fingers. The DM rolls **d100**.
3. **Describe the effect** from the table, then pay out.

### Payouts

| Bet | Pays |
|---|---|
| **Single Surge** | 45:1 |
| **Band** | 15:4 (win 15 for every 4 bet) |

## Dice Examples

**At the booth:** Thokk (NPC) bets **4 gp on the Third Band (41–60)**. Mirela (PC) bets **1 gp on the Single Surge 07–08**.

**Round 1**
- d100: **47**. That's in the Third Band.
- Thokk wins 15:4 on 4 gp: **15 gp**. Mirela **loses** 1 gp.

**Round 2**
- d100: **08**. Mirela's effect!
- Mirela wins 45:1: **45 gp**. Thokk **loses** 4 gp.

**Round 3**
- d100: **93**. Fifth Band. Both **lose**.

**Edge case: a 00**
- d100: **00** (counts as 100). That's the last effect on the table (99–00) and in the Fifth Band (81–00).
- Anyone on the Fifth Band or on Single Surge 99–00 wins.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any bet within the table limits.
- Cheering for an effect, booing the sorcerer and throwing flowers.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the surge, the d100 or the sorcerer.
- Bribing the sorcerer to "aim" the surge, which she can't really do, but she might pretend to.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late bet ("past posting") | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Place a bet after the effect is known |
| Hidden spell (*silvery barbs*, Tides of Chaos, any reroll) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Reroll the d100 once, keep either |
| Fake effect (*minor illusion* in the booth) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting | The crowd sees a different effect. The DM moves the result up to 2 effects up or down |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Surge Roulette is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Mirela bets 2 gp on a Single Surge. The d100 lands two effects away, and she casts a Subtle *minor illusion* in the booth.
- Mirela: Deception with advantage **3** and **14**, keeps 14: 14 + 7 = **21**. The Watcher: **13** + 5 = **18**. **Mirela wins the contest.**
- The crowd sees her effect. She collects **90 gp** before anyone checks. The sorcerer, though, knows what she really did.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Zephyra Crackleflame**, the House sorcerer, is getting tired of the booth. She'd love to join an adventuring party and needs someone to buy out her contract.
- **One effect** keeps coming up three times a night. Zephyra swears she isn't doing it on purpose.
