# Kraken's Grasp

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Four sea-green dice clatter across a table painted like a stormy ocean. Every eight is a tentacle rising from the deep. "One tentacle catches the ship," roars the dealer. "Two drags it under!"*

**Description:** A sailor's dice game. The player rolls **4d8**. Every **8** is a tentacle. If the kraken grabs the ship with at least one tentacle, the player wins. Bet on two tentacles for a much bigger prize.

## How to Play

1. **Bet.** Choose one or both:

   | Bet | Wins when… | Pays |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Grasp** | At least one die shows 8 | 6:5 |
   | **Drag Under** | At least two dice show 8 | 11:1 |

2. **Roll.** Roll **4d8** once.
3. **Pay out.** A Drag Under also counts as a Grasp, so both bets win.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** "Fat" Oswin (NPC) bets **5 gp on Grasp** and **1 gp on Drag Under** every roll.

**Roll 1**
- Dice: **3, 8, 5, 1**. One tentacle.
- Grasp wins 6:5: **6 gp**. Drag Under **loses** 1 gp.

**Roll 2**
- Dice: **2, 7, 4, 6**. No tentacles. Both **lose**.

**Roll 3**
- Dice: **7, 7, 1, 5**. No eights. Both **lose**.

**Edge case: dragged under**
- Dice: **8, 2, 8, 6**. Two tentacles!
- Grasp wins **6 gp**, and Drag Under wins 11:1: **11 gp**. The dealer rings the ship's bell.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Either bet or both, within the table limits.
- Sea shanties. The louder the better.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the dice.
- Loaded d8s, or tipping a die after it lands.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tip a die | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Change one d8 to an 8 |
| Loaded dice swap | Player or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Roll 5d8 instead of 4d8 for the rest of the night |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand* nudge) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Change one d8 to an 8 |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Kraken's Grasp is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

![[Cheating Penalties#Casino]]

### Cheating dice example

Quill (PC) bets 10 gp on Drag Under and rolls **8, 3, 5, 2**. He tips the 5.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **8** + 7 = **15**. The Watcher: **12** + 5 = **17**. **Caught.**
- He's confiscated, filed and banned for a month, and owes **50 gp** (five times his 10 gp bet) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Captain Brinemaw**, a retired pirate, deals the table. He claims he lost his ship to a real kraken and still owes it a debt.
- **The ship's bell** above the table was salvaged from a wreck. On foggy nights it rings by itself.
