# Wizard's Four Towers

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Four tiny crystal pyramids tumble across a starry cloth. Each one lands point-up, showing a single rune. "Four different towers," the dealer says, "and the wizard's council is complete."*

**Description:** A quick dice game with four-sided dice. The player rolls **4d4**. If all four dice show **different** numbers (1, 2, 3 and 4), the wizard's council is complete and the player wins big. If all four show the **same** number, they win even bigger.

## How to Play

1. **Bet.** Choose one or both:

   | Bet | Wins when… | Pays |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Council** | All four dice show different numbers (1, 2, 3, 4 in any order) | 9:1 |
   | **Lone Tower** | All four dice show the same number | 60:1 |

2. **Roll.** Roll **4d4** once.
3. **Pay out.** Any other result loses.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Mirela (PC) bets **1 gp on Council**. Thokk (NPC) bets **1 gp on Lone Tower**.

**Roll 1**
- Dice: **3, 1, 4, 2**. All different!
- Mirela wins 9:1: **9 gp**. Thokk **loses**.

**Roll 2**
- Dice: **2, 2, 4, 1**. A pair of 2s: not all different, not all the same. Both **lose**.

**Roll 3**
- Dice: **4, 4, 4, 3**. So close to a Lone Tower. Both **lose**.

**Edge case: a Lone Tower**
- Dice: **1, 1, 1, 1**. All the same!
- Thokk wins 60:1: **60 gp**. Mirela **loses**.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Either bet or both, within the table limits.
- Naming the four wizards of your council as you roll.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the dice.
- Loaded d4s, or tipping a die over after it lands.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tip a die | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Change one d4 to a face of your choice |
| Loaded dice swap | Player or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Reroll one d4 each roll for the rest of the night |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand* nudge) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Change one d4 to a face of your choice |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Wizard's Four Towers is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Thokk bets 2 gp on Lone Tower and rolls **4, 4, 4, 3**. He reaches over to tip the 3.
- Thokk: Sleight of Hand **20** − 1 = **19**. The Watcher: **13** + 5 = **18**. **Not caught.**
- The 3 becomes a 4. Thokk wins **120 gp**, and the Watcher is suddenly very interested in him.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The crystal d4s** are said to be shards of a real wizard's tower that collapsed a century ago. On a Lone Tower, they hum.
- **Four retired wizards** play here every night, each insisting the council needs their vote.
