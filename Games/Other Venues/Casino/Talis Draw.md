# Talis Draw

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A fortune teller in silver bangles shuffles a deck of painted Talis cards and turns one face-up. The Sun blazes from the card. Somewhere at the table, a gambler laughs out loud.*

**Description:** A one-card fortune game. The dealer draws a single card from a twenty-card **Talis** deck. Five of the cards are lucky and pay out; the rest are unlucky and lose. Every bet is on the same draw. It's quick, superstitious and popular with sailors.

## How to Play

### The deck

The dealer draws one card. At the table, roll a **d20** to find it.

| d20 | Card | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Sun** | 7:1 |
| 2 | **The Moon** | 4:1 |
| 3 | **The Star** | 1:1 |
| 4 | **The Comet** | 1:1 |
| 5 | **The Throne** | 1:1 |
| 6–20 | The Key, Tower, Tomb, Raven, Serpent, Mask, Chains, Hag, Gallows, Storm, Wolf, Fool, Beggar, Ruin or Void | Lose |

### A round, step by step

1. **Bet.** Each player places a bet. There's only one kind of bet: that the card is lucky.
2. **Draw.** The dealer turns one card. Roll a **d20**.
3. **Pay out** by the card drawn. The deck is reshuffled after every draw.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Thokk (NPC) bets **5 gp**, Mirela (PC) bets **2 gp**.

**Draw 1**
- d20: **4**, **The Comet**. Pays 1:1.
- Thokk wins **5 gp**; Mirela wins **2 gp**.

**Draw 2**
- d20: **13**, **The Hag**. Both **lose**.

**Draw 3**
- d20: **2**, **The Moon**. Pays 4:1.
- Thokk wins **20 gp**; Mirela wins **8 gp**.

**Edge case: The Sun**
- d20: **1**, **The Sun**. Pays 7:1.
- Thokk wins **35 gp**; Mirela wins **14 gp**. The fortune teller declares it a blessed night and buys the table a round.

## Odds

| Card | Chance |
|---|---|
| The Sun | 5% |
| The Moon | 5% |
| Star, Comet or Throne | 15% |
| Any lucky card | 25% |
| An unlucky card | 75% |

**House edge: 5%.** Most draws lose, but a lucky card pays enough to make up for it.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any bet within the table limits.
- Asking the fortune teller what your card means. She always has an answer.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the draw.
- Marked cards, or a partner who helps the dealer "lose" the unlucky cards.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swap in a marked deck | Player or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Reroll the d20 once on each of the next 3 draws, keep the better |
| Bribe the dealer | Player | Cha (Persuasion) against the dealer's Wisdom (Insight) +2 first, then the dealer's Dex (Sleight of Hand) +4 against the Watcher | The dealer draws The Throne (1:1) |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand* on the deck) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Reroll the d20 once, keep either |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Talis Draw is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Quill (PC) swaps in a marked deck before a 10 gp bet at the Gold Table.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **6** + 7 = **13**. The Watcher: **9** + 5 = **14**. **Caught.**
- He's confiscated, filed and banned for a month, and owes **50 gp** (five times his 10 gp bet) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The Void card** has started appearing even when the d20 says otherwise. Orsolya won't touch the deck.
