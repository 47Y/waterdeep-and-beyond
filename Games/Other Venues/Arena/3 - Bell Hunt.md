# Bell Hunt

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A blindfolded hunter stands in a padded field. Somewhere nearby, a tiny clockwork mouse with a bell on its back scurries and jingles. Listen, guess where it's going, then pounce. The crowd giggles at every face-plant.*

**Description:** The arena's daily comedy game. A **blindfolded** hunter tries to catch a **clockwork bell-mouse** scurrying around a padded field. **Find it, predict it, catch it**, each a check. Every attempt takes a turn. **Catch it in 3 turns** and you double your money.

## How to Play

### A hunt, step by step

1. **Pay 1 sp** and put on the blindfold.
2. **Work through three stages, in order.** Each attempt takes **1 turn**. On a failure, try that stage again next turn.

| Stage | Check | DC |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Find it (listen for the bell) | Wisdom (Perception) | 12 |
| 2. Predict where it's going | Wisdom (Survival) | 15 |
| 3. Pounce and catch it | Dexterity (Acrobatics) | 18 |

3. **Count the turns** it takes to catch it.

**The field is padded. No damage.** No spells and no magic items.

**Netted curtain:** down.

## Dice Examples

**Quill** (PC, Perception +6, Survival +2, Acrobatics +5).

| Turn | Stage | Roll | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find (DC 12) | **9** + 6 = 15 | ✓ |
| 2 | Predict (DC 15) | **11** + 2 = 13 | ✗ |
| 3 | Predict | **16** + 2 = 18 | ✓ |
| 4 | Catch (DC 18) | **10** + 5 = 15 | ✗ Face-plant. |
| 5 | Catch | **14** + 5 = 19 | ✓ **Caught!** |

**5 turns.** No prize, but the crowd loved the face-plant.

**Edge case:** if Quill had made every stage first time, he'd have caught it in **3 turns** and won **2 sp**.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **A hunt:** 1 sp.
- **Caught in 3 turns:** 2 sp (double your money).
- **Caught in 4 turns:** your 1 sp back.
- **The day's fastest catch** goes on the board.
- **The House keeps:** everything else.

## Odds

If a hunter has the same bonus in all three skills:

| Bonus | +2 | +5 | +7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caught in 3 turns | 5.5% | 15% | 26% |
| Caught in 4 turns | 10% | 21% | 27% |
| Average turns | 8.3 | 5.75 | 4.8 |

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Asking the crowd to be quiet. (They won't be.)
- Crawling, rolling and diving.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- A blindfold you can see through, or a friend calling out where the mouse is.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| A thin blindfold | Hunter | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on the Find and Catch stages |
| A friend coughing signals | Anyone in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to look innocent | Advantage on the Predict stage |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Bell Hunt is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 1 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Lark coughs signals to Quill from the crowd.
- Lark: Deception **12** + 4 = **16**. The Steward: **15** + 5 = **20**. **Caught.**
- Quill's hunt is void. Lark is banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 1 gp, 1 day serving (winding the mice), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **One of the bell-mice** has stopped coming back to its winding-box at night. Someone's heard jingling in the walls.
- **A young hunter** comes every day and has never caught a mouse. They're saving every copper to try again.
