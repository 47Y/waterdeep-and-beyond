# Venatio

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The beast-gate rises, chain by chain. Something huge breathes in the dark. A team of hunters stands in the middle of the sand, back to back. The House wants the beast alive. The crowd wants a show.*

**Description:** A monthly beast hunt. A team of hunters fights a **beast** chosen to match them. The beast is taken **alive**: at 0 HP it's stable, captured and sold to a collector, and the hunters share the purse. Spectators bet on the beast and on how long the hunt lasts.

## How to Play

### Choose the beast

The beast's CR should be about the **hunters' level**.

| Tier | Example beasts | Purse (shared by the hunters) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (levels 1–4) | Brown bear, owlbear | 20 gp |
| 2 (levels 5–10) | A pair of owlbears, a troll | 100 gp |
| 3 (levels 11–16) | A young dragon (no flying: clipped wings or a chain) | 500 gp |

### Magic items

![[Arena Combat Rules#Magic Items]]

### A hunt, step by step

1. **Roll initiative** and fight with normal combat rules. Your own weapons, armour, magic items and spells are allowed.
2. **The hunt ends** when the beast drops to **0 HP** (captured: the hunters win), or every hunter drops or yields (the beast wins).
3. **Count the rounds.** Spectators bet on how many rounds it lasts.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Arena Ward]]

**Netted curtain:** up.

### NPC hunts

When no PC is hunting, settle it with **Clash**, using the **Groups** rule: see [[Arena Combat Rules#Clash]].

## Dice Examples

**Hunters:** the party at level 3: Thokk, Quill, Mirela and Oswin.
**The beast:** an owlbear (HP 59, AC 13).

**The bookie:**
- The hunters are Pit regular (+4), +3 for having three more fighters: **+7**. The owlbear is a Veteran: **+6**. The hunters win **59.5%**, so the beast wins 40.5%.
- **Beast Wins** (40.5%) is between the 50% and 40% rows, so the bookie uses the lower payout: **1 gp 8 sp** per 1 gp.
- **Over/Under 4½ rounds:** the bookie sets the line at the likely middle. Both sides pay **1 gp 8 sp** per 1 gp.

**The hunt (summary)**
- **Round 1:** the owlbear mauls Thokk with beak and claws for 16 damage (Thokk is at 12). Quill's Sneak Attack lands for **13**, and Mirela's *dissonant whispers* deals **11**. The owlbear is at 35.
- **Round 2:** Thokk's Action Surge lands two hits for **14**. Oswin's club hits for **5**. The owlbear is at 16.
- **Round 3:** Quill hits for **12**, and Mirela's *vicious mockery* deals **4**. **The owlbear drops at 0.** It's stable, and the handlers chain it.

**Three rounds:** the Under wins. The hunters split **20 gp** (5 gp each).

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Your own weapons, armour, magic items, spells and class features.
- Nets, ropes and other capture gear.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any help from outside the pit.**
- Drugging the beast before the hunt, or bribing the handlers to send out a weaker beast.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drug the beast | Anyone with access to the beast-pens | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The beast is **poisoned** for the hunt |
| Bribe the handlers to delay the gate (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The hunters get a free round before the beast acts |
| Hidden spell from the rim | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Venatio is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. If the hunters were in on it, they forfeit the purse.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 20 gp | 2 days |

### Cheating dice example

Someone in the crowd has bet heavily on the Under. They bribe the handlers 5 gp to hold the gate.
- The briber: Persuasion **16** + 2 = **18**. The Steward: **17** + 5 = **22**. **Caught.**
- The hunt starts normally. The briber is banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear their name: 20 gp, 2 days serving (mucking out the beast-pens), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The collector** who buys every captured beast never shows their face. Their agent pays in very old coins.
- **Next month's beast** escaped during delivery and is loose somewhere in the city.
