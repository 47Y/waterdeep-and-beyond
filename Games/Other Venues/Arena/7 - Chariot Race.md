# Chariot Race

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Eight chariots burst from the gates in a storm of dust and hooves. Seven laps. Every turn, someone tries to run someone else into the wall, and somebody always ends up in a heap at the far post.*

**Description:** A monthly chariot race over **seven laps**. Each lap, every driver rolls to handle their team. **The highest seven-lap total wins.** Drivers can **ram** rivals to slow them down, and a **natural 1** means a crash, and that driver is out.

*This game doesn't fit the arena pit. It's kept here for a future venue with a full-size track.*

## How to Play

### A race, step by step

1. **Each lap, every driver** makes a **Wisdom (Animal Handling)** check. Add your proficiency bonus if you're proficient with **land vehicles**.
2. **Add up each driver's laps.** After **7 laps**, the **highest total wins.**
3. **Ram (once per lap, optional):** before the lap's checks, a driver may ram one rival. Both make an **Animal Handling contest**. **The loser takes −5 on that lap's check.** (If the rammer loses, the rammer takes the −5.)
4. **A natural 1 on a lap check is a crash** (a *naufragium*). **That driver is out** of the race.
5. **A tied total** after 7 laps: the tied drivers make one more Animal Handling check, a final sprint. Highest wins.

**Crashes:** the driver is out, but **takes no damage**. The chariot is wrecked, the horses run on, and the driver limps back to the stands.

No spells and no magic items.

## Dice Examples

**Mirela** (PC, Animal Handling +3, no vehicle proficiency) against **Vessa** (NPC, Animal Handling +5).

| Lap | Mirela | Vessa | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **12** + 3 = 15 | **9** + 5 = 14 | |
| 2 | **8** + 3 = 11 | **14** + 5 = 19 | |
| 3 | **10** + 3 = 13 | **13** + 5 − 5 = 13 | **Mirela rams Vessa:** **16** + 3 = 19 against **11** + 5 = 16. Vessa takes −5 this lap. |
| 4 | **15** + 3 = 18 | **7** + 5 = 12 | |
| 5 | **11** + 3 = 14 | **17** + 5 = 22 | |
| 6 | **9** + 3 = 12 | **12** + 5 = 17 | |
| 7 | **14** + 3 = 17 | **10** + 5 = 15 | |
| **Total** | **100** | **112** | **Vessa wins** |

**Edge case: a crash.** On lap 4, another driver rolls a **natural 1**. Their chariot flips at the far post, and **they're out**, whatever their total was.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 2 gp per driver.
- **Purse:** 15 gp to the winner, 5 gp to second.
- **Spectators bet** on **Win**, **Place** (first or second) and **Exacta** (first and second in order).

## Odds

- **Finishing all 7 laps without a crash:** 70%, for any driver.
- **Head to head** (crashes included):

| Bonus difference | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The better driver wins | 50% | 56.5% | 62% | 67% | 72% |

Crashes keep this race wide open. Even a much better driver loses often.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Ramming, once per lap.
- Your own horses, if the race officials approve them.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- A cut harness, a drugged team, or a loosened wheel.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loosen a rival's wheel | Anyone in the stables | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival crashes on a natural 1 or 2 |
| Drug a rival's horses | Anyone in the stables | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival takes −2 on every lap |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

The Chariot Race is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Quill sneaks into the stables to loosen Vessa's wheel.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **15** + 7 = **22**. The Steward: **12** + 5 = **17**. Not caught.
- Vessa now crashes on a natural 1 **or 2**. Her chance of finishing all 7 laps drops from 70% to 48%.
