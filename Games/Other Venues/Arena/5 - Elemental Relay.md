# Elemental Relay

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Four legs, four elements. A wall of roaring wind, a field of shifting stones, a current that drags at your legs, and a ring of real fire at the end. Each runner hands off to the next, and the crowd stamps along to the clock.*

**Description:** A weekly team relay through **four elemental legs**: wind, earth, water and fire. Each team member runs **one leg**. A failed leg adds **10 seconds**, and failing the **fire** leg burns. **The team with the faster time wins.**

## How to Play

### The legs

| Leg | Check | DC |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Wind (push through a gale) | Strength (Athletics) | 12 |
| 2. Earth (cross shifting stones) | Dexterity (Acrobatics) | 14 |
| 3. Water (read the current in the flooded trench) | Wisdom (Survival) | 16 |
| 4. Fire (find the gap in the flame ring) | Intelligence (Arcana) | 18 |

### A relay, step by step

1. **Both teams run at the same time.** Every relay starts at **2 minutes**.
2. **Each runner makes one check** for their leg.
   - **Success:** a clean leg.
   - **Failure:** **add 10 seconds.** You still get through and hand off.
   - **Failing the fire leg** also deals **1d6 fire damage**.
3. **The faster team wins.** If times are tied, the fire runners make an **Arcana roll-off**.

No spells and no magic items.

## Dice Examples

**The Red team:** Thokk (wind, Athletics +5), Mirela (earth, Acrobatics +2), Oswin (NPC, water, Survival +0) and Quill (fire, Arcana +2).

| Leg | Runner | Roll | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wind (DC 12) | Thokk | **11** + 5 = 16 | ✓ |
| Earth (DC 14) | Mirela | **10** + 2 = 12 | ✗ +10 s |
| Water (DC 16) | Oswin | **17** + 0 = 17 | ✓ |
| Fire (DC 18) | Quill | **13** + 2 = 15 | ✗ +10 s, and **4** fire damage |

**Red's time:** 2 min + 20 s = **2 minutes 20 seconds.** Blue fails only one leg (**2 minutes 10 seconds**). **Blue wins.**

**Edge case: a tie.** If Blue had also failed two legs, the fire runners would roll off. Quill: **14** + 2 = 16. Blue's runner: **11** + 4 = 15. **Red would win.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Purse:** 8 gp to the winning team, shared.
- **The House keeps:** the entries, the betting cut and the seats.

## Odds

If every runner on a team has the same bonus:

| Bonus | +2 | +5 | +7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A clean relay (no failures) | 2.2% | 8.4% | 16.8% |
| Average failures | 2.4 | 1.8 | 1.4 |

Two equal teams are 50/50. Putting the right runner on each leg matters most.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any running order, set before the start.
- Shouting directions to your runner.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic** (no *protection from energy*, no *guidance*).
- A soaked cloak for the fire leg, or a bribed wind-mage.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| A soaked cloak under your shirt | The fire runner | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | No fire damage on a failure |
| Bribe the House wind-mage to ease off (2 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Your wind leg drops to DC 8 |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Elemental Relay is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A team that's in on it is disqualified.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 5 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Quill wears a soaked cloak under his shirt for the fire leg.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **8** + 7 = **15**. The Steward: **6** + 5 = **11**. Not caught.
- When Quill fails the fire leg, he takes no damage. The 10 seconds still count.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The House wind-mage** is a retired ship's weatherworker. She's terrified of the fire leg and won't say why.
- **The flame ring** is fed by a real fire elemental, bound under the pit. It's been testing its bindings.
