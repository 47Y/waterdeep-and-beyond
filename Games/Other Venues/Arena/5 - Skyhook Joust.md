# Skyhook Joust

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *High above the pit, two contestants in harnesses step off gantries slung under the second floor. They swing out over the sand, grabbing for rings that hang in their path, and meet in the middle with a thump the whole arena hears.*

**Description:** A monthly event in the air above the pit. Two contestants in **harnesses** swing from **opposite gantries** under the second floor. On each swing they try to hook **three rings**, each harder and worth more than the last. Halfway along, they **collide**. Most points after **three passes** wins.

## How to Play

### The rig

- **Two gantries** hang under the second floor, on opposite sides of the pit opening.
- **Each contestant wears a harness on a rope.** Nobody flies: you swing, and the rope catches you if you slip. **Falling does no damage.**
- **No spells and no magic items.**

### A pass, step by step

Both contestants swing at the same time.

1. **Ring 1:** a **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check, **DC 12**. Success: **1 point.**
2. **Ring 2:** **DC 15**. Success: **2 points.**
3. **Collision:** the swingers meet in the middle. Make a **Strength (Athletics)** contest. **Ties: nobody is knocked.**
   - **The loser takes 1d4 bludgeoning damage** and has **disadvantage on ring 3.**
4. **Ring 3:** **DC 18**. Success: **3 points.**

**Three passes.** The most points wins. If it's tied, swing one more pass.

**Netted curtain:** up.

## Dice Examples

**Quill** (PC, Acrobatics +5, Athletics +0) against **Mirela** (PC, Acrobatics +2, Athletics +0).

**Pass 1**

| Step | Quill | Mirela |
|---|---|---|
| Ring 1 (DC 12) | **9** + 5 = 14: **1 point** | **13** + 2 = 15: **1 point** |
| Ring 2 (DC 15) | **12** + 5 = 17: **2 points** | **10** + 2 = 12: miss |
| Collision | **6** + 0 = 6 | **14** + 0 = 14: **Mirela knocks Quill.** He takes **3** damage. |
| Ring 3 (DC 18) | Disadvantage: **15** and **7**, keeps 7: 7 + 5 = 12, miss | **16** + 2 = 18: **3 points** |
| **Score** | **3** | **4** |

**Passes 2 and 3:** Quill hooks rings 1 and 2 both times (+6). Mirela hooks ring 1 twice and ring 2 once (+4), and misses ring 3 both times.

**Final:** Quill **9**, Mirela **8**. **Quill wins.**

**Edge case: a tied collision.** If both had rolled 14, neither would be knocked, and both would try ring 3 normally.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Kicking off the gantry as hard as you like.
- Twisting to dodge the collision. (It's still a contest.)

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic** (no *feather fall*, no *jump*).
- A shortened rival rope, a weighted harness, or grip chalk swapped for flour.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shorten the rival's rope a notch | Anyone on the gantry | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival has disadvantage on ring 3 every pass |
| Swap the rival's chalk for flour | Anyone on the gantry | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | −2 to the rival's ring checks |
| Hidden spell (*enhance ability*) | A caster on the second floor | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Mirela, waiting on her gantry, tries to swap Quill's chalk for flour.
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **18** + 2 = **20**. The Steward: **13** + 5 = **18**. Not caught.
- Quill's ring checks are at −2 all bout. His pass 1 ring 2 roll becomes 12 + 5 − 2 = 15, which still just hooks it.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The rigger, Tamsin,** checks every rope herself. Last month a rope she checked snapped anyway.
- **A wealthy patron** books the same second-floor seat for every Skyhook night and always leaves before the last pass.
