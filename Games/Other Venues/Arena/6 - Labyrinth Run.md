# Labyrinth Run

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The pit has become a hedge maze. Every time a runner reaches a junction, the walls grind along their rails and the paths change. From the second-floor balcony, the crowd can see the whole thing, and they shout bad advice.*

**Description:** A monthly maze race. The pit is filled with a **living labyrinth** of hedge walls on rails that **shift after every junction**. Each runner faces **six junctions**. Read each one right and you're through; get it wrong and you lose **2 minutes**. **Fastest time wins.**

## How to Play

### A run, step by step

1. **Every run starts at 6 minutes.**
2. **At each of the six junctions**, make one check, **DC 13**. Choose the skill:
   - **Intelligence (Investigation):** work out the pattern of the walls.
   - **Wisdom (Survival):** follow the tracks and the wind.
   - **Wisdom (Perception):** hear the rails moving and spot the open path.
   - **You can't use the same skill at two junctions in a row.**
3. **Success:** you take the right path. **Failure:** a wrong turn. **Add 2 minutes**, but you find your way to the next junction.
4. **Fastest total time wins.** If times are tied, the tied runners share the purse.

No spells and no magic items.

## Dice Examples

**Quill** (PC, Perception +6, Investigation +2). He alternates the two skills.

| Junction | Skill | Roll | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Perception | **12** + 6 = 18 | ✓ |
| 2 | Investigation | **9** + 2 = 11 | ✗ +2 min |
| 3 | Perception | **7** + 6 = 13 | ✓ (meets the DC) |
| 4 | Investigation | **15** + 2 = 17 | ✓ |
| 5 | Perception | **4** + 6 = 10 | ✗ +2 min |
| 6 | Investigation | **13** + 2 = 15 | ✓ |

**Quill's time:** 6 + 2 + 2 = **10 minutes.**

**Edge case:** Quill would love to use Perception at every junction, but he can't use it twice in a row. A runner with **two or three good skills** does much better.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Listening to the crowd. (They're usually wrong.)
- Chalk marks on the hedges, for yourself. They're gone when the walls move.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic** (no *find the path*, no *locate object*).
- A signaller on the balcony, or a bribed rail operator.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| A signaller on the balcony | A friend on the second floor | Dex (Sleight of Hand) for hand signals | Advantage on every junction |
| Bribe the rail operator (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The walls don't move after junctions 1–3: those count as automatic successes |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*) | A caster on the balcony | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Mirela stands on the second-floor balcony to signal Quill through the maze.
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **10** + 2 = **12**. The Steward: **15** + 5 = **20**. **Caught.**
- Quill's run is disqualified. Mirela is banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear her name: 10 gp, 1 day serving (trimming hedges), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The maze-keeper** designs a new layout every month and keeps the plans in a locked book. Someone offered him 100 gp for next month's.
- **One hedge** in the maze has started growing back after it's cut, overnight. It wasn't planted by anyone at the arena.
