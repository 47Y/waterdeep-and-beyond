# Whisper Chain

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Four of you in a line, in the loudest tavern in town. The barkeep whispers a ridiculous sentence to the first of you. Pass it down the line, ear to ear, and the last one has to say it out loud. What comes out is usually nothing like what went in.*

**Description:** A daily team game, like Telephone. The barkeep whispers a **bizarre sentence** to the first player of each team. It's passed along **three handoffs**, and each listener makes a **Perception** check to hear it right. The **last player** makes an **Intelligence check** to put it back together. **Every success scores 1 point.** The team with the most points wins.

## How to Play

### A chain, step by step

1. **The team lines up**, four players.
2. **The barkeep whispers a sentence** to the first player. (No roll; the barkeep speaks clearly.)
3. **Three handoffs:** each player whispers to the next. The **listener** makes a **Wisdom (Perception)** check, **DC 12**.
   - **Success:** 1 point. They heard it right.
   - **Failure:** no point. The DM garbles one word of the sentence.
4. **The last player** makes an **Intelligence check**, **DC 15**, to piece it together and say it out loud.
   - **Success:** 1 point.
   - **Failure:** no point, and the DM garbles one more word.
5. **The team's score** is 0 to 4 points.

### Winning

- **The highest score wins.**
- **Ties:** the team whose last player had the higher Intelligence total wins.
- **The funniest garbled sentence** (the crowd decides) wins a free plate of appetisers, whatever the score.

## Dice Examples

**Team Party:** Mirela (first), Thokk (NPC, Perception +1), "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Perception +0) and Quill (PC, Perception +6, Int +2) last.

The barkeep whispers: *"The bishop's goat ate seven purple candles at the funeral."*

| Handoff | Listener | Roll | Result | Sentence now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mirela → Thokk | Thokk | **14** + 1 = 15 | ✓ 1 point | *"The bishop's goat ate seven purple candles at the funeral."* |
| Thokk → Oswin | Oswin | **8** + 0 = 8 | ✗ | *"The bishop's goat ate seven purple **sandals** at the funeral."* |
| Oswin → Quill | Quill | **13** + 6 = 19 | ✓ 1 point | (unchanged) |
| Quill says it | Quill (Int) | **15** + 2 = 17 | ✓ 1 point | *"The bishop's goat ate seven purple sandals at the funeral!"* |

**Team Party scores 3.** The Dockhands' team scores 2. **Team Party wins.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 sp per player (4 sp per team).
- **The winning team** splits the pool, minus 10% for the House.
- **The funniest failure:** a free plate of appetisers.
- **The House keeps:** the 10% and the drinks.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Choosing the order of your team.
- Whispering slowly, cupping hands, leaning in close.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *message*, *telepathic bond*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Hand signs (including Thieves' Cant), lip-reading the barkeep, or writing anything down.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thieves' Cant or hand signs | A player who knows them, to a teammate who does too | Cha (Deception) | One handoff succeeds automatically |
| Bribe the barkeep for the sentence (2 sp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Your last player has advantage on the Intelligence check |
| Hidden spell (*message*) | A caster on the team | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | One handoff succeeds automatically |

Whisper Chain is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. The cheater's team scores 0.

### Cheating dice example

Before the next game, Quill slips the barkeep 2 sp for the sentence.
- Quill: Persuasion **11** + 1 = **12**. The Watcher: **7** + 5 = **12**. **A tie goes to the Watcher. Caught.**
- Team Party scores 0 in the next game. Quill is banned from Whisper Chain for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 1 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The barkeep's sentences** are drawn from a jar. One slip says something that only one person in the city could know.
- **A team of four kenku** play every day, and always score a perfect chain. Nobody can work out whether that's cheating.
