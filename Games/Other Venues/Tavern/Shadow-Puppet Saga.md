# Shadow-Puppet Saga

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The lamps go down. A linen screen glows at the end of the hall, and behind it, dragons and heroes made of hands and cardboard come to life. Two minutes, one story. The best show of the night earns a month on this stage.*

**Description:** A monthly shadow-puppet show held as a ticketed dinner show. Each act performs a two-minute story behind a lit screen and makes **three checks**: one for the figures, one for the story and one for the timing. **Each success is 1 point.** The act with the most points wins, and ties go to the loudest applause.

## How to Play

### An act, step by step

Make all three checks. A troupe can split them up: each check can be made by a different member, but no one helps on another member's check.

| Check | What it shows | DC |
|---|---|---|
| **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** | The figures: clean shapes, smooth movement | 12 |
| **Charisma (Performance)** | The story: drama, voices, laughs | 15 |
| **Wisdom check** | The timing: lamp, music and cues all land together | 18 |

Each success scores **1 point** (0 to 3).

### Winning

- The highest score wins.
- **Ties: the applause.** Each tied act rolls **Charisma (Performance)** once more. The higher total gets the louder applause and wins. If they tie again, they share the prize.

## Dice Examples

**Acts:** Mirela (PC, solo: Sleight of Hand +2, Performance +7, Wisdom +1) and Lark Thistlewhite (NPC, solo: Sleight of Hand +5, Performance +6, Wisdom +0).

**Mirela: "The Bard Who Out-Sang a Dragon"**
- Figures (DC 12): **11** + 2 = **13**. ✓
- Story (DC 15): **6** + 7 = **13**. ✗
- Timing (DC 18): **17** + 1 = **18**. ✓
- **2 points.**

**Lark: "The Halfling and the Hungry Moon"**
- Figures (DC 12): **9** + 5 = **14**. ✓
- Story (DC 15): **12** + 6 = **18**. ✓
- Timing (DC 18): **10** + 0 = **10**. ✗
- **2 points.**

**Tie: the applause**
- Mirela: Performance **15** + 7 = **22**. Lark: **14** + 6 = **20**. **Mirela wins.**

**Edge case: a troupe**
- If Mirela had teamed up with Quill (Sleight of Hand +7), Quill could take the figures check and Mirela the story. Whoever has the best Wisdom would take the timing.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per act.
- **Winner:** 3 gp and a **month-long residency**: one show a week, paid 1 gp per show.
- **The House keeps:** the entries and the dinner-show ticket sales.

## Odds

**Chance to pass each check, and to score 2 or more:**

| Bonus on all three | Figures (DC 12) | Story (DC 15) | Timing (DC 18) | 2+ points | All 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +2 | 55% | 40% | 25% | 35% | 5.5% |
| +5 | 70% | 55% | 40% | 58% | 15% |
| +7 | 80% | 65% | 50% | 72.5% | 26% |

**Troupes do better,** because each check goes to whoever is best at it.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any story, any puppets, any music.
- Splitting the checks across a troupe of up to 3.
- Bringing your own cut-outs and props.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *minor illusion*, *dancing lights*, *prestidigitation*, *enhance ability* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A fourth troupe member hidden behind the screen, or sabotaging a rival's lamp.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Minor illusion* on the screen | A caster behind the screen | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on the figures check |
| A hidden extra puppeteer | Anyone | Dex (Stealth) | Advantage on the timing check |
| Trim a rival's lamp wick | Anyone | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival has disadvantage on the timing check |
| Planted cheering in the crowd (2 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Deception) | Advantage on the applause roll |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Shadow-Puppet Saga is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A troupe is disqualified together.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 10 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Lark trims Mirela's lamp wick before her show.
- Lark: Sleight of Hand **13** + 5 = **18**. The Watcher: **4** + 5 = **9**. Not caught.
- Mirela rolls timing with disadvantage: **17** and **6**, keeps 6: 6 + 1 = **7**. ✗ Mirela scores only 1 point, and Lark wins with 2.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **One shadow on the screen** moves when no puppeteer is touching it. It only appears during stories about the old war.
- **A noble patron** hires this month's winner for a private show, and wants a very specific story told to a very specific guest.
