# Candle Gale

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Four candles stand in a row down the long table: two feet away, four, six, eight. Take a deep breath and blow. Each one out takes you further. The one who blows out the farthest candle wins.*

**Description:** A daily lung-power game. Four candles stand at **2, 4, 6 and 8 feet**. Each player tries to blow them out **in order**, with breath alone. Each candle needs a harder **Constitution check**. **Your run ends at your first failure.** The farthest candle blown out wins.

## How to Play

### Your turn, step by step

1. **Blow out the candles in order**, with a **Constitution check** for each:

   | Candle | 2 ft | 4 ft | 6 ft | 8 ft |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | DC | 10 | 13 | 16 | 19 |

   - **Success:** it goes out. Try the next one.
   - **Failure:** your turn ends.
2. **Your score** is the farthest candle you blew out (or 0).

### Winning

- **The farthest candle wins.**
- **Ties:** each tied player makes one more Constitution check at the **10-foot candle** (DC 22). **The highest total wins**, whether or not anyone blows it out.

**Being Drunk hurts here:** if you're Drunk (see [[Drunk Track]]), you're poisoned, which gives disadvantage on Constitution checks.

## Dice Examples

**Players:** "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Con +4), Thokk (NPC, Con +3) and Mirela (PC, Con +1).

| Player | 2 ft (DC 10) | 4 ft (DC 13) | 6 ft (DC 16) | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oswin | **11** + 4 = 15 ✓ | **10** + 4 = 14 ✓ | **8** + 4 = 12 ✗ | 4 ft |
| Thokk | **9** + 3 = 12 ✓ | **13** + 3 = 16 ✓ | **7** + 3 = 10 ✗ | 4 ft |
| Mirela | **6** + 1 = 7 ✗ | | | 0 |

**Oswin and Thokk tie at 4 ft.** The 10-foot candle (DC 22):
- Oswin: **14** + 4 = **18**. Thokk: **17** + 3 = **20**.
- Neither blows it out, but **Thokk's total is higher. Thokk wins.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 sp per player.
- **The winner** takes the pot, minus 10% for the House, and a candle with their name carved in it.
- **The House keeps:** the 10%, and sells candle sets and birthday packages.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any breathing technique: deep breaths, short bursts, cupped hands around the mouth.
- Leaning as far as you like, as long as your feet stay behind the line.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *gust*, *prestidigitation* (which can snuff out a candle), *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Thin-wicked candles, a friend opening a shutter, or stepping over the line.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swap in thin-wicked candles | Anyone, before the turn | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | +2 to every check this turn |
| Open a shutter for a draught | Anyone near a window | Dex (Stealth) | Advantage on one candle |
| Hidden spell (*prestidigitation*, *gust*) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | One candle goes out automatically |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Candle Gale is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A candle put out by a cheat doesn't count.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 1 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Lark Thistlewhite has money on Oswin. On Oswin's 6-foot candle, Lark snuffs it out with a murmured *prestidigitation*.
- Lark: Deception **13** + 4 = **17**. The Watcher: **16** + 5 = **21**. **Caught.**
- The 6-foot candle doesn't count, so Oswin's score stays at 4 ft. Oswin wasn't in on it, so he isn't penalised. Lark is banned from Candle Gale for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 1 gp, 1 day serving (trimming wicks), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The 10-foot candle** has never been blown out by a fair breath. A storm giant once tried, and blew out the windows instead.
- **A sailor** wins every day, and swears he learned it calling the wind. When he plays, the sails on the harbour flutter.
