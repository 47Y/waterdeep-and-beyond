# Coin in the Tankard

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Bounce a silver piece off the table and into the tankard in the middle. Make it, and you choose who drinks. Make three in a row, and you make the rules.*

**Description:** A daily table drinking game. Players take turns bouncing a coin into a tankard. **Every success lets you name someone to drink** and go again. **Three in a row** lets you add a new table rule. Anyone can bet the House that they'll hit three in a row.

## How to Play

### Your turn, step by step

1. **Bounce the coin:** make a **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check at **DC 13**.
   - **Success:** name another player. They **drink** (Constitution save, DC 10). Then go again.
   - **Failure:** your turn ends. Pass the coin to the left.
2. **Three in a row:** after your third success in a row, you set a **table rule**. Anyone who breaks it drinks.
   - Example rules: "Drink with your left hand only." "Nobody says 'dwarf'." "Toast the barkeep before every sip."

### The side bet

Before your turn, you may bet **1 sp** with the House that you'll hit **three in a row**. The House pays by your Sleight of Hand bonus:

| Sleight of Hand | Pays | Sleight of Hand | Pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| −1 | 20 to 1 | +5 | 2 to 1 |
| +0 | 13 to 1 | +6 | 3 to 2 |
| +1 | 8 to 1 | +7 | 1 to 1 |
| +2 | 6 to 1 | +8 | 3 to 4 |
| +3 | 4 to 1 | +9 | 2 to 5 |
| +4 | 3 to 1 | | |

### Ending the game

The game goes on as long as people want to play. There's no winner, just whoever's still standing.

## Dice Examples

**Players:** Mirela (PC, Sleight of Hand +2, Con save +1), Thokk (NPC, Sleight of Hand −1, Con save +5 with advantage) and Quill (PC, Sleight of Hand +7, Con save +1).

**Mirela's turn.** She bets 1 sp on three in a row, at 6 to 1.
- Bounce 1: **15** + 2 = **17**. ✓ She names Thokk. He drinks: **6** and **12**, keeps 12: **17**. Pass.
- Bounce 2: **11** + 2 = **13**. ✓ She names Quill. He drinks: **4** + 1 = **5**. Fail: **Quill is Tipsy.**
- Bounce 3: **13** + 2 = **15**. ✓ **Three in a row!** She wins **6 sp** (and keeps her 1 sp stake), and sets a rule: "Nobody may say 'dwarf'."
- Bounce 4: **5** + 2 = **7**. ✗ Her turn ends.

**Thokk's turn**
- Bounce 1: **9** − 1 = **8**. ✗ "Blasted dwarf-hands..." He's broken the rule. **Thokk drinks.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **No entry.** The game is played for drinks.
- **The side bet:** 1 sp at the odds above.
- **The House keeps:** about 10% of everything bet on three in a row, and the drinks.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any bouncing technique.
- Choosing whoever you like to drink (yes, the same person every time).
- Dwarven Resilience on the drink saves.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *mage hand*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A lead-weighted coin, or moving the tankard.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weighted coin | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) to swap it in | +2 to every bounce |
| Nudge the tankard | Anyone at the table | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | One missed bounce goes in |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand*) | A caster at the table | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | One missed bounce goes in |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Coin in the Tankard is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A caught cheater's side bet is lost.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 1 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Quill bets on three in a row at 1 to 1, then swaps in a weighted coin.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **18** + 7 = **25**. The Watcher: **14** + 5 = **19**. Not caught.
- Quill bounces at +9 instead of +7. He makes his three easily and collects 1 sp, for what that's worth.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The table rules** pile up through the night. By closing time, the regulars have made a dozen, and a newcomer breaking all of them is the evening's entertainment.
- **One coin** keeps turning up in the tankard after closing, even though nobody's playing. It's very old, and it isn't from this city.
