# Bottomless Stein Course

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A brimming stein of ale and an obstacle course through the tavern: down the cellar stairs, along the rail, a pour through a keyhole, and out through the spinning door. Every slip spills ale. Finish with the most left in your stein, and you drink it for free.*

**Description:** A daily obstacle run. Each runner carries a **full stein** (four quarters of ale) through **four obstacles**. Each **failed** obstacle **spills one quarter**. **The runner with the most ale left wins.**

## How to Play

### Your run, step by step

Make one check at each obstacle, in order:

| # | Obstacle | Check | DC |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The cellar stairs | Strength (Athletics) | 10 |
| 2 | The rail walk | Dexterity (Acrobatics) | 12 |
| 3 | The keyhole pour (pour your ale through a keyhole into a second stein) | Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) | 15 |
| 4 | The spinning door | Dexterity (Acrobatics) | 18 |

- **Success:** no spill.
- **Failure:** you **spill one quarter** of your stein. Keep going.

### Winning

- **The most ale left wins** (4 quarters is a full stein).
- **Ties:** add up each runner's four check totals. The highest total had the fastest run, and wins.

## Dice Examples

**Runners:** Quill (PC, Athletics +0, Acrobatics +5, Sleight of Hand +7) and Thokk (NPC, Athletics +5, Acrobatics −1, Sleight of Hand −1).

| Obstacle | Quill | Thokk |
|---|---|---|
| Stairs (DC 10) | **11** + 0 = 11 ✓ | **7** + 5 = 12 ✓ |
| Rail (DC 12) | **9** + 5 = 14 ✓ | **15** − 1 = 14 ✓ |
| Keyhole (DC 15) | **10** + 7 = 17 ✓ | **14** − 1 = 13 ✗ spill |
| Spinning door (DC 18) | **12** + 5 = 17 ✗ spill | **19** − 1 = 18 ✓ |
| **Ale left** | **3 quarters** | **3 quarters** |

**A tie.** Quill's totals: 11 + 14 + 17 + 17 = **59**. Thokk's: 12 + 14 + 13 + 18 = **57**. **Quill wins** and drinks his three quarters for free.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 sp per runner (it pays for the ale).
- **The winner** takes the pot, minus 10% for the House, and drinks what's left in their stein.
- **The House keeps:** the 10% and the spilled ale's worth in drinks sold to the crowd.

## Odds

| Bonus | Chance of a full stein | Average quarters left |

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- One hand or two on the stein.
- Any route through each obstacle.
- Features that don't use magic, such as a monk's Step of the Wind.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *guidance*, *enhance ability*, *mage hand* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A wax film over your stein, tripping a rival, or skipping an obstacle.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wax film over the ale | Runner | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Your first failure spills nothing |
| Trip a rival | Anyone along the course | Dex (Stealth) | The rival has disadvantage on their next obstacle |
| Hidden spell (*enhance ability*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

As Thokk reaches the spinning door, Mirela sticks a foot out.
- Mirela: Stealth **16** + 2 = **18**. The Watcher: **11** + 5 = **16**. Not caught.
- Thokk rolls with disadvantage: **19** and **6**, keeps 6: 6 − 1 = **5**. He spills. Thokk finishes with 2 quarters, and Quill wins without needing the tie-break.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The spinning door** was put in by a gnome who claimed it would "keep the draughts out." It sometimes spins on its own.
- **The cellar stairs** have a loose step that the regulars all know about. Newcomers don't.
