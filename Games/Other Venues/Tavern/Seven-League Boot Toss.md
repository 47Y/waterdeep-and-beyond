# Seven-League Boot Toss

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The cobbler's sign hangs over the courtyard: a giant padded boot, big enough for an ogre. Throw it through the hoop hanging from the oak tree, thirty feet away and ten feet up. Three throws. Make one and the crowd roars. Make all three and you get boots of your own.*

**Description:** A weekly throwing contest sponsored by the local cobbler. Each thrower gets **three throws** of an oversized padded boot at a hoop hanging from a tree. First you need the **strength to reach** the hoop, then the **aim to go through** it. The most boots through the hoop wins.

## How to Play

### A throw, step by step

1. **Reach:** make a **DC 12 Strength (Athletics)** check.
   - **Failure:** the boot falls short. No score.
   - **Success:** go to step 2.
2. **Through:** make a **DC 15 Dexterity** check.
   - **Success:** through the hoop! **1 point.**
   - **Failure:** it bounces off the rim. No score.

### Winning

- Everyone throws three times. The most points wins.
- **Ties: sudden death.** The tied throwers each throw once. A score beats a miss. Repeat until one is left.

## Dice Examples

**Throwers:** Thokk (NPC, Athletics +5, Dex −1), Quill (PC, Athletics +0, Dex +3) and Mirela (PC, Athletics +0, Dex +2).

**Thokk**
- Throw 1: Reach **9** + 5 = **14** ✓. Through **15** − 1 = **14** ✗.
- Throw 2: Reach **12** + 5 = **17** ✓. Through **18** − 1 = **17** ✓. **1 point.**
- Throw 3: Reach **3** + 5 = **8** ✗.
- **Thokk: 1.**

**Quill**
- Throw 1: Reach **13** + 0 = **13** ✓. Through **12** + 3 = **15** ✓. **1 point.**
- Throw 2: Reach **7** ✗.
- Throw 3: Reach **11** ✗.
- **Quill: 1.**

**Mirela**
- Throw 1: Reach **6** ✗.
- Throw 2: Reach **15** ✓. Through **9** + 2 = **11** ✗.
- Throw 3: Reach **2** ✗.
- **Mirela: 0.**

**Sudden death: Thokk vs Quill**
- Thokk: Reach **16** + 5 = **21** ✓. Through **10** − 1 = **9** ✗.
- Quill: Reach **14** ✓. Through **13** + 3 = **16** ✓. **Quill wins.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 5 sp per thrower.
- **Winner:** 70% of the entries.
- **All three through the hoop:** a pair of fine boots from the cobbler (worth 5 gp), whoever wins.
- **The House keeps:** 30% of entries. The cobbler pays the House to hang their sign.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any throwing style: overarm, underarm or the spinning hammer throw.
- Rage and other features that don't use magic.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *gust*, *mage hand*, *enhance ability*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A boot stuffed with sand, or shaking the tree branch.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sand in the boot's toe | Thrower | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on every reach check |
| Shake the branch on a rival's throw | Anyone near the tree | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's through check has disadvantage |
| Hidden spell (*gust*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | One rim bounce falls through instead (a failed through check succeeds) |

The Seven-League Boot Toss is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

On Thokk's sudden-death throw, Mirela shakes the branch.
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **12** + 2 = **14**. The Watcher: **16** + 5 = **21**. **Caught.**
- Mirela is banned for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. (She was already out of the contest.) Thokk throws again. To clear her name: 5 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The cobbler, Mistress Hobb,** swears the giant boot is a real seven-league boot, just "worn out." Every so often, somebody throws it and it lands a mile away.
- **A giant's child** has wandered into town and wants a turn.
