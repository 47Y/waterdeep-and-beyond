# Spoon-and-Bean Relay

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *One dried bean, balanced on one wooden spoon, held in your teeth. Pass it down the line, over the benches, under the rope, and into the cup at the end. Drop it and you scrabble on the floor while the crowd shrieks with laughter.*

**Description:** A weekly team relay across the tavern floor. Each team of four carries a **bean on a spoon** through **four legs**, one runner per leg. Each leg has its own check. **Drop the bean and it costs 10 seconds** to pick it up and carry on. The lowest time wins.

## How to Play

### The legs

Before the race, each team decides **who runs which leg**.

| Leg | Obstacle | Check | DC |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The straight | Dexterity check | 10 |
| 2 | Over the benches | Dexterity (Acrobatics) | 12 |
| 3 | Under the rope and round the barrel | Strength (Athletics) | 15 |
| 4 | Into the cup | Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) | 18 |

### Running the race

1. Each leg takes **15 seconds**, so a clean race takes **60 seconds**.
2. Each runner makes their leg's check once.
   - **Success:** clean leg.
   - **Failure:** dropped the bean. **+10 seconds.** (They still finish the leg.)
3. Add up each team's time.

### Teams of fewer than four

A team of two or three can still enter. Some runners run two legs.

### Winning

- The lowest time wins.
- **Ties:** the tied team with the **higher leg-4 total** wins.

## Dice Examples

**Team Party:** "Fat" Oswin (leg 1, Dex +0), Mirela (leg 2, Acrobatics +2), Thokk (leg 3, Athletics +5), Quill (leg 4, Sleight of Hand +7).

- **Leg 1** (DC 10): Oswin **12** + 0 = **12**. ✓
- **Leg 2** (DC 12): Mirela **8** + 2 = **10**. ✗ **+10 seconds.**
- **Leg 3** (DC 15): Thokk **11** + 5 = **16**. ✓
- **Leg 4** (DC 18): Quill **9** + 7 = **16**. ✗ **+10 seconds.**
- **Time:** 60 + 20 = **80 seconds.**

**The Kitchen Crew** (NPCs) drop the bean once: **70 seconds. The Kitchen Crew win.**

**Edge case: a tie**
- If the Kitchen Crew had also taken 80 seconds, the team with the higher leg-4 total would win. Quill's 16 against the Crew's last runner.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per team.
- **Winning team:** 80% of the entries and a free dinner for the team.
- **The House keeps:** 20% of entries and the food sales to hungry teams.

## Odds

**If every runner has the same bonus:**

| Bonus | Leg 1 | Leg 2 | Leg 3 | Leg 4 | Clean race (60 seconds) | Average time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +2 | 65% | 55% | 40% | 25% | 4% | 81 seconds |
| +5 | 80% | 70% | 55% | 40% | 12% | 75 seconds |
| +7 | 90% | 80% | 65% | 50% | 23% | 71 seconds |

**Putting the right runner on the right leg** is most of the strategy. Save your best hands for leg 4.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any choice of who runs which leg.
- Holding the spoon in your teeth or your hand (the House allows either).

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *mage hand*, *guidance*, *enhance ability* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A sticky spoon, a bean glued to it, or tripping another team's runner.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Honey on the spoon | Runner | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on your leg |
| Trip another team's runner | Anyone on the course | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The runner has disadvantage on their leg |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand*, *guidance*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Spoon-and-Bean Relay is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A caught team is disqualified together.

### Cheating dice example

Before leg 4, Quill dabs honey on his spoon.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **19** + 7 = **26**. The Watcher: **20** + 5 = **25**. Not caught, barely.
- Quill rolls leg 4 with advantage: **9** and **14**, keeps 14: 14 + 7 = **21**. ✓ Team Party finish in 70 seconds and tie with the Kitchen Crew. Quill's leg-4 total of 21 beats the Crew's 17, so **Team Party wins.**

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The Kitchen Crew** are the tavern's own cooks. They practise every morning, and they've never lost at home.
- **A noble's children** have entered with a team of hired professionals. Their tutor is paying the House to make sure they win.
