# Foam-Beard Court

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *"The Court is in session!" Each contestant gets a fresh pint with a thick white head, and the Chief Justice announces the theme: "The Dwarf King!" Scoop, sculpt and shape the foam into a beard fit for royalty, then wear it before the Court.*

**Description:** A monthly comic contest over **three themed rounds**. Each round the Court names a famous figure, and contestants sculpt a beard from beer foam. **How good the beard is** scores 0 to 3 points, and **matching the theme** scores 1 more. Foam is fragile: a bad slip and the whole beard collapses. The highest total after three rounds wins.

## How to Play

### Each round, step by step

1. **The theme.** The Chief Justice announces a famous figure (for example, "the Dwarf King", "the Storm Giant", "the Archmage").
2. **Sculpt.** Make a **Dexterity check**. Add your proficiency bonus if you're proficient with **brewer's supplies**.

   | Total | Beard | Points |
   |---|---|---|
   | 9 or less | A sad dribble | 0 |
   | 10–14 | Respectable | 1 |
   | 15–19 | Magnificent | 2 |
   | 20 or more | Legendary | 3 |

   **Natural 1:** the beard **collapses**. 0 points this round, and no theme point.
3. **Match the theme.** If your beard scored at least 1 point, make an **Intelligence (History)** check at **DC 15**. On a success, you shaped it just like the real figure's: **+1 point**.

### Winning

- The highest total after three rounds wins.
- **Ties: the Court decides.** Each tied sculptor presents their beard with a **Charisma (Performance)** check. The highest total wins.

## Dice Examples

**Sculptors:**
- Mirela (PC, Dex +2, History +1, Performance +7).
- Thokk (NPC, proficient with brewer's supplies: Dex +1; History +0, Performance −1).

**Round 1: "The Dwarf King"**
- Mirela sculpts: **13** + 2 = **15**, magnificent (2). History: **9** + 1 = **10**. No theme point. **2 points** (total 2).
- Thokk sculpts: **16** + 1 = **17**, magnificent (2). History: **15** + 0 = **15**. Theme point! **3 points** (total 3).

**Round 2: "The Storm Giant"**
- Mirela sculpts: **18** + 2 = **20**, legendary (3). History: **14** + 1 = **15**. Theme point! **4 points** (total 6).
- Thokk rolls a **natural 1**. The beard collapses into his ale. **0 points** (total 3).

**Round 3: "The Archmage"**
- Mirela sculpts: **7** + 2 = **9**, a sad dribble (0). No theme roll. **0 points** (total 6).
- Thokk sculpts: **19** + 1 = **20**, legendary (3). History: **4** + 0 = **4**. **3 points** (total 6).

**Tie at 6: the Court decides**
- Mirela: Performance **10** + 7 = **17**. Thokk: **12** − 1 = **11**. **Mirela wins.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 5 sp per sculptor (the pints are included).
- **Winner:** 4 gp, and their **foam-bearded portrait** goes on the posters for the featured ale.
- **The House keeps:** the rest of the entries, plus a great deal of beer sold for "practice".

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Fingers, spoons and the House's sculpting sticks.
- Any interpretation of the theme, as long as the Court can recognise it.
- Proficiency with brewer's supplies.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *prestidigitation*, *mold earth*, *minor illusion*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Egg white or soap mixed into your foam, or jostling a rival's pint.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Egg white in your foam | Sculptor | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Ignore a natural 1 collapse once (reroll it) |
| Jostle a rival's pint | Anyone | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's sculpt check has disadvantage this round |
| Hidden spell (*prestidigitation*, *minor illusion*) | A caster, the sculptor or a friend | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on the sculpt check |
| Bribe the Chief Justice for the themes (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Advantage on every History check |

### Cheating dice example

Before round 1, Mirela tries to buy the themes from the Chief Justice.
- Mirela: Persuasion **4** + 5 = **9**. The Watcher: **10** + 5 = **15**. **Caught.**
- The Chief Justice bangs his gavel and declares her "in contempt of Court." Mirela is disqualified, banned for 3 months, and pinned to the notice board wearing a foam beard in the sketch. To clear her name: 5 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Chief Justice Barnabus Froth** takes the Court completely seriously. He was once a real judge and was thrown out for it.
- **A dwarf delegation** is offended by this month's theme, "the Dwarf King." They want an apology, or a rematch.
