# Blind Bard

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The bard plays a jaunty tune on her lute, but something's off. It's in a minor key, at half speed, with the chorus first. Somewhere in there is a song you know. Can you name it before she finishes?*

**Description:** A weekly music game. The house bard plays **three famous songs**, each disguised: wrong key, wrong speed, scrambled order. Listeners try to name each one. **Each song is harder than the last.** The most songs named wins a share of the pot and a private performance from the bard.

## How to Play

### Each song, step by step

1. **The bard plays a disguised song.**
2. **Every listener** makes an **Intelligence (History)** or **Wisdom (Perception)** check (their choice, for each song). They write their answer on a slate.

   | Song | DC |
   |---|---|
   | 1 | 12 |
   | 2 | 15 |
   | 3 | 18 |

   - **Success:** named it. **1 point.**
   - **Failure:** no point.

### Solving it for real

If the DM hums or plays a real tune at the table and a player names it **out of character**, they score that song without rolling.

### Winning

- The most points (out of 3) wins.
- **Ties:** the bard plays a fourth song (DC 20). Each tied listener rolls once more, and the **highest total wins**, even if nobody reaches 20.

## Dice Examples

**Listeners:** Quill (PC, Perception +6), Mirela (PC, Perception +3) and Thokk (NPC, Perception +1). Everyone uses Perception.

| | Song 1 (DC 12) | Song 2 (DC 15) | Song 3 (DC 18) | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quill | **8** + 6 = 14 ✓ | **10** + 6 = 16 ✓ | **9** + 6 = 15 ✗ | **2** |
| Mirela | **13** + 3 = 16 ✓ | **4** + 3 = 7 ✗ | **15** + 3 = 18 ✓ | **2** |
| Thokk | **11** + 1 = 12 ✓ | **6** + 1 = 7 ✗ | **12** + 1 = 13 ✗ | **1** |

**Tie at 2: a fourth song (DC 20)**
- Quill: **13** + 6 = **19**. Mirela: **17** + 3 = **20**. **Mirela wins.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 5 sp per listener.
- **Winner:** 70% of the entries, and **one free private performance** by the bard (normally 5 gp).
- **The House keeps:** 30% of entries and a full room every music night.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Choosing History or Perception for each song.
- Humming along, tapping your foot and arguing with your neighbour (but writing your own answer).

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *guidance*, *detect thoughts* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Copying another listener's slate, or bribing the bard for the set list.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copy a neighbour's slate | Listener | Dex (Stealth) | Use the neighbour's result instead of your own for one song |
| Bribe the bard for the set list (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Name all three songs automatically |
| Hidden spell (*detect thoughts* on the bard) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Name the current song automatically |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Blind Bard is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A bribed bard loses the job.

### Cheating dice example

On song 3, Thokk leans over to copy Quill's slate.
- Thokk: Stealth **14** − 1 = **13**. The Watcher: **7** + 5 = **12**. Not caught.
- But Quill noticed: Perception **11** + 6 = **17**. He beats 13 and **sees it**. He demands Thokk buy him a drink to keep quiet. Thokk pays.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Velisande the bard** can disguise any song so well that nobody has ever named all three. She says the song she keeps for the final round was written for a queen who never existed.
- **One song** she plays makes an old patron weep and leave. Next week, he's back, asking her where she learned it.
