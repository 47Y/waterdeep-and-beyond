# Brewer's Nose

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Six small glasses on a wooden paddle, no labels. One is the house lager. One is a dark stout from the hill country. And one, they say, is brewed by dwarves under a mountain and has never been sold above ground. Name them all.*

**Description:** A weekly blind tasting. Each taster gets a **flight of six unmarked brews**: two common, two regional and two exotic. Name each brew to score a point. The flight counts as one drink. The highest score wins a cash prize and the chance to brew a batch with the House.

## How to Play

### Step by step

1. **Taste each brew** and make a **Wisdom (Perception)** check. If you're proficient with **brewer's supplies**, you can make a Wisdom check with brewer's supplies instead.

   | Brews | Type | DC |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 and 2 | Common | 10 |
   | 3 and 4 | Regional | 15 |
   | 5 and 6 | Exotic or dwarven | 20 |

   - **Success:** you named it. **1 point.**
   - **Failure:** no point.
2. **Take a drink.** After the flight, make one **Constitution saving throw at DC 10** for the Drunk Track.

### Winning

- The highest score (out of 6) wins.
- **Ties:** the tied tasters get one more mystery brew (DC 20). The highest total wins.

### Drunk Track

![[Drunk Track#Rules]]

**Being Drunk hurts here:** a taster who is already Drunk when the flight starts has disadvantage on every check.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 5 sp per taster (the flight is included).
- **Winner:** 4 gp and a **guest-brewer slot**: they brew one batch with the House, sold under their name.
- **The House keeps:** the entries, and sells a great many flights to the crowd.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Sniffing, swirling, sipping and spitting.
- Proficiency with brewer's supplies.
- Water and bread between brews.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *guidance*, *detect poison and disease*, *identify* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Peeking at the barrels being poured, or bribing the server.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Watch the pour | Taster or a helper | Dex (Stealth) | Name both common brews automatically |
| Bribe the server for the list (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Name all six automatically |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Brewer's Nose is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|

### Cheating dice example

Quill bribes the server to slip Mirela the list.
- Quill: Persuasion **11** + 1 = **12**. The Watcher: **14** + 5 = **19**. **Caught.**
- Quill (the cheater) and Mirela (the accomplice) are both penalised: Mirela is disqualified, and both are banned for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear their names: 5 gp each, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The exotic brew** this week isn't on anyone's list. Brother Aldous turns pale when he tastes it: it's from his monastery's cellar, which was sealed a century ago.
- **A rival tavern** is sending tasters to steal the House's recipes, one flight at a time.
