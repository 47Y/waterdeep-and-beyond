# Ale Alchemist

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Each brewer gets a tasting flight: three cups, each hiding one secret ingredient. Name them. Then take what you've learned and blend a brew of your own. Win, and your ale is on tap all next month, with your name on the barrel.*

**Description:** A monthly brewing contest. Each contestant tastes **three mystery cups** and tries to name the hidden ingredient in each. Then they **blend** their own brew for the judges. **Each correct ingredient is worth 1 point, and a successful blend is worth 2.** The highest score wins, and the winning recipe becomes next month's special.

## How to Play

### Step by step

1. **Identify three ingredients.** For each cup, make an **Intelligence check** at **DC 12**. Add your proficiency bonus if you're proficient with **brewer's supplies**.
   - **Success:** you name it correctly. **1 point.**
   - **Failure:** no point.
2. **Blend.** Make a **Wisdom check** at **DC 15**, adding proficiency with brewer's supplies if you have it.
   - **Success:** the judges love it. **2 points.**
   - **Failure:** no points.
   - You blend whether or not you identified the ingredients.

### Winning

- The highest score (out of 5) wins.
- **Ties:** the tied brewer with the higher **blend total** wins. If they're still tied, they share the prize and the tap.

## Dice Examples

**Brewers:**
- Thokk (NPC dwarf, proficient with brewer's supplies: Int +2, Wis +3).
- Brother Aldous (NPC brewer-monk: Int +5, Wis +7 with brewer's supplies).
- Mirela (PC, not proficient: Int +1, Wis +1).

| | Cup 1 (DC 12) | Cup 2 (DC 12) | Cup 3 (DC 12) | Blend (DC 15) | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thokk | **15** + 2 = 17 ✓ | **8** + 2 = 10 ✗ | **11** + 2 = 13 ✓ | **13** + 3 = 16 ✓ | 2 + 2 = **4** |
| Aldous | **6** + 5 = 11 ✗ | **14** + 5 = 19 ✓ | **9** + 5 = 14 ✓ | **7** + 7 = 14 ✗ | **2** |
| Mirela | **18** + 1 = 19 ✓ | **11** + 1 = 12 ✓ | **3** + 1 = 4 ✗ | **19** + 1 = 20 ✓ | 2 + 2 = **4** |

**Edge case: a tie**
- Thokk and Mirela both score 4. The higher blend total wins: Mirela's 20 beats Thokk's 16. **Mirela wins**, to Thokk's loud disgust.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 2 gp per brewer (covers the ingredients).
- **Winner:**
  - 5 gp.
  - Their recipe is **next month's special**, named after them.
  - A royalty of **1 cp for every mug sold** that month (often 5–15 gp).
- **The House keeps:** the rest of the entries and the profit on the special.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Smelling, sipping, swirling and spitting.
- Any blend you like from the House's ingredient shelf.
- Proficiency with brewer's supplies (or cook's utensils for the blend, at the DM's call).

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *guidance*, *detect poison and disease*, *identify* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Peeking at the ingredient list, bribing the cellar-hand, or bringing your own ingredients.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bribe the cellar-hand for the list (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | All three ingredients identified automatically |
| Smuggle in your own ingredient | Brewer | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on the blend |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*, *detect poison and disease*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |
| Salt a rival's blend | Anyone | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's blend has disadvantage |

### Cheating dice example

Thokk, furious at losing the tiebreak, accuses Mirela of salting his blend. Nobody saw anything, so Mirela keeps the win. Next month, Thokk tries to bribe the cellar-hand.
- Thokk: Persuasion **15** − 1 = **14**. The Watcher: **12** + 5 = **17**. **Caught.**
- Thokk is disqualified, banned for 3 months, and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 20 gp, 2 days serving (scrubbing the vats), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Brother Aldous** brews for a monastery that badly needs the royalties. He'll make a deal with anyone who helps him win.
- **Last month's special** made a few drinkers glow faintly. The winning brewer swears they only used hops.
