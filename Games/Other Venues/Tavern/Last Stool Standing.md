# Last Stool Standing

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *New Year's Eve. A ring of bar stools stands in the middle of the floor, and on each one sits someone determined to stay there. No weapons, no magic, no feet on the floor. When the bell rings, the shoving begins.*

**Description:** A yearly free-for-all. Contestants sit on stools in a ring and try to shove each other off. Each turn you either **shove** someone or **brace** yourself. Anyone pushed off their stool is out. The last one sitting wins a brass stool with their name on it.

## How to Play

### Setting up

1. Every contestant sits on a stool in a ring. Everyone can reach everyone.
2. Everyone rolls **initiative**. Turns go in initiative order, round after round.

### On your turn, choose one

- **Shove:** pick any contestant. Roll **Strength (Athletics)**. They roll **Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics)**, their choice.
  - **Your total is higher:** they're **pushed off** and out.
  - **Their total is equal or higher:** they stay. (**Ties go to the one sitting tight.**)
- **Brace:** grip the stool. Until the start of your next turn, you roll **with advantage** whenever someone shoves you.

### Out of the contest

A contestant who is pushed off is out right away and doesn't take any more turns. No damage, just a bruised pride and a laughing crowd.

### Winning

The last contestant on a stool wins.

## Dice Examples

**Contestants** (in initiative order): Quill (PC, Athletics +0, Acrobatics +5), Thokk (NPC, Athletics +5, Acrobatics −1), Mirela (PC, Athletics +0, Acrobatics +2) and "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Athletics +2, Acrobatics +0).

**Round 1**
- **Quill** braces.
- **Thokk** shoves Oswin: Athletics **12** + 5 = **17**. Oswin defends with Athletics: **15** + 2 = **17**. **A tie goes to Oswin.** He stays.
- **Mirela** shoves Oswin: Athletics **14** + 0 = **14**. Oswin: **6** + 2 = **8**. **Oswin is pushed off. Out.**
- (Oswin's turn is skipped.)

**Round 2**
- **Quill** shoves Mirela: Athletics **18** + 0 = **18**. Mirela defends with Acrobatics: **9** + 2 = **11**. **Mirela is out.**
- **Thokk** shoves Quill: Athletics **10** + 5 = **15**. Quill is not braced any more (his brace ended at the start of his turn). Quill defends with Acrobatics: **13** + 5 = **18**. Quill stays.

**Round 3**
- **Quill** braces.
- **Thokk** shoves Quill: Athletics **16** + 5 = **21**. Quill rolls Acrobatics with advantage: **7** and **17**, keeps 17: 17 + 5 = **22**. Quill stays.

**Round 4**
- **Quill** shoves Thokk: Athletics **19** + 0 = **19**. Thokk defends with Athletics: **11** + 5 = **16**. **Thokk is pushed off. Quill wins!**

**Edge case:** if Quill had braced every turn, Thokk would need to beat Quill's advantage roll each time. Bracing only defends; at some point, you have to shove to win.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 2 gp per contestant.
- **Winner:** 50% of all entries and a **brass stool engraved with their name**, kept at the bar for their use only.
- **The House keeps:** 50% of entries, bar sales, and its cut of spectator bets.
- **Breakages** (tables, mugs, other patrons) are billed to whoever was shoved into them.

## Odds

**Chance a shove pushes the defender off**, by how much higher the shover's modifier is:

| Shover vs defender | Normal | Defender braced |
|---|---|---|
| 3 lower | 34% | 19% |
| Equal | 47.5% | 31% |
| 3 higher | 62% | 46% |
| 5 higher | 70% | 55.5% |

**Ties favour the defender,** so shoving an equal opponent fails more often than it works. Pick on the weakest, and brace when everyone's looking at you.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any target, any order. Alliances and betrayals are encouraged.
- Using whichever of Athletics or Acrobatics is better to defend.
- Rage, Action Surge and other features that don't use magic. (A barbarian's rage gives advantage on Strength checks.)

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *enlarge/reduce*, *enhance ability*, *guidance*, Bardic Inspiration or *thunderwave*.
- Hooking your feet around the stool legs, greasing your seat, or touching the floor.
- Anyone in the crowd pushing, tripping or yanking a stool.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook your feet on the stool | Contestant | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on defense for the rest of the contest |
| Grease a rival's stool | Anyone, before the contest | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | That rival has disadvantage on defense |
| Kick a stool from the crowd | Anyone in the crowd | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The target defends with disadvantage this round |
| Hidden spell | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Thokk hooks his boots around his stool legs in round 1.
- Thokk: Sleight of Hand **6** − 1 = **5**. The Watcher: **3** + 5 = **8**. **Caught.**
- Thokk is disqualified, banned from next year, and his portrait goes up on the Cheater's Wall. The town crier announces it at midnight, which ruins his new year. He pays double for drinks until he clears his name: 20 gp, 2 days in the stocks or serving, or a year.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Old Brannoc** has won the brass stool twenty times. He's eighty-four. Nobody has ever seen him shoved, and nobody has ever seen him shove either.
- **A visiting goliath** has entered this year. Every other contestant has quietly agreed to shove her first.
