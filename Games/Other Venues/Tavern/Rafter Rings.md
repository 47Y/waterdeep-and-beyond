# Rafter Rings

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Five sets of antlers hang from the rafters on chains, swaying gently, each one higher and further than the last. Five rope rings for a silver piece. Ring the antlers, and win a wooden token, a plush owlbear, or, if you ring all five, the giant one by the door.*

**Description:** A daily ring-toss game. For **1 sp**, you get **five rope rings** to throw at **five swaying antlers**, one ring each. Each antler is harder to hit than the last and **worth more points**. Your total score wins a prize.

## How to Play

### A flight, step by step

1. **Pay 1 sp** for a flight of five rings.
2. **Throw one ring at each antler.** Each throw is a **ranged attack**: d20 + your Dexterity modifier + your proficiency bonus (everyone counts as proficient with the rings).

   | Antler | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | AC | 12 | 14 | 16 | 18 | 20 |
   | Points | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |

   - **A natural 20** always rings the antler. **A natural 1** always misses.
3. **Add up your points.**

### Prizes

| Score | Prize |
|---|---|
| 10–19 | A carved wooden owlbear token |
| 20–29 | A plush owlbear |
| 30 (all five) | The giant plush owlbear, and your name on the board |

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any throwing style, from behind the line.
- Features that add to ranged attacks without magic, such as the Archery Fighting Style.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *mage hand*, *bless*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Weighted rings, leaning over the line, or a friend holding an antler still.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weighted rings | Thrower | Dex (Sleight of Hand) to swap them in | +2 to every throw this flight |
| Steady the antler chains (bribe the rafter-boy, 2 sp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Advantage on one throw |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand*) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | One miss becomes a hit |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Rafter Rings is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A caught cheater forfeits their prize.

| Fine | Or days serving the event |

### Cheating dice example

Quill swaps in weighted rings for his next flight.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **19** + 7 = **26**. The Watcher: **18** + 5 = **23**. Not caught.
- Quill throws at +7 instead of +5. His average score jumps from 13 to 16.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The antlers** came from a stag the owner shot in the Feywild. On the night of the full moon, they grow a little.
- **The giant plush owlbear** has hung by the door for a year, unclaimed. A child keeps coming in to look at it, with a silver piece every day.
