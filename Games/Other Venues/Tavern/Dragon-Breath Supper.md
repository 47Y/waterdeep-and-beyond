# Dragon-Breath Supper

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Six covered dishes, each one hotter than the last. The first smells of pepper. The last is glowing faintly. Eat all six without crying for milk and your portrait goes up on the kitchen wall.*

**Description:** A monthly spicy-food challenge. Diners eat **six courses**, each hotter than the last. Every course needs a **Constitution save**. Each failure earns a **flame token**, and three flame tokens means you've had enough. Eat all six with fewer than three tokens and you're in the running; the fewest tokens wins. Flame tokens are just wooden tokens: painful pride, no damage.

## How to Play

### Each course, step by step

1. **Eat the course.** Every diner still in makes a **Constitution saving throw** against the course's DC.

   | Course | DC |
   |---|---|
   | 1 | 10 |
   | 2 | 12 |
   | 3 | 14 |
   | 4 | 16 |
   | 5 | 18 |
   | 6 | 20 |

   - **Success:** no token.
   - **Failure:** take **1 flame token**.
2. **Three tokens:** you call for milk and you're **out**.
3. **Next course.**

### Winning

- **Diners who finish all six courses:** the one with the **fewest flame tokens** wins.
  - **Tied:** the tied diners eat the **Dragon's Tongue**, one last bite: a DC 22 Constitution save. The highest total wins.
- **Nobody finishes:** whoever lasted the most courses wins. If they went out on the same course, the higher save total on that course wins.

## Dice Examples

**Diners:** Thokk (NPC, Con save +5), "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Con save +4) and Mirela (PC, Con save +1).

| Course (DC) | Thokk | Oswin | Mirela |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (DC 10) | **11** + 5 = 16. Pass | **4** + 4 = 8. **Token** (1) | **13** + 1 = 14. Pass |
| 2 (DC 12) | **6** + 5 = 11. **Token** (1) | **15** + 4 = 19. Pass | **9** + 1 = 10. **Token** (1) |
| 3 (DC 14) | **14** + 5 = 19. Pass | **10** + 4 = 14. Pass | **7** + 1 = 8. **Token** (2) |
| 4 (DC 16) | **9** + 5 = 14. **Token** (2) | **12** + 4 = 16. Pass | **5** + 1 = 6. **Token** (3). **Out** |
| 5 (DC 18) | **15** + 5 = 20. Pass | **13** + 4 = 17. **Token** (2) | — |
| 6 (DC 20) | **12** + 5 = 17. **Token** (3). **Out** | **16** + 4 = 20. Pass | — |

**Oswin finishes all six with 2 tokens and wins.**

**Edge case: nobody finishes**
- If Oswin had also failed course 6, both he and Thokk would be out on course 6. Thokk's total was 17; Oswin's would be compared, and the higher total wins.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per diner (all six courses included).
- **Winner:** 5 gp, a free supper, and their **portrait on the kitchen wall**.
- **The House keeps:** the rest of the entries, plus a great deal of milk and ale sold to the diners.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Water, bread and deep breaths between courses. (Milk is only for those who are out.)
- Natural traits. Dwarven Resilience doesn't help: pepper isn't poison. Resistance to fire damage doesn't help either: the food isn't on fire, it just feels like it.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *resistance*, *bless*, *enhance ability* or *prestidigitation* to cool the food.
- Swapping your dish, hiding food in a napkin, or a sip of milk before you're out.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hide a mouthful in a napkin | Diner | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Pass that course automatically |
| Sneak a sip of milk | Diner or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on this course's save |
| Hidden spell (*prestidigitation*, *resistance*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on this course's save |
| Extra pepper on a rival's dish | Anyone near the kitchen | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's save has disadvantage this course |

### Cheating dice example

On course 4, Mirela (2 tokens) casts *prestidigitation* to chill her dish.
- Mirela: Deception **17** + 7 = **24**. The Watcher: **13** + 5 = **18**. Not caught.
- Mirela saves with advantage: **5** and **16**, keeps 16: 16 + 1 = **17**. Pass. She survives course 4.
