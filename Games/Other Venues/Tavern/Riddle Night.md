# Riddle Night

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The quizmaster rings a little bell and the room goes quiet. "Round one: Arcana! Easy, medium or hard?" Teams huddle over their slates, arguing in whispers. Somewhere at the back, a wizard is already scribbling.*

**Description:** A weekly team quiz with **five rounds**, one for each category. Each round, a team picks how hard a question it wants: an easy question is worth 1 point, a medium one 2, and a hard one 3. One team member answers, another can help, and if the players solve a real riddle at the table, they get it right automatically. The highest score after five rounds wins.

## How to Play

### The categories (one round each)

| Round | Category | Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arcana | Intelligence (Arcana) |
| 2 | History | Intelligence (History) |
| 3 | Nature | Intelligence (Nature) |
| 4 | Religion | Intelligence (Religion) |
| 5 | Local Lore (the city, its people, its gossip) | Intelligence (History) |

### Each round, step by step

1. **Pick a difficulty:**

   | Difficulty | DC | Points |
   |---|---|---|
   | Easy | 10 | 1 |
   | Medium | 15 | 2 |
   | Hard | 20 | 3 |

2. **Answer.** One team member makes the check. One other team member can **Help**, giving **advantage**.
   - **Success:** score the points.
   - **Failure:** no points.

### Solving it for real

The DM can ask a real riddle or trivia question (about the campaign world or the real one). If the players answer it correctly **out of character**, they succeed without rolling.

### Winning

- The highest total after five rounds wins.
- **Ties:** the tied teams answer one last hard question (DC 20). The highest total wins.

## Dice Examples

**Team "Bard and Burglar":** Quill (PC, Int +2 for every category) answers, and Mirela (PC, Int +1) helps.

- **Round 1, Arcana.** Medium (DC 15). Quill: **8** and **14**, keeps 14: 14 + 2 = **16**. ✓ **2 points** (total 2).
- **Round 2, History.** Hard (DC 20). Quill: **12** and **17**, keeps 17: 17 + 2 = **19**. ✗ (total 2).
- **Round 3, Nature.** Easy (DC 10). Quill: **5** and **11**, keeps 11: 11 + 2 = **13**. ✓ **1 point** (total 3).
- **Round 4, Religion.** Hard (DC 20). The DM asks a real riddle: *"What has roots nobody sees, is taller than trees, up, up it goes, and yet never grows?"* Mirela's player answers "a mountain." **✓ 3 points without a roll** (total 6).
- **Round 5, Local Lore.** Medium (DC 15). Quill: **3** and **9**, keeps 9: 9 + 2 = **11**. ✗ (total 6).

**Final:** Bard and Burglar 6, the Candlemakers' Guild 5. **Bard and Burglar win.**

**Edge case: a tie**
- If the Candlemakers had also scored 6, both teams would answer a DC 20 question. The higher total wins, even if neither reaches 20.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per team.
- **First place:** 50% of entries. **Second place:** 20%.
- **The House keeps:** 30% of entries and a full room of thirsty thinkers.

## Odds

**Average points per question, with Help:**

| Bonus | Easy (DC 10) | Medium (DC 15) | Hard (DC 20) |
|---|---|---|---|
| +2 | 0.88 | **1.28** | 0.83 |
| +5 | 0.96 | **1.6** | 1.53 |
| +7 | 0.99 | 1.76 | **1.92** |

**Medium is usually the best pick.** Only teams at +7 or better should go hard every round. Without someone to Help, the hard questions are much worse.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Arguing, whispering and drawing on the team slate.
- Any team member can answer any round.
- Solving a real riddle at the table.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *guidance*, *detect thoughts*, *legend lore*, *commune* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Notes or books, a fifth team member, or reading another team's slate.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| A crib sheet up the sleeve | Team member | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | +2 to every check in one category |
| Read another team's slate | Team member | Dex (Stealth) | Copy their answer: use their total instead of yours for one round |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*, *detect thoughts*) | A caster on the team or in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |
| Bribe the quizmaster (5 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Learn one round's hard answer in advance |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Riddle Night is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A caught team is disqualified together.

### Cheating dice example

In round 2, Quill sneaks a look at the Candlemakers' slate.
- Quill: Stealth **10** + 7 = **17**. The Watcher: **6** + 5 = **11**. Not caught.
- The Candlemakers' answer was a total of 21. Quill copies it: ✓ **3 points**, and Bard and Burglar win comfortably.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The quizmaster, Professor Inkwell**, writes every question himself. One of this week's answers is something nobody should know, and he's watching to see who gets it right.
- **The Candlemakers' Guild** take Riddle Night very seriously. Their captain has never forgiven a team that beat them on a technicality.
