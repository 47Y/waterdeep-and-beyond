# Hi-Lo Pint

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The barkeep slaps a d20 on the counter. Fourteen. "Higher or lower?" You say lower. She rolls: six. "Again?" Get five right in a row and the next pint's on the house, with silver on the side.*

**Description:** A quick daily guessing game at the bar. The barkeep rolls a d20 and shows it. You guess whether the next roll will be **higher or lower**. Guess right and keep going; a tie counts as wrong. **Five right in a row** wins 5 sp and a free pint.

## How to Play

### A run, step by step

1. **Pay 1 sp.**
2. **The barkeep rolls a d20** and shows it.
3. **Guess:** higher or lower.
4. **The barkeep rolls again.**
   - **Right:** the new number is now the one showing. Guess again.
   - **Wrong, or the same number (a tie):** your run is over.
5. **Five right in a row:** you win **5 sp** and a **free pint**.

### Best play

Always guess toward the bigger side. If the d20 shows 1 to 10, guess **higher**. If it shows 11 to 20, guess **lower**.

## Dice Examples

**Thokk** pays 1 sp.
- The barkeep shows **7**. Thokk guesses higher. She rolls **15**. ✓ (1 right)
- **15** shows. He guesses lower. She rolls **3**. ✓ (2)
- **3** shows. He guesses higher. She rolls **3**. **A tie is wrong.** Run over.

**Mirela** pays 1 sp.
- The barkeep shows **18**. Lower: **9**. ✓ (1)
- **9**. Higher: **14**. ✓ (2)
- **14**. Lower: **2**. ✓ (3)
- **2**. Higher: **19**. ✓ (4)
- **19**. Lower: **11**. ✓ (5) **Five in a row! Mirela wins 5 sp and a free pint.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Cost:** 1 sp per run.
- **Five right in a row:** 5 sp and a free pint.
- **The House keeps:** about 15% of what's played, over time.

## Odds

**Chance of guessing right, by the number showing** (guessing the best way):

| Showing | 1 or 20 | 5 or 16 | 8 or 13 | 10 or 11 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chance | 95% | 75% | 60% | 50% |

- **One guess on average:** 72.5%.
- **Five right in a row:** about **17%**, roughly one run in six.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Guessing whichever way you like.
- Stopping whenever you like (but only five in a row pays).

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *mage hand*, *prestidigitation* or *guidance*.
- Swapping the barkeep's d20 for a loaded one, or bribing the barkeep.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loaded d20 (rolls 10 or 11 often) | Anyone who can reach the bar | Dex (Sleight of Hand) to swap it in | Nothing for you, but every guess after a 10 or 11 is a coin flip: useful for ruining a rival's run |
| *Mage hand* on the die | A caster at the bar | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Turn one roll to any face |
| Bribe the barkeep (2 sp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | She "accidentally" rerolls one wrong guess |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Hi-Lo Pint is played in the open bar, so it uses the **public event** penalties. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A caught cheater loses their run and their stake.

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 1 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

On guess 4, Mirela slips the barkeep 2 sp to reroll a wrong guess.
- Mirela: Persuasion **15** + 5 = **20**. The Watcher: **11** + 5 = **16**. Not caught.
- The barkeep "fumbles" the die and rolls again. Mirela's run goes on.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The barkeep, Dasha,** has a die she's used for twenty years. Regulars say it likes some people more than others.
- **A traveller** has won five in a row seven times today. He's very polite and very pale, and the d20 always seems to land the way he says.
