There is also a second entrance/exit from the barn to the street that could be used to quickly take a carriage. We saw a guard enter the barn. There is at least 2 guards at each entrance. Twizzle sent a Paper Bird to Dick with the information we found.


**Court**
Lilith refused to answer questions without a lawyer and has been held in a cell all night.
Dick used Disguise Self to turn into his attorney friend, Richard Nixon. Richard is an elderly elf. He was let in to a private room with Lilith. Lilith asked if he was her lawyer and Dick replied in his real voice saying "I am" while winking. He then switched to using Richard's voice for the remainder of the conversation.
Lilith gave Dick a recap of what happened from the point she approached the gate until the present time. Dick told her not to answer any questions without him telling her to. She also told Dick that she has not been informed of the charges being brought against her. Dick asked for the list from a guard.
- Assaulting a noble (Elias Chadwell)
- Attempted burglary
- Disturbing the peace
- Assaulting a lord (Lord Cassalanter)
Dick requested sketches of both individuals to check if they were the ones who carried Lilith out of the villa.
The hearing is set for "2 hours from now".
He asked for the evidence they currently have.
- There is physical evidence (a stone)
- It is mostly testimony from the nobles
The Paper Bird arrived and Dick replied with his own bird explaining the charges and who it involved.
Dick's plan is to get the charges dropped to only Trespassing since Lilith never attacked either noble or lord and was not attempting to steal anything. He thinks disturbing the peace is a dumb charge since it should only be a charge when things happen in public.
