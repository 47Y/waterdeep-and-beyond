We started in The Yawning Portal with Dick and Cordan already seated as the others started arriving. Joe was sent by Saeth Cromley. Lilith was invited by Dick due to a previous connection. Mr T was sent by Melannor Fellbranch. Twizzle was sent by Obaya Uday.
Dick offered everyone a contract: "I will pay 100 gold to you for one season of work with the ability for a bonus depending on quality of your work." He later clarified that the work is "whatever I need as long as it does not seem morally reprehensible" and that he will provide any equipment that is required for the work. He paid half up front to each person who agreed. Joe was the only person who did not agree immediately.

We mostly followed the adventure's first chapter. During the brawl, Dick spoke up and attempted to end the fight immediately with words. The troll encounter ended quickly with everyone taking part other than Cordan who remained seated and asked Dick "do I have to?" when his turn came around and the fight was nearly over. Durnan, Yagra, and Lilith worked together to push the troll back into the well while it was unconscious instead of using fire or acid to kill it.

We found out the reason Dick gathered us was to help Volo who approached us and gave his quest. We accepted and after allowing people to gather any equipment they may need from their respective residences started the search. In the Dock Ward, we stopped at the bloody clash and Joe managed to use his position as a guard to get some extra information about what happened. Meanwhile, Twizzle snuck off and looked around Old Xoblob Shop.

At the Skewered Dragon, Dick and Twizzle worked together to get information from the patrons and we learned about Floon leaving with another fancy looking person (unnamed at the time). We also found out about the warehouse on Candle Lane.

The Zhentarim Hideout resulted in a fight that left 2 kenku alive to be interrogated. While Dick, Joe, Mr. T, Lilith, and Twizzle were questioning them, Cordan looked around for anything of note. He found crates of various mundane items and a hidden door. Behind the hidden door he found 4 unknown (to him) cities and 15 bars of silver. He decided to leave them behind for the future, closing up the door behind him. He also heard whimpering and found Renaer Neverember in a closet under the stairs. Dick recognized him after being called over.

Guards were called, the kenku were taken into custody, Renaer was taken home (his offer to join the party was denied), and the party moved on to the sewers using the information the kenku provided.

In the sewers, a short encounter with a gazer happened before a more prolonged search and fight with goblins, a mind flayer (the mind flayer placed a stone on a pedestal and disappeared via teleportation on its first turn), a half-orc, and an intellect devourer. Lilith went down during the fight and the intellect devourer attempted to take her body. It failed and was killed shortly after.

After the fight, Dick used Identify ritually to get information on the pedestal and stone left behind. As he picked up the stone, Cordan told him "Do not let curiosity become the hand that turns the final key” as he was inspecting the intellect devourer while the others explored. Floon was found and led out of the hideout. Cordan secretly stashed the intellect devourer's corpse for study later.

The party returned Floon to The Ywaning Portal to reunite with Volo. Volo then offered the deed to Trollskull Manor. After some negotiation from Dick and a tour, it was agreed that Volo would pay the fee to have the deed transferred. Twizzle used Divine Sense and discovered the spectral bartender.

The party decided to stay at Dick's manor for the night and split off into their own rooms, ending the session.

Between sessions, Cordan is using the 4 hours while the non-elves are still asleep to do a sort of autopsy on the intellect devourer. He doesn't have experience with doing so and doesn't expect to learn much. It is mostly because the creature is strange and he wants to see what he can find out.
