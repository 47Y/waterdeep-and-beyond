### 1. Home — Astraelith, the City of the Starbloom

- Cordan, age 130, was born and raised in Astraelith, a luminous Eladrin city nestled among the branches of an ancient World Tree.
    
- Constellations drift between the rooftops, and the night sky changes subtly with the emotions of the city's inhabitants.
    
- Astraelith's druids study both:
    

- The stars above.
    
- The flowers and plants below.
    

- Cordan grew up surrounded by people who treated the night sky as something worthy of study rather than merely something beautiful.
    

### 2. Family

- Father — Theron Vaelith  
      
    

- Astronomer and druid associated with Astraelith's Starbloom observatory.
    
- Patient, knowledgeable, and comfortable discussing celestial phenomena.
    
- Helped teach Cordan to distinguish meaningful patterns from coincidence.
    
- Still lives in Astraelith.
    
- Proud of Cordan's curiosity but may not fully understand where that curiosity has taken him.
    

- Mother — Maelira Vaelith  
      
    

- Gardener and druid who tends some of Astraelith's oldest starbloom gardens.
    
- More socially perceptive than Cordan.
    
- Frequently explained to Cordan why certain things he said or did were unusual.
    
- Encouraged him not to suppress his curiosity simply because other people found it strange.
    
- Still lives in Astraelith.
    

- Younger sister — Seris Vaelith  
      
    

- Around 90 years old.
    
- More socially graceful and conventional than Cordan.
    
- Cordan and Seris are close despite their differences.
    
- She is interested in his travels and probably knows more about him than anyone else.
    
- Could eventually become involved in his story if the DM wants to use her.
    

### 3. Cordan's Oddness

- Even by Feywild standards, Cordan has always been somewhat unusual.
    
- He:
    

- Notices details other people overlook.
    
- Asks questions other people consider strange.
    
- Sometimes becomes fascinated by seemingly insignificant things.
    
- Takes statements more literally than people expect.
    
- Treats strange phenomena as interesting mysteries rather than immediate dangers.
    

- Rather than learning to hide these traits, he learned to leverage them.
    
- People remember him because he is unusual.
    
- His questions sometimes cause people to reveal things they would otherwise keep to themselves.
    
- His strange observations occasionally earn him favors.
    

Example favor:

- Cordan noticed something unusual about a traveling merchant's behavior and questioned it until he uncovered that the merchant was being manipulated.
    
- His intervention saved the merchant from a significant loss.
    
- The merchant subsequently became someone willing to provide Cordan with information or favors.
    

Example trouble:

- Cordan once became interested in someone's suspicious behavior at a social gathering and asked increasingly direct questions.
    
- He unintentionally exposed something the person was trying to hide.
    
- He didn't initially understand why everyone was upset with him; from his perspective, he had simply noticed something strange and asked about it.
    
- Experiences like this taught him about social conventions without necessarily making him conform to them.
    

### 4. His Connection to the Stars

- Cordan became particularly interested in astronomy and the druidic traditions surrounding the stars.
    
- He was fascinated by the idea that celestial patterns could reveal things about events beyond ordinary perception.
    
- He began studying the relationship between stars, constellations, and omens.
    
- His interest eventually became more serious than simple curiosity.
    

### 5. The Starless Convergence

- Cordan witnessed a phenomenon known as the Starless Convergence.
    
- It occurs only once every several thousand years.
    
- The constellations of the Feywild began drifting out of alignment.
    
- Stars disappeared one by one.
    
- They were not extinguished; something appeared to be passing between them and the Feywild.
    
- During the convergence, the sky formed a vast dark shape resembling:
    

- An open eye.
    
- Surrounded by broken antlers.
    

- Cordan became convinced this was an astronomical omen rather than a natural celestial event.
    
- He had no explanation for what the shape represented.
    
- His curiosity turned into a need to understand what he had seen.
    

### 6. The Starroot and Elyndra Moonbough

- Near Cordan's home stood an ancient ash tree called the Starroot.
    
- The tree was inhabited and guarded by a dryad named Elyndra Moonbough.
    
- Cordan knew Elyndra before his eventual journey to the Material Plane.
    
- A cruel fomorian trapped a fragment of a fallen star in the tree's roots.
    
- The star's presence poisoned the grove with cold, colorless light.
    
- Elyndra could not leave the tree to confront the fomorian herself.
    
- Cordan followed the star's reflection through the Feywild and confronted the fomorian.
    
- He freed the fragment, but discovered that it had become tangled in the Starroot's heartwood.
    
- Using his knowledge of the night sky and old druidic rites, Cordan helped Elyndra guide the star back into the heavens.
    
- The Starroot bloomed for the first time in centuries.
    
- It produced a single living branch covered in tiny naturally glowing knots resembling constellations.
    
- Elyndra gave the branch to Cordan as a reward.
    

“Carry a piece of the Starroot, and wherever you walk, the heavens will remember the way.”

- The branch became Cordan's staff.
    
- It remains alive and connected to the Feywild.
    
- Its leaves change with Cordan's Eladrin season.
    
- On clear nights, its markings rearrange themselves into unfamiliar constellations.
    
- These constellations may reveal omens of events yet to come.
    

### 7. Leaving the Feywild

- Cordan eventually decided that the Starless Convergence could not be understood from the Feywild alone.
    
- He believed the Material Plane might provide information that was impossible to obtain in Astraelith.
    
- He left the Feywild approximately a year and a half ago.
    
- He intended his journey to be an investigation rather than a permanent departure.
    
- He still intends to return to Astraelith eventually.
    
- He considers himself a traveler, not an immigrant or permanent resident of the Material Plane.
    

### 8. Studying the Material Plane Sky

- One of the first things Cordan discovered was that the stars are genuinely different on the Material Plane.
    
- Some constellations are absent.
    
- Others are arranged differently.
    
- Some stars have no obvious counterpart in the Feywild.
    
- This confirmed that his journey was worthwhile.
    
- Cordan began recording and comparing the two skies.
    
- He keeps most of his research private.
    
- He has told some people why he is interested in the stars, but nobody knows the full significance of the Starless Convergence.
    

### 9. Waterdeep

- Cordan came to Waterdeep relatively early in his travels.
    
- Because of something he learned before or during his travels, Waterdeep became his primary base of operations.
    
- He has explored elsewhere on the Material Plane, but has returned repeatedly to Waterdeep.
    
- He has been back in Waterdeep continuously for approximately three months.
    
- He has spent roughly most of his year and a half on the Material Plane based in or around the city.
    
- He has learned enough about Waterdeep to function comfortably but still views it as a place he is studying.
    
- He uses his unusual nature to his advantage.
    
- Many people know his staff is from the Feywild, but almost nobody knows its true history.
    

### 10. Marnock Stonewhistle's Warning

- Before settling into Waterdeep, Cordan encountered a korred named Marnock Stonewhistle.
    
- Marnock knew something about Waterdeep and gave Cordan an unusually specific warning:
    

“Go to Waterdeep if you must, star-reader—but do not trust the city’s skyline. Waterdeep keeps more beneath its streets than it keeps above them. Its deepest road has no signposts, its oldest king has no grave, and its dark places remember every foot that falls upon them.  
When the well begins to laugh, do not climb down. When the stones whisper your name, do not answer. And if a door opens where there was only wall, close your eyes before you cross the threshold.”

- Cordan has kept the warning private.
    
- Nobody in Waterdeep knows about it.
    
- He does not yet know exactly what Marnock meant.
    
- The warning is one of the reasons Waterdeep became his main base of operations.
    

### 11. Meeting Dick Mixon

- Cordan first met Dick Mixon entirely by chance at a festival in Waterdeep approximately a year ago.
    
- They had no prior reason to know each other.
    
- The encounter was brief enough that Cordan did not initially consider it particularly important.
    
- Dick later heard about Cordan from one of the people he pays to identify interesting people in Waterdeep.
    
- The report specifically mentioned the strange wooden staff Cordan carried.
    
- Dick sought Cordan out.
    
- They began working together on a few minor matters over the following months.
    
- Their relationship has primarily been business.
    
- Dick's ambition to become Open Lord does not strike Cordan as strange.
    

- From Cordan's perspective, ambitious people seeking power and titles are entirely ordinary.
    
- Fey courts have taught him that political ambition is nothing unusual.
    

- Cordan has occasionally given Dick advice based on Fey court politics.
    
- Some of this advice has been useful.
    
- Some has required clarification because Waterdeep's political and social customs don't operate according to Fey conventions.
    
- Their repeated dealings have gradually started turning the business relationship into something resembling friendship.
    

### 12. Where Cordan Stands at the Beginning

- He has been on the Material Plane for approximately 18 months.
    
- He has been back in Waterdeep for approximately three months.
    
- He considers himself a traveler who is temporarily based there.
    
- He intends to eventually return to Astraelith.
    
- He continues to study the differences between the Feywild and Material Plane skies.
    
- He is still trying to understand the meaning of the Starless Convergence.
    
- He has a growing friendship with Dick.
    
- He knows Waterdeep contains secrets but does not yet understand the significance of Marnock's warning.
    
- He has deliberately kept the most important parts of his history private.
