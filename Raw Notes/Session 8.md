The session started with the ceremony for Dick taking over as Open Lord. The previous Open Lord gave a speech giving him a short introduction before he walked in past all the nobles in attendance. Dick used Disguise Self to appear slightly skinnier and with no visible blemishes. He followed protocol for the most part. He took a knee and placed the crown on his head. The crown is made of orichalcum with purple, blue, and red gems and named Crown of Silver Tongues.

"Good day Lords. Good day Ladies. I believe that this is going to be a monumentous day for this city. I have very big plans to turn this city into a sprawling hub of commerce and trade. I have ways to enrich our city; not just ourselves but the entirety of our population. I hope you guys can bare with me as I am still getting my feet wet but I do promise to make you all proud. Now if you would like to come up to see me feel free." *Gestures to Secret Lords, the party, then everyone else.*

Various Lords/Ladies approached and gave gifts to Dick. After them, Dick had the party members approach and gave each of them various gifts. After that, others were able to approach and give him gifts or make their names known.

After the ceremony, Dick and the party walked to The Black Key (the tavern/casino that Joe created during his downtime). The event had been advertised extensively so it became a sort of parade and everyone was encouraged to join in the festivities. This created a bit of a traffic jam but it wasn't too bad since the route and such was known ahead of time. Upon arrival at the Black Key, many people went to the nearby Market where banquet tables and various stalls were set up. Dick ensured that all food vendors were paid to provide food for free to everyone. Other stalls selling trinkets and such were not covered but did exist. Dick also paid any nearby taverns to shut down for the day, only allowing alcohol to be served at The Black Key.

This is the grand opening of The Black Key. Dick set up on the second floor in the VIP booth so people could come visit him with some amount of privacy without being completely secluded.

The Black Key has its own folder in the notes section with details provided by Joe's player. I don't know if it provides information about the tavern or inn sections of the business. Today's events are Sausage Roulette, Pit Fighting, Beholder's Eye Darts, and Lizard Races in the evening.

Cordan fought an NPC (bandit captain) and won by forfeit after a few rounds. In the middle of the fight, he managed to spot 2 people cheating while gambling and narratively used Faerie Fire to highlight them for security to deal with. Lilith fought a level 5 character I had created for a different campaign. It was extremely close but Lilith won with Massive Damage resulting in Dick jumping down from the VIP booth on the 2nd floor to cast Revivify. Joe and Twizzle fought, with Joe winning in a fairly domineering manner.

After Joe and Twizzle's fight, Lord Zulpair came up to Dick. This lord is the one who (according to what Twizzle has been able to find in her downtime) had her home destroyed in her backstory. His family runs a merchant shipping company that is quite profitable most of the year outside of winter. He showed interest in making shipping deals with Dick. Dick asked why he approached at the Black Key instead of the main ceremony. He claimed to have other things to attend to during the ceremony. Dick understood that to mean that he is a Secret Lord. Twizzle joined the group and glared at him after being told via a Subtle Spell Sending from Dick to "play it cool". She was a bit too aggressive so Cordan gave her a drink and subtly sent her to a nearby table to calm down. Dick asked the lord if he was completely above board and the lord took minor offense saying his business is beyond reproach. Dick said he would like to discuss more with him the following morning at Dick's estate. Dick asked him to bring shipping ledgers for the last year, hoping to find evidence of the attack on Twizzle's home/family.

Another big event hosted was Bagman's Bet, a game Cordan brought from the Feywild. It is designed as a way for the common folk to hear about and vicariously experience adventures they will never go on. A custom Bag of Holding has been blessed by Tymora, resulting in it producing a random item stored in the bag each time someone reaches in instead of being able to choose which item to take out. 100 items are placed into the bag with a set distribution(1 Legendary item, 3 Dangerous items, 7 Very expensive items, 10 Rare items, 19 Miscellaneous magical items, 20 Uncommon items, and 40 Mundane items). Bets can be placed on what category the next item will be from. The game runner reaches in and takes out an item (or slip of paper representing an item if the person who is providing the story does not want to hand the item over) and the story about where the item came from, why they have it, the journey its been on, or anything else exciting about it. The story may or may not be true but it is always plausible enough and entertaining for the viewers. While bets are made, the main goal of the game is to entertain and share adventures with those who will never have the opportunity to experience them.
