The party staked out Gralhund Villa before deciding to enter it. Cordan used Thieves' Tools to pick the lock on the gate and the party entered. They went to a side door and went inside. The room they entered looked to be a barracks or servants chambers of some kind. They went through a bit more of the house without seeing anyone until reaching the kitchen. From there they could hear noises upstairs and see some Zhentarim thugs in the next room. The party decided to sneak upstairs instead of dealing with the thugs.

Upstairs, the party made out the sounds of fighting on the other side of the wall. Joe kicked in a door to try to interrupt the fight but the door didn't lead to the room with the fighting. One of the thugs from the dining room downstairs came up to investigate. Dick told him we were part of the Zhentarim and were here to help. He managed to convince him and the thug lead the party to the proper area. Once shown upstairs to the correct location, the party ended up joining the fight and killing the Zhentarim thugs with the help of the Gralhund house guards.

Lilith, Mr. T, and Joe went downstairs to finish the last two thugs while the others stayed behind to chat with the lady of the house. Shortly after the fight started (18 seconds or so; 3 rounds of combat) Joe yelled about Lilith needing help so Cordan ran to the stairs and cast Healing Word. Lilith had been knocked unconscious by one of the thugs who then used his second attack to critically hit her for 2 failed death saves.

Dick continued the conversation with Lady Gralhund and learned that the Stone had already been sent off to its next owners, the Cassalanters. The city watch could be heard approaching so the party went off the back balcony to avoid being seen.

As the group left, Dick noticed some magic (using Detect Magic) coming from the barn. He determined they were horseshoes of speed but decided to leave them alone.

The party went to the Cassalanter Villa to hopefully intercept the stone before it arrived. While setting up for stealth, Lilith did poorly and decided to approach the guards at the gate directly instead. She was sent away. She then returned after disguising herself with some dirt and told the guards she had been sent to deliver the stone. The head servant, a well dressed tiefling, was fetched and brought Lilith inside. Only Cordan saw/heard any of the interaction between them and he didn't do anything immediately.

Lilith was taken to the cellar where the tiefling turned on her and asked forcefully how she knew about the stone. After handing him the random rock she had picked up to use as a stand-in, she tried casting Command to make him sleep. He made his save and instead they entered combat.

Somehow Lilith managed to kill him with only taking a single hit. He turned into his true form (a doppleganger) upon his death. Lilith then heard footsteps coming from a different direction than where they came from and hid in a nearby clothing chest. She was immediately found and survived multiple rounds of being attacked by Victoro Cassalanter, Ammalia Cassalanter, and an unnamed Noble. She eventually told them that one of her friends had bet her that she couldn't get into the Cassalanter house. Victoro told her they would turn her over to the city watch then and she agreed. She was bound and blindfolded then lead outside.

During the second fight, Cordan heard sounds that were concerning and called the party together. He offered to turn into a spider and take a look at what was happening. He did so after the party agreed and proceeded to follow where Lilith had gone.

As he entered the cellar, Lilith was being carried out by the three who had been fighting. Cordan decided that while they were distracted with the guards he could take a look at what happened. He sneaked in as a spider and stayed on the upper wall out of sight. He saw the dead doppleganger as well as a room with a painting of a holy symbol of Siamorphe (he didn't recognize the symbol but later drew it for the party. Mr. T, Dick, and Twizzle all recognized it). He didn't see anything of note before the Cassalanters returned (without the third noble). The two of them did not see him in his spider form so he was able to follow them to see more.

The two dragged the doppleganger body to the holy symbol where they pushed on the sun part of the symbol and a secret door opened up. Cordan followed them through to continue observing. They stored the body in one of several empty cells. They then went downstairs where they then went to a closet and collected red robes and devil masks that they put on. They went to a larger room that appeared to be an underground temple to Asmodeus based on the 30 foot tall statue of him. There were around a dozen other robed and masked figures in the room. After some brief greetings from the two newcomers, the whole group started chanting in a language Cordan didn't recognize (though he assumes Infernal due to the connection to Asmodeus) and he started backing away. When the chanting was finished, Victoro said "Founder's Day Approaches" in Common and the group cheered.

Cordan decided to leave before the robed people left and caught him. Instead of opening the secret door, he used Fey Step to teleport through the small gap of the imperfect seal. He then left the villa before returning to his eladrin form and meeting up with the rest of the party.

While he was gone, Lilith was taken by the city guards. The remainder of the party did not go with Lilith, knowing where she was being taken and that they would be of no use without information Cordan may be gathering.

Cordan led the group back to Mixon Manor before speaking about what he found. While they had a very late (around midnight) dinner, he told the group what he had discovered and drew sketches of the holy symbol he didn't recognize as well as the masks and amulets the robed figures were wearing. He also said that in the morning he would draw a map of the rooms he had explored (using his Eladrin trance trait to gain proficiency in Cartographer's Tools during the night).

Before resting, Dick suggested that he can go to the guards/courts in the morning to attempt to get Lilith sorted out while the rest of the group return to the villa to attempt to gather evidence. The group didn't come to a decision about how they might go about getting access to the villa to get the doppleganger body.

During the long rest, everyone except Lilith leveled up to level 4. Lilith might level up as well but if so it will happen between sessions.
