The party had a meeting with some of the guilds to negotiate pricing. Dick managed to convince them that due to Cordan's assistance, the price should be reduced to 1000gp total. As the meeting was coming to a close, an explosion went off outside. After some investigation, the party discovered dead bodies with Zhentarim tattoos and the cause of the explosion was a Fireball spell. A gnome that was dead had dried waste on his boots which led the party to the conclusion that he was recently in the sewers. Twizzle found 5 gems worth 100gp in their pockets.

Dick, using Detect Magic, detects something magical in a rain barrel. It is a necklace with a broken clasp and 2 orange beads (the players know this is a Necklace of Fireballs but the characters do not currently). He pockets it into his magical jacket (modified version of a handy haversack) without anyone noticing.

The investigation of the incident was taken over by Barnabus Blastwind.

Dick went inside to identify the gems to see if they are more than basic jewels. They are fairly high quality but other than that there is nothing of note from them.

Joe asked Cromley if Fireballs are being used in multiple attacks around the city or just this one. He doesn't answer, partly because we were in the area of the attack and as such are suspects by default.

Joe and Lilith go to talk to the neighbors to get more information.
- Fala Legaliir, owner of Corellon's Crown (apothecary): "I was watering plants in the greenhouse on the second floor of my shop when the blast blew out some of the windows. Lucky I wasn't injured! Through the smoke, I saw a cloaked man take something from the body of a dead gnome, then start limping away. He was badly burned and casting glances over his shoulder, like he was afraid someone might be following him. He was headed toward the Bent Nail."
- Jezrynne Hornraven, a noblewoman leaving Tiger's Eye (private investigator's office): "I tell you, it was not a man. More like a puppet shaped like a man. A puppet without strings. It was on the rooftop. It hurled something into the crowd below that caused the explosion. I saw those halflings burned alive! I saw them!"

Dick takes the party to the second story of the tavern to have a private conversation. He pulled out the necklace and asked if anyone knew what it is. Twizzle recognized it as a Necklace of Fireball. Dick asked if he should turn it into the guards or search for the merchant who sold it. Cordan mentioned that withholding it is a crime against the city and Dick decided to turn in the gems and the necklace.

Dick asked the guards for a copy of the sketch artist's depiction of the puppet shaped like a man that was spotted on the roof. They plan to cast Speak With Dead to get information from the corpses. Dick asked to be there when it happens. The whole party was invited to witness the interrogation.

The party was given a copy of the sketch of the puppet. The closest resemblance is an automaton from the Day of Wonders parade, supplied by the Temple of Gond.
