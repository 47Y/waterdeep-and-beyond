# Session 2 Recap

Full notes: [[Session 2]] · All recaps: [[Recaps]]

- **Blackstaff summons:** [[Vajra Safahr]] invited the party to join the [[Gray Hands]]. [[Joe Bauers]], [[Lilith D Gluv]], and [[Twizzle]] accepted; [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] sought the Emerald Enclave's advice first.
- **Manor ownership:** The party collected the [[Trollskull Manor]] deed. [[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]] agreed to credit them in his book and provide an advance copy in exchange for studying the manor's [[Spectral Bartender|poltergeist]].
- **Hlam's warning:** After a difficult climb up [[Mount Waterdeep]], [[Hlam]] warned: "Evil's twin hides its face for now, expect that to change before winter's end." He welcomed Tathlynd to return.
- **Personal inquiries:** Twizzle raised guard corruption with Vajra. Lilith sought information about [[Damien L Gluv]] and later learned he had transferred to [[Starshine Academy]] in Neverwinter about seven months earlier.
- **Warehouse loot:** [[Dick Mixon]] and [[Cordan]] returned to the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]], killed its two guards, and recovered the hidden crates. Dick fenced the contents for **1,050 gp**, with proceeds shared among the party.
- **Scarecrow assignment:** A talking cat summoned Tathlynd to [[Phaulkonmere]]. The [[Emerald Enclave]] asked the party to stop animated scarecrows terrorizing farms. The party defeated three hostile constructs and learned the family had bought them from traveling merchant [[Fenrick Loin]].
- **Tavern supply lead:** The grateful family offered cider. The party proposed sourcing cider and hard cider for the tavern, and Dick planned to negotiate exclusive rights to sell [[Snobeedle Cider]].
- **Faction ties:** Cordan, Twizzle, and Joe joined the Emerald Enclave. Melannor confirmed Tathlynd could also join the Gray Hands, which Tathlynd planned to do later.
- **Level and downtime:** The party reached **level 3** and had two weeks of downtime. Cordan helped renovate the manor and earned guild invitations; Dick bought magic items and marketed the opening; Joe earned 200 gp pit fighting; the others pursued research, books, herbs, and another visit to Hlam.
- **Renovation estimate:** The manor's cost was established as **1,000 gp plus 250 gp in guild fees**. The talking cat's identity remained unknown.
