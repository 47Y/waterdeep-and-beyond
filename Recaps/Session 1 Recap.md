# Session 1 Recap

Full notes: [[Session 1]] · All recaps: [[Recaps]]

- **Party formation:** At [[The Yawning Portal]], [[Dick Mixon]] offered the group 100 gp each for a season of work, with half paid in advance to those who agreed. [[Joe Bauers]] did not immediately accept.
- **Tavern trouble:** A brawl was interrupted by a troll. The party helped fight it, while [[Cordan]] remained seated. [[Durnan]], [[Yagra]], and [[Lilith D Gluv]] pushed the unconscious troll back into the well.
- **Volo's request:** [[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]] hired the party to find [[Floon Blagmaar]]. Their Dock Ward investigation led through [[The Skewered Dragon]] to a warehouse on Candle Lane.
- **Warehouse rescue:** The party defeated kenku at the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]] and found [[Renaer Neverember]]. Cordan also discovered hidden silver bars and paintings, leaving them for later. The City Watch took the surviving kenku into custody, and Renaer was escorted home.
- **Sewer battle:** Following the kenku's information, the party entered the sewers and fought through the [[Xanathar Hideout]]. A mind flayer teleported away; Lilith fell unconscious, but an intellect devourer's attempt to take over her body failed.
- **Recovered objects:** Dick identified a [[Unknown Stone and Pedestal|stone and pedestal]] that together teleport one person to an unknown destination. He kept the stone. Cordan secretly collected the intellect devourer's corpse for study.
- **Reward:** The party rescued Floon and accepted the deed to [[Trollskull Manor]] as Volo's payment, with Volo covering the transfer fee. [[Twizzle]] detected a [[Spectral Bartender|spectral bartender]] there.
- **Session end:** The party stayed at [[Dick's Manor]]. Cordan began examining the intellect devourer's corpse while the others slept.
