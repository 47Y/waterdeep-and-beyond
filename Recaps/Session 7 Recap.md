# Session 7 Recap

Full notes: [[Session 7]] · All recaps: [[Recaps]]

- **Divine appeals:** The party sought help for the [[Cassalanter Children|Cassalanter twins]]. [[Waukeen]], [[Tyr]], and [[Hadar]] declined or failed to help, while [[Eilistraee]] promised to try to claim the twins' souls.
- **Osvaldo Cassalanter:** The party learned that the Cassalanters' missing older son, [[Osvaldo Cassalanter]], had been transformed into a chain devil and chained in the villa attic.
- **Founders' Day:** [[Elzerina Cassalanter]] and [[Terenzio Cassalanter]] suffered an apparent supernatural attack. The party rushed them to the [[Temple of Eilistraee]] and joined the effort to protect them.
- **Twins saved:** Eilistraee prevailed in the struggle over the twins, saving their souls from [[Asmodeus]].
- **Six-month downtime:** The planned downtime was completed. All proposed activities are believed to have occurred, and Cordan worked with [[Laeral Silverhand]] at various points to study the stars.
- **Campestri rescue:** At [[Hlam]]'s request, the party recovered all eight [[Campestri]] choir members associated with choirmaster [[Gwendolan]]. They were not slaves. The party defeated a [[Giant Snail|giant snail]], a wyvern, and bandits along the way.
- **Wyvern hide:** The party retained approximately 150 pounds of [[Wyvern Hide]].
- **Employment offer:** Dick paid the party for their time working for him and offered another year of employment; the amount, terms, and acceptance were not recorded.
- **Replacement weapons:** Lilith and Joe permanently surrendered their greataxe and spear during the prayers, then obtained replacements.
- **New Open Lord:** After interviewing with the [[Lords of Waterdeep]], [[Dick Mixon]] became Open Lord. [[Laeral Silverhand]] now serves as an advisor; no other role was disclosed.
- **Unresolved:** Archbishop Francis's name and title, Gwendolan's name spelling, and Dick's payment and employment offer.
