# Session 7 Recap

Full notes: [[Session 7]] · All recaps: [[Recaps]]

- **Divine appeals:** The party sought help for the [[Cassalanter Children|Cassalanter twins]]. [[Waukeen]], [[Tyr]], and [[Hadar]] declined or failed to help, while [[Eilistraee]] promised to try to claim the twins' souls.
- **Older Cassalanter son:** The party learned that a chain devil chained in the villa attic was the Cassalanters' missing older son. His name was recorded as “El Waldo,” but the spelling and exact circumstances remain unconfirmed.
- **Founders' Day:** [[Elzerina Cassalanter]] and [[Terenzio Cassalanter]] suffered an apparent supernatural attack. The party rushed them to the [[Temple of Eilistraee]] and joined the effort to protect them.
- **Twins saved:** Eilistraee prevailed in the struggle over the twins, saving their souls from [[Asmodeus]].
- **Downtime began:** A downtime period occurred, though its duration and whether it was the planned six-month downtime are unconfirmed.
- **Campestri rescue:** At [[Hlam]]'s request, the party recovered all eight missing [[Campestri]] associated with choirmaster [[Gwendolan]], defeating a giant mollusk-like creature, a wyvern, and bandits along the way.
- **Wyvern hide:** The party retained approximately 150 pounds of [[Wyvern Hide]].
- **Employment offer:** Dick paid the party for their time working for him and offered another year of employment; the amount, terms, and acceptance were not recorded.
- **New Open Lord:** [[Dick Mixon]] became Open Lord of [[Waterdeep]]. The transition details and [[Laeral Silverhand]]'s new role were not recorded.
- **Unresolved:** The older son's correct name and condition, Archbishop Francis's name and title, surrendered weapons, downtime duration and outcomes, Gwendolan's relationship to the Campestri, the mollusk's identity, Dick's payment and employment offer, and the Open Lord transition.
