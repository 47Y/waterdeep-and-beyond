# Session 5 Recap

Full notes: [[Session 5]] · All recaps: [[Recaps]]

- **Lilith released:** [[Dick Mixon]] impersonated his attorney friend to help [[Lilith D Gluv]], who faced charges including burglary and assaulting nobles. After challenging the investigators' reliance on noble testimony and revealing his identity, Dick persuaded the authorities to drop charges on both sides and left with her.
- **Following the Cassalanters:** The others found increased security at [[Cassalanter Villa]] and followed its carriage to the [[City of the Dead]]. In spider form, [[Cordan]] overheard a tiefling questioning a woman about the Stone, learned it had gone to a southern-ward windmill, and later found two dead men in red robes.
- **Stone recovered:** Reunited at the [[Abandoned Windmill|abandoned windmill]], the party defeated or captured the cultists, and Dick took the [[Stone of Golorr]]. Three spined devils fled through a window, but the party caught and killed them. Cordan and Lilith each took a corpse for later examination.
- **Cult revelations:** Captives [[Arn Xalrondar]] and [[Seffia Naelryke]] said Lord Cassalanter ordered killings and that the cult kidnapped destitute people for sacrifice. They reported plans for [[Founders' Day]], one tenday away, and rumors that the Cassalanter children were endangered by a deal with [[Asmodeus]]. Both agreed to surrender to the City Watch.
- **Familiars and the Stone:** Cordan and Dick each summoned a Fey spider familiar using the [[Ring of Spell Storing]]. Dick identified and attuned to the Stone, a sentient transformed aboleth. It granted Lightning Bolt but caused blindness whenever he was more than ten feet away from it.
- **Vault location and keys:** The Stone placed the [[Vault of Dragons]] beneath the [[Old Tower]] and named three keys: **a silvered warhammer, sunlight, and a unicorn**. The party obtained a decorative silvered hammer from [[Steam and Steel]], assembled twenty mirrors, and bought a toy unicorn at [[Old Xoblob Shop]].
- **Opening the Vault:** Beneath the tower, the hammer and toy unicorn satisfied two locks. After nine hours arranging mirrors and a rest nearby, sunrise supplied the final key and opened the Vault.
- **Vault exploration:** The party navigated false doors and damaged bridges, freed Tathlynd from an enthralling fresco, and recovered treasure including a [[Ring of Warmth]]. A magical forge granted several members temporary hit points. Tathlynd opened another door by confessing in Dwarvish that he had attempted to poison the matron.
- **Aurinax's bargain:** [[Aurinax]], an adult gold dragon disguised as an elderly dwarf, guarded the gold for [[Dagult Neverember]]. Dick persuaded him to submit to Zone of Truth and release the gold for its return to Waterdeep, along with thirty gemstones worth 100 gp each.
- **Gold divided:** Of the **500,000 gp**, each party member received **20,000 gp**, **120,000 gp** was reserved for business expenses, and **260,000 gp** was set aside for return to Waterdeep. Dick retained Aurinax's gemstones; the recovered side-room treasure was stored beneath [[Dick's Manor]].
- **Session end:** Cordan examined his spined-devil corpse and learned that it had twelve projectile tail spines that later regrow. After a long rest, **every party member reached level 5**.
- **Unresolved:** The mausoleum tiefling's identity, why only two bodies were found despite the captives reporting three killings, and what happened to the devil corpse carried by Lilith.
