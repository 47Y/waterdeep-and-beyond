# Session 4 Recap

Full notes: [[Session 4]] · All recaps: [[Recaps]]

- **Gralhund infiltration:** The party staked out [[Gralhund Villa]], entered quietly, and discovered fighting inside. [[Dick Mixon]] convinced a Zhentarim thug that they were reinforcements, then the party helped the house guards defeat the attackers.
- **Lilith rescued:** [[Lilith D Gluv]] fell unconscious fighting downstairs and suffered two failed death saves after another blow. [[Joe Bauers]] called for help, and [[Cordan]] revived her with Healing Word.
- **Stone lead:** [[Yalah Gralhund]] claimed the [[Stone of Golorr]] had been sent to the Cassalanters. The party escaped as the City Watch approached. Dick identified [[Horseshoes of Speed]] in the barn but left them behind.
- **Cassalanter entry:** At [[Cassalanter Villa]], Lilith claimed she was delivering the Stone and was taken to the cellar by a servant. Her ordinary-rock decoy led to a confrontation; she killed the servant, who reverted to a [[Cassalanter Doppelganger|doppelganger]].
- **Lilith detained:** [[Victoro Cassalanter]], [[Ammalia Cassalanter]], and an unidentified noble found Lilith and fought her. Victoro decided to surrender her to the City Watch, and she was bound, blindfolded, and taken into custody.
- **Cordan's reconnaissance:** In spider form, Cordan followed the Cassalanters through a secret door concealed behind a holy-symbol painting. They moved the doppelganger's body into a cell, donned red robes and devil masks, and joined around a dozen similarly dressed figures in an underground chamber resembling a temple to [[Asmodeus]].
- **Ritual discovery:** The group chanted in an unidentified language. Cordan suspected Infernal but could not verify it. Victoro declared, "Founder's Day approaches," and the gathering cheered.
- **Evidence recorded:** Cordan escaped and reported his findings at [[Dick's Manor]]. Tathlynd, Dick, and Twizzle identified the painting's symbol as [[Siamorphe]]. Cordan completed a [[Cordan's Map of Cassalanter Villa|map of the explored rooms]] the next morning.
- **Next steps and level-up:** Dick proposed approaching the guards or courts to help Lilith while the others sought evidence at the villa. Everyone except Lilith reached **level 4**; her possible level-up was deferred.
- **Unresolved:** Lilith's holding location and charges, how to regain access to the villa, the Founder's Day plans, the ritual's language, the unidentified noble, and whether the Cassalanters currently possess the Stone.
