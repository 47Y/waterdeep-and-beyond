# Session 8 Recap

Full notes: [[Session 8]] · All recaps: [[Recaps]]

- **Open Lord ceremony:** At the [[Palace of Waterdeep]], [[Laeral Silverhand]] introduced [[Dick Mixon]] before Waterdeep's nobles. Dick donned the [[Crown of Silver Tongues]] and promised to make the city a center of commerce and broadly shared prosperity.
- **Ceremonial gifts:** Dick gave each companion an [[Earring of Message]] and two [[Clockwork Amulet|Clockwork Amulets]]. [[Cordan]] also received a [[Survival Mantle]]. Joe's additional personal gift and the nobles' gifts to Dick remain unidentified.
- **The Black Key opens:** The ceremony became a parade to [[The Black Key]], [[Joe Bauers]]' tavern, casino, and arena. Dick funded free food at the nearby [[Market]] and paid nearby taverns to close for the day.
- **Arena results:** [[Cordan]] won by forfeit against a bandit captain and marked two gambling cheats for security. [[Lilith D Gluv]] narrowly defeated [[Paradox]], a Warforged Chronurgy wizard, on whom Dick cast Revivify. Joe decisively defeated [[Twizzle]].
- **Lord Zulpair:** Twizzle's downtime investigation connected [[Lord Zulpair]] to the destruction of her home and attack on her family. Dick arranged a first-light meeting and requested a year of shipping ledgers.
- **Possible concealed Lord:** Dick suspects Zulpair is one of Waterdeep's concealed Lords because he missed the investiture ceremony, but this is unconfirmed.
- **Bagman's Bet:** Cordan hosted [[Bagman's Bet]]. Dick drew a spyglass, Cordan weaver's tools, Twizzle a Wand of Fireballs, Joe an Alchemy Jug, and Lilith a Ring of Spell Storing. The bag remains with The Black Key and is not readily accessible outside the event.
- **Laeral's Cubic Gate:** Laeral shared the story of [[Laeral's Cubic Gate]]. Cordan noticed a hooded observer showing unusual interest and wrote a description to give Laeral.
- **Next:** The party returned to [[Dick's Manor]] to prepare for Zulpair's arrival at first light.
- **Pending details:** The crown's statblock and The Black Key's tavern, inn, and guest-room specifics have not yet been provided.
- **Unresolved:** The nobles' gifts, Joe's additional gift, Bagman's Bet stories and item retention, Zulpair's status and ledgers, and the hooded observer.
