# Session 8 Recap

Full notes: [[Session 8]] · All recaps: [[Recaps]]

- **Open Lord ceremony:** [[Laeral Silverhand]] introduced [[Dick Mixon]] before Waterdeep's nobles. Dick donned the [[Crown of Silver Tongues]] and promised to make the city a center of commerce and broadly shared prosperity.
- **Ceremonial gifts:** Nobles presented Dick with gifts, and Dick gave gifts to each party member. The individual gifts were not recorded.
- **The Black Key opens:** The ceremony became a parade to [[The Black Key]], [[Joe Bauers]]' tavern, casino, and arena. Dick funded free food at the nearby [[Market]] and paid nearby taverns to close for the day.
- **Arena results:** [[Cordan]] won by forfeit against a bandit captain and marked two gambling cheats for security. [[Lilith D Gluv]] narrowly defeated an unnamed opponent, on whom Dick cast Revivify. Joe decisively defeated [[Twizzle]].
- **Lord Zulpair:** Twizzle's downtime investigation connected [[Lord Zulpair]] to the destruction of her home and attack on her family. Dick arranged a first-light meeting and requested a year of shipping ledgers.
- **Possible concealed Lord:** Dick suspects Zulpair is one of Waterdeep's concealed Lords because he missed the investiture ceremony, but this is unconfirmed.
- **Bagman's Bet:** Cordan hosted [[Bagman's Bet]], a Feywild storytelling game using a Tymora-blessed Bag of Holding with one hundred randomized items.
- **Laeral's Cubic Gate:** Laeral shared the story of [[Laeral's Cubic Gate]]. Cordan noticed a hooded observer showing unusual interest and wrote a description to give Laeral.
- **Next:** The party returned to [[Dick's Manor]] to prepare for Zulpair's arrival at first light.
- **Unresolved:** The ceremony location and gifts, crown properties, Black Key inn details, Bagman's Bet draws and bag ownership, Zulpair's status and ledgers, and the hooded observer.
