# Session 3 Recap

Full notes: [[Session 3]] · All recaps: [[Recaps]]

- **Renovation negotiations:** [[Dick Mixon]] persuaded the guilds to reduce [[Trollskull Manor]]'s total renovation cost to **1,000 gp**, citing [[Cordan]]'s assistance.
- **Fireball attack:** A [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor|fireball exploded outside the manor]], killing a gnome, halflings, and people bearing [[Zhentarim]] tattoos. [[Barnabus Blastwind]] took charge of the investigation.
- **Recovered evidence:** [[Twizzle]] found **five gems worth 500 gp total**. Dick secretly retrieved a necklace with two orange beads from a rain barrel; Twizzle later identified it as a [[Necklace of Fireballs]].
- **Witness accounts:** [[Fala Legaliir]] saw a burned, cloaked man take something from the dead gnome and limp toward [[The Bent Nail]]. [[Jezrynne Hornraven]] saw a humanoid puppet on a rooftop throw something that caused the explosion.
- **Evidence surrendered:** After Cordan warned against withholding evidence, Dick turned over the necklace and gems to the authorities and requested a sketch of the rooftop puppet.
- **Dalakhar's testimony:** During Speak with Dead, [[Dalakhar]] said he had stolen the [[Stone of Golorr]] from [[Xanathar]]'s lair and intended to entrust it to the party. He suspected the Zhentarim had killed him.
- **Zhentarim testimony:** Dick impersonated a Zhentarim member while questioning the dead sellswords. [[Bashekk Ortallis]] and [[Wern Malkrave]] named [[Gralhund Villa]] as the Stone's destination and [[Urstul Floxin]] as an associate. Their accounts conflicted about their main hideout: [[Darkhold Castle]] versus the sewers.
- **Next leads:** [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] requested information on Gralhund Villa from [[Melannor Fellbranch]]. The [[Rooftop Puppet Sketch|puppet sketch]] resembled an automaton supplied by the [[Temple of Gond]] for the [[Day of Wonders]] parade.
- **Unresolved:** The burned man's identity, the Stone's whereabouts, the conflicting hideout accounts, and the Emerald Enclave's response.
