# Session 5

## Split Investigation

The party split up. [[Dick Mixon]] went to the courthouse to help [[Lilith D Gluv]], while [[Cordan]], [[Joe Bauers]], [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]], and [[Twizzle]] returned to [[Cassalanter Villa]] to seek evidence or another entrance.

Before separating, Dick gave Cordan his [[Ring of Spell Storing]], which contained Revivify and two castings of Find Familiar.

## Cassalanter Villa Surveillance

The villa group surveyed the property and determined:

- Security had increased, including four guards instead of two at the main gate.
- At least two guards watched each entrance.
- The barn had a street exit suitable for quickly deploying a carriage.
- A street door led directly into the manor.
- They were reasonably certain there were no other entrances, secret or otherwise.

A carriage bearing Cassalanter symbols left the villa. The group hired a carriage and followed it. Twizzle sent Dick a Paper Bird reporting their findings.

## Lilith's Release

Lilith had spent the night in a cell and refused to answer questions without a lawyer. Dick used Disguise Self to impersonate his attorney friend, [[Richard Nixon]], an elderly elf, and met Lilith privately.

Lilith recounted the events at Cassalanter Villa. Dick instructed her not to answer questions without his approval. The charges were:

- Assaulting a noble, [[Elias Chadwell]].
- Attempted burglary.
- Disturbing the peace.
- Assaulting [[Victoro Cassalanter|Lord Cassalanter]].

Dick requested sketches of Chadwell and Lord Cassalanter to compare with the people who had carried Lilith from the villa. A hearing was scheduled for two hours later. The authorities cited a stone as physical evidence, with most other evidence consisting of noble testimony.

After receiving the Paper Bird, Dick replied with the charges, implicated names, and hearing time, while telling the party to continue investigating.

Dick argued that only trespassing was supportable. When the investigator said a noble's word would always outweigh that of a nobody, Dick dropped his disguise, threatened legal action and an appeal to the Open Lord over apparent corruption, and claimed Lilith was his ward. He persuaded the authorities to drop charges on both sides and left with Lilith.

Lilith recovered to full health after a short rest but had not yet completed a long rest.

## Cassalanter Mausoleum

The Cassalanter carriage traveled to the noble mausoleums in the [[City of the Dead]]. A tiefling entered the mausoleum marked with the Cassalanter name. Cordan cast Pass without Trace, transformed into a spider, and followed.

Below the sarcophagi, Cordan overheard a male tiefling questioning a distressed human woman beside a dead man:

- She said the other two had taken the Stone to the windmill.
- She asked where Holiver was; the tiefling did not recognize the name.
- She said the windmill was in the southern ward and that its occupants held ceremonies there.
- The tiefling promised that the Cassalanters would help her obtain vengeance.

After they left, Cordan found two dead human men. Both wore red robes beneath their cloaks and appeared to have died from dagger wounds and possible necromancy.

Joe recognized the likely destination as an [[Abandoned Windmill|abandoned windmill]] occupied by squatters whom the City Guard periodically removed. The party hurried there, and Tathlynd sent Dick a Paper Bird with the new destination.

## Windmill Fight

Dick and Lilith reached the windmill and rejoined the party. Joe warned the ground-floor squatters that guards were coming, causing them to leave.

On an upper floor, the party approached a southern room containing something magical. As a Cassalanter carriage approached, Joe opened the door and combat began. An imp-like creature alerted figures on the far side of the room.

One cultist was knocked unconscious and the other surrendered. Dick took the [[Stone of Golorr]] and restrained the conscious captive.

Three spined devils fled through a window. Cordan caught all three with Entangle and used Fey Step to reach a nearby roof. Twizzle joined him with Misty Step, and Lilith jumped to the roof. The party killed all three devils.

Cordan and Lilith each carried away one devil corpse. The third was left for investigators.

## Familiars and Interrogation

During the return to [[Dick's Manor]], Cordan and Dick planned their questioning. Cordan used the Ring of Spell Storing to cast Find Familiar, allowing the summoned Fey spirit to choose its form; it became a spider. Dick later attuned to the ring and did the same, also receiving a spider. Cordan then stored two castings of Charm Person in the ring.

Dick questioned the captured cultists separately while using deception and Charm Person. They were identified as [[Arn Xalrondar]] and [[Seffia Naelryke]].

They revealed:

- Arn and Seffia had taken the Stone as ordered.
- On orders from [[Victoro Cassalanter|Lord Cassalanter]], they killed three low-ranking members named [[Vaelle]], [[Holiver]], and [[Kaeth]] as a security measure.
- The spined devils served Lord Cassalanter, who could summon dangerous creatures.
- The cult met at Cassalanter Villa and had also operated from the windmill.
- Their memories of who took the Stone and why they brought it to the windmill were impaired or missing. Seffia eventually realized Dick had taken it.
- The Stone was important to Lord Cassalanter and was meant to be delivered to his estate.
- Cult leaders, including Lord and Lady Cassalanter, ordered members to kidnap destitute people for sacrifice.
- The cult planned something significant for [[Founders' Day]], which was one tenday away.
- The oldest Cassalanter child had disappeared years earlier, and rumors said the other two children were also in danger because of a deal with [[Asmodeus]].
- The Stone was some kind of transformed creature that knew the location of the [[Vault of Dragons]].

Arn and Seffia agreed to surrender to the City Watch. Dick arranged transportation to the nearest guard station, and his spider familiar followed to observe what happened. It was later confirmed that both surrendered and repeated substantially the same testimony.

## Attuning to the Stone

In the morning, Cordan cast Find Traps and detected no unwanted effect from touching the Stone. Dick cast Identify and attuned to it.

The Stone was revealed to be a sentient transformed aboleth. Its effects on Dick included:

- The ability to cast Lightning Bolt. After use, a d6 is rolled; on 1-5, it cannot be used again until the next dawn.
- Blindness whenever Dick is more than 10 feet from the Stone.

Cordan and Tathlynd searched Dick's library for information about aboleths but found nothing.

Through telepathy, the Stone told Dick that the Vault lay beneath the [[Old Tower]] and required three keys:

- A silvered warhammer.
- Sunlight.
- A unicorn.

The Stone said any silvered warhammer would work and that a toy unicorn in the Cassalanter children's room would satisfy the third key.

Privately, without telling the party, Dick told the Stone that he intended to become Waterdeep's next Open Lord. He proposed that they help each other, and offered access to his library and future knowledge. The Stone described itself as hungry for knowledge.

## Gathering the Keys

At [[Steam and Steel]], [[Embric]] and [[Avi]] agreed to make a decorative silvered warhammer from one silver bar by the next day, free of labor charges because the party were neighbors. They could complete it in three hours because it did not need to function as a weapon.

The party purchased nineteen steel mirrors, adding Tathlynd's existing mirror for twenty total, to direct sunlight underground.

At [[Old Xoblob Shop]], Dick spent 10 gp on a toy unicorn and several unusual [[Old Xoblob Curios|curios]].

After collecting the decorative hammer, Dick gave Steam and Steel two additional silver bars and commissioned a functional silvered warhammer, allowing the smiths to keep the excess silver.

## Old Tower

At the Old Tower, the party descended a central spiral staircase to a heavy, iron-banded wooden door. Dick detected magic on the floor beyond it. Tathlynd found no traps, Cordan picked the lock, and Joe pulled the door open from ten feet away using a grappling hook and rope.

Inside was a teleportation circle and a secret door leading to a winding staircase. At its bottom stood adamantine double doors bearing a Dwarvish inscription: "THE THREE KEYS. BRING THEM FORTH."

Dick presented the toy unicorn and silvered warhammer, producing audible clicks. The party spent nine hours arranging mirrors to carry sunlight to the door, then rested nearby. Tathlynd took the first watch and Cordan the second.

At sunrise, the final key was satisfied and the Vault opened.

## Vault of Dragons

Inside the [[Vault of Dragons]], the party encountered:

- Several sets of false iron doors; only the upper-left set was real.
- A fresco of dwarves fighting goblins that magically enthralled Tathlynd. The party restrained him and destroyed the fresco, ending the effect.
- Three bridges over the entrance foyer, two partly unstable and one with a fifteen-foot gap.
- A secret treasure room containing urns, gems, coins, rings, a [[Ring of Warmth]], and a silver statuette of a priest of Moradin.
- A magically sealed forge room that opened when Dick approached while disguised as a dwarf. Striking its magical anvil with a magical hammer granted Dick, Joe, Tathlynd, and Twizzle 10 temporary hit points.
- A door opened by telling a previously unrevealed secret in Dwarvish. Tathlynd confessed, "It was me, I was the one who attempted to poison the matron."

Below, the party encountered [[Aurinax]], an adult gold dragon disguised as an elderly dwarf. He guarded the gold on behalf of [[Dagult Neverember]] and carried the [[Dragonstaff of Ahghairon]] as payment.

Dick persuaded Aurinax to willingly submit to Zone of Truth. Aurinax acknowledged his suspicion that Neverember had embezzled the gold. Dick promised to return it to Waterdeep and convinced Aurinax to release all the gold and thirty gemstones worth 100 gp each.

The party transferred the gold to a vault beneath Dick's Manor, then deposited their funds at the [[Temple of Waukeen]]. The 500,000 gp was divided as follows:

- 20,000 gp for each party member: 120,000 gp total.
- 120,000 gp reserved for business expenses.
- 260,000 gp set aside for return to Waterdeep.

The party also recovered the treasure from the secret room, including the urns and copper initially left behind.

Dick retained Aurinax's thirty gemstones. The side-room treasure was stored with the remaining Vault assets in the vault beneath [[Dick's Manor]].

After the session, Cordan experimented on the spined-devil corpse he had carried away, similarly to his earlier examination of the intellect devourer. It is unknown whether he learned anything.

The session ended with a long rest, and every party member reached level 5.
