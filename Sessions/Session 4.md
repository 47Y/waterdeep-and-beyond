# Session 4

## Gralhund Villa

The party staked out [[Gralhund Villa]] before entering. [[Cordan]] picked the gate lock with thieves' tools, and the party entered through a side door into what appeared to be barracks or servants' quarters.

After moving through the house unseen, they reached the kitchen. They could hear activity upstairs and saw Zhentarim thugs in the next room, but chose to sneak upstairs rather than confront them.

The party heard fighting through a wall. [[Joe Bauers]] kicked in the wrong door while trying to intervene. A thug came upstairs to investigate, and [[Dick Mixon]] convinced him that the party were Zhentarim reinforcements. The thug led them to the correct room.

The party joined the fight and killed the Zhentarim attackers with help from the Gralhund house guards. [[Lilith D Gluv]], [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]], and Joe then went downstairs to fight the two remaining thugs.

Lilith was knocked unconscious. The thug who felled her struck her again, causing two failed death saves. Joe called for help, and Cordan ran to the stairs and cast Healing Word.

Dick continued speaking with [[Yalah Gralhund]]. She claimed the [[Stone of Golorr]] had already been sent to its next owners, the Cassalanters. As the City Watch approached, the party escaped from the back balcony to avoid being seen.

Dick detected magic from the barn and identified [[Horseshoes of Speed]], but left them in place.

## Cassalanter Villa

The party went to [[Cassalanter Villa]] hoping to intercept the Stone. While preparing to infiltrate, Lilith failed to remain stealthy and instead approached the gate guards directly. They sent her away.

Lilith returned after disguising herself with dirt and claimed she had been sent to deliver the Stone. A well-dressed tiefling serving as the head servant brought her inside. Only Cordan observed this exchange, and he did not act immediately.

The servant took Lilith to the cellar, confronted her about how she knew of the Stone, and received an ordinary rock she had selected as a decoy. Lilith cast Command in an attempt to make him sleep, but he resisted and combat began.

Lilith killed the servant after taking one hit. On death, he reverted to his true form as a [[Cassalanter Doppelganger|doppelganger]]. Hearing approaching footsteps, Lilith hid in a clothing chest but was immediately found.

Lilith survived several rounds of attacks from [[Victoro Cassalanter]], [[Ammalia Cassalanter]], and an unidentified noble. She claimed that a friend had bet she could not enter the villa. Victoro decided to turn her over to the City Watch. Lilith agreed, and was bound, blindfolded, and led outside.

## Cordan's Reconnaissance

Hearing concerning sounds, Cordan gathered the rest of the party and proposed scouting as a spider. After the party agreed, he transformed and followed Lilith's route.

As he entered the cellar, Lilith was being carried out by the three people who had fought her. While they were occupied with the guards, Cordan searched the cellar. He found:

- The dead doppelganger.
- A room containing a painting of the holy symbol of [[Siamorphe]]. Cordan did not recognize it at the time; Tathlynd, Dick, and [[Twizzle]] later identified his drawing.

Victoro and Ammalia returned without the unidentified noble. They did not notice Cordan, who followed them while remaining in spider form.

The Cassalanters dragged the doppelganger's body to the holy-symbol painting and pressed the sun portion, opening a secret door. Beyond it, they placed the body in one of several empty cells.

They then descended, retrieved red robes and devil masks from a closet, and entered a large chamber resembling an underground temple to [[Asmodeus]], dominated by a 30-foot statue. Around a dozen other masked and robed figures were present.

The group chanted in a language Cordan did not recognize. He suspected Infernal because of the connection to Asmodeus, but this was not verified. When the chanting ended, Victoro declared, "Founder's Day approaches," and the group cheered.

Cordan withdrew before the gathering dispersed. Rather than open the secret door, he used Fey Step to teleport through a small gap in its imperfect seal. He left the villa, returned to his Eladrin form, and rejoined the party.

## Aftermath

Lilith was taken into City Watch custody. The rest of the party did not follow because they knew where she was being taken and believed they could do little until Cordan returned with information.

Cordan led the group to [[Dick's Manor]]. Over a late dinner around midnight, he described what he had seen and drew the holy symbol, devil masks, and amulets worn by the robed figures. Tathlynd, Dick, and Twizzle recognized the symbol of Siamorphe.

Cordan used Eladrin Trance to gain proficiency with cartographer's tools overnight and completed a [[Cordan's Map of Cassalanter Villa|map of the explored rooms]] in the morning.

Dick proposed going to the guards or courts in the morning to help Lilith while the others returned to Cassalanter Villa for evidence. The party had not decided how to regain access or recover the doppelganger's body.

During the long rest, everyone except Lilith reached level 4. Lilith's possible level-up was deferred until between sessions.
