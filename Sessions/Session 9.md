# Session 9

## Backfilled Dawnveil Lead

Information omitted from the previous session established that [[Fuski and Springmaul]], two guards involved in killing members of the [[Dawnveil Family]], had already provided [[Twizzle]] with some information about the attack.

## Zulpair's Records

Shortly after an early breakfast, [[Lord Zulpair]] arrived at [[Dick's Manor]] with a guard, a butler, and several boxes of business records. He continued to insist that his affairs were legitimate.

During the meeting, Zulpair confirmed to [[Dick Mixon]] that he is one of Waterdeep's Secret, or [[Lords of Waterdeep|Masked]], Lords.

[[Dick Mixon]] had [[Dick's House Wizard|his house wizard]] help inspect the books. Because the wizard has Keen Mind, the plan was to memorize and reproduce the records after Zulpair's departure. Copying the books was expected to take several days. Zulpair left his butler with the records to retrieve them after Dick finished.

Zulpair said his family worships [[Umberlee]], whom the notes call “the Sea Bitch,” primarily for ancestral reasons.

Dick questioned Zulpair about the [[Dawnveil Family]]. Zulpair described them as pirates who entered Waterdeep's harbor disguised as merchants and admitted that he had participated in the operation against them. He maintained that the family was killed during an attempted capture and said he did not believe the accusation had been mistaken. At Dick's request, Zulpair agreed to send over the original investigation materials.

Several days later, the [[Dawnveil Investigation Papers]] arrived. Twizzle found a list of her murdered relatives and said that most of the account was false. The paperwork contained substantial circumstantial claims but no concrete evidence that the Dawnveils were pirates.

The business records also contained multiple references to [[Skyhorn]], a functional lighthouse that Zulpair had purchased for much less than its expected value.

## Joe's City Watch Retirement

During the first days of the records review, [[Joe Bauers]] privately met his supervisor, [[Saeth Cromley]], without informing the party.

Saeth asked Joe's opinion of Dick as Open Lord. Joe said Dick's position was good for Waterdeep's economy and the city overall. When Saeth asked whether Joe still wanted his City Watch job, Joe said he was unsure but still wanted to help the Watch.

Saeth proposed that Joe retire and instead accept contracts for work when needed. Joe had done little ordinary Watch work in recent months because of [[The Black Key]] and his assignment to Dick's group, so he was not reprimanded. Paperwork was prepared for Joe to retire within the week. He must return all Watch equipment, including his uniform and guard-summoning horn.

Joe was visibly distracted and unhappy afterward. [[Cordan]] questioned him directly and continued asking follow-up questions despite Joe's repeated deflections, then eventually walked away.

## Confessions from Fuski and Springmaul

Joe asked Saeth about Fuski and Springmaul and was directed to another, unnamed supervisor. The party prepared to meet with the guards. Dick disguised himself as the captain of the guard, placed Fuski and Springmaul in separate interrogation rooms, and demanded the full story regardless of their written reports.

Their accounts were substantially the same:

- A superior told the guards that merchant ships entering the harbor secretly belonged to pirates carrying stolen goods.
- [[Lord Zulpair]] sent money and a note asking guards to provoke a fight.
- The Dawnveils' cargo logs were legitimate; they were merchants, not pirates.
- The guards and officials maintained the pirate story to conceal that Waterdeep's guards had murdered a merchant family.
- About a dozen guards received similar payments and instructions.
- The named guards on the list matched the names in the Dawnveil investigation paperwork.
- The guards believed everyone had been killed except [[Azura Dawnveil]], Twizzle's grandmother.
- Azura was last known to have been taken to Lord Zulpair.

One guard said he accepted the money because his wife had a gambling addiction. The other expressed remorse for betraying his creed and said several other guards had privately admitted receiving similar notes and payments. The notes do not establish which statement belonged to Fuski and which belonged to Springmaul.

Still disguised, Dick left the interrogation rooms and had both guards and the other named participants arrested. He then briefed the actual guard captain, who agreed to investigate further.

## Journey to Skyhorn

The party hired a ship to take them to Skyhorn in search of further evidence and possibly Azura. Before departure, Cordan ritually cast Water Breathing on the party, then also cast it on the ship's crew during the journey.

As the ship approached, Cordan and [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] saw that the lighthouse was operating, but its door was repeatedly slamming in the wind. A large wave approached the vessel, followed by a fight with a water elemental. Tathlynd inflicted most of the damage while under Haste from Dick.

After reaching Skyhorn's dock, the ship's captain refused to leave until she had thoroughly inspected the vessel for damage. This gave the party time to investigate the lighthouse.

- Which confession details came from Fuski and which came from Springmaul?
- Who were the unnamed supervisor, actual guard captain, Zulpair guard, Zulpair butler, house wizard, ship, and ship captain?
