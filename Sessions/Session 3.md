# Session 3

## Renovation Negotiations

The party met with guild representatives to negotiate the cost of renovating [[Trollskull Manor]]. [[Dick Mixon]] persuaded them to reduce the total cost to 1,000 gp because of [[Cordan]]'s assistance.

## Fireball

As the meeting ended, a [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor|fireball exploded outside]]. The dead included a gnome, halflings, and people bearing [[Zhentarim]] tattoos. Dried waste on the gnome's boots led the party to believe he had recently been in the sewers.

[[Twizzle]] found five gems worth 100 gp each, for a total value of 500 gp.

Dick used Detect Magic and found a necklace with a broken clasp and two orange beads in a rain barrel. He secretly placed it in his [[Dick's Magical Jacket|magical jacket]]. The players knew it was a [[Necklace of Fireballs]], but the characters did not identify it until later.

[[Barnabus Blastwind]] took control of the official investigation.

Dick examined the gems and determined they were high quality but otherwise unremarkable. [[Joe Bauers]] asked [[Saeth Cromley]] whether fireballs had been used in other attacks around the city. Cromley did not answer, partly because the party was near the explosion and therefore treated as suspect by default.

## Witnesses

Joe and [[Lilith D Gluv]] questioned nearby witnesses.

### Fala Legaliir

[[Fala Legaliir]], owner of [[Corellon's Crown]], had been watering plants in the second-floor greenhouse when the blast shattered windows. Through the smoke, Fala saw a badly burned, cloaked man take something from the dead gnome and limp toward [[The Bent Nail]], repeatedly looking behind him as though someone might be following.

### Jezrynne Hornraven

[[Jezrynne Hornraven]], a noblewoman leaving [[Tiger's Eye]], said the attacker was not a man but a puppet shaped like one. She saw it on a rooftop hurl something into the crowd that caused the explosion and witnessed halflings burn to death.

## Turning Over the Evidence

Dick took the party to the tavern's second floor for a private conversation. He revealed the necklace, and Twizzle recognized it as a Necklace of Fireballs. Dick considered either surrendering it to the City Watch or searching for the merchant who sold it. Cordan warned that withholding it would be a crime against the city.

Dick surrendered both the necklace and the five gems to the authorities. He also requested a copy of the sketch artist's depiction of the rooftop puppet.

## Speak with Dead

The authorities planned to cast Speak with Dead on the corpses. Dick asked to attend, and the entire party was invited to witness the questioning.

### Dalakhar

The dead gnome identified himself as [[Dalakhar]]. He said:

- He did not know who killed him but suspected the Zhentarim.
- He was coming to Trollskull Manor because he could not leave the city and had heard of the adventurers who saved the Open Lord's son.
- He intended to entrust the [[Stone of Golorr]] to the party.
- He stole the Stone from the lair of [[Xanathar]], a beholder deep beneath the city.

### Zhentarim Sellswords

Dick pretended to be a Zhentarim member while questioning the dead sellswords.

[[Bashekk Ortallis]] said:

- The Stone was to be taken to [[Gralhund Villa]].
- It was an artifact that would make them rich as kings.
- Their main hideout was [[Darkhold Castle]].
- His associates were [[Wern Malkrave]] and [[Urstul Floxin]].

Wern Malkrave independently said:

- He did not know who threw the fireball.
- The Stone was to be taken to Gralhund Villa.
- It would make them rich.
- Their main hideout was in the sewers, contradicting Bashekk's answer.
- He had been with Bashekk and Urstul.

## Leads

Cordan asked a guard about Darkhold Castle, but the guard did not recognize the name.

[[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] sent a courier to [[Melannor Fellbranch]] asking what the [[Emerald Enclave]] knew about Gralhund Villa. A response has not yet been recorded.

The party received a copy of the [[Rooftop Puppet Sketch]]. Its closest resemblance was an automaton from the [[Day of Wonders]] parade supplied by the [[Temple of Gond]].

## Open Questions

- Who was the burned, cloaked figure who took something from Dalakhar?
- Why did Bashekk and Wern give different locations for the main Zhentarim hideout?
- What does the Emerald Enclave know about Gralhund Villa?
