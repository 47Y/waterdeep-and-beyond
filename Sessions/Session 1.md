# Session 1

## Party Formation

The party gathered at [[The Yawning Portal]]. [[Dick Mixon]] and [[Cordan]] were already seated when the others arrived.

- [[Joe Bauers]] was sent by [[Saeth Cromley]].
- [[Lilith D Gluv]] was invited by Dick because of a prior connection.
- [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] was sent by [[Melannor Fellbranch]].
- [[Twizzle]] was sent by [[Obaya Uday]].

Dick offered each person 100 gp for one season of work, with a possible bonus based on the quality of the work. The work could be anything Dick needed, provided it did not seem morally reprehensible, and Dick would supply required equipment. He paid half in advance to those who agreed. Joe was the only person who did not agree immediately.

## The Yawning Portal

During a brawl, Dick tried to end the fight with words. A troll then emerged, and everyone except Cordan participated in the fight. With the troll nearly defeated, Cordan remained seated and asked Dick, "Do I have to?"

[[Durnan]], [[Yagra]], and Lilith worked together to push the unconscious troll back into the well instead of killing it with fire or acid.

[[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]] approached the party with a job to find [[Floon Blagmaar]]. Dick had assembled the group to help him. The party accepted, then stopped at their respective residences to gather equipment before beginning the search.

## Dock Ward Investigation

At the site of a bloody clash in the Dock Ward, Joe used his authority as a City Watch guard to obtain additional information. Twizzle slipped away to investigate [[Old Xoblob Shop]].

At [[The Skewered Dragon]], Dick and Twizzle questioned the patrons. They learned that Floon had left with an unidentified, well-dressed companion and found a lead pointing to a warehouse on Candle Lane.

## Zhentarim Warehouse

The party fought at the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]], leaving two kenku alive for questioning. Dick, Joe, Tathlynd, Lilith, and Twizzle interrogated them while Cordan searched the building.

Cordan found crates containing various mundane goods and a hidden door. Behind it were 15 silver bars and four paintings depicting [[Luskan]], [[Neverwinter]], [[Silverymoon]], and [[Baldur's Gate]]. Because Cordan is not from the Material Plane, he likely did not recognize the cities by sight. He closed the hidden door and left the contents behind for later.

Cordan heard whimpering and found [[Renaer Neverember]] in a closet beneath the stairs. Dick recognized Renaer after Cordan called him over.

The City Watch was summoned, the surviving kenku were taken into custody, and Renaer was escorted home. The party declined Renaer's offer to accompany them. Information from the kenku led the party into the sewers.

## [[Xanathar Hideout]]

The party had a brief encounter with a gazer, then fought goblins, a half-orc, an intellect devourer, and a mind flayer. On its first turn, the mind flayer placed a stone on a pedestal and teleported away.

Lilith fell unconscious during the fight. The intellect devourer tried and failed to take control of her body, then was killed shortly afterward.

After the fight, Dick ritually cast Identify on the [[Unknown Stone and Pedestal|stone and pedestal]]. He learned that they combine to teleport one individual to an unknown destination. Dick kept the stone; the pedestal remained attached to the floor in the hideout. When he picked up the stone, Cordan warned him, "Do not let curiosity become the hand that turns the final key."

The party found Floon and led him out. Cordan secretly took the intellect devourer's corpse to study later.

## Trollskull Manor

The party returned Floon to Volo at The Yawning Portal. Volo offered the deed to [[Trollskull Manor]] as payment. After Dick negotiated and the party toured the property, they agreed to accept it, with Volo paying the deed transfer fee.

Twizzle used Divine Sense and discovered a [[Spectral Bartender|spectral bartender]] in the manor.

The party spent the night at [[Dick's Manor]], each taking a separate room.

## Between Sessions

While the non-elves continued sleeping, Cordan used part of his four waking hours to perform an inexperienced examination resembling an autopsy on the intellect devourer's corpse. He did not expect to learn much and was motivated primarily by curiosity about the strange creature.
