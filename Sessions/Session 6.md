# Session 6

## Deferred Downtime

The group discussed a possible six-month downtime period, but it has not begun and is not expected for at least another session or two. All proposed activities are recorded in [[Planned Six-Month Downtime]] as future intentions only.

## Backlog Results

- [[Cordan]] learned that a spined devil has twelve tail spines that it can fire and later regrow.
- [[Arn Xalrondar]] and [[Seffia Naelryke]] reached the City Watch and repeated substantially the same information they had given [[Dick Mixon]].

## Audience with the Open Lord

The day after clearing the [[Vault of Dragons]], Dick secured an audience with [[Laeral Silverhand]], Open Lord of Waterdeep, at [[Castle Waterdeep]]. He brought a banknote for 250,000 gp, exactly half of the recovered 500,000 gp, and persuaded the whole party to attend. The party still intends to return the remaining 10,000 gp of the 260,000 gp allocated to Waterdeep.

Before the meeting, Dick's butler, [[Wentworth]], reported a string of bank robberies attributed to the [[Zhentarim]]. Dick contacted [[Yagra]], who denied that her organization was involved. He warned her that the Zhentarim name was being used.

Dick presented Laeral with:

- The 250,000 gp banknote, described as part of the money [[Dagult Neverember]] embezzled from Waterdeep.
- Information about the Cassalanters and their relationship with [[Asmodeus]].
- A request for permission to investigate more deeply and for the Cassalanters to be detained during that investigation.

Dick declined to identify every source or say exactly where the remaining money was stored, explaining that he did not want helpers implicated. [[Cordan]] provided [[Cordan's Map of Cassalanter Villa|his map]] and sketches of the masks and amulets seen beneath [[Cassalanter Villa]].

Dick requested that, if the evidence proved his suspicions, the Cassalanters be stripped of title and land and the party be permitted to take desired property from the estate. He said the children should receive housing and education but no longer remain nobility. Laeral's exact agreement to these proposed terms was not recorded at this stage.

Dick also alleged that a Secret Lord might be supporting the Cassalanters; this was his belief, not confirmed evidence.

## Clearing the Zhentarim

Dick summoned Yagra to Castle Waterdeep and promised payment and protection for truthful testimony. Laeral guaranteed Yagra's safety during the meeting.

Within a Zone of Truth, Yagra identified herself as a middle-ranking Zhentarim member and testified that the organization was not responsible for the recent bank robberies, although it might be responsible for other crimes. She did not know who was committing the robberies under its name.

## Conversations with Laeral

Dick told Laeral he believed he could do better as Open Lord because he wanted the position while she did not. He framed this as respect for her performance despite her reluctance.

Laeral spoke with each party member:

- [[Joe Bauers]] said his strongest trait was protecting people who could not protect themselves and described balance as necessary to understand happiness and creativity.
- [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] revealed his face and described escaping darkness through a personal rebirth and a friend's aid. He was willing to discuss his above-ground life later but not his life below ground.
- [[Lilith D Gluv]] recounted her experience at Cassalanter Villa and said her only substantial [[Gray Hands|Force Grey]] work so far had been visiting [[Hlam]].
- Cordan studied Laeral's books and decorations, concluding that she had been unprepared for office but worked hard to learn, spent extensive time there, and surrounded herself with personally meaningful nature art. He demonstrated his Star Map, projecting the Feywild sky across the ceiling. Laeral wondered whether similar crystals could depict Material Plane stars.
- [[Twizzle]] explained that being surrounded by guards made her uneasy. Laeral left open a future private conversation about the reason.

## Plan to Expose Victoro

The group considered several methods of investigating the Cassalanters without warning them. The final plan placed Laeral, Lilith, Joe, Tathlynd, and Twizzle in a hidden scrying room beside a study at [[Dick's Manor]], with a magical means of recording Dick's conversation with [[Victoro Cassalanter]].

## Emerald Enclave Mission

Before resting, an [[Talking Cat|Emerald Enclave cat messenger]] warned Tathlynd that the [[Xanathar Guild]] was releasing monsters to distract the City Watch and City Guard. A grell had abducted an elderly woman in the Dock Ward.

Everyone except Dick traveled to the Dock Ward, followed grell droppings into an alley, and killed two [[Grell|grells]]. Cordan retained one corpse for examination; Tathlynd presented the other to the [[Emerald Enclave]].

The four party members of the Enclave each gained 2 renown, raising them from 1 to 3. [[Melannor Fellbranch]] also gave each a [[Charm of Heroism]].

Tathlynd asked Melannor about Asmodeus and was warned: "Be very careful. He is the supreme master of the Nine Hells."

Overnight, Cordan dissected the grell and discovered firsthand that it produces paralytic poison. He planned to attempt collecting poison the following night, but that collection has not yet occurred.

## Victoro's Confession

For the meeting, Cordan cast Enhance Ability on Dick, used Meld into Stone to hide in the study ceiling, and observed through his spider familiar. Laeral and the other party members waited in the adjacent scrying room.

Dick posed as a favored servant of Asmodeus, displayed an [[Amulet of Asmodeus]] that [[Wentworth]] had obtained overnight, and showed Victoro the [[Stone of Golorr]]. The amulet was a separate piece made in the same or a similar style as the Cassalanter amulets, not one taken from their villa. Dick persuaded Victoro that Asmodeus was dissatisfied with him and demanded proof that Victoro could fulfill his bargain.

Victoro confessed:

- The Cassalanters had sacrificed peasants to Asmodeus for years.
- The final payment was due on [[Founders' Day]].
- The required payment was 999,999 gold dragons and 99 souls.
- The souls were the true prize; the gold was largely a nuisance.
- He believed that, with the Stone, he could fulfill the contract.

At Dick's demand, Victoro placed his hand on the Asmodean emblem and swore by Asmodeus that receiving the Stone would allow him to honor the deal.

Laeral entered. Cordan dropped from the ceiling and attempted Hold Person, but Victoro resisted. Joe placed him in manacles. Victoro did not resist further and was taken into custody.

## Raid on Cassalanter Villa

Laeral ordered the rest of the Cassalanter household detained. The party reached the villa as guards brought out the residents. Cordan and Dick used Healing Word on injured guards.

[[Ammalia Cassalanter]], [[Elzerina Cassalanter]], and [[Terenzio Cassalanter]] were detained. Guards and staff were taken for questioning, including the unidentified tiefling previously seen at the [[Cassalanter Mausoleum]].

Using Cordan's map, guards swept the hidden cellar and temple. Behind the secret door they found sacks containing approximately 980,000 gp.

Ammalia fully disclosed the bargain: the Cassalanters owed Asmodeus 999,999 gp and 99 souls; if they failed, the twins' souls would be taken instead. She cooperated because she wanted to protect her children.

The party decided to try to save the twins' souls, considering either taking them to the Feywild, where Asmodeus might have less influence, or seeking another deity's protection. Neither approach has been verified or attempted.

The party now owns resources from the Cassalanter estate, but the DM has not yet provided a complete inventory or final disposition.
