# Session 2

## Morning

[[Cordan]] learned very little from examining the intellect devourer. He left [[Dick Mixon]] a note about the hidden loot in the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]].

During breakfast, [[Joe Bauers]] received a mental message from [[Vajra Safahr]]: "I am Vajra Safahr, the Blackstaff. Come to [[Blackstaff Tower]] in the Castle Ward at once. Bring your friends."

## The Gray Hands

At Blackstaff Tower, Vajra invited the party to join the [[Gray Hands]]. Joe, [[Lilith D Gluv]], and [[Twizzle]] accepted immediately. [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] wanted to consult the [[Emerald Enclave]] first.

Vajra assigned the party to [[Consult Hlam|seek out Hlam]] on [[Mount Waterdeep]] and ask what he had heard about threats to the city without annoying him or overstaying their welcome.

The party purchased climbing gear. Dick paid for his own gear and gear for Cordan and Joe under their contracts. Twizzle altered her contract so Dick would also pay for hers.

## Trollskull Deed

The party collected the deed to [[Trollskull Manor]] from the magistrate's office with [[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]], who paid the 25 gp transfer fee. Volo then invited them for drinks at [[The Yawning Portal]] and discussed his upcoming book.

Dick told Volo about the [[Spectral Bartender|poltergeist]] in Trollskull Manor. Volo asked to study it for his book. During the negotiation, Cordan told him, "When someone gives more than was owed, take care before teaching them to regret it." Volo ultimately agreed to credit the party in the book and provide an advance copy.

## Hlam's Warning

The party climbed Mount Waterdeep to find [[Hlam]]. Twizzle and Tathlynd each gained two levels of exhaustion during the climb.

Hlam was annoyed by the visit, but Tathlynd and Dick persuaded him to share a warning: "Evil's twin hides its face for now, expect that to change before winter's end."

Hlam offered the party water and told Tathlynd he was welcome to return. Joe pretended to choke and slide down the mountain, but everyone recognized that he was faking. The party returned to the city in the late evening.

## Conversations with the Blackstaff

Twizzle told Vajra about her goal of removing corruption from the guard. Vajra said she would take the concern under advisement and inform Twizzle if she learned anything relevant.

Twizzle also asked where she could buy magic items, particularly a Ring of Protection. Vajra explained that Waterdeep did not have a consistently reliable magic shop; availability depended on what entered the city and current demand.

Lilith asked about her brother, [[Damien L Gluv]]. Vajra knew nothing about him but directed Lilith to a secretary because Damien had studied wizardry in Waterdeep.

## Warehouse Retrieval

That night, Dick and Cordan returned to the Zhentarim Warehouse. They entered through a second-story office window and killed the two Zhentarim thugs guarding the building.

Cordan took a heavy crossbow and bolts from one thug and later gave them to Joe. Dick and Cordan recovered the two hidden crates without further trouble. Dick fenced the contents over the following days for 1,050 gp.

The proceeds were divided as follows:

- Cordan: 40%, or 420 gp.
- Dick: 30%, or 320 gp.
- Joe, Lilith, Tathlynd, and Twizzle: 7.5% each, or 78 gp each.

## Emerald Enclave Assignment

Tathlynd awoke to a talking cat that offered him a job and summoned him to [[Phaulkonmere]], the Emerald Enclave's headquarters. The Enclave reported that an animated scarecrow was terrorizing outlying farms, killing livestock, chasing horses, and frightening farmers. No people had yet been killed, and the City Guard had been slow to act.

At Phaulkonmere, Tathlynd asked [[Melannor Fellbranch]] whether he could also join the Gray Hands. Melannor said there was no rule against it and described Force Grey as reputable. Tathlynd planned to join when he next had the opportunity.

At the affected farm, the party encountered five scarecrows, three of which were hostile constructs. After the fight, Dick heard the halfling family hiding in their cellar.

The family said they had bought the scarecrows from [[Fenrick Loin]], a traveling merchant from [[Neverwinter]] who was making a circuit toward [[Baldur's Gate]]. They offered cider as payment. After tasting it, the party proposed that the family supply cider and hard cider for their future tavern. Dick planned to negotiate exclusive rights to sell [[Snobeedle Cider]].

On the return trip through the southern gate, Cordan, Twizzle, and Joe joined the Emerald Enclave.

## Level Up and Downtime

The party returned to [[Dick's Manor]], reached level 3 during the long rest, and then had two weeks of downtime.

- Cordan used temporary tool proficiencies gained through Eladrin Trance to help renovate Trollskull Manor. His work earned invitations to the [[Mason's Guild]] and [[Carpenter's Guild]].
- Dick bought +1 half plate for 1,500 gp and a Tome of Leadership and Influence for 15,000 gp. He also began marketing Trollskull Manor's planned grand opening.
- Joe spent both weeks pit fighting and earned 200 gp total.
- Twizzle researched Hadar and was told she would receive information between sessions.
- Lilith researched intellect devourers, beginning in Dick's library, and was told she would receive information between sessions. She also learned that Damien transferred to [[Starshine Academy]] in Neverwinter about seven months earlier.
- Tathlynd bought books and tea before returning to visit Hlam. One book was *Demonology: Incubi and Succubi*. He also foraged for and sold 100 gp worth of herbs and spices to an apothecary.

The full renovation cost for Trollskull Manor was established as 1,000 gp plus 250 gp in guild fees.
