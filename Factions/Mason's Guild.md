# Mason's Guild

[[Cordan]] was invited to join after using mason's tools to help renovate [[Trollskull Manor]] during the downtime following [[Session 2]]. He has not decided whether to accept.

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “Guilds and Guild Law”; related sections where noted).

- The guide lists the **Guild of Stonecutters, Masons, Potters, and Tile-makers** among the guilds involved in construction in [[Waterdeep]], including decorative stone and ceramic work.
- Guild membership is not legally mandatory for practicing a trade, but unaffiliated tradespeople may find guild members unwilling to sell them goods or services.
- Guildhall Day (Kythorn 14) features displays of guild work and recruitment; new building projects are traditionally begun on Stoneshar (Marpenoth 7).
