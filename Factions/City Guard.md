# City Guard

[[Waterdeep]]'s soldiers protect its walls, approach roads, civic buildings, and magisters. Street policing belongs to the [[City Watch]].

- The Guard maintains a training camp in [[Undercliff]].
- Guard patrols collect the monthly resident tax; see [[Waterdeep Money and Taxes]].

## Griffon Cavalry

- The city's aerial defense force, based at Peaktop Aerie atop [[Mount Waterdeep]].
- Riders use trained griffons and rings of feather falling for aerial maneuvers and rescues.
- Flying over Waterdeep requires special licensing. Unauthorized aerial arrivals should expect interception.
- Riders generally stay above rooftops because horses in the streets can excite the griffons.
