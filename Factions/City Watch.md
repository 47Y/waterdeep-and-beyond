# City Watch

The street police of [[Waterdeep]], responsible for patrols, investigations, and arrests under the [[Code Legal]].

- Recognizable by green-and-goldenrod doublets and tall steel helmets; typical equipment includes a truncheon, dagger, and buckler.
- Watch posts display green-and-gold magical lanterns, even during the day. Crimes can be reported there.
- Patrols operate day and night. Officers summon reinforcements with shrill whistles; Dock Ward patrols are particularly large after dark.
- The Watch does not patrol the unofficial Field Ward.
- Volo praises the Watch's discipline and reliability; this is his assessment of the institution.
- The [[City Guard]] handles walls, roads, and civic protection instead.

## Useful Watch Terms

- **Hold:** Stay still.
- **Down arms:** Drop weapons.
- **Talk truth:** Answer fully.
- **Robes:** A magister; a threat to involve one means the situation is escalating.
