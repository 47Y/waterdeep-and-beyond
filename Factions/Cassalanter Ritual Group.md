# Cassalanter Ritual Group

[[Cordan]] observed roughly a dozen masked figures performing a ritual in a hidden chamber beneath [[Cassalanter Villa]]. [[Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[Ammalia Cassalanter]] joined them wearing red robes and devil masks.

The chamber contained a 30-foot statue of [[Asmodeus]].

## Information from Captured Members

[[Arn Xalrondar]] and [[Seffia Naelryke]] said:

- [[Victoro Cassalanter]], [[Ammalia Cassalanter]], and other senior members ordered cultists to kidnap destitute people for sacrifice.
- The group planned something significant for [[Founders' Day]], then one tenday away.
- Victoro ordered Arn and Seffia to kill [[Vaelle]], [[Holiver]], and [[Kaeth]] as a security measure.
- Spined devils served Victoro.
- The group met at [[Cassalanter Villa]] and had also operated from the [[Abandoned Windmill]].

The group's formal name remains unknown.

## Session 6

- [[Victoro Cassalanter]] confessed to years of sacrifices and a final payment of 999,999 gp and 99 souls due on [[Founders' Day]].
- Victoro, [[Ammalia Cassalanter]], their children, guards, and staff were detained.
- Approximately 980,000 gp was found behind the secret door beneath [[Cassalanter Villa]].
- The party intends to prevent [[Asmodeus]] from claiming the twins' souls.
