# Xanathar Guild

According to an [[Emerald Enclave]] message, the Xanathar Guild was releasing monsters to distract Waterdeep's City Watch and City Guard while its members caused trouble elsewhere.

The party killed two released [[Grell|grells]] in the Dock Ward during [[Session 6]].
