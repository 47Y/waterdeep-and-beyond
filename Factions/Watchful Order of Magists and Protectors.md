# Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors

An association of arcane spellcasters in [[Waterdeep]], headed by the Blackstaff (see [[Vajra Safahr]]).

- Arcane spellcasters intending to stay in the city must register with the city; joining the order is strongly encouraged.
- Members can be called into temporary service with the [[City Watch]] or [[City Guard]].
- Duties include investigating magical crimes and helping during fires, mass-casualty events, and other disasters.
- Members also keep watch on spellcasters who choose not to join, since magical havoc risks the wrath of the [[Lords of Waterdeep]].
