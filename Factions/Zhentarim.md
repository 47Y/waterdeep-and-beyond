# Zhentarim

The party has repeatedly encountered members and locations associated with the Zhentarim.

## Known Activity

- Used the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]] on Candle Lane.
- [[Dalakhar]] suspected the Zhentarim were responsible for killing him during the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]].
- Dead sellswords said the [[Stone of Golorr]] was to be delivered to [[Gralhund Villa]].
- The identified sellswords were [[Bashekk Ortallis]], [[Wern Malkrave]], and [[Urstul Floxin]].
- Bashekk named [[Darkhold Castle]] as their main hideout, while Wern said it was in the sewers.
- Zhentarim attackers fought the house guards at [[Gralhund Villa]]. The party helped the guards kill the attackers.
- Recent bank robberies were blamed on the Zhentarim, but [[Yagra]] testified under Zone of Truth that the organization was not responsible and that someone was using its name.
