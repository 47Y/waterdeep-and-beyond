# Dawnveil Family

The Dawnveils are [[Twizzle]]'s family. They entered Waterdeep's harbor as legitimate merchants but were falsely accused of piracy.

[[Lord Zulpair]] paid about a dozen guards to provoke a confrontation. The family members aboard were killed, their legitimate cargo records were ignored, and officials preserved a false story that they had been pirates disguised as merchants.

[[Azura Dawnveil]], Twizzle's grandmother, is the only known survivor. She was last known to have been taken to Zulpair.
