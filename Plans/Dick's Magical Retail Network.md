# Dick's Magical Retail Network

Out of character, this concept is called "Magical Wal-Mart." That is not currently an established in-world name.

[[Dick Mixon]] wants to build multiple stores around the world offering a broad selection of magic items. His intended system would:

- Store inventory in a pocket dimension.
- Use a Feywild creature or entity as storage manager.
- Magically transport requested items to individual stores.
- Employ enchanters to build inventory before storage and distribution are operational.

Dick hopes [[Cordan]] can help contact a suitable Feywild entity. No entity, pocket dimension, store location, or confirmed hire has yet been established.
