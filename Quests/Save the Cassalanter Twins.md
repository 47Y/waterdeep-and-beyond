# Save the Cassalanter Twins

The party sought to prevent [[Asmodeus]] from claiming the souls of [[Elzerina Cassalanter]] and [[Terenzio Cassalanter]] after their parents failed to fulfill the Cassalanter bargain.

## Outcome

On [[Founders' Day]], the twins suffered an apparent supernatural attack. The party carried them to the [[Temple of Eilistraee]], where their prayers and offerings supported Eilistraee's effort to claim and protect the souls. Eilistraee prevailed, and the twins' souls were saved from Asmodeus.
