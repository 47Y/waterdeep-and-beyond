# Scarecrow Trouble

The [[Emerald Enclave]] asked [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] to investigate animated scarecrows terrorizing outlying farms.

## Outcome

- The party encountered five scarecrows, three of which were hostile constructs.
- No people had been killed before the party intervened.
- The affected halfling family had purchased the scarecrows from [[Fenrick Loin]].
- The party began discussing an exclusive arrangement to sell [[Snobeedle Cider]] at [[Trollskull Manor]].
