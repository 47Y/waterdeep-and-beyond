# Find Azura Dawnveil

The confessions of [[Fuski and Springmaul]] established that [[Azura Dawnveil]], [[Twizzle]]'s grandmother, survived the attack on the [[Dawnveil Family]] and was last known to have been taken to [[Lord Zulpair]].

Zulpair's business records pointed the party toward [[Skyhorn]], a lighthouse he purchased for far less than its expected value. The party reached Skyhorn's dock after fighting a water elemental and is preparing to search the lighthouse.
