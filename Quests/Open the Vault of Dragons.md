# Open the Vault of Dragons

The [[Stone of Golorr]] directed the party to the [[Old Tower]] and identified the three [[Vault Keys]].

The party opened the [[Vault of Dragons]], negotiated with [[Aurinax]], retained 240,000 gp for personal and business use, and returned 260,000 gp to Waterdeep.
