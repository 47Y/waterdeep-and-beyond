# Investigate Lord Zulpair

[[Twizzle]]'s downtime investigation identified [[Lord Zulpair]] as responsible for destroying her home and attacking her family.

During [[The Black Key]]'s grand opening, Zulpair approached [[Dick Mixon]] about possible shipping agreements. Dick asked him to bring the previous year's shipping ledgers to [[Dick's Manor]] at first light.

## Findings

- Zulpair admitted participating in the operation against the [[Dawnveil Family]] but claimed they were pirates disguised as merchants.
- The [[Dawnveil Investigation Papers]] contained circumstantial accusations but no concrete evidence. Twizzle said most of the account was false.
- [[Fuski and Springmaul]] confessed that Zulpair paid guards to provoke the confrontation and that the Dawnveils were legitimate merchants.
- About a dozen guards were implicated and arrested.
- [[Azura Dawnveil]] survived and was last known to have been taken to Zulpair.
- [[Zulpair Business Records|Zulpair's business records]] revealed his suspiciously cheap purchase of [[Skyhorn]].
- Zulpair confirmed to Dick that he is one of Waterdeep's Secret, or [[Lords of Waterdeep|Masked]], Lords.

The party has traveled to Skyhorn to continue the investigation and [[Find Azura Dawnveil|search for Azura]].
