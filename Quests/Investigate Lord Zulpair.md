# Investigate Lord Zulpair

[[Twizzle]]'s downtime investigation identified [[Lord Zulpair]] as responsible for destroying her home and attacking her family.

During [[The Black Key]]'s grand opening, Zulpair approached [[Dick Mixon]] about possible shipping agreements. Dick asked him to bring the previous year's shipping ledgers to [[Dick's Manor]] at first light.

The party intends to inspect the ledgers for evidence related to the attack. Dick also suspects Zulpair may be one of Waterdeep's concealed Lords, but this is unconfirmed.
