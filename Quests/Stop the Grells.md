# Stop the Grells

The [[Emerald Enclave]] asked [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] to intervene after the [[Xanathar Guild]] released monsters as distractions and a grell abducted an elderly woman in the Dock Ward.

The party tracked and killed two grells. Whether the abducted woman was rescued or found was not recorded.

Each participating party member belonging to the Enclave gained 2 renown and received a [[Charm of Heroism]].
