# Abandoned Windmill

An old windmill in a run-down part of Waterdeep's southern ward. It predates much of the surrounding city and was occupied by squatters whom the City Guard periodically removed.

## Session 5

- Cassalanter cultists used it as a temporary base and brought the [[Stone of Golorr]] there.
- The party recovered the Stone and captured [[Arn Xalrondar]] and [[Seffia Naelryke]].
- Three spined devils responded to the fighting and were killed by the party.
- One devil corpse was left for investigators; [[Cordan]] and [[Lilith D Gluv]] carried away the other two.
