# Mount Waterdeep

[[Hlam]] lives in a cave on the mountain's side. The party climbed it during [[Consult Hlam]]. [[Twizzle]] and [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] each gained two levels of exhaustion during the climb.

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “Landmarks”; related sections where noted).

- A major western landmark overlooking the harbor; the mountain lies within Castle Ward, with Castle Waterdeep on its eastern spur.
- Peaktop Aerie at the summit houses the Griffon Cavalry. The walking statue called the Griffon stands nearby, facing southeast over Dock Ward.
- Mount Melody Walk is a tunnel through the mountain leading to New Olamn's academy of music and other arts.
- Volo recounts that Ao appeared on the slopes at the end of the Time of Troubles. Gods' Day (Marpenoth 15) includes a summit bonfire honoring the gods associated with those events.
- Dragondown (Kythorn 20) includes burning effigies of Kistarianth the Red and its dracolich form on the slopes.
