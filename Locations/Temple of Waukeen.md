# Temple of Waukeen

The Temple of Waukeen operates as a bank. After recovering the gold from the [[Vault of Dragons]], [[Dick Mixon]] brought the party here to deposit their funds.

[[Archbishop Francis]] is associated with [[Waukeen]], though the session notes do not confirm whether Dick's conversation with him took place at this temple.
