# Dick's Manor

[[Dick Mixon]] has a manor in Waterdeep. The party stayed here after rescuing [[Floon Blagmaar]] in [[Session 1]], with each party member taking a separate room.

The party continued using it as their nightly residence during [[Session 2]]. Lilith began her downtime research into intellect devourers in Dick's library.

After infiltrating [[Cassalanter Villa]] in [[Session 4]], the rest of the party returned here for a late dinner while [[Lilith D Gluv]] remained in City Watch custody. [[Cordan]] described his reconnaissance and drew the symbols, masks, and amulets he had observed.

During [[Session 5]], [[Dick Mixon]] questioned [[Arn Xalrondar]] and [[Seffia Naelryke]] here. The 500,000 gp recovered from the [[Vault of Dragons]] was temporarily transferred into a vault beneath the manor before being divided and deposited.

The [[Vault Side-Room Treasure]] and remaining party Vault assets are stored in the manor's vault. Dick personally holds the thirty gemstones received from [[Aurinax]].
