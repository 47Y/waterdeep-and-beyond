# Undercliff

Farmland, rolling grassland, and small wooded areas east of [[Waterdeep]], with farming, livestock, and accommodation for travelers.

- Many residents are gnomes and halflings; buildings often reflect their stature.
- It is not an official city ward and is outside the regular resident tax and the protections of Guild Law and Watch patrols described by Volo.
- The [[City Guard]] maintains a protected training camp here. The [[City Watch]] operates Amendsfarm, a prison farm for people working off minor sentences.
- The [[Snobeedle Orchard and Meadery]] offers a drinking hall, shop, and seasonal fruit picking.
