# Old Xoblob Shop

[[Twizzle]] investigated the shop while the rest of the party examined the site of a bloody clash in the Dock Ward during [[Session 1]].

During [[Session 5]], [[Dick Mixon]] purchased a toy unicorn and several [[Old Xoblob Curios|unusual curios]] for 10 gp. The toy unicorn served as one of the [[Vault Keys]].
