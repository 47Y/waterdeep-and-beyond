# Xanathar Hideout

A sewer hideout visited during [[Session 1]].

## Known Events

- The party encountered a gazer before fighting goblins, a half-orc, an intellect devourer, and a mind flayer.
- The mind flayer used an [[Unknown Stone and Pedestal|unknown stone and pedestal]] to teleport away.
- [[Lilith D Gluv]] fell unconscious, but an intellect devourer failed to take control of her body.
- [[Floon Blagmaar]] was found and rescued.
- [[Dick Mixon]] kept the teleportation stone.
- The pedestal was left attached to the floor.
