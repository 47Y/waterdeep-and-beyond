# Undermountain

A vast dungeon beneath [[Waterdeep]], publicly accessible through the Well at [[The Yawning Portal]].

- [[Durnan]] charges adventurers to descend. Visitors to the tavern can watch expeditions leave and sometimes return with treasure.
- Volo traces the original excavations to the Melairkyn dwarves, followed by alterations and expansion by Halaster Blackcloak and his apprentices.
- Reports describe as many as twenty levels and drow tunnels reaching upward from below. Halaster and his apprentices are believed to remain underground; these are public reports, not confirmed party discoveries.
- Volo recounts that Durnan and Mirt returned from an expedition with great wealth.
