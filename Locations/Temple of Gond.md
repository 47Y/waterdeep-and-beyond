# Temple of Gond

The rooftop figure depicted in the [[Rooftop Puppet Sketch]] most closely resembled an automaton supplied by the Temple of Gond for the [[Day of Wonders]] parade.

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “Sea Ward”; related sections where noted).

- The guide names Gond's temple in the Sea Ward the **House of Inspired Hands**.
- It is a working center for invention and experimentation, from flying machines to improved hinges.
- Gondwatch Lane, at its southern entrance, is used to test inventions considered too dangerous to operate indoors; spectators and food vendors gather there.
- The [[Day of Wonders]] on Marpenoth 3 showcases Gondar inventions in a city parade.
