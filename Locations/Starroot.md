# Starroot

The Starroot is an ancient ash tree near [[Cordan]]'s home in [[Astraelith]].

## Details

- Inhabited and guarded by [[Elyndra Moonbough]].
- A cruel fomorian once trapped a fragment of a fallen star in its roots.
- The star fragment poisoned the grove with cold, colorless light.
- Cordan helped Elyndra guide the star back into the heavens.
- Afterward, the Starroot bloomed for the first time in centuries and produced the [[Fey Branch]].
