# Blackstaff Tower

Blackstaff Tower is in Waterdeep's Castle Ward and is associated with [[Vajra Safahr]]. The party visited after Vajra mentally summoned [[Joe Bauers]] during [[Session 2]].

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “Castle Ward”; related sections where noted).

- Volo describes the Castle Ward landmark as a squat black tower.
- He reports that staring at it can provoke unease and the impression of being watched; this is his description, not a recorded party experience.
