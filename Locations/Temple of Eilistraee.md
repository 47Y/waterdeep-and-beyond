# Temple of Eilistraee

[[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] appealed to [[Eilistraee]] here for protection of the [[Cassalanter Children|Cassalanter twins]]. The twins were enrolled in the church, and Eilistraee ultimately saved their souls from [[Asmodeus]] during [[Founders' Day]].

The temple's exact location and the high priestess's name are unknown.
