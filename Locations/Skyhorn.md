# Skyhorn

Skyhorn is a functional lighthouse recognized by [[Twizzle]]. [[Zulpair Business Records|Zulpair's business records]] showed that he purchased it for much less than its expected value.

The party hired a ship to investigate it during [[Session 9]]. On approach, the light was operating while the door repeatedly slammed open and closed in the wind. A water elemental attacked the ship before it reached the dock.

The party reached the lighthouse while the ship's captain remained behind to inspect her vessel for damage.
