# Zhentarim Warehouse

A warehouse on Candle Lane visited during [[Session 1]].

## Known Details

- The party fought there and left two kenku alive for the City Watch.
- [[Renaer Neverember]] was found in a closet beneath the stairs.
- [[Cordan]] found mundane goods and a hidden door while the others questioned the kenku.
- Behind the hidden door were 15 silver bars and four paintings depicting [[Luskan]], [[Neverwinter]], [[Silverymoon]], and [[Baldur's Gate]].
- Cordan likely did not recognize the depicted cities because he is not from the Material Plane.
- Cordan closed the hidden door and left the contents in place for later.

## Session 2

- [[Dick Mixon]] and Cordan entered through a second-story office window at night.
- They killed the two Zhentarim thugs guarding the warehouse.
- Cordan took a heavy crossbow and bolts from one thug and later gave them to [[Joe Bauers]].
- They recovered both hidden crates.
- Dick fenced the contents for 1,040 gp over the following days.
