# The Yawning Portal

The party first gathered here during [[Session 1]].

## Events

- [[Dick Mixon]] presented his employment contract to the group.
- A brawl and troll attack occurred.
- [[Durnan]], [[Yagra]], and [[Lilith D Gluv]] pushed the unconscious troll back into the well.
- [[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]] hired the party to find [[Floon Blagmaar]].
- The party later returned Floon here.

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “Castle Ward”; related sections where noted).

- A Castle Ward tavern owned by [[Durnan]], built over the Well, an entrance to Undermountain.
- Durnan charges adventurers to descend; visitors can watch expeditions depart and sometimes return.
- Volo describes Undermountain as an enormous dungeon beneath the city, first excavated by the Melairkyn dwarves and later expanded by Halaster and his apprentices. Its reputed depth and the claim that those wizards still dwell below are presented as reports, not party discoveries.
