# Trollskull Manor

[[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]] offered the manor's deed to the party as payment for rescuing [[Floon Blagmaar]] during [[Session 1]]. After negotiation and a tour, the party accepted, and Volo agreed to pay the deed transfer fee.

[[Twizzle]] used Divine Sense and discovered a [[Spectral Bartender|spectral bartender]] in the manor.

## Session 2

- The party collected the deed from the magistrate's office with [[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]], who paid the 25 gp transfer fee.
- Volo received permission to study the poltergeist in exchange for crediting the party in his book and providing an advance copy.
- Full renovation costs were established as 1,000 gp plus 250 gp in guild fees.
- [[Cordan]] used mason's and carpenter's tools to shorten the renovation work.
- [[Dick Mixon]] began marketing the planned grand opening.
- Dick plans to negotiate exclusive rights to sell [[Snobeedle Cider]].

## Session 3

- [[Dick Mixon]] negotiated the renovation cost down to 1,000 gp total because of [[Cordan]]'s assistance.
- A [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor|Fireball spell exploded outside]] as the guild meeting ended.
- [[Dalakhar]] had been coming to the manor to entrust the [[Stone of Golorr]] to the party.
