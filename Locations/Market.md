# Market

The Market is a large public marketplace in Waterdeep's Castle Ward near [[The Black Key]].

For The Black Key's grand opening, banquet tables and vendor stalls were arranged here. [[Dick Mixon]] paid food vendors to serve everyone for free, while trinket sellers continued charging normally.
