# Temple of Tyr

[[Lilith D Gluv]] prayed to [[Tyr]] here for help protecting the [[Cassalanter Children|Cassalanter twins]]. Darkness and unnatural cold filled the room, and Lilith concluded that she had angered Tyr and that he would not help, at least for the time being.

The temple's exact location is unknown.
