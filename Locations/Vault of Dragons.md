# Vault of Dragons

The party opened the Vault beneath the [[Old Tower]] during [[Session 5]].

## Known Areas and Hazards

- False iron doors surrounding the first chamber; only the upper-left set was real.
- A fresco of dwarves battling goblins that enthralled [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] until the party destroyed it.
- Three bridges over the entrance foyer, including unstable sections and a fifteen-foot gap.
- A secret treasure room.
- A forge room containing a magical hammer and anvil.
- A hidden passage opened by speaking a secret never before told in Dwarvish.
- A lower chamber containing the hoard guarded by [[Aurinax]].

## Recovered Wealth

The primary hoard contained 500,000 gp. [[Aurinax]] also released thirty gemstones worth 100 gp each.

The party kept 20,000 gp per member, reserved 120,000 gp for business expenses, and returned 260,000 gp to Waterdeep.

[[Dick Mixon]] retained the thirty gemstones. The [[Vault Side-Room Treasure]] was stored with the remaining Vault assets beneath [[Dick's Manor]].

See [[Vault Side-Room Treasure]] for the additional recovered items.
