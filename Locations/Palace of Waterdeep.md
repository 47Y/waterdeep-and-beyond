# Palace of Waterdeep

The seat of [[Waterdeep]]'s government in Castle Ward, also called **Piergeiron's Palace**.

- The [[Lords of Waterdeep]] hold audiences here, and its halls host officials, guildmasters, nobles, and court proceedings.
- The audience chamber contains Ahghairon's old throne.
- Copies of the [[Code Legal]] are available in several languages.
- Palace exchequers exchange currency without a fee, though appointments may require a wait; see [[Waterdeep Money and Taxes]].
- Castle Waterdeep is a separate defensive stronghold on a spur of [[Mount Waterdeep]].
