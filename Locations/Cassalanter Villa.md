# Cassalanter Villa

The party went to Cassalanter Villa hoping to intercept the [[Stone of Golorr]] during [[Session 4]].

## Known Areas

- Gate and guarded entrance.
- Cellar rooms.
- A room with a painting of the holy symbol of [[Siamorphe]].
- A secret door opened by pressing the sun portion of the symbol.
- Several empty cells beyond the secret door.
- A closet containing red robes and devil masks.
- A large underground chamber resembling a temple to [[Asmodeus]], dominated by a 30-foot statue.

## Events

- [[Lilith D Gluv]] entered by claiming she had been sent to deliver the Stone.
- She killed the [[Cassalanter Doppelganger|doppelganger]] serving as head servant.
- [[Victoro Cassalanter]], [[Ammalia Cassalanter]], and an unidentified noble captured Lilith and turned her over to the City Watch.
- [[Cordan]] infiltrated in spider form and secretly observed Victoro and Ammalia concealing the doppelganger's body and joining roughly a dozen robed, masked figures in a ritual.
- After the ritual, Victoro declared, "Founder's Day approaches."

## Unverified Detail

Cordan suspected the ritual language was Infernal, but he did not recognize it.
