# Old Tower

The [[Stone of Golorr]] directed the party to the Old Tower as the entrance to the [[Vault of Dragons]].

## Known Layout

- A central spiral staircase leads down to a heavy wooden door with iron banding and a built-in lock.
- Beyond the door is a teleportation circle.
- A secret door leads to a winding staircase.
- Adamantine doors at the bottom bear the Dwarvish inscription: "THE THREE KEYS. BRING THEM FORTH."

## Opening the Vault

The three keys were a silvered warhammer, sunlight, and a unicorn. The party used a decorative silvered warhammer, a toy unicorn, and sunlight redirected underground through twenty steel mirrors.
