# Astraelith

Astraelith is a luminous Eladrin city in the Feywild, nestled among the branches of an ancient World Tree.

## Details

- Known as the City of the Starbloom.
- Constellations drift between the rooftops.
- The night sky changes subtly with the emotions of the city's inhabitants.
- Its druids study both the stars above and the flowers and plants below.
- [[Cordan]] was born and raised here.
