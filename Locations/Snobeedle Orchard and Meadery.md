# Snobeedle Orchard and Meadery

A business in [[Undercliff]], east of [[Waterdeep]], owned and operated by the Snobeedle halflings.

- Volo recommends its drinking hall and shop, which accommodate larger visitors.
- Visitors can pick their own fruit in season.
- See [[Snobeedle Cider]] for the party's proposed tavern supply arrangement; the guide does not record a completed agreement.
