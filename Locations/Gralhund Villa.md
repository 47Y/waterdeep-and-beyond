# Gralhund Villa

The dead Zhentarim sellswords [[Bashekk Ortallis]] and [[Wern Malkrave]] both said the [[Stone of Golorr]] was to be taken to Gralhund Villa.

[[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] asked [[Melannor Fellbranch]] what the [[Emerald Enclave]] knew about the villa. A response has not yet been recorded.

## Session 4

- The party entered after [[Cordan]] picked the gate lock.
- Zhentarim attackers were fighting Gralhund house guards inside.
- The party helped the guards kill the attackers.
- [[Lilith D Gluv]] was knocked unconscious and suffered two failed death saves before Cordan healed her.
- [[Yalah Gralhund]] claimed the [[Stone of Golorr]] had already been sent to the Cassalanters.
- The party fled from a rear balcony when the City Watch approached.
- [[Dick Mixon]] detected [[Horseshoes of Speed]] in the barn but left them there.
