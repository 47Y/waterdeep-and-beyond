# Waterdeep

Waterdeep is [[Cordan]]'s main base of operations on the Material Plane.

It is also the current base of the [[Party]], which formed at [[The Yawning Portal]] during [[Session 1]] and acquired [[Trollskull Manor]] after rescuing [[Floon Blagmaar]].

## Cordan's Connection

- Cordan came to Waterdeep relatively early in his travels.
- He has spent most of his year and a half on the Material Plane based in or around the city.
- He has been back in Waterdeep continuously for about three months.
- He understands the city well enough to function comfortably, but still studies it as an outsider.
- [[Marnock Stonewhistle]]'s warning is one reason Waterdeep became important to him.
- He remained based in Waterdeep primarily because of that warning and his curiosity about it.

## Public Knowledge — Volo's Enchiridion

Source: [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] (especially “Entering Waterdeep”; related sections where noted).

### City and Government

- Known as the City of Splendors, Waterdeep stands on a plateau beside a deepwater harbor, with [[Mount Waterdeep]] to the west.
- Lady Laeral Silverhand is the Open Lord; the other ruling lords keep their identities masked. Government business is conducted at the Palace of Waterdeep, while Castle Waterdeep is a defensive stronghold.
- Volo traces the city's history through elven Aelinthaldaar, the Melairkyn dwarves, and Nimoar's settlement. Ahghairon established the system of Open and Masked Lords in 1032 DR.
- The Spellplague of 1385 DR caused the walking statues to rampage. They now stand as prominent landmarks; the guide describes them as dormant for more than a decade.

### Wards and Surroundings

- **Sea Ward:** Wealthy homes and major temples, including the [[Temple of Gond|House of Inspired Hands]].
- **North Ward:** A quiet residential district, with eastern countryside views from the public Cliffwatch walkway.
- **Castle Ward:** Government, courts, military facilities, the Market, [[Blackstaff Tower]], [[The Yawning Portal]], and Mount Waterdeep.
- **Trades Ward:** A busy shopping and entertainment district with many guildhalls and a strong Watch presence. Spellcasting and magic-item use are forbidden in the Court of the White Bull because magic there can go awry.
- **Southern Ward:** A district of traveling merchants, laborers, stables, and inns, with communities from many other lands.
- **Dock Ward:** Warehouses, tenements, steep streets, and unreliable street lighting; visitors should take care after dark.
- **City of the Dead:** A landscaped cemetery and public park, with monuments and mausoleums connected to extradimensional burial spaces.
- **Field Ward and Undercliff:** Neither is an official ward or subject to the city's resident tax. The Field Ward lacks Watch patrols and sewers; Undercliff is farming country east of the city, home to the Snobeedle Orchard and Meadery (see [[Snobeedle Cider]]).

### Arrival, Law, and Magic

- Large caravans and ships must register with a magister on arrival. Individuals and small overland parties must register if staying longer than a tenday; arrivals at night or in winter should expect registration.
- Magisters wear black robes and have broad sentencing discretion. Copies of the Code Legal are available at the palace and from gate or harbor magisters.
- The City Guard protects roads, walls, civic buildings, and magisters; the City Watch polices the streets. Watch posts display green-and-gold lanterns and accept crime reports.
- Carrying weapons and armor is legal, but dueling and assault are not. Sport fighting requires a registered venue.
- Arcane spellcasters intending to stay must register with the city and are strongly encouraged to join the Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors, headed by the Blackstaff, [[Vajra Safahr]]. Members may be called on for investigations, defense, or disaster relief.
- Flight over the city requires special licensing; the Griffon Cavalry intercepts unauthorized aerial arrivals.

### Money, Travel, and Everyday Life

- Local coins: **nib** (1 cp), **shard** (1 sp), **dragon** (1 gp), **sun** (1 pp), **taol** (2 gp locally), and **harbor moon** (50 gp locally). Taols and harbor moons lose much of their value outside Waterdeep.
- Taxes, fines, and guild fees require Waterdavian or Lords' Alliance currency. The palace exchanges currency without a fee, but appointments can take a day or more.
- Residents over ten owe **1 sp monthly**, collected on the month's last day. Paying **1 gp** buys a twelve-month exemption writ that must be shown at each collection.
- Drays generally cost **2–4 cp**; hire-coach fares are agreed and paid in advance and rarely exceed **6 sp**. Street names are usually marked high on building corners at intersections.
- Public fountains and wells supply clean water. The Font of Knowledge offers free reading instruction and houses the city's largest public library.
- Volo describes a diverse city welcoming different cultures and gender expressions. Ward loyalties, festivals, and sporting rivalries are important parts of civic life.

### Guilds and Nobility

- More than forty guilds shape city trade. Working outside a guild is legal, but guild members may refuse goods and services to nonmembers practicing their trade.
- Construction involves several guilds, including the [[Carpenter's Guild]] and [[Mason's Guild]]; Volo recommends a trusted local solicitor for navigating business requirements.
- Address nobles as Lord or Lady. Seeking patronage normally requires an introduction through an employee or acquaintance.
- Noble houses may retain up to seventy equipped soldiers in house colors; others, including foreign envoys, are limited to sixteen.
- Existing festival notes: [[Founder's Day]] and [[Day of Wonders]].
