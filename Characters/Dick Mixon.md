# Dick Mixon

## Summary

- Player character.
- Level 5 Variant Human Bard.
- Subclass listed in export: College of Eloquence.
- Background: Noble.
- Rules: D&D 5e 2014.

## Narrative Details

- Alignment: Unknown.
- Ideals, bonds, flaws, appearance, and personality: To be added.
- Became Open Lord of Waterdeep after interviewing with the [[Lords of Waterdeep]] at the end of [[Session 7]]. [[Laeral Silverhand]] now serves as an advisor.
- While attuned to the Stone, becomes blinded if more than 10 feet away from it.
- Plans to create [[Dick's Magical Retail Network]], a worldwide magic-item business using pocket-dimensional storage and a Feywild entity as inventory manager. The plan is not yet operational.

## Campaign Events

### Session 1

- Gathered and hired the party at [[The Yawning Portal]].
- Offered each person 100 gp for one season of morally acceptable work, paid half in advance, promised a possible performance bonus, and agreed to provide required equipment.
- Attempted to stop the opening brawl with words.
- Questioned patrons at [[The Skewered Dragon]] with [[Twizzle]].
- Recognized [[Renaer Neverember]] at the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]].
- Ritually cast Identify on the [[Unknown Stone and Pedestal|stone and pedestal]] in the sewer hideout, learning that they combine to teleport one individual to an unknown destination.
- Kept the stone.
- Negotiated with [[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]] over payment and the transfer of [[Trollskull Manor]].
- Hosted the party overnight at [[Dick's Manor]].

### Session 2

- Paid for climbing gear for himself, [[Cordan]], and [[Joe Bauers]] under their contracts, and for [[Twizzle]] after she altered her contract.
- Negotiated permission for [[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]] to study the [[Spectral Bartender|poltergeist]] in exchange for book credit and an advance copy.
- Helped persuade [[Hlam]] to share his warning.
- Returned to the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]] with Cordan, killed the two guards, and recovered the hidden crates.
- Fenced the recovered goods for 1,040 gp and received a 30% share, or 312 gp.
- Heard the halfling family hiding in their cellar after the scarecrow fight.
- Plans to negotiate exclusive rights to sell [[Snobeedle Cider]] at [[Trollskull Manor]].
- Reached level 3.
- During downtime, bought +1 half plate for 1,500 gp and gave it to [[Joe Bauers]].
- Bought a Tome of Leadership and Influence for 15,000 gp.
- Began marketing Trollskull Manor's planned grand opening.

### Session 3

- Negotiated the renovation price for [[Trollskull Manor]] down to 1,000 gp total because of [[Cordan]]'s assistance.
- Used Detect Magic after the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]] and found a [[Necklace of Fireballs]] in a rain barrel.
- Secretly placed the necklace in [[Dick's Magical Jacket]] before revealing it to the party.
- Examined the five recovered gems and found them high quality but otherwise unremarkable.
- Surrendered the necklace and gems to the authorities after discussing the legal and investigative implications with the party.
- Requested and received a copy of the [[Rooftop Puppet Sketch]].
- Successfully posed as a Zhentarim member while questioning dead sellswords under Speak with Dead.

### Session 4

- Convinced a Zhentarim thug at [[Gralhund Villa]] that the party were reinforcements, causing him to lead them to the fighting.
- Learned from [[Yalah Gralhund]] that the [[Stone of Golorr]] had reportedly been sent to the Cassalanters.
- Detected and identified [[Horseshoes of Speed]] in the Gralhund barn but left them there.
- Recognized Cordan's drawing as the holy symbol of [[Siamorphe]].
- Proposed approaching the guards or courts to help [[Lilith D Gluv]] while the others sought evidence at [[Cassalanter Villa]].
- Reached level 4.

### Session 5

- Lent the [[Ring of Spell Storing]] to [[Cordan]] with Revivify and two Find Familiar castings stored in it.
- Impersonated [[Richard Nixon]] to meet Lilith at the courthouse, then revealed himself while confronting the investigator over noble privilege and apparent corruption.
- Persuaded the authorities to drop all charges connected to Lilith's infiltration.
- Recovered the [[Stone of Golorr]] from a cultist at the [[Abandoned Windmill]].
- Questioned [[Arn Xalrondar]] and [[Seffia Naelryke]], persuading them to surrender to the City Watch.
- Sent his spider familiar to observe Arn and Seffia's trip to the guard station. It was later confirmed that both surrendered and repeated substantially the same testimony.
- Attuned to the Stone and learned the location and keys of the [[Vault of Dragons]].
- Privately told the Stone that he intended to become Waterdeep's next Open Lord and proposed a partnership based on shared access to knowledge.
- Purchased a toy unicorn and several [[Old Xoblob Curios]] for 10 gp.
- Commissioned decorative and functional [[Silvered Warhammers|silvered warhammers]] from [[Steam and Steel]].
- Disguised himself as a dwarf to open the Vault's forge room.
- Used Zone of Truth while negotiating with [[Aurinax]] and persuaded him to release the hoard for return to Waterdeep.
- Received 20,000 gp as his personal share and helped reserve 120,000 gp for business expenses.
- Retained the thirty gemstones given up by [[Aurinax]], worth 100 gp each.
- Reached level 5.

### Session 6

- Presented [[Laeral Silverhand]] with a 250,000 gp banknote as the first portion of the 260,000 gp intended for Waterdeep, leaving 10,000 gp still to return.
- Recruited [[Yagra]] to testify under Zone of Truth that the Zhentarim were not responsible for the recent bank robberies.
- Told Laeral directly that he wanted to become the next Open Lord.
- Devised the recorded meeting used to expose [[Victoro Cassalanter]].
- Used the [[Stone of Golorr]] and an [[Amulet of Asmodeus]] obtained overnight by [[Wentworth]] to pose as a favored servant of Asmodeus.
- Deceived Victoro into confessing the required payment of 999,999 gp and 99 souls and swearing to the bargain by Asmodeus's name.
- Helped heal injured guards during the raid on [[Cassalanter Villa]].

### Session 7

- Appealed to [[Waukeen]] through [[Archbishop Francis]] for help protecting the [[Cassalanter Children|Cassalanter twins]], then discussed money with Francis after Waukeen declined.
- Used Comprehend Languages to understand [[Eilistraee]]'s promise: “I will try to take these souls.”
- Gave [[Elzerina Cassalanter]] and [[Terenzio Cassalanter]] coins bearing his sigil that would grant them an audience with him at any time.
- Used Sending to warn the high priestess at the [[Temple of Eilistraee]] when the twins came under supernatural attack.
- Supported the prayers for the twins by singing “Somewhere Over the Rainbow.”
- Identified the missing mushroom singers as [[Campestri]] that were not under any spells.
- Helped recover all eight Campestri and invited [[Gwendolan]] and the choir to the grand opening of [[Trollskull Manor]].
- Paid the party for their time working for him and offered another year of employment. The amount, terms, and acceptance are unknown.
- During the six-month downtime, removed, dismantled, and sold the adamantine doors from the [[Vault of Dragons]] to fund [[Dick's Magical Retail Network]].
- Interviewed with the [[Lords of Waterdeep]] and became Open Lord of [[Waterdeep]]. [[Laeral Silverhand]] now serves as an advisor; no other role was disclosed to the players.

### Session 8

- Was formally invested as Open Lord after Laeral introduced him before Waterdeep's nobles.
- Used Disguise Self to appear slightly thinner and without visible blemishes during the ceremony.
- Knelt and placed the [[Crown of Silver Tongues]] on his head.
- Promised to make Waterdeep a center of commerce and trade whose prosperity would benefit the whole population.
- Received unspecified gifts from nobles.
- Gave each of his five companions an [[Earring of Message]] and two [[Clockwork Amulet|Clockwork Amulets]], and gave [[Cordan]] a [[Survival Mantle]]. Joe's additional personal gift remains unidentified.
- Led the public procession to the grand opening of [[The Black Key]].
- Paid Market food vendors to serve everyone for free and paid nearby taverns to close for the day.
- Cast Revivify on [[Lilith D Gluv]]'s defeated arena opponent.
- Used Subtle Spell Sending to warn [[Twizzle]] to remain calm around [[Lord Zulpair]].
- Arranged a first-light meeting with Zulpair and requested the previous year's shipping ledgers.
- Suspects Zulpair may be one of Waterdeep's concealed Lords, but this is unconfirmed.
- Drew a [[Spyglass|spyglass]] during [[Bagman's Bet]]; whether he retained it is unknown.
