## Campaign Events

### Session 1

- Gathered and hired the party at [[The Yawning Portal]].
- Offered each person 100 gp for one season of morally acceptable work, paid half in advance, promised a possible performance bonus, and agreed to provide required equipment.
- Attempted to stop the opening brawl with words.
- Questioned patrons at [[The Skewered Dragon]] with [[Twizzle]].
- Recognized [[Renaer Neverember]] at the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]].
- Ritually cast Identify on the [[Unknown Stone and Pedestal|stone and pedestal]] in the sewer hideout, learning that they combine to teleport one individual to an unknown destination.
- Kept the stone.
- Negotiated with [[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]] over payment and the transfer of [[Trollskull Manor]].
- Hosted the party overnight at [[Dick's Manor]].

### Session 2

- Paid for climbing gear for himself, [[Cordan]], and [[Joe Bauers]] under their contracts, and for [[Twizzle]] after she altered her contract.
- Negotiated permission for [[Volothamp Geddarm|Volo]] to study the [[Spectral Bartender|poltergeist]] in exchange for book credit and an advance copy.
- Helped persuade [[Hlam]] to share his warning.
- Returned to the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]] with Cordan, killed the two guards, and recovered the hidden crates.
- Fenced the recovered goods for 1,040 gp and received a 30% share, or 312 gp.
- Heard the halfling family hiding in their cellar after the scarecrow fight.
- Plans to negotiate exclusive rights to sell [[Snobeedle Cider]] at [[Trollskull Manor]].
- Reached level 3.
- During downtime, bought +1 half plate for 1,500 gp and gave it to [[Joe Bauers]].
- Bought a Tome of Leadership and Influence for 15,000 gp.
- Began marketing Trollskull Manor's planned grand opening.

### Session 3

- Negotiated the renovation price for [[Trollskull Manor]] down to 1,000 gp total because of [[Cordan]]'s assistance.
- Used Detect Magic after the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]] and found a [[Necklace of Fireballs]] in a rain barrel.
- Secretly placed the necklace in [[Dick's Magical Jacket]] before revealing it to the party.
- Examined the five recovered gems and found them high quality but otherwise unremarkable.
- Surrendered the necklace and gems to the authorities after discussing the legal and investigative implications with the party.
- Requested and received a copy of the [[Rooftop Puppet Sketch]].
- Successfully posed as a Zhentarim member while questioning dead sellswords under Speak with Dead.

### Session 4

- Convinced a Zhentarim thug at [[Gralhund Villa]] that the party were reinforcements, causing him to lead them to the fighting.
- Learned from [[Yalah Gralhund]] that the [[Stone of Golorr]] had reportedly been sent to the Cassalanters.
- Detected and identified [[Horseshoes of Speed]] in the Gralhund barn but left them there.
- Recognized Cordan's drawing as the holy symbol of [[Siamorphe]].
- Proposed approaching the guards or courts to help [[Lilith D Gluv]] while the others sought evidence at [[Cassalanter Villa]].
- Reached level 4.
