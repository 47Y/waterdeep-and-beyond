# Twizzle

## Summary

- Player character.
- Level 5 Half-Elf Warlock.
- Patron: The Hexblade.
- Background: Sailor.
- Rules: D&D 5e 2014.

## Narrative Details

- Age: 80.
- Gender: Female.
- Alignment: chaotic good.
- Faith: [[Hadar]].
- Appearance: hazel eyes, dark brown hair, light skin, 6'3", 150 lb.
- Survivor of a vicious attack on her family's merchant ship.
- Her downtime investigation identified [[Lord Zulpair]] as responsible for destroying her home and attacking her family.
- Is in hiding because she suspects they are looking for her.
- Contact: [[Obaya Uday]], a cleric of [[Waukeen]]. The nature of their connection is unknown.
- Trait: never passes up a friendly wager.
- Ideal: Fairness.
- Bond: has a paramour in a harbor town whose eyes nearly stole her from the sea.
- Flaw: once she starts drinking, it is hard for her to stop.

## Campaign Events

### Session 1

- Was sent by [[Obaya Uday]] to meet the party at [[The Yawning Portal]].
- Slipped away to investigate [[Old Xoblob Shop]] while the party examined the site of a bloody clash in the Dock Ward.
- Questioned patrons at [[The Skewered Dragon]] with [[Dick Mixon]].
- Helped question the surviving kenku at the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]].
- Used Divine Sense in [[Trollskull Manor]] and discovered a [[Spectral Bartender|spectral bartender]].

### Session 2

- Joined the [[Gray Hands]].
- Altered her contract so [[Dick Mixon]] would pay for her climbing gear.
- Gained two levels of exhaustion while climbing [[Mount Waterdeep]].
- Asked [[Vajra Safahr]] to help with her goal of removing corruption from the guard.
- Asked Vajra about purchasing magic items, particularly a Ring of Protection.
- Received 78 gp from the fenced warehouse loot.
- Joined the [[Emerald Enclave]].
- Reached level 3.
- Researched Hadar during downtime and was told she would receive information between sessions.

### Session 3

- Found five high-quality gems worth 100 gp each after the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]].
- Recognized the recovered necklace as a [[Necklace of Fireballs]].
- Witnessed the Speak with Dead questioning connected to the investigation.

### Session 4

- Fought Zhentarim attackers at [[Gralhund Villa]].
- Recognized Cordan's drawing as the holy symbol of [[Siamorphe]].
- Reached level 4.

### Session 5

- Used a Paper Bird to update [[Dick Mixon]] about the surveillance of [[Cassalanter Villa]].
- Used Misty Step to join [[Cordan]] on a rooftop and helped kill three trapped spined devils.
- Used Misty Step to reach the middle bridge door in the [[Vault of Dragons]] and secured a rope for the others.
- Received 20,000 gp as her personal share of the Vault hoard.
- Reached level 5.

### Session 6

- Told [[Laeral Silverhand]] that being surrounded by guards made her uneasy. They left open a future private conversation.
- Helped kill two [[Grell|grells]] in the Dock Ward.
- Gained 2 [[Emerald Enclave]] renown, reaching 3, and received a [[Charm of Heroism]].
- Waited in the hidden scrying room during [[Dick Mixon]]'s meeting with [[Victoro Cassalanter]].

### Session 7

- Contacted [[Hadar]] with a drop of blood and a prayer while seeking help for the [[Cassalanter Children|Cassalanter twins]]. She experienced a sensation of being underwater followed by amusement and indifference; Hadar did not help.
- Joined the prayers at the [[Temple of Eilistraee]] that saved the twins' souls.
- Shot a moving bag carried by an old man, injuring his hand and freeing two [[Campestri]]. She tied him up and questioned him before releasing him.
- Helped recover all eight missing Campestri for [[Gwendolan]].
- During the six-month downtime, obtained +1 half plate and watched the guards for unusual activity. No surveillance results were recorded.

### Session 8

- Received an [[Earring of Message]] and two [[Clockwork Amulet|Clockwork Amulets]] from [[Dick Mixon]] during the investiture ceremony.
- Fought [[Joe Bauers]] in [[The Black Key]]'s arena and lost decisively.
- Confronted [[Lord Zulpair]], whom her downtime investigation connected to the destruction of her home and attack on her family.
- Tried to follow Dick's Subtle Spell Sending instruction to “play it cool,” but glared at Zulpair and became aggressive.
- Was given a drink and subtly directed to a nearby table by [[Cordan]].
- Drew a [[Wand of Fireballs]] during [[Bagman's Bet]]; whether she retained it is unknown.
