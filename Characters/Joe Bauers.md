## Narrative Details

- Born, raised, and lives in [[Waterdeep]].
- Age: 34.
- Gender: Male.
- Appearance: blue eyes, dark brown hair, white skin, 5'11", 210 lb.
- Works as City Watch cavalry in Waterdeep.
- Enlisted at nineteen and served for eleven years.
- Has a northern heritage through his mother, who came south out of the Spine of the World.
- Had a partner named [[Ketch]] who died during an incident in the Dock Ward.
- Joe cannot account for the last two minutes of that incident.
- Since then, when fighting starts, something old seems to rise out of him.
- Still wears the uniform.
- His superiors sometimes second him to matters the Watch would rather not formally own.
- Personality trait: answers with the shortest true sentence available, then stops talking.
- Personality trait: puts himself between people and doorways without deciding to.
- Ideal: Interposition.
- Bond: Ketch died at his feet in a doorway he was holding, and the report was only four lines long.
- Flaw: does not step back, even when it would be the correct decision.
- Flaw: has never tried to account for the missing two minutes.

## Campaign Events

### Session 1

- Was sent to meet the party at [[The Yawning Portal]] by [[Saeth Cromley]].
- Was the only person who did not immediately accept [[Dick Mixon]]'s employment offer.
- Used his City Watch position to obtain additional information at the site of a bloody clash in the Dock Ward.
- Helped question the surviving kenku at the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]].

### Session 2

- Received a mental summons from [[Vajra Safahr]] and brought the party to [[Blackstaff Tower]].
- Joined the [[Gray Hands]].
- Received climbing gear paid for by [[Dick Mixon]] under their contract.
- Pretended to choke and slide down [[Mount Waterdeep]], but everyone recognized the performance as fake.
- Received a heavy crossbow and bolts from [[Cordan]].
- Received +1 half plate from [[Dick Mixon]] and replaced his scale mail with it.
- Received 78 gp from the fenced warehouse loot.
- Joined the [[Emerald Enclave]].
- Reached level 3.
- Spent two weeks pit fighting during downtime and earned 200 gp, or 100 gp per week.

### Session 3

- Asked [[Saeth Cromley]] whether fireballs had been used in other attacks around Waterdeep, but Cromley declined to answer because the party was considered suspect by default.
- Questioned witnesses with [[Lilith D Gluv]], obtaining accounts from [[Fala Legaliir]] and [[Jezrynne Hornraven]].
- Witnessed the Speak with Dead questioning of [[Dalakhar]], [[Bashekk Ortallis]], and [[Wern Malkrave]].

### Session 4

- Kicked in the wrong door while trying to interrupt fighting at [[Gralhund Villa]].
- Fought Zhentarim attackers alongside the Gralhund house guards.
- Went downstairs with [[Lilith D Gluv]] and [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]] to fight the remaining thugs.
- Called for help after Lilith was knocked unconscious, prompting [[Cordan]] to heal her.
- Reached level 4.
