## Campaign Events

### Session 1

- Joined the party at [[The Yawning Portal]] at [[Dick Mixon]]'s invitation because of an unspecified prior connection.
- Worked with [[Durnan]] and [[Yagra]] to push an unconscious troll back into the well.
- Helped question the surviving kenku at the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]].
- Fell unconscious during the sewer fight. An intellect devourer tried and failed to take control of her body before it was killed.

### Session 2

- Joined the [[Gray Hands]].
- Asked [[Vajra Safahr]] about her brother, [[Damien L Gluv]].
- Received 78 gp from the fenced warehouse loot.
- Reached level 3.
- Researched intellect devourers during downtime, beginning in Dick's library, and was told she would receive information between sessions.
- Learned from [[Blackstaff Academy]] that Damien transferred to [[Starshine Academy]] in [[Neverwinter]] about seven months earlier.

### Session 3

- Questioned witnesses with [[Joe Bauers]], obtaining accounts from [[Fala Legaliir]] and [[Jezrynne Hornraven]].
- Witnessed the Speak with Dead questioning connected to the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]].

### Session 4

- Fought Zhentarim attackers at [[Gralhund Villa]].
- Was knocked unconscious and suffered two failed death saves before [[Cordan]] healed her.
- Attempted to enter [[Cassalanter Villa]] by posing as a courier delivering the [[Stone of Golorr]].
- Used an ordinary rock as a decoy Stone.
- Killed the villa's [[Cassalanter Doppelganger|doppelganger]] head servant after her Command spell failed.
- Was discovered, attacked, and captured by [[Victoro Cassalanter]], [[Ammalia Cassalanter]], and an unidentified noble.
- Claimed she had entered because a friend bet that she could not.
- Was bound, blindfolded, and turned over to the City Watch.

### Session 5

- Refused questioning without a lawyer after spending the night in a cell.
- Briefed [[Dick Mixon]] while he impersonated [[Richard Nixon]].
- Was released after Dick persuaded the authorities to drop all charges.
- Recovered to full health with a short rest before rejoining the party at the [[Abandoned Windmill]].
- Helped kill three spined devils and carried one corpse away.
- Destroyed the enchanted fresco that had enthralled [[Mr. T (Tathlynd)]].
- Helped open the secret treasure room in the [[Vault of Dragons]].
- Received 20,000 gp as her personal share of the Vault hoard.
- Reached level 5.

### Session 6

- Recounted her experience at [[Cassalanter Villa]] to [[Laeral Silverhand]].
- Said her only substantial [[Gray Hands|Force Grey]] work so far had been visiting [[Hlam]].
- Helped kill two [[Grell|grells]] in the Dock Ward.
- Waited in the hidden scrying room during [[Dick Mixon]]'s meeting with [[Victoro Cassalanter]].
