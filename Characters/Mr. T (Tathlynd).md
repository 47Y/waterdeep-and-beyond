# Mr. T (Tathlynd)

## Summary

- Player character.
- Level 5 Drow Ranger.
- Subclass listed in export: Gloom Stalker.
- Background: Haunted One.
- Rules: D&D 5e 2014.

## Narrative Details

- Name: Tathlynd.
- Age: 175.
- Alignment: Chaotic Good.
- Gender: Male.
- Appearance: dark gray skin, extensive scars from punishments in drow society, lavender mohawk, glowing violet eyes, 6' tall, 145 lb.
- Faith: Eilistraee.
- Deserted drow society during his first raid.
- Was hunted by four members of the raid and nearly killed.
- Rescued by an ancient dragon named [[Dirlem]], who later brought him to an elven settlement and vouched for him.
- Earned a place among the settlement's defenders and later trained as a ranger.
- Helped defend the settlement from a drow raiding party.
- Eventually began traveling to atone for the atrocities of drow society.
- Joined the [[Emerald Enclave]].
- When in Waterdeep, often checks in with [[Melannor Fellbranch]].
- Trait: a worthless gold chain with the symbol of Eilistraee is an emblem of who he is.
- Ideal: Balance.
- Bond: has seen great darkness and is committed to being a light against it.
- Flaw: believes something is after him, appearing in mirrors, dreams, and places where no one could.

## Campaign Events

### Session 1

- Was sent by [[Melannor Fellbranch]] to meet the party at [[The Yawning Portal]].
- Helped question the surviving kenku at the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]].

### Session 2

- Wanted to consult the [[Emerald Enclave]] before joining the [[Gray Hands]].
- Gained two levels of exhaustion while climbing [[Mount Waterdeep]].
- Helped persuade [[Hlam]] to share his warning and was invited to return.
- Received 78 gp from the fenced warehouse loot.
- Was contacted by a [[Talking Cat|talking cat]] and assigned to investigate [[Scarecrow Trouble]].
- Received [[Melannor Fellbranch]]'s approval to join the Gray Hands and plans to do so when able.
- Reached level 3.
- Bought books and [[Sunbeam Tea]] before returning to visit Hlam.
- Purchased *[[Demonology - Incubi and Succubi|Demonology: Incubi and Succubi]]*.
- Foraged for and sold 100 gp worth of herbs and spices to an apothecary.

### Session 3

- Sent a courier to [[Melannor Fellbranch]] asking what the [[Emerald Enclave]] knew about [[Gralhund Villa]].
- Witnessed the Speak with Dead questioning connected to the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]].

### Session 4

- Fought Zhentarim attackers at [[Gralhund Villa]].
- Went downstairs with [[Lilith D Gluv]] and [[Joe Bauers]] to fight the remaining thugs.
- Recognized Cordan's drawing as the holy symbol of [[Siamorphe]].
- Reached level 4.

### Session 5

- Sent [[Dick Mixon]] a Paper Bird with the party's destination after the [[Cassalanter Mausoleum]] investigation.
- Helped evacuate the prisoners from the [[Abandoned Windmill]] and helped kill the trapped spined devils.
- Searched Dick's library with [[Cordan]] for information on aboleths but found nothing.
- Found no traps on the door beneath the [[Old Tower]].
- Took the first watch while the party rested before opening the [[Vault of Dragons]].
- Was enthralled by a magical fresco and resisted the party's attempts to pull him away until they destroyed it.
- Opened a hidden passage by confessing in Dwarvish, "It was me, I was the one who attempted to poison the matron."
- Received 20,000 gp as his personal share of the Vault hoard.
- Reached level 5.

### Session 6

- Told [[Laeral Silverhand]] about his escape from darkness and personal rebirth while declining to revisit details of his life below ground.
- Used Disguise Self while [[Yagra]] testified under Zone of Truth.
- Helped kill two [[Grell|grells]] and delivered one corpse to the [[Emerald Enclave]].
- Gained 2 Enclave renown, reaching 3, and received a [[Charm of Heroism]].
- Asked [[Melannor Fellbranch]] about [[Asmodeus]] and was warned that he is the supreme master of the Nine Hells.
- Waited in the hidden scrying room during [[Dick Mixon]]'s meeting with [[Victoro Cassalanter]].
