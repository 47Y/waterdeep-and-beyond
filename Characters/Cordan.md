# Cordan

## Summary

- Player character.
- Level 4 Eladrin Druid.
- Subclass: Circle of Stars.
- Background: Far Traveler.
- Rules: D&D 5e 2014.

## Narrative Details

- Born and raised in [[Astraelith]], a luminous Eladrin city in the Feywild.
- Age: 130.
- Left the Feywild about a year and a half ago to investigate the [[Starless Convergence]] and compare Feywild stars with the Material Plane sky.
- Has spent most of his time on the Material Plane based in or around [[Waterdeep]].
- Has been back in Waterdeep continuously for about three months.
- Considers himself a traveler rather than a permanent resident of the Material Plane.
- Keeps most of his star research private.
- Carries the [[Fey Branch]], a living staff given to him by [[Elyndra Moonbough]].
- Has privately kept [[Marnock Stonewhistle]]'s warning about Waterdeep.
- Father: [[Theron Vaelith]].
- Mother: [[Maelira Vaelith]].
- Younger sister: [[Seris Vaelith]].
- Personality: curious, observant, literal-minded, and unusually drawn to strange details.
- Appearance: pale skin and shoulder-length dark hair. He normally wears basic clothing over his armor with little ornamentation.
- A section of skin on the right side of his face and down his neck changes color with his current seasonal attunement: pale blue in winter, pale green in spring, pale orange in autumn, and pale yellow in summer.
- Alignment: Lawful Neutral.
- Ideals, bonds, flaws, and additional traits: To be added.
- Current goal: understand the Starless Convergence and why Waterdeep may matter to it.
- Remained in Waterdeep primarily because of [[Marnock Stonewhistle]]'s warning and his curiosity about it.

## Campaign Events

### Session 1

- Was already seated with [[Dick Mixon]] at [[The Yawning Portal]] when the party gathered.
- Remained seated during most of the troll fight and, when called upon near its end, asked Dick, "Do I have to?"
- Searched the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]] while the others questioned two captured kenku.
- Found mundane goods, a hidden door, 15 silver bars, and four paintings depicting [[Luskan]], [[Neverwinter]], [[Silverymoon]], and [[Baldur's Gate]]. As someone not from the Material Plane, he likely did not recognize the cities by sight.
- Closed the hidden door and left the paintings and silver behind for later.
- Found [[Renaer Neverember]] in a closet beneath the stairs.
- Warned Dick, "Do not let curiosity become the hand that turns the final key," when Dick picked up the [[Unknown Stone and Pedestal|unknown stone]].
- Secretly took the dead intellect devourer for later study.
- Between sessions, performed an inexperienced examination of the corpse while the non-elves slept and learned very little.

### Session 2

- Left [[Dick Mixon]] a note about the hidden loot in the [[Zhentarim Warehouse]].
- Received climbing gear paid for by Dick under their contract.
- Warned Volo during negotiations over studying the [[Spectral Bartender|poltergeist]]: "When someone gives more than was owed, take care before teaching them to regret it."
- Returned to the Zhentarim Warehouse with Dick, killed the two guards, and recovered the hidden crates.
- Took a heavy crossbow and bolts from one guard and later gave them to [[Joe Bauers]].
- Received 40% of the fenced warehouse loot, or 416 gp.
- Joined the [[Emerald Enclave]].
- Reached level 3.
- During downtime, used mason's and carpenter's tools to help shorten the renovation of [[Trollskull Manor]].
- Was invited to join the [[Mason's Guild]] and [[Carpenter's Guild]] but has not decided whether to join either.

### Session 3

- His renovation work helped [[Dick Mixon]] negotiate the total [[Trollskull Manor]] renovation cost down to 1,000 gp.
- Told Dick that withholding the recovered [[Necklace of Fireballs]] would be a crime against the city, leading Dick to surrender it and the gems.
- Asked a guard whether he knew of [[Darkhold Castle]], but the guard did not recognize the name.
- Witnessed the Speak with Dead questioning connected to the [[Fireball at Trollskull Manor]].

### Session 4

- Picked the gate lock at [[Gralhund Villa]] with thieves' tools.
- Cast Healing Word on [[Lilith D Gluv]] after she was knocked unconscious and suffered two failed death saves.
- Saw Lilith taken inside [[Cassalanter Villa]] by its head servant.
- Scouted the villa in spider form after hearing concerning sounds.
- Found the dead [[Cassalanter Doppelganger|doppelganger]] and a painting of [[Siamorphe]]'s holy symbol.
- Secretly followed [[Victoro Cassalanter]] and [[Ammalia Cassalanter]] through a hidden door and observed them concealing the body, donning red robes and devil masks, and joining a ritual before a statue of [[Asmodeus]].
- Suspected, but could not verify, that the ritual language was Infernal.
- Heard Victoro declare, "Founder's Day approaches."
- Escaped through the secret door's imperfect seal using Fey Step.
- Drew the holy symbol, masks, and amulets for the party.
- Used Eladrin Trance to gain temporary proficiency with cartographer's tools and completed a [[Cordan's Map of Cassalanter Villa|map of the explored rooms]] the following morning.
- Reached level 4.
