# Bierstiefel Relay

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A glass boot, two litres of ale, passed hand to hand around the table. The trick is the toe: tilt it wrong and an air bubble rushes down and the beer explodes all over your face. The deeper the boot goes, the harder the toe is to manage.*

**Description:** A drinking game around **one shared glass beer boot**. The boot holds **8 gulps**. It goes around the table, and on your turn you drink **1, 2 or 3 gulps**, then pass it on. Every gulp is a **Sleight of Hand** check, and the emptier the boot gets, the harder it is: most of the air is trapped in the toe, waiting to splash you. Splash and you sit out the rest of the boot. **Drink the 8th gulp cleanly and you win the pot.**

## How to Play

### How the boot works

- **There is only one boot.** Everyone drinks from it, one at a time, passing it to the left.
- **Count the gulps for the whole boot**, not for each drinker. The first gulp anyone takes is gulp 1, the next gulp anyone takes is gulp 2, and so on up to **gulp 8**, the last of the beer.
- **The gulp number sets the DC** (see the table below).
- **The boot keeps going around** until gulp 8 is drunk, or until everyone has splashed or gone Out.

| Gulp (for the whole boot) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DC | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 18 | 20 | 22 |

### Setting up

- Every drinker puts **5 sp** into the pot. This is your entry for this boot.
- If the last boot had no winner, its pot carries over into this one.
- The House pours the boot (**8 gulps**) and takes **2 sp** from the pot for the beer.
- Pick who drinks first. The boot passes to the left.

### More than 8 drinkers

- **No table seats more than 8.** Split the drinkers into tables as evenly as you can: 9 drinkers make tables of 5 and 4, and 21 make three tables of 7.
- **There is one shared pot.** Every entry and every splash from every table goes into it, and the House takes its 2 sp for each boot poured at each table.
- **No waiting.** As soon as a table finishes a boot, the House pours it the next one. A fast table drinks more boots, so the more drinkers there are, the bigger the rush.
- **The first clean gulp 8 at any table wins the whole pot.** If drinkers at two tables drink gulp 8 cleanly at the same moment, they split the pot. The Floor Watcher calls it.

### Your turn, step by step

1. **Take the boot and declare** how many gulps you'll drink: **1, 2 or 3**.
2. **Drink them one at a time.** Each gulp is a **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check against the next gulp's DC.
   - **Success:** a clean gulp. Go on to the next one you declared.
   - **Failure:** **splash!** One gulp's worth of beer goes over your face. That gulp is gone, but the rest of the boot is still there: the next drinker carries on from the next gulp. You put **5 sp into the pot**, your turn ends, and you **sit out the rest of this boot**. You can buy into the next boot as normal.
3. **Constitution save.** At the end of your turn (splash or not), make one **Constitution saving throw** at **DC 10** for the beer you've just drunk (see the Drunk Track).
4. **Pass the boot** to the next drinker on your left who is still in this boot.

### Winning

- Whoever drinks **gulp 8 cleanly** wins the whole pot.
- **If gulp 8 splashes**, or every drinker has splashed or gone Out first, nobody wins. The pot carries over to the next boot.
- **If nobody has won by the end of the night**, the pot carries over to next week's relay. It keeps growing, week after week, until someone drinks gulp 8 cleanly.
- **After a win**, the next boot starts a new pot.

### Drunk Track

![[Drunk Track#Rules]]

**Being Drunk hurts here:** a Drunk drinker has disadvantage on the Sleight of Hand checks.

**Going Out:** a drinker who goes **Out** on the Drunk Track is **done for the night**. They can't drink from this boot or any other boot, or play any other drinking game, until they've had a long rest. Their entry stays in the pot.

## Dice Examples

**Drinkers** (in order around the table): Thokk (NPC, Sleight of Hand −1, Con save +5 with advantage), Mirela (PC, Sleight of Hand +2, Con save +1), "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Sleight of Hand +0, Con save +4) and Quill (PC, Sleight of Hand +7, Con save +1). Each puts in 5 sp, so the pot is **20 sp**. The House takes 2 sp for the beer: **18 sp**.

**Thokk** takes the full boot and declares 2.
- Gulp 1 (DC 8): **12** − 1 = **11**. Clean.
- Gulp 2 (DC 10): **13** − 1 = **12**. Clean.
- Con save: **8** and **15**, keeps 15: 15 + 5 = **20**. Pass. He hands the boot to Mirela. **6 gulps left.**

**Mirela** declares 1.
- Gulp 3 (DC 12): **11** + 2 = **13**. Clean.
- Con save: **14** + 1 = **15**. Pass. **5 gulps left.**

**Oswin** declares 3.
- Gulp 4 (DC 14): **15** + 0 = **15**. Clean.
- Gulp 5 (DC 16): **9** + 0 = **9**. **Splash!** Gulp 5 is gone. Oswin puts 5 sp into the pot (**23 sp**) and sits out the rest of this boot. His third gulp never happens.
- Con save: **11** + 4 = **15**. Pass. **3 gulps left.**

**Quill** declares 3, going for the win.
- Gulp 6 (DC 18): **14** + 7 = **21**. Clean.
- Gulp 7 (DC 20): **16** + 7 = **23**. Clean.
- Gulp 8 (DC 22): **17** + 7 = **24**. **Clean! Quill wins the pot: 23 sp (2 gp 3 sp).**

**Edge case: gulp 8 splashes**
- If Quill had rolled 14 on gulp 8 (14 + 7 = 21), he'd splash and put 5 sp into the pot. Nobody wins, and the 28 sp carries over to the next boot. Everyone, Oswin included, can buy into that boot.

**Edge case: counting the boot**
- Say only Quill and Thokk are still in, and Quill takes the boot with **5 gulps left** (gulps 4 to 8). He declares **1**, leaving 4 for Thokk. Whatever Thokk takes (1, 2 or 3), Quill can take the rest on his next turn and drink gulp 8, as long as nobody splashes.

**Edge case: the boot comes round again**
- If Quill had declared only 1 (gulp 6), the boot would go back round to Thokk for gulp 7, then to Mirela. After Mirela it would skip Oswin, who is sitting out this boot, and come back to Quill.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 5 sp from every drinker, every boot.
- **Splash:** 5 sp into the pot.
- **Winner:** takes the whole pot. If there's no winner, the pot carries over to the next boot, and from night to night until someone wins.
- **The House keeps:** 2 sp from the pot for each boot it pours.

## Odds

**Chance of a clean gulp:**

| Sleight of Hand | Gulp 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| −1 | 60% | 50% | 40% | 30% | 20% | 10% | 0% | 0% |
| +2 | 75% | 65% | 55% | 45% | 35% | 25% | 15% | 5% |
| +3 | 80% | 70% | 60% | 50% | 40% | 30% | 20% | 10% |
| +7 | 100% | 90% | 80% | 70% | 60% | 50% | 40% | 30% |

**A night at one table of 8 drinkers**, where every drinker has the same bonuses (20,000 simulated nights). Each drinker takes 1 gulp a turn, unless they can reach gulp 8 this turn, in which case they go for it.

| Sleight of Hand / Con save | Pot won tonight | Boots until it's won | Pot when it's won | Paid per drinker by then | Still in (not Out) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +0 / +0 | **Never** | — | — | — | — |
| +2 / +1 | 19% | About 3 | About 170 sp | About 22 sp | 7 of 8 |
| +5 / +2 | 69% | About 3 | About 155 sp | About 20 sp | 7 of 8 |

- **A drinker with +0 can never win.** The best roll is 20 + 0 = 20, short of gulp 8's DC 22.
- **Wins come early.** Once drinkers are Drunk, they have disadvantage on their gulps, and gulp 8 is almost out of reach.
- **When nobody wins**, everyone is Out after about 9 or 10 boots, and about **400 sp** rolls over to next week.
- **More tables** mean more tries at gulp 8, so a big night is won more often.

**Counting is everything.** Only gulp 8 wins. On your turn, count around the table: if you take 1, 2 or 3 gulps, who will be holding the boot at gulp 8? Pick the number that puts gulp 8 in your hands, or keeps it away from a strong rival. But every extra gulp is another, harder roll, and one splash means you sit out the rest of the boot.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any choice of 1, 2 or 3 gulps.
- Turning the boot toe-sideways (the correct technique, and part of the Sleight of Hand check).
- Dwarven Resilience on the Constitution saves.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *mage hand*, *prestidigitation*, *guidance*, *lesser restoration* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Jostling another drinker.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jostle the drinker's elbow, made to look like an accident | Anyone at the table | Cha (Deception) | That drinker's next gulp has disadvantage |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant*) | A caster at the table or in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The invisible servant steadies the boot: advantage on one gulp |
| Hidden *guidance*, by touch: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the shoulder | The drinker or a friend | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | +1d4 on one gulp |

### Cheating dice example

During Oswin's turn, Mirela jostles his elbow as he lifts the boot.
- Mirela: Deception **6** + 7 = **13**. The Watcher: **14** + 5 = **19**. **Caught.**
- Mirela is disqualified from the boot (her 5 sp stays in the pot), banned for 3 months, and pinned to the notice board. To clear her name: 5 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The Big Boot** is a five-litre monster kept behind the bar, brought out only for weddings and wakes. Tonight's wedding party has dared the groom to finish it alone.
- **A drinker who never splashes** has won six weeks running. His boots always seem to have a tiny chip in the toe.
