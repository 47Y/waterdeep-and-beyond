# Sausage Roulette

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Six fat sausages on a plate, steaming and identical. One of them is stuffed with dragon's-breath pepper. Pick one and bite. Get the hot one and keep a straight face, and the whole pot is yours. Cough and splutter, and the pot rolls on to the next plate.*

**Description:** A daily dare at the bar. Players take turns picking from a plate of **six sausages**. **One is the hot one.** Whoever bites it makes a **Constitution save**. Pass and you **win the pot**; fail and you're left red-faced and streaming (**poisoned for 10 minutes**), and the pot **rolls over** to the next plate.

## How to Play

### A plate, step by step

1. **Everyone puts 1 sp** into the pot.
2. **The cook secretly picks the hot one.** The DM numbers the sausages 1 to 6 and rolls a d6 in secret.
3. **Players pick in turn**, starting with whoever paid first and going around to the left. Each player picks one sausage and eats it on the spot. A picked sausage is gone.
   - **Sniff test (optional):** before you pick, you may sniff **one** sausage with a **Wisdom (Perception)** check, **DC 18**. On a success, the DM tells you privately whether that one is the hot one. On a failure, you can't tell.
4. **Whoever bites the hot one** makes a **Constitution saving throw, DC 15**. The plate is over, and nobody else picks.
   - **Success:** a straight face. **You win the pot**, minus the House's 10%.
   - **Failure:** coughing, streaming eyes, frantic grabbing for milk. You're **poisoned for 10 minutes**, and the pot **rolls over** to the next plate.
5. **If everyone has picked and nobody bit the hot one** (fewer than 6 players), the pot rolls over.

**Pepper isn't poison**, but the burn has the same effect as the poisoned condition. Dwarven Resilience and other bonuses against poison don't help with the save.

### The next plate

Each new plate starts with the player after whoever picked last. Everyone still playing puts in another 1 sp, which joins any pot that rolled over. You can drop out before any plate. Plates keep coming until the players stop or the kitchen runs out. A pot nobody has won by then carries over to tomorrow's first plate.

## Dice Examples

**Players** (in order): Quill (PC, Perception +6, Con save +1), Thokk (NPC, Con save +5) and Mirela (PC, Con save +1). The pot is 3 sp. The DM rolls a **4**: sausage 4 is the hot one.

- **Quill** sniffs sausage 4: Perception **13** + 6 = **19**. Success. The DM tells Quill privately: "That one's the hot one." With a Con save of +1, Quill doesn't want it. He picks **sausage 2**. Nothing happens.
- **Thokk** picks **sausage 4**: the hot one! Con save **11** + 5 = **16**. **A straight face.** Thokk wins the 3 sp, minus 10%: **2 sp 7 cp**.

**Next plate** (the DM rolls a **1**). The pot is a fresh 3 sp, and Mirela goes first.
- **Mirela** picks **sausage 1**: the hot one! Con save **9** + 1 = **10**. **She fails.** Mirela is poisoned for 10 minutes. The pot rolls over: once everyone puts in another 1 sp, the next plate is worth 6 sp.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Cost:** 1 sp per player, per plate.
- **The pot:** goes to whoever bites the hot one and passes the save, or rolls over.
- **The House keeps:** 10% of each pot when it's won, for the kitchen.

## Odds

**With 6 players**, each player has a 1 in 6 chance of picking the hot one.

| Con save | Chance to pass DC 15 | Chance you win this plate |
|---|---|---|
| +1 | 35% | 5.8% |
| +3 | 45% | 7.5% |
| +5 | 55% | 9.2% |

**Sniff test:** Perception +6 succeeds 45% of the time; Perception +0 succeeds 15%.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- The sniff test, once per pick.
- Choosing to avoid the hot one (or go for it) once you know.
- Milk afterwards. Lots of milk.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *resistance*, *guidance* or *bless*. (*Detect poison and disease* does nothing: pepper isn't poison.)
- Bribing the cook, or holding butter in your mouth before you bite.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bribe the cook to tell you which one (2 sp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | You know which sausage is hot |
| Slip a lump of butter into your cheek before you bite (fat kills the burn) | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on the Constitution save |
| Hidden spell: *bless* from within 30 ft, or *resistance* by touch before you bite: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the back | A caster at the bar | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | +1d4 on the save |
| Hidden spell: *guidance* by touch before you sniff: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the back | A caster at the bar | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | +1d4 on the sniff test |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Sausage Roulette is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A caught cheater forfeits any pot they won on that plate.

### Cheating dice example

Mirela slips the cook 2 sp to learn the hot sausage on the next plate.
- Mirela: Persuasion **8** + 5 = **13**. The Watcher: **12** + 5 = **17**. **Caught.**
- Mirela is banned from Sausage Roulette for 3 months and pinned to the notice board, and the cook gets a telling-off from the barkeep. To clear her name: 1 gp, 1 day serving (washing the plates), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The cook, Big Hettie,** grows the dragon's-breath peppers herself, from seeds she says came out of a real dragon's lair. This week's batch is noticeably hotter.
- **A fire genasi** has won six plates in a row. The regulars want him banned. The House wants him back every night.
