# Stare-Down

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Two chairs, knee to knee. Lock eyes. Don't blink, don't look away, don't laugh. The crowd leans in, making faces and whistling, and the first one to break loses.*

**Description:** A weekly staring contest. Two contestants lock eyes, and each **round** both make a **Wisdom saving throw**. The DC goes up every round. **The first to fail loses the match.** Once per match, each contestant may pull a **funny face** to try to break their opponent.

## How to Play

### Setting up

Two chairs, knee to knee, with the Floor Watcher sitting beside them to judge. Blinking, looking away or laughing is a **break**. A round is about 30 seconds of staring, called by the Watcher.

### A round, step by step

1. **Both contestants make a Wisdom saving throw** at **DC 4 + 2 × the round number**.

   | Round | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | DC | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 18 | 20 |

2. **Results:**
   - **One fails:** they break. **The other wins.**
   - **Both pass:** go on to the next round.
   - **Both fail:** they break almost together, and the **higher total** held out longer and wins. If the totals are equal, play the same round again.

### The funny face (once per match)

- At the **start of any round**, you may pull a face. Make a **Charisma (Performance)** check. If both contestants want to, they do it at the same time.
- That round, your opponent's save DC is your Performance total, **or** the round's DC if that's higher.
- You still make your own save at the round's normal DC, unless your opponent pulled a face too.

## Dice Examples

**Contestants:** Mirela (PC, Wis save +1, Performance +7) and Thokk (NPC, Wis save +1).

| Round | DC | Mirela | Thokk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | **12** + 1 = 13 ✓ | **9** + 1 = 10 ✓ |
| 2 | 8 | **15** + 1 = 16 ✓ | **14** + 1 = 15 ✓ |
| 3 | 10 | Funny face: Performance **13** + 7 = **20**. Her own save: **17** + 1 = 18 ✓ | His DC is now **20**: **16** + 1 = 17 ✗ |

**Thokk snorts with laughter. Mirela wins** the 2 sp, minus 5%: **1 sp 9 cp**. Thokk also buys her a mug of ale.

**Edge case: both fail**
- If, in round 3, Mirela had rolled **8** + 1 = 9 and Thokk 17, both would have failed. Thokk's 17 is higher than her 9, so Thokk wins. Mirela's funny face didn't save her.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Stakes:** 1 sp a side, with a 5% House fee. The loser also buys the winner a mug of ale (4 cp). It's just a drink, with no save.
- **The House keeps:** the 5% fee, the price of the ale and a cut of the crowd's bets.

## Odds

**Your Wisdom save bonus minus your opponent's:**

| Bonus gap | Equal | +2 | +4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chance you win | 50% | about 65% | about 80% |

Without funny faces, a match lasts about **2 to 4 rounds** on average. Higher Wisdom saves last longer.

**Chance of passing each round** at Wis save +3:

| Round | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pass | 90% | 80% | 70% | 60% | 50% | 40% | 30% | 20% |

**Being Drunk barely matters here:** poisoned gives disadvantage on ability checks, not saving throws. It only hurts your funny face.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- One funny face per match.
- The crowd may whistle, shout and make faces at both contestants.
- Your own features that add to Wisdom saves, such as a paladin's Aura of Protection.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *resistance*, *bless*, *Tasha's hideous laughter* or Bardic Inspiration from someone else.
- Flashing light in the opponent's eyes, blowing smoke at them, or a second funny face.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catch the candlelight on a polished ring and flash it into the opponent's eyes | Anyone in the crowd | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The opponent has disadvantage on this round's save |
| Let pipe smoke drift into the opponent's eyes, made to look like an accident | Anyone in the crowd with a pipe | Cha (Deception) | The opponent has disadvantage on this round's save |
| Hidden spell: *bless* from within 30 ft, or *resistance* by touch before you sit: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the back | A caster in the crowd, or the contestant | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | *Bless*: +1d4 on your Wisdom saves for 1 minute (2 rounds). *Resistance*: +1d4 on one Wisdom save. |
| Hidden spell: *Tasha's hideous laughter* on the opponent, from within 30 ft | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The opponent makes a Wisdom save against your spell save DC. On a failure they burst out laughing, which is a break, and they lose. |
| Bardic Inspiration, shouted as a cheer | A bard in the crowd, within 60 ft | Cha (Deception) to pass it off as ordinary crowd noise | The contestant adds the die to one Wisdom save |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Stare-Down is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. If the cheat helped a contestant, that contestant loses the match.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 1 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

In round 2, Quill flashes candlelight off his ring at Thokk.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **9** + 7 = **16**. The Watcher: **14** + 5 = **19**. **Caught.**
- Mirela loses the match. Quill is banned from Stare-Down for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. If Mirela is found to be in on it, she gets the same penalty. To clear his name: 1 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The champion, a half-orc called Unblinking Vey,** hasn't lost in two years. Some say she's a warforged in disguise.
- **A medusa's portrait** hangs over the contest chairs. Stare-Down regulars avoid looking at it.
