# The Singdown

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *One singer steps onto the bench and belts out a verse. The room roars. Then the next singer climbs up, and has to do better. Every song raises the bar. Fall short and you're booed off. The last voice standing gets a month on the tavern's stage.*

**Description:** A monthly singing contest where each performance sets the bar for the next. The first singer's **Performance** total becomes the target. Every singer after them must **meet or beat** the current target to stay in, and their total becomes the new target. Fall short and you're out. Singers keep going around until only one is left.

## How to Play

### Setting up

Singers draw lots for the order. They keep that order all night.

### Step by step

On your turn, climb onto the bench and sing one verse. Your roll is how well the room takes it.

1. **The first singer** rolls **Charisma (Performance)**. Their total becomes the **target**.
2. **The next singer** rolls Charisma (Performance).
   - **Meets or beats the target:** they stay in, and their total becomes the **new target**.
   - **Below the target:** they're **booed off** and out. The target stays the same.
3. **Keep going around** in order, skipping anyone who is out. When it comes back to a singer, they must meet or beat the current target again, even if they set it.
4. **The last singer standing wins.**

## Dice Examples

**Singers** (in order): Thokk (NPC, Performance −1), Mirela (PC, Performance +7) and Lark Thistlewhite (NPC halfling minstrel, Performance +6).

**First time around**
- **Thokk** sings first: **14** − 1 = **13**. The target is **13**.
- **Mirela:** **9** + 7 = **16**. She beats 13 and stays in. The target is **16**.
- **Lark:** **12** + 6 = **18**. He beats 16 and stays in. The target is **18**.

**Second time around**
- **Thokk:** **15** − 1 = **14**. Below 18. **Thokk is booed off.**
- **Mirela:** **11** + 7 = **18**. She **meets** 18, so she stays in. The target stays **18**.
- **Lark:** **8** + 6 = **14**. Below 18. **Lark is booed off.**
- Mirela is the only singer left. **Mirela wins.**

**Edge case: an impossible target**
- If the target were 24 when Thokk's turn came, he'd need a 25 on the d20. Even a natural 20 gives him 19. He's out without a chance.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 5 sp per singer.
- **Winner:** 5 gp, plus a **one-month residency**: they sing in the tavern every evening for 2 gp a night. The winner can turn the residency down, or give it up at any time, and keeps the 5 gp.
- **The House keeps:** all entries and a very busy bar.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any song, any instrument, any language.
- Expertise and other natural bonuses to Performance.
- Playing your own instrument while you sing.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *enhance ability* (Eagle's Splendor), *guidance*, or Bardic Inspiration from someone else.
- A planted clapper in the crowd, or spoiling a rival's voice.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hidden spell (*enhance ability*, *guidance*), cast by touch before the singer climbs up | The singer, or a friend with a pat on the back | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | *Enhance ability*: advantage on Performance checks. *Guidance*: +1d4 on one Performance check. |
| Planted clappers in the crowd (2 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Deception) | +2 to one Performance total |
| Spike a rival's ale with pepper | Anyone | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's next Performance check has disadvantage |
| Bardic Inspiration from a friend | A bard in the crowd | Cha (Deception) | Add the die to one Performance check |

### Cheating dice example

Before the second time around, Lark slips pepper into Mirela's ale.
- Lark: Sleight of Hand **10** + 5 = **15**. The Watcher: **8** + 5 = **13**. Not caught.
- Mirela is sitting beside him: Perception **14** + 3 = **17**. She beats 15 and **sees it**. She can report Lark, demand a bribe, or keep quiet.
- She reports him. Lark is disqualified, banned for 3 months, and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 5 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Lark Thistlewhite** had won five Singdowns in a row until the hooded singer turned up. He needs the residency money badly, and he'll do almost anything to get it back.
- **A hooded singer** now enters every month, wins easily, and never collects the residency. Her voice makes the candles flicker.
