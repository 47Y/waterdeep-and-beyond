# Old Wizard

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A worn deck of cards, with one card nobody wants: the Old Wizard, a cackling grey-beard. Take a card from your neighbour's hand, try to read their face, and pray you don't pull the old man. Whoever's holding him at the end buys the round.*

**Description:** A monthly card game. Everyone holds a hand of cards, and one card, the **Old Wizard**, is the one nobody wants. Players take turns taking a card from their neighbour's hand, so the Old Wizard moves around the table in secret. **Whoever holds it after three times around the table loses** and buys a round for the table.

## How to Play

### Getting started

1. **Deal the cards.** The House deals out a whole deck, the same number of cards to each player. Only the Old Wizard matters, so nobody keeps track of the other cards. You don't need real cards at your table: the dice decide everything.
2. **Choose who starts with the Old Wizard.** The DM secretly picks a player at random and tells only that player.
3. **Choose who goes first.** Everyone rolls a d20. The highest goes first, then play goes left.

### Your turn

On your turn, you take one card from the player on your left. Follow these steps in order.

1. **Call it out, if you want to.** You can do this once per game. Name the player you think holds the Old Wizard.
   - **If you're right:** they show the card and buy you a drink. Now everyone knows who holds it.
   - **If you're wrong:** you buy the player you named a drink.
   - Either way, carry on with your turn.
2. **You both roll.** Always do this, even if the player on your left doesn't have the Old Wizard. That way, nobody can tell who has it.
   - **The player on your left** tries to fool you. They roll **Charisma (Deception)** or **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)**.
   - **You** try to read them. You roll **Wisdom (Insight)** or **Wisdom (Perception)**.
   - The higher total wins. **If it's a tie, you win.**
3. **Roll a d6 to take a card.** If the player on your left holds the Old Wizard, this decides whether you get it:

   | Who won step 2 | You get the Old Wizard on a… |
   |---|---|
   | The player on your left | 1–4 |
   | You | 1 only |

   If they don't hold it, you take a harmless card and nothing happens.
4. **If you got the Old Wizard,** the DM tells you privately. Only you and the player you took it from know.

### Ending the game

- Every player takes **three turns**.
- After the last turn, **whoever holds the Old Wizard** shows it and **buys a round for the table**.

## Dice Examples

**Players** (in seat order): Mirela (PC, Deception +7, Perception +3), Thokk (NPC, Deception −1, Insight +1) and Quill (PC, Sleight of Hand +7, Perception +6). Mirela takes a card from Thokk, Thokk takes one from Quill, and Quill takes one from Mirela.

**Getting started:** the DM secretly gives **Thokk** the Old Wizard.

**First time around**
- **Mirela takes a card from Thokk.** Thokk's Deception **12** − 1 = **11**. Mirela's Perception **9** + 3 = **12**. Mirela wins, so she gets the Old Wizard only on a 1. Her d6: **1**. Bad luck. **Mirela now has the Old Wizard.**
- **Thokk takes a card from Quill.** Quill's Sleight of Hand **8** + 7 = **15**. Thokk's Insight **14** + 1 = **15**. A tie, so Thokk wins. His d6: **3**. Quill doesn't have the Old Wizard, so nothing happens.
- **Quill takes a card from Mirela.** Mirela's Deception **16** + 7 = **23**. Quill's Perception **10** + 6 = **16**. Mirela wins, so Quill gets it on 1–4. His d6: **4**. **Quill now has the Old Wizard.**

**Second time around**
- **Thokk takes a card from Quill.** Quill's Sleight of Hand **11** + 7 = **18**. Thokk's Insight **6** + 1 = **7**. Quill wins, so Thokk gets it on 1–4. His d6: **5**. Thokk misses it.
- (The other two turns don't involve the Old Wizard.)

**Third time around**
- **Thokk takes a card from Quill.** Quill's Sleight of Hand **3** + 7 = **10**. Thokk's Insight **13** + 1 = **14**. Thokk wins, so he gets it only on a 1. His d6: **2**. Quill is stuck with it.

**Quill holds the Old Wizard at the end. Quill buys the round.**

**Edge case: calling it out**
- On her last turn, Mirela says "It's Thokk." It isn't, so she buys Thokk a drink, then takes a card from him as normal. If she'd named Quill, he'd have shown the card and bought her a drink.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **No entry.** The loser buys a round for the table. Ale is 4 cp a mug.
- **Calling it out:** if you're right, the holder buys you one drink. If you're wrong, you buy the player you named one drink.
- **The House keeps:** the round and the drinks.

## Odds

**Chance you get the Old Wizard on your turn, when the player on your left holds it:**

| Their bonus compared to yours | −4 | −2 | Equal | +2 | +4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| They win the roll | 30% | 38% | 47.5% | 57% | 66% |
| **You get the Old Wizard** | 32% | 36% | 40% | 45% | 50% |

**Chance of losing the game, between equal players:**

| Players | Whoever goes first | Everyone else |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 23% | 37–39% |
| 4 | 17% | 26–29% |
| 6 | 11% | 17–20% |

Whoever goes first is safest. The last turn of the game takes a card from their hand, so they always get one more chance to get rid of the Old Wizard. That's why the highest roll goes first.

**Calling it out** is a guess between the other players: 1 in 2 at a table of 3, and 1 in 5 at a table of 6. **There's one sure call:** if the Old Wizard has just left your hand, you know who took it, so name them on your next turn.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Bluffing, sighing, fake relief and any amount of acting.
- Choosing which skill to roll, on each turn.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *detect thoughts*, *friends*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A marked Old Wizard, a friend signalling from behind a player, or peeking at a hand.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nick the corner of the Old Wizard, so you can spot it later | The holder | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | For the rest of the game, you win step 2 on your own turn |
| Peek at a neighbour's hand | Anyone at the table | Dex (Stealth) | Learn whether that player holds the Old Wizard |
| Signal what a rival holds | A friend standing behind the rival | Cha (Deception) | The player you're helping wins step 2 on their next turn against that rival |
| Hidden spell (*detect thoughts*) | A caster at the table | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Learn whether one player holds the Old Wizard |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Old Wizard is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A caught cheater also buys the round, whoever ends up holding the Old Wizard. The fine is set as if the entry were 1 sp.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 1 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Stuck with the Old Wizard on the second time around, Quill nicks its corner so he can spot it later.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **17** + 7 = **24**. The Watcher: **15** + 5 = **20**. Not caught.
- Quill still has to get rid of it. But once it's gone, he'll win step 2 on all his later turns, because he can see which card to avoid.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The House's deck** has a hand-painted Old Wizard. Lately, the painted face seems to change expression.
- **An old wizard** drinks in the corner and takes the game very personally.
