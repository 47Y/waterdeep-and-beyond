# Bagman's Bet

Bagman's Bet is a Feywild game brought to Waterdeep by [[Cordan]]. It is intended to let ordinary people hear and vicariously experience adventures they may never undertake.

The game uses the [[Bagman's Bet Bag]], which contains one hundred items. A participant draws a random item—or a slip representing an item its owner does not want to surrender—and tells an entertaining, plausible story about where it came from or the journey it has taken. The story may be true or invented.
