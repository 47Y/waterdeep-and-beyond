# Phoenix Rebirth

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A golden phoenix is painted across the table, its wings spread wide. Roll a five and it lifts your coin. Roll a six and the phoenix bursts into flame and rises again, and your fortune rises with it.*

**Description:** A one-die game with one of the lowest House edges on the floor. The player rolls one die. Most results lose, but a **5** wins, and a **6** gives a second roll with bigger payouts. Two sixes in a row is the **Grand Rebirth**.

## How to Play

1. **Bet.** Place your bet. The dealer hands each player their own House die.
2. **First roll.** Roll **1d6**.
   - **1–4:** the ashes stay cold. You **lose**.
   - **5:** the phoenix rises. You **win 1:1**.
   - **6:** **Rebirth!** Roll again.
3. **Rebirth roll.** Roll **1d6** again.
   - **1–5:** you **win 2:1**.
   - **6:** **Grand Rebirth!** You **win 7:1**.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Mirela (PC) bets **3 gp** each round.

**Round 1**
- First roll: **2**. She **loses** 3 gp.

**Round 2**
- First roll: **5**. She **wins 1:1: 3 gp**.

**Round 3**
- First roll: **6**. **Rebirth!** Rebirth roll: **4**.
- She **wins 2:1: 6 gp**.

**Edge case: Grand Rebirth**
- First roll: **6**. Rebirth roll: **6**. **Grand Rebirth!**
- She **wins 7:1: 21 gp**. Golden sparks shower the table (a harmless *prestidigitation* the House paid for).

## Odds

| Result | Chance | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| Lose (1–4) | 66.7% | — |
| Rise (5) | 16.7% | 1:1 |
| Rebirth (6, then 1–5) | 13.9% | 2:1 |
| Grand Rebirth (6, then 6) | 2.8% | 7:1 |

**House edge: 2.8%.** The player loses two rounds in three, but the wins are big enough to nearly even it out.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any bet within the table limits.
- Blowing on the die "to light the flame".

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the die.
- A loaded die, or rolling so softly that the die barely turns.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soft roll: set the die with the 6 face up and let it barely turn | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Roll this d6 twice and keep the better |
| Swap your House die for your own loaded one | Player or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | For 10 rolls, until the House changes the dice, a 2 on that die counts as a 5 |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant* nudge) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | As the die settles, the invisible servant turns it to a face of your choice |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Phoenix Rebirth is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

![[Cheating Penalties#Casino]]

### Cheating dice example

Thokk bets 10 gp and tries a soft roll, setting the die with the 6 face up.
- Thokk: Sleight of Hand **7** − 1 = **6**. The Watcher: **3** + 5 = **8**. **Caught.**
- His chips and the night's winnings are taken, his portrait goes in the House's file, he's banned for a month, and he owes **50 gp** (five times his 10 gp bet) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **A man who faked his own death** last month comes here whenever this table runs to bet what's left of his funeral collection, because one Grand Rebirth would pay his way out of the city. He has just rolled a 6 and has his second roll to make. His widow has just walked in, still in black, and she's heading for this table. He begs the party to keep her looking the other way until he's thrown it.
- **A cobbler whose shop burned down** has the street's rebuilding collection, 70 gp, in his apron, and has decided the painted phoenix is a sign. He'll lose it all two rolls in three. His hands are shaking too much to hold the die, so he asks a party member to roll it for him. The whole street will thank them for it, or blame them.
