# The Spinner's Gambit

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A great wheel of red and black numbers spins beneath a silver ball, with two golden pockets among them. At the center, a golden spider waits in its web. The ball rattles, bounces, and settles... "Seven, red!"*

**Description:** A House employee, the spinner, sends a ball around a wheel of **20 pockets**: 18 numbers (odd numbers are red, even are black) and two golden pockets, **the Web** and **the Spider**. Players bet on single numbers, groups of numbers, colors, or a golden pocket. Every bet on the table has the same small House edge.

## How to Play

### The wheel

- **Numbers 1–18.** Odd numbers are **red**; even numbers are **black**.
- **The Web and the Spider** are the two golden pockets.
- The pockets sit in a circle: 1, 2, 3 ... 18, the Web, the Spider, and then back to 1.

### Rolling the wheel at the table

Roll one **d20**. On **1–18**, the ball lands on that number. A **19** is **the Web** and a **20** is **the Spider**.

### Bets

| Bet | What you pick | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| **Single** | One number | 17:1 |
| **Split** | Two neighboring numbers (such as 4–5) | 8:1 |
| **Neighbors** | Three neighboring numbers (such as 6–7–8) | 5:1 |
| **Third** | 1–6, 7–12 or 13–18 | 2:1 |
| **Red** or **Black** | All odd or all even numbers | 1:1 |
| **Low** or **High** | 1–9 or 10–18 | 1:1 |
| **Web** or **Spider** | One golden pocket | 18:1 |

**When the ball lands in a golden pocket, the spider takes half.** Every bet on the numbers loses half and gets the other half back. A bet on that golden pocket wins, and a bet on the other one loses.

### A round, step by step

1. **Bet.** Players put their chips on the bets they like, on the painted table beside the wheel.
2. **Spin.** The spinner sends the ball around and calls "no more bets". Roll the d20.
3. **Pay out.** The spinner calls the result, pays every winning bet and clears the losing ones.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Mirela (PC) bets **1 gp on Single 7**. Thokk (NPC) bets **5 gp on Red**. Quill (PC) bets **2 gp on Neighbors 16–17–18**. Oswin (NPC) bets **1 gp on Spider**.

**Round 1**
- d20: **7**. **Seven, red.**
- Mirela wins 17:1: **17 gp**. Thokk wins 1:1: **5 gp**. Quill and Oswin **lose**.

**Round 2**
- d20: **18**. **Eighteen, black.**
- Quill wins 5:1: **10 gp**. Mirela, Thokk and Oswin **lose**.

**Round 3**
- d20: **17**. **Seventeen, red.**
- Thokk wins **5 gp** (red). Quill wins **10 gp** (17 is in 16–17–18). Mirela and Oswin **lose**.

**Edge case: the Spider**
- d20: **20**. **The Spider!**
- Oswin wins 18:1: **18 gp**.
- The spider takes half of every bet on the numbers. Mirela gets **5 sp** back, Thokk **2 gp 5 sp** and Quill **1 gp**.

**Edge case: the Web**
- d20: **19**. **The Web!**
- Oswin bet on the Spider, not the Web, so he **loses** his whole 1 gp.
- Mirela, Thokk and Quill each get half their bets back, the same as above.

## Odds

| Bet | Chance to win | Pays | House edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single | 5% | 17:1 | 5% |
| Split | 10% | 8:1 | 5% |
| Neighbors | 15% | 5:1 | 5% |
| Third | 30% | 2:1 | 5% |
| Red, Black, Low or High | 45% | 1:1 | 5% |
| Web or Spider | 5% | 18:1 | 5% |

**A golden pocket comes up 10% of the time.** Bets on the numbers get half back when it does, and that's counted in their edge.

**Every bet has a 5% edge.** Pick the thrill you want; the long-run cost is the same.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any combination of bets, within the table limits.
- Lucky numbers, superstitions and shouting at the ball.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the ball or wheel.
- A wedge under the wheel, touching the wheel, or adding chips to a bet after "no more bets".

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| As the ball settles and every eye is on it, slide extra chips onto your winning bet | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | A winning bet pays as if you'd bet double |
| Drop a chip, and as you pick it up, slip a thin wedge under the wheel's foot to tilt it toward a number you choose | Player or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | For the next 5 spins, until the House levels the wheel, when the d20 shows the pocket directly across the wheel (10 away), the ball rolls down to your number instead |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant* on the ball) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. Roll again for every nudge. | As the ball settles, the invisible servant nudges it one pocket up or down |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Spinner's Gambit is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Mirela bets 10 gp on Single 4, and casts *unseen servant* before the spin. The ball is settling on **5** when the servant nudges it back one pocket.
- Mirela: Deception **11** + 7 = **18**. The Watcher: **15** + 5 = **20**. **Caught.**
- Her chips and the night's winnings are taken, her portrait goes in the House's file, she's banned for a month, and she owes **50 gp** (five times her 10 gp bet) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **A regular with a notebook** has spotted it: for a tenday the ball has kept stopping dead in the Spider, because someone swapped the pocket's real gold for soft gilded lead. She isn't the thief, and she has told nobody. She bets the Spider at 18:1 every night, and she offers the party a fifth of her winnings to stand around her and make it all look like luck. They could also tell the spinner.
- **A gambler** brings a live spider in a matchbox, sets it on the betting table and bets whichever number it walks to. The Watcher has checked it for magic and found none, and he's up 400 gp. Tonight the box is empty. He offers the party 100 gp to find the spider before the next spin, while the crowd who've been copying his bets search under each other's boots. A rival at the rail looks very pleased with himself.
