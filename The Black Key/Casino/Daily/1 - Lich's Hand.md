# Lich's Hand

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Gilded cards snap across green felt. Get closer to twenty-one than the dealer without going over, and pray for the Lich: the card that refuses to die, and turns a good hand into a legendary one.*

**Description:** Each player tries to get a hand closer to **21** than the dealer's without going over. Luck decides the cards, but a sharp player can try to **read the dealer** to guess their hidden card. The best possible hand, a Lich plus a 10-value card, is called a **Lich's Hand** and pays extra.

## How to Play

### The cards

In-world it's a deck of cards. At the table, **every card is one d12 roll**:

| d12 roll | Card | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lich** | 11, or 1 if 11 would take you over 21 (like its namesake, it won't die: it drops to 1 instead) |
| 2–9 | Number card | Face value |
| 10 | Knight | 10 |
| 11 | Queen | 10 |
| 12 | King | 10 |

A hand with a Lich counted as 11 is **soft** (e.g. Lich + 6 = "soft 17"). The next card can't take it over 21, because the Lich drops to 1 if needed. A hand with no Lich counted as 11 is **hard**.

### A hand, step by step

1. **Bet.** Each player places a bet within the table's limits (see Prizes & Stakes).
2. **Deal.**
   - Each player gets **two cards, face up**. Roll 2d12 for each player.
   - The dealer gets **one card face up** and **one hidden card**. The DM rolls the hidden card in secret.
3. **The dealer peeks at the hidden card** every hand, and checks for a Lich's Hand.
   - **If it makes a Lich's Hand,** the dealer reveals it and the hand ends. Every player loses their bet, except players who also have a Lich's Hand, who get their bet back.
4. **Player Lich's Hand.** A player whose first two cards are a **Lich + a 10-value card** is paid **3:2** right away, and their hand is over.
5. **Each player's turn**, in seat order. Choose:
   - **Hit:** take another card (roll a d12). You can keep hitting.
   - **Stand:** keep your total and end your turn.
   - **Double:** on your first two cards only. Double your bet, take **exactly one** more card, then stand.
   - **Read the Dealer** (optional, once per hand, before your first hit, stand or double): see Reading the Dealer below.
   - Those are your only choices: a pair can't be split into two hands.
6. **Bust.** Going over 21 is called a **bust**. You lose your bet immediately, even if the dealer busts later.
7. **Dealer's turn.** The dealer reveals the hidden card, then **must hit until reaching 17 or more**, soft 17 included, and then stands. The dealer has no choices.
8. **Settle.**
   - Beat the dealer, or the dealer busts: you win **1:1** (bet 2 gp, win 2 gp).
   - Same total as the dealer: a tie, and your bet comes back.
   - Lower than the dealer: you lose your bet.

### Reading the Dealer

Once per hand, a player can study the dealer: **Wisdom (Insight)** against the dealer's **Charisma (Deception)**. Ties go to the dealer.
- **Proficient with a playing card set?** You roll with **advantage**.
- **Success:** the dealer's face tells you whether the hidden card is **High** (a 10-value card or a Lich) or **Low** (2–9).
- **Failure:** you learn nothing. The dealer doesn't react.

This is a legal skill, not cheating.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Mirela (PC, Insight +2, proficient with playing cards) bets **2 gp**. Thokk (NPC) bets **5 gp**. The dealer is Sable (NPC, Deception +4).

**Hand 1: hit, double, dealer busts**
- Mirela's cards: **7** and **11** (a Queen, worth 10), for **17**.
- Thokk's cards: **5** and **4**, for **9**.
- The dealer shows a **6**, and peeks at the hidden card.
- Mirela **stands** on 17.
- Thokk **doubles**, so his bet goes to 10 gp. His one card is **12** (a King, worth 10), for **19**.
- The dealer reveals a **9**: 6 + 9 = 15. Below 17, so the dealer must hit: **8**. Total 23, a **bust**.
- Mirela wins **2 gp**. Thokk wins **10 gp**.

**Hand 2: reading the dealer**
- Mirela's cards: **1** (Lich) and **6**, for **soft 17**.
- Thokk's cards: **10** and **9**, for **19**.
- The dealer shows a **10**, and peeks: the hidden card isn't a Lich, so play continues.
- Mirela reads the dealer. She rolls with advantage, gets 8 and **15**, and keeps the 15: 15 + 2 = **17**. Sable rolls **10** + 4 = **14**. **Success:** the hidden card is **High**. The dealer's peek ruled out a Lich, so the dealer has 20.
- Her 17 would lose to 20, so Mirela **hits**: **3**, for soft 20. She **stands**.
- Thokk **stands** on 19.
- The dealer reveals **12** (King): 10 + 10 = **20**.
- Mirela **ties** and gets her 2 gp back. Thokk **loses** 5 gp.

**Hand 3: Lich's Hand and a bust**
- Mirela's cards: **1** (Lich) and **10** (Knight). **Lich's Hand!** She's paid 3:2 right away: **3 gp**.
- Thokk's cards: **10** and **6**, for **16**. The dealer shows a **7**. Thokk **hits**: **9**, for 25. **Bust.** He loses 5 gp.
- The dealer reveals a **10**, for 17, and stands. Nobody is left to compare against.

**Edge case: the dealer's Lich's Hand**
- The dealer shows a **1** (Lich) and peeks: the hidden card is **11** (Queen). **The dealer has a Lich's Hand.** The hand ends immediately.
- Mirela had 18, so she **loses** 2 gp.
- Thokk had his own Lich's Hand, so he gets his 5 gp back.

## Prizes & Stakes

![[Casino Tables#Tables]]

![[Casino Tables#Reading Payouts]]

| Result | Pays |
|---|---|
| Lich's Hand | 3:2 (bet 2 gp, win 3 gp) |
| Beat the dealer | 1:1 |
| Tie | Bet returned |

**Table limit and doubling:** doubling can take you over the table maximum. The House allows it.

**How the House makes money:** players act first, so a player who busts loses even if the dealer busts later. That gives the House its edge (see Odds).

## Odds

**Each card:** a Lich is 8%, each number from 2 to 9 is 8%, and a 10-value card is 25%.

**Chance of a Lich's Hand on the deal:** 4.2%.

**Chance to bust if you hit on a hard total** (no Lich counted as 11):

| Your total | 12 | 13 | 14 | 15 | 16 | 17 | 18 | 19 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bust chance | 25% | 33% | 42% | 50% | 58% | 67% | 75% | 83% | 92% |

**What the House keeps, by how you play:**

| How you play | House edge |
|---|---|
| Copy the dealer (hit until 17, never double) | 5.3% |
| Simple rule: hit until 17, but stand on a hard 12 or more when the dealer shows 2–6, and never double | 5.0% |
| Perfect strategy | 2.8% |
| Perfect strategy plus a successful Read the Dealer on every hand | About even |

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Reading the Dealer, including advantage from playing card set proficiency.
- Keeping track of what you've seen, doing maths and following hunches.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that gives information or changes cards, such as *detect thoughts* on the dealer.
- Adding to your bet after the deal, peeking at the hidden card, or bribing the dealer.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| While the dealer looks at the hidden card, slide extra chips onto your bet | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | A winning bet pays as if you'd bet double |
| Catch the hidden card in a polished ring as the dealer peeks | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Learn the dealer's exact hidden card this hand |
| Hidden spell (*detect thoughts*) | Player | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Learn the exact hidden card for the spell's 1 minute, about two hands |
| Bribe the dealer (Silver Table 1 gp, Gold Table 10 gp, Platinum Salon 100 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | For 10 hands, until the next dealer takes over, the dealer flashes you the hidden card |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Lich's Hand is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

At the Silver Table, Thokk bets 5 gp and is dealt 20. While the dealer looks at the hidden card, he tries to slide extra chips onto his bet.
- Thokk: Sleight of Hand **9** − 1 = **8**. The Watcher: **7** + 5 = **12**. **Caught.**
- Two House guards quietly walk him to the back office. His 5 gp bet and the 15 gp he won earlier that night are confiscated. His portrait goes in the House's file, copies go to the other gambling dens, and he's banned from the casino floor for a month.
- The House gives him a debt of **25 gp** (five times his 5 gp bet, which is at the Silver Table's cap), or he can owe them a favour instead.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **A quiet gnome** plays perfect strategy and reads the dealer on every hand, all of it legal, and just about breaks even, which pays for a warm seat every night. Now her eyesight is going, and she can't read a face across the table. She offers the party her strategy notebook if one of them will sit beside her and tell her what the dealer's face says.
- **An old soldier**, told by the healers that he has weeks left, has sworn he'll go happily the day he's dealt a Lich's Hand, and not a day before. He has never played. Tonight he wants to be carried to the table, where the chance is 1 in 24 on every hand. He offers 5 gp to anyone who'll carry his chair. His daughter begs the party to take him home instead.
