# Lucky Sevens

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Two thrones are painted on the felt: one gold for the dawn, one silver for the dusk, and between them a single lucky seven. "Under, over, or the seven?" calls the dealer, and two dice tumble.*

**Description:** The casino's beginner table. The dealer rolls two dice, and players bet whether the total will be **under 7**, **over 7**, or **exactly 7**. It's easy to learn and fast to play, and the House edge is high, so most regulars move to other tables once they've learned the ropes.

## How to Play

1. **Bet.** Each player puts chips on one or more of the spots painted on the felt:

   | Bet | Wins on | Pays |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Dawn Throne** (under 7) | 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 | 1:1 |
   | **Dusk Throne** (over 7) | 8, 9, 10, 11 or 12 | 1:1 |
   | **Lucky Seven** | Exactly 7 | 4:1 |

2. **Roll.** The dealer rolls **2d6** and adds them.
3. **Pay out.** A total of **7 loses both Thrones**.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** "Fat" Oswin (NPC) bets **2 gp on Dawn**. Mirela (PC) bets **2 gp on Dusk**. Thokk (NPC) bets **1 gp on Lucky Seven**.

**Round 1**
- Dice: **2** and **3** = **5**. Under 7.
- Oswin wins **2 gp**. Mirela and Thokk **lose**.

**Round 2**
- Dice: **6** and **4** = **10**. Over 7.
- Mirela wins **2 gp**. Oswin and Thokk **lose**.

**Round 3**
- Dice: **5** and **2** = **7**.
- Thokk wins 4:1: **4 gp**. Oswin **and** Mirela both **lose**, because 7 beats both Thrones.

**Edge case: covering every bet**
- Quill (PC) bets **1 gp on each** of Dawn, Dusk and Lucky Seven (3 gp total). Dice: **3** and **3** = **6**.
- Dawn wins **1 gp**; Dusk and Lucky Seven lose 1 gp each. He's **down 1 gp**. Covering everything loses 1 gp on any total other than 7, and wins 2 gp on a 7 (4 gp on Lucky Seven minus the two lost Thrones).

## Odds

| Bet | Chance to win | Pays | House edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dawn Throne | 41.7% | 1:1 | 16.7% |
| Dusk Throne | 41.7% | 1:1 | 16.7% |
| Lucky Seven | 16.7% | 4:1 | 16.7% |

**Every bet has the same edge.** This is one of the worst tables in the casino for the player, and one of the friendliest for beginners.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any bet within the table limits, including several bets on the same roll.
- Calling "Seven!" as loudly as you like.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the dice.
- Moving a bet after the dice land, or switching the House dice.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| As the dice land, while every eye is on them, slide your bet to the winning throne | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Move your bet after the dice land |
| While leaning over to bet, swap one of the dealer's dice on the felt for your own loaded one | Player or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | For 10 rolls, until the House changes the dice, a 1 on that die counts as a 6 |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant* nudge) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | As the dice settle, the invisible servant turns one die to a face of your choice |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Lucky Sevens is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Thokk bets 2 gp on Dawn; the dice land on **4** and **4** = 8. He tries to slide his chips to Dusk.
- Thokk: Sleight of Hand **9** − 1 = **8**. The Watcher: **4** + 5 = **9**. **Caught.**
- His chips and the night's winnings are taken, his portrait goes in the House's file, he's banned for a month, and he owes **10 gp** (five times his 2 gp bet) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **A nervous clerk** has been betting his employer's strongbox on Lucky Seven, one stake at a time, sure the next 7 wins it all back. His employer's people have just walked in.
- **A feuding couple** have agreed to settle everything on one roll. Dawn Throne, they keep the farm. Dusk Throne, they sell it and move to the city. A 7, which loses both Thrones, and they part for good. That's a chance of 1 in 6. Their grown son begs the party to talk them out of it before the dealer picks up the dice.
