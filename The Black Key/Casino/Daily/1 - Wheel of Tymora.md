# Wheel of Tymora

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A great wheel of a hundred brass slots ticks past a pointer shaped like a beckoning finger. Silver, gold, platinum, blood-red ruby, and one single slot painted with the smile of Tymora, goddess of luck. The spinner hauls it round. Click... click... click...*

**Description:** A big spinning wheel with a hundred slots in five colors. Players bet on which color the pointer stops on. Common colors pay little; rare colors pay a lot. There's one decision (which color) and one spin, so it's easy for anyone to join.

## How to Play

### The wheel

| Slots | Color | How many slots | Pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–47 | **Silver** | 47 | 1:1 |
| 48–77 | **Gold** | 30 | 2:1 |
| 78–92 | **Platinum** | 15 | 5:1 |
| 93–99 | **Ruby** | 7 | 11:1 |
| 100 | **Tymora's Smile** | 1 | 85:1 |

### Rolling the wheel at the table

Roll a **d100**: the number is the slot the pointer stops on. A roll of 00 on both dice is slot **100**.

### A round, step by step

1. **Bet.** Each player puts chips on one or more colors, on the painted table at the rail.
2. **Spin.** The spinner calls "no more bets" and hauls the wheel round. Roll the d100.
3. **Pay out.** The spinner pays the bets on that slot's color. Every other bet loses.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Mirela (PC) bets **2 gp on Gold**. Thokk (NPC) bets **5 gp on Silver**. Quill (PC) bets **1 gp on Tymora's Smile**.

**Round 1**
- d100: slot **52**, **Gold**.
- Mirela wins 2:1: **4 gp**. Thokk and Quill **lose**.

**Round 2**
- d100: slot **9**, **Silver**.
- Thokk wins 1:1: **5 gp**. Mirela and Quill **lose**.

**Round 3**
- d100: slot **94**, **Ruby**.
- Nobody bet on Ruby. **Everyone loses.**

**Edge case: Tymora smiles**
- d100: **00**, slot **100**, **Tymora's Smile**.
- Quill wins 85:1: **85 gp** on his 1 gp. The spinner rings the bell and the whole floor cheers.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any color, any amount within the table limits.
- Prayers to Tymora are welcome. Her clergy say she likes bold bets.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the wheel, such as *unseen servant*.
- Knocking the wheel's stand, adding chips to a bet after "no more bets", or bribing the spinner.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| As the wheel slows and every eye is on the pointer, knee its stand so that it jumps | Player at the rail | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Move the result 1 slot up or down |
| As the pointer stops and every eye is on it, slide extra chips onto your winning bet | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | A winning bet pays as if you'd bet double |
| Bribe the spinner to tuck a lead weight behind the wheel, so that it favours a color you choose (Silver Table 1 gp, Gold Table 10 gp, Platinum Salon 100 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | For the next 5 spins, until the House checks the wheel, when the slot isn't that color, roll the d100 again. The second roll counts |
| Hidden spell (*unseen servant* brake) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. Roll again for every spin. | As the wheel slows, the invisible servant drags on it and moves the result up to 2 slots up or down |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Wheel of Tymora is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Mirela bets 5 gp on Platinum at the Silver Table. The wheel is slowing toward slot 77 (Gold), one slot short, and she knees its stand.
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **15** + 2 = **17**. The Watcher: **8** + 5 = **13**. **Mirela wins the contest.**
- The wheel jumps to slot 78, Platinum. She wins **25 gp** and nobody noticed.
- If she'd been caught, she'd owe **25 gp** (five times her 5 gp bet, which is also the Silver Table cap) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Sister Aubrielle**, a priestess of Tymora, dreamed the goddess told her to bet her temple's 200 gp roof fund on the Smile, 10 gp a spin. She's down to her last 40 gp and still beaming, and one hit would pay 850 gp. A furious deacon offers the party 50 gp to drag her out before the next spin. She begs them to stay and pray with her.
- **A bored young noble** has sworn before witnesses to marry whoever is standing beside them when the Smile next comes up, and to come back every night until it does. Hopefuls now jostle for a place at the rail, and the noble's family offers the party 100 gp to make sure it's somebody suitable.
