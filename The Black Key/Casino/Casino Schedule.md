## How the Calendar Works

- **The year** has **12 months of 30 days**, plus **5 festival days** that fall between months (Calendar of Harptos).
- **Each month** has **3 tendays**: days 1–10, 11–20 and 21–30.
- **At most 15 games are open at once:** 10 staples and 5 rotating games.
- **The rotating games change every tenday**, on days 1, 11 and 21. Festival days keep the games from the days just before them.
- **"Weekly" events** run **once a tenday**.
- **The casino has no monthly or yearly games.**
