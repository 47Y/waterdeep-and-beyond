# The Sharks' Table

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The table in the back corner has no game on it: just cards, a candle and a smiling stranger who works for the House. "You read people, friend? Let's see who reads who."*

**Description:** A card game where the cards matter less than the people holding them. The player faces a House professional, a **sharp**, three times each round: reading the sharp, bluffing the sharp and pressuring the sharp. The more of the three the player wins, the more they take home.

## How to Play

### A round, step by step

1. **Bet.** Place your bet.
2. **Deal.** The sharp deals you each a hand from a House deck. The cards are for show: you don't need real cards at the table, and the DM describes the hands to fit the results.
3. **Three face-offs.** The player and the sharp each roll once on every line. **Ties go to the sharp.**

   | Face-off | What's happening | The player rolls | The sharp rolls |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | **Read** | You work out how good the sharp's hand is | Wisdom (Insight) | Charisma (Deception) |
   | **Bluff** | You make the sharp believe in your hand | Charisma (Deception) | Wisdom (Insight) |
   | **Pressure** | You stare the sharp down until they give way | Charisma (Intimidation) | Wisdom (Insight) |

4. **Count the player's wins** and settle the bet:

   | Player wins | Result |
   |---|---|
   | 0 | Lose the whole bet |
   | 1 | Lose half the bet |
   | 2 | Win half the bet |
   | 3 | Win the whole bet (1:1) |

### Sharps and seating

You can sit at any table. The table only sets the limits on your bet.

**The House sizes up every new player and sends a sharp to match.** Add the player's Insight, Deception and Intimidation, divide by 3 and round up. That's the sharp's modifier for all three of their rolls.

| Sharp's modifier | Who sits down |
|---|---|
| +4 or lower | A card-room regular |
| +5 to +8 | A professional |
| +9 to +13 | A legend of the circuit |
| +14 or higher | **The Shark** |

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Mirela (PC bard, Insight +2, Deception +7, Intimidation +4) sits at the Gold Table. Her skills add up to 13, and 13 divided by 3 rounds up to 5, so a +5 sharp faces her. She bets **20 gp**.

**Round 1**
- Read: Mirela **15** + 2 = **17**. Sharp **6** + 5 = **11**. **Mirela wins.**
- Bluff: Mirela **9** + 7 = **16**. Sharp **12** + 5 = **17**. Sharp wins.
- Pressure: Mirela **14** + 4 = **18**. Sharp **13** + 5 = **18**. **A tie goes to the sharp.**
- **1 win:** Mirela loses half her bet, **10 gp**.

**Round 2**
- Read: **17** + 2 = **19** vs **9** + 5 = **14**. **Mirela.**
- Bluff: **19** + 7 = **26** vs **13** + 5 = **18**. **Mirela.**
- Pressure: **4** + 4 = **8** vs **11** + 5 = **16**. Sharp.
- **2 wins:** Mirela wins half her bet, **10 gp**.

**Round 3**
- Read: **20** + 2 = **22** vs **5** + 5 = **10**. **Mirela.**
- Bluff: **13** + 7 = **20** vs **7** + 5 = **12**. **Mirela.**
- Pressure: **16** + 4 = **20** vs **3** + 5 = **8**. **Mirela.**
- **3 wins:** Mirela wins the whole bet, **20 gp**. The sharp tips their hat.

**Edge case: a clean sweep for the sharp**
- Thokk (NPC, Insight +1, Deception −1, Intimidation +2) sits at the Silver Table with **5 gp**. His skills add up to 2, and 2 divided by 3 rounds up to 1, so a +1 sharp faces him.
- Read: **4** + 1 = **5** vs **11** + 1 = **12**. Sharp.
- Bluff: **8** − 1 = **7** vs **9** + 1 = **10**. Sharp.
- Pressure: **6** + 2 = **8** vs **15** + 1 = **16**. Sharp.
- **0 wins:** he loses the whole **5 gp**.

## Odds

| Player | Insight / Deception / Intimidation | Sharp | Player's average result |
|---|---|---|---|
| All three skills the same | +5 / +5 / +5 | +5 | Loses 5.6% of the bet |
| Thokk | +1 / −1 / +2 | +1 | Loses 8.8% of the bet |
| Mirela | +2 / +7 / +4 | +5 | Loses 12.1% of the bet |
| One big skill | +0 / +10 / +0 | +4 | Loses 13.1% of the bet |

**House edge: about 6% to 13%,** whoever sits down, because the sharp is always matched to the player. Getting better doesn't beat this game. Only a character with two huge skills and a third near −1 comes out ahead, and by no more than 5.8%.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any skill proficiency or Expertise.
- Acting, table talk, compliments and threats (within reason).

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic** on the sharp or on yourself, such as *charm person*, *friends*, *enhance ability*, *detect thoughts*, Bardic Inspiration or *guidance*.
- A polished ring that catches the sharp's cards, a helper signalling them to you, or bribing the sharp.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep a polished ring tilted on the table, so that it catches the sharp's cards as they're dealt | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on Read this round |
| Stand where you can see the sharp's cards, and signal them with a cough or a scratch | A helper in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to look casual | Advantage on Read this round |
| Bribe the sharp to lose on purpose (Silver Table 1 gp, Gold Table 10 gp, Platinum Salon 100 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | For your next 5 rounds, the sharp rolls with disadvantage |
| Hidden spell (*detect thoughts* on the sharp, surface thoughts only) | A caster within 30 feet | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on Read for the 1 minute the spell lasts |
| Hidden spell (*enhance ability* for Charisma, by touch) | A caster touching the player | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on Bluff and Pressure for up to 1 hour |

**Don't use *friends* or *charm person*:** the sharp knows what happened when the spell ends and tells the Watcher, so you're caught even if nobody saw the casting.

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Sharks' Table is played against the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

![[Cheating Penalties#Casino]]

### Cheating dice example

Mirela casts *enhance ability* on herself under the table before a 50 gp round at the Gold Table.
- Mirela: Deception **18** + 7 = **25**. The Watcher: **20** + 5 = **25**. **A tie goes to the Watcher. Caught.**
- Her chips and the night's winnings are taken, her portrait goes in the House's file, she's banned for a month, and she owes **250 gp** (five times her 50 gp bet) or a favour.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The Shark** has never been seen to lose a night. They wear a different face every time, which suggests a changeling, or a very good disguise kit.
- **A young sharp** has started losing on purpose to a certain merchant. The House wants to know what the merchant is paying.
