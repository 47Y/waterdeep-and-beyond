# Baldur's Bones

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *"Twenty-one, or bust," the old dockhand grins, rattling three bone dice in a leather cup. The pot sits in the middle of the table. Push your luck one die too far, and it's gone.*

**Description:** A push-your-luck dice game from Baldur's Gate, played between players. Everyone rolls three dice, then takes turns adding one more die at a time to get as close to **21** as possible without going over. The highest total takes the pot. The House runs the table and takes a small cut.

## How to Play

### A hand, step by step

1. **Ante.** Every player puts the same ante into the pot.
2. **Pick the Host.** The **Host** plays last, which is a small advantage. The Host seat moves one player clockwise every hand.
3. **Opening roll.** Every player rolls **3d6** openly. That's their starting total.
4. **Take turns.** Starting left of the Host and going clockwise, each player either:
   - **Stands:** keeps their total and ends their turn, or
   - **Adds a die:** rolls **1d6** and adds it to their total. They can keep adding one die at a time until they stand.
   - **Over 21 is a bust.** A busted player is out of this hand.
5. **The Host goes last**, knowing every other total.
6. **Showdown.** The highest total of 21 or less wins the pot, minus the House's **5% rake**.
   - **Tied totals** split the pot equally.
   - **Everyone busts:** the pot carries over to the next hand.

## Dice Examples

**At the table:** Mirela (PC), Quill (PC) and Thokk (NPC) each ante **10 gp**. The pot is **30 gp**; the rake is **1 gp 5 sp**, so the winner takes **28 gp 5 sp**. Thokk is the Host this hand.

**Mirela**
- Opening roll: **4 + 5 + 3 = 12**.
- Adds a die: **6**. Total **18**. She stands.

**Quill**
- Opening roll: **6 + 6 + 2 = 14**.
- He needs to beat 18. Adds a die: **3**. Total **17**.
- Adds another: **5**. Total **22**. **Bust.**

**Thokk (Host, last)**
- Opening roll: **5 + 4 + 4 = 13**. He knows he needs 19 or more.
- Adds a die: **2**. Total **15**. Adds another: **4**. Total **19**. He stands.
- **Thokk wins** with 19 and takes **28 gp 5 sp**.

**Edge case: a tie**
- Next hand (Quill is Host). Mirela stands on **20**. Thokk adds his way to **20**. Quill busts at **23**.
- Mirela and Thokk tie at 20 and **split** the 28 gp 5 sp pot: **14 gp 2 sp 5 cp** each. The House rounds down to 14 gp 2 sp each and keeps the coppers.

| Your total | 15 or less | 16 | 17 | 18 | 19 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bust chance | 0% | 17% | 33% | 50% | 67% | 83% |

- **Equal players** each win about 1 hand in N (for N players), and the Host seat evens out as it rotates.
- **The House's cut** is 5% of every pot, so on average each player loses 5% of their ante.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Standing or adding as many dice as you like.
- Table talk, taunts and bluffing about whether you'll add another die.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that affects the dice.
- Palming a die, swapping in loaded dice, or misreading a die out loud.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** Other players at the table can also try to spot you (see Spotting a cheat).

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Palm a die | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Replace one die you just rolled with a face of your choice |
| Loaded dice swap | Player or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Your opening roll is 5 + 5 + 5 = 15 |
| Misread a die | Player | Cha (Deception) | Change one die by 1 up or down |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand* nudge) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Turn one die to a face of your choice |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Baldur's Bones is played between players, but the table belongs to the House, so cheating at it is cheating the House. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. The cheated players get their antes back from the hand.

![[Cheating Penalties#Casino]]

### Cheating dice example

Quill (PC) is at 17 and adds a die: **5**, for 22. He tries to palm it and swap in a **3**.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **11** + 7 = **18**. The Watcher: **12** + 5 = **17**. **Quill wins the contest.**
- But Thokk is watching too: Perception **16** + 1 = **17**, which doesn't beat Quill's 18. Nobody sees it.
- Quill stands on 20 and wins the pot.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Tarina**, a quick-handed card sharp from Baldur's Gate, plays here when she's in town. She cheats every game and has never been caught.
- **The dice cup** at the high table is said to have belonged to a Duke of Baldur's Gate who lost the city's harbor rights in a single hand.
