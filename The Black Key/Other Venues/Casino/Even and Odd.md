# Even and Odd

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The dealer kneels on a straw mat, shakes two dice in a bamboo cup and slams it face-down. "Even or odd?" Coins slide across the mat. The cup lifts.*

**Description:** An ancient eastern dice game and the simplest bet in the casino. The dealer shakes two dice under a cup. Players bet whether the total is **even** or **odd**. It's a true 50/50, so the House takes a small commission from every win.

## How to Play

1. **Shake.** The dealer shakes **2d6** in a cup and slams it face-down.
2. **Bet.** Players bet **Even** or **Odd**.
3. **Reveal.** The dealer lifts the cup and adds the two dice.
   - **Even total** (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12): Even wins.
   - **Odd total** (3, 5, 7, 9, 11): Odd wins.
4. **Pay out.** Winning bets pay **1:1, minus a 5% commission on the winnings**. The House rounds the commission up to the nearest silver.

## Dice Examples

**At the mat:** Thokk (NPC) bets **10 gp on Even**. Mirela (PC) bets **2 gp on Odd**.

**Round 1**
- Dice: **3** and **5** = **8**. **Even.**
- Thokk wins 10 gp, minus a 5% commission of 5 sp: he collects **9 gp 5 sp**. Mirela **loses** 2 gp.

**Round 2**
- Dice: **6** and **1** = **7**. **Odd.**
- Mirela wins 2 gp, minus a 5% commission (1 sp): she collects **1 gp 9 sp**. Thokk **loses** 10 gp.

**Round 3**
- Dice: **2** and **2** = **4**. **Even.** Thokk collects **9 gp 5 sp**; Mirela loses 2 gp.

**Edge case: a tiny win**
- "Fat" Oswin (NPC) bets **1 sp on Odd**, and the dice show **4** and **5** = **9**.
- His commission would be half a copper. The House rounds it up to 1 sp, so he wins **nothing** on that bet; his 1 sp comes back. Regulars bet at least 2 gp.

## Odds

| Bet | Chance to win | Pays | House edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Even | 50% | 1:1 minus 5% | 2.5% |
| Odd | 50% | 1:1 minus 5% | 2.5% |

**Bets under 2 gp lose more** because of the rounding.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Betting either side, any amount within the table limits.
- Asking the dealer to shake again before bets are placed.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell** that sees under the cup or turns the dice.
- Changing your bet after the cup lifts.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.**

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch your bet as the cup lifts | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Your bet is on the winning side |
| Bribe the dealer | Player | Cha (Persuasion) against the dealer's Wisdom (Insight) +2 first, then the dealer's Dex (Sleight of Hand) +4 against the Watcher | The dealer peeks and signals the result |
| Hidden spell (*mage hand* under the cup) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Turn one die so the total is even or odd |

**Crooked dealers:** a dealer with loaded dice can cheat the players. The Watcher spots it with Perception against the dealer's Sleight of Hand.

### Cheating dice example

Mirela bets 5 gp on Odd and tries to slide her chips to Even as the cup lifts on **6** and **2**.
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **18** + 2 = **20**. The Watcher: **6** + 5 = **11**. **Not caught.**
- Her bet now sits on Even. She collects **4 gp 7 sp** (5 gp minus a 3 sp commission, rounded up).

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Old Hiroko** has dealt Even and Odd for fifty years and has never been seen to blink. Gamblers say she can hear the dice settle.
- **A dealer** on the night shift has been using loaded dice. The House wants him caught in the act, not just fired.
