# Echo Tankard

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The barkeep taps a rhythm on an empty tankard: tap, tap-tap. Your turn. Tap it back. Then she adds a beat, and another. How long can you keep up before you lose the thread?*

**Description:** A daily memory game at the bar. The barkeep taps a rhythm on a tankard, adding **one beat each time**. Repeat it back with an **Intelligence check**, at a higher DC each beat. **One wrong beat and your run ends.** The highest score of the day goes on the board.

## How to Play

### Your run, step by step

1. **The barkeep taps the first beat.** Repeat it with an **Intelligence check**. If you're **proficient with a drum** (or any percussion instrument), add your proficiency bonus.
2. **Each beat after that** adds one more tap, and a harder DC:

   | Beat | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | DC | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 18 | 20 |

   - **Success:** you repeat it. Go on to the next beat.
   - **Failure:** your run ends.
3. **Your score** is the number of beats you repeated (0 to 7).

### Winning

- **The highest score at closing** wins a free supper and their name on the chalkboard.
- **Ties at closing:** the tied players share the supper.

**Being Drunk hurts here:** if you're Drunk (see [[Drunk Track]]), you're poisoned, which gives disadvantage on Intelligence checks.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **First try each day:** free.
- **Each try after that:** 1 sp.
- **The day's highest score:** a free supper and their name on the chalkboard.
- **The House keeps:** the retry fees.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Tapping along on your own knee while the barkeep taps.
- Proficiency with any percussion instrument.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *guidance*, *message* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A friend tapping the rhythm under the table, or bribing the barkeep for a rhythm you know.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| A friend taps along under the bar | Anyone next to you | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on one beat |
| Bribe the barkeep for a rhythm you've practised (2 sp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | +2 to every beat this run |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Echo Tankard is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. The cheater's score is wiped from the board. The fine is set as if the entry were 1 sp.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 1 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

On Mirela's second try, Quill taps along under the bar for beat 5.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **14** + 7 = **21**. The Watcher: **10** + 5 = **15**. Not caught.
- Mirela rolls beat 5 with advantage: **8** and **17**, keeps 17: 17 + 1 = **18**. ✓ She goes on to beat 6.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The barkeep's rhythms** are old sailors' signals. A retired captain at the bar goes pale at one of them.
- **A dwarven drummer** has held the board for a month with a perfect 7. Every night, someone different accuses him of cheating.
