# Fingerhakeln

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Two pullers sit across a heavy oak table, middle fingers hooked through a leather loop. The referee drops a handkerchief. Both lean back and pull, faces red, veins standing out, until one of them gets dragged across the table.*

**Description:** A monthly finger-pulling tournament from the mountain villages. Two pullers hook their middle fingers through a short leather loop and pull until one drags the other over the line on the table. Each match is **best of three pulls**. The tournament is **single elimination**: lose a match and you're out.

## How to Play

### Setting up

- Draw the bracket. If there's an odd number of pullers, one draws a **bye** and goes straight to the next round.

### A pull

- Both pullers roll **Strength (Athletics)**.
- **The higher total wins the pull.**
- **Ties: pull again.** Keep re-pulling until someone wins.

### A match

- **Best of three pulls.** The first to win 2 pulls wins the match and moves on in the bracket.
- The loser is out of the tournament. Nobody gets hurt: they just get dragged across the table.

### Winning

The puller who wins the final match is champion.

## Dice Examples

**Semi-final:** Thokk (NPC, Athletics +5) vs "Fat" Oswin (NPC, Athletics +2).

**Pull 1**
- Thokk: **8** + 5 = **13**. Oswin: **11** + 2 = **13**. **Tie: pull again.**
- Thokk: **14** + 5 = **19**. Oswin: **6** + 2 = **8**. **Thokk wins the pull** (1–0).

**Pull 2**
- Thokk: **4** + 5 = **9**. Oswin: **15** + 2 = **17**. **Oswin wins the pull** (1–1).

**Pull 3**
- Thokk: **12** + 5 = **17**. Oswin: **13** + 2 = **15**. **Thokk wins the match, 2–1,** and moves on to the final.

**Edge case: a bye**
- Seven pullers enter. The draw gives Quill a bye, so he goes straight to the semi-finals without pulling.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per puller.
- **Champion:** 6 gp and the **champion's leather loop**, which hangs behind the bar with their name burned into it.
- **The House keeps:** whatever is left of the entries, plus its cut of spectator bets.

## Odds

**How much a bigger Athletics bonus helps:**

| Your bonus is higher by | Win one pull | Win a best-of-3 match |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 50% | 50% |
| 1 | 55% | 58% |
| 2 | 60% | 65% |
| 3 | 64.5% | 71% |
| 5 | 73% | 82% |

**Best of three favours the stronger puller.** One lucky pull isn't enough.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any pulling stance, as long as you stay seated and keep one hand on the table.
- Rage, Action Surge and other features that don't use magic. (A barbarian's rage gives advantage on Strength checks.)
- Chalk on your fingers, from the House's chalk pot.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *enhance ability* (Bull's Strength), *enlarge/reduce*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A loop with wire sewn inside, greasing your rival's finger, or bracing your foot on the table leg.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** (The Watcher referees every match.) The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brace your foot on the table leg | Puller | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on the rest of this match |
| Grease your rival's finger | Puller or a helper | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Your rival has disadvantage on the next pull |
| Hidden spell (*enhance ability*, *enlarge/reduce*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Before pull 3, Oswin hooks his boot around the table leg.
- Oswin: Sleight of Hand **18** + 0 = **18**. The Watcher: **6** + 5 = **11**. Not caught.
- Thokk is staring at him across the table: Perception **19** + 1 = **20**. He beats 18 and **sees it**.
- Thokk reports him. Oswin is disqualified, Thokk goes through, and Oswin is banned for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 10 gp, 1 day serving, or wait 3 months.
