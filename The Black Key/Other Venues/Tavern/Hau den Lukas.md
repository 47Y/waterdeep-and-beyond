# Hau den Lukas

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A tall wooden tower with a bell at the top and a striker plate at the bottom. Swing the great mallet, and the iron puck shoots up the rail. Does it ring the bell? Everyone in the courtyard holds their breath.*

**Description:** A daily strength test in the courtyard, sometimes called the **High Striker**. Swing a mallet at the plate, and your **Strength (Athletics)** total decides how high the puck goes. Small prizes go to good strikes, and **ringing the bell** (25 or more) puts your name on the board. The House bookie takes bets on whether someone will ring it.

## How to Play

### A strike, step by step

1. **Pay 1 sp** for a strike.
2. **Make a Strength (Athletics) check.**

   | Total | Prize |
   |---|---|
   | 14 or less | A laugh from the crowd |
   | 15–19 | A tin pin |
   | 20–24 | A free pint |
   | 25 or more | **The bell rings!** Your name goes on the board, and a free pint. |

### The bookie's bet

Before a strike, anyone can bet the House bookie that the striker will **ring the bell**. The bookie sets the price from the striker's Athletics bonus (see [[Proposition Betting]]):

| Striker's Athletics | Chance to ring | Bookie pays (back, per 1 sp bet) |
|---|---|---|
| +5 | 5% | 18 sp |
| +6 | 10% | 9 sp |
| +7 | 15% | 6 sp |
| +8 | 20% | 4 sp 5 cp |
| +9 | 25% | 3 sp 6 cp |
| +10 | 30% | 3 sp |
| +11 | 35% | 2 sp 6 cp |

Strikers below +5 can't ring the bell at all, and the bookie won't take the bet. **If the striker has advantage** (such as a raging barbarian), the bookie reprices the bet as if their chance were higher.

## Dice Examples

**Thokk** (NPC, Athletics +5) pays 1 sp. Mirela bets 1 sp that he'll ring the bell, at 18 sp back.
- Strike: **18** + 5 = **23**. **A free pint.** But no bell. Mirela loses her bet.

Thokk pays for another strike. Nobody bets this time.
- Strike: **20** + 5 = **25**. **The bell rings!** Thokk's name goes on the board. (If Mirela had bet again, she'd have collected 18 sp.)

**Edge case: a striker who can't ring**
- Mirela (Athletics +0) strikes: even a natural 20 gives her 20. She can win a pint, but she can never ring the bell.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Cost:** 1 sp per strike.
- **Prizes:** a tin pin, a free pint, or your name on the board.
- **Bookie bets:** priced with a 10% House edge.
- **The House keeps:** the strike fees and the bookie's edge.

## Odds

| Athletics | Chance of 15+ (a pin or better) | Chance of 20+ (a pint or better) | Chance to ring the bell (25+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| +0 | 30% | 5% | 0% |
| +5 | 55% | 30% | 5% |
| +7 | 65% | 40% | 15% |
| +9 | 75% | 50% | 25% |

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Rage and other features that don't use magic (rage gives advantage on Strength checks).
- Spitting on your hands, cracking your knuckles and posing for the crowd.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *enhance ability* (Bull's Strength), *enlarge/reduce*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- A weighted mallet, or a friend jerking the puck rope.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weighted mallet | Striker | Dex (Sleight of Hand) to swap it in | +2 to the strike |
| Yank the puck rope from behind the tower | Anyone | Dex (Stealth) | +5 to one strike |
| Hidden spell (*enlarge/reduce*, *enhance ability*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally (advantage on the strike) |

**Cheating on a strike someone bet on** is fixing a bet, as well as cheating at the game. See [[Proposition Betting]].

### Cheating dice example

Mirela bets 1 sp on Thokk, then Quill sneaks behind the tower to yank the puck rope.
- Quill: Stealth **14** + 7 = **21**. The Watcher: **13** + 5 = **18**. Not caught.
- Thokk's strike: **16** + 5 = 21, plus 5 from the rope = **26**. **The bell rings.** Mirela collects 18 sp.
- But the bookie is watching his money: Perception **17** + 3 = **20**. Not enough to beat Quill's 21. He pays, scowling.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The board** of names goes back sixty years. One name at the top has rung the bell so hard it cracked, and the House has never replaced it.
- **The bookie, Fennick,** pays well on bets against the strongest strikers. He's very interested in anyone who keeps losing on purpose.
