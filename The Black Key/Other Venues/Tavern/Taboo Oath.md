# Taboo Oath

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Everyone at the table wears a card pinned to their hat. Yours says three words you mustn't say. You can't see it. Everyone else can, and they're trying very hard to get you talking about gold, or swords, or your mother.*

**Description:** A weekly conversation game. Each player wears a card with **three forbidden words**. Everyone can read everyone else's card, but not their own. Over five rounds of conversation, players try to **bait** each other into saying their words. **Every slip costs a penalty token and a drink.** The fewest tokens wins.

## How to Play

### Setting up

- The House hands out a paper hat to each player. Each player draws a face-down card with **three common words** (such as "gold", "sword", "ale"). The player on their left pins it to their hat, so they never see it.
- Out of game, the DM shows each card's words to everyone except the person wearing it.
- Everyone puts **5 sp** in the pot.
- Everyone rolls a d20. The highest goes first each round, then play goes left.

### Each round, step by step

The table chats for about a minute. Then, in turn, each player may make **one bait**:

1. **Pick a target** and steer the conversation towards one of their words.
2. **Roll:** the baiter rolls **Charisma (Deception)**. The target rolls **Wisdom (Insight)**.
   - **The baiter's total is higher:** the target **says the word**. They take **1 penalty token** and **drink** one mug of ale, paid for by the House: one Constitution save, DC 10, on the Drunk Track.
   - **The target's total is equal or higher:** they dodge it. (**Ties go to the target.**)

**A word only catches you once.** Now you know it, so nobody can bait you with it again. Once all three of your words have caught you, nobody can bait you at all.

### Slipping for real

If a real player says one of their own forbidden words aloud at the table during the five rounds, in or out of character, they take a penalty token and drink, with no roll. This counts even for a word that has already caught them. NPCs only slip on a bait.

### Winning

- After **five rounds**, the player with the **fewest tokens** wins the pot.
- **Ties:** the tied players share the pot.
- A player who goes **Out** on the Drunk Track is done for the night and can't win. If only one player is left, they win the pot.

### Drunk Track

![[Drunk Track#Rules]]

**Being Drunk hurts here:** a Drunk player has disadvantage on their Insight and Deception checks, so they slip more and catch fewer people.

## Dice Examples

**Players:** Mirela (PC, Deception +7, Insight +2, Con save +1), Quill (PC, Deception +5, Insight +2, Con save +1) and Thokk (NPC, Deception −1, Insight +1, Con save +5 with advantage).

**Round 1**
- **Mirela baits Thokk** towards "hammer": Deception **11** + 7 = **18**. Thokk: Insight **14** + 1 = **15**. Thokk says it. **1 token.** He drinks: **3** and **15**, keeps 15: 15 + 5 = **20**. Pass.
- **Quill baits Mirela** towards "gold": Deception **9** + 5 = **14**. Mirela: Insight **12** + 2 = **14**. A tie goes to the target. **Mirela dodges it.**
- **Thokk baits Quill** towards "knife": Deception **16** − 1 = **15**. Quill: Insight **8** + 2 = **10**. Quill says it. **1 token.** He drinks: **6** + 1 = **7**. Fail: **Quill is Tipsy.**

**After five rounds:** Mirela 1 token, Quill 2, Thokk 3. **Mirela wins the pot.**

**Edge case: slipping for real**
- In round 3, Quill's player says "...so I cut it with my knife" while describing what Quill does. That's Quill's word: **1 token and a drink, no roll.** It counts even though "knife" already caught him in round 1.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Pot:** 5 sp per player. The winner takes 90%.
- **The House keeps:** 10% of the pot, which pays for the drinks.

## Odds

**Chance a bait works** (ties go to the target):

| Baiter's Deception vs target's Insight | Chance |
|---|---|
| 4 lower | 30% |
| 2 lower | 38% |
| Equal | 47.5% |
| 2 higher | 57% |
| 4 higher | 66% |

**Bards and rogues** are dangerous at this table. **Drunk players** become easy targets.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any conversation topic, any trick, any leading question.
- Talking about the forbidden words of other players (just not your own).
- Dwarven Resilience on the drink saves.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *friends*, *charm person*, *suggestion*, *detect thoughts* or Bardic Inspiration from someone else.
- Looking at your own card (in a mirror, a window or a polished spoon), or a friend signalling your words to you.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Read your own card in a polished spoon, a mirror or a window | Player | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The DM tells you your three words. Advantage on every Insight check. |
| Signal a friend's words to them | Anyone at the table | Cha (Deception) | The friend has advantage on Insight for one round |
| Hidden spell: *friends*, *charm person* or *suggestion* on a target | A caster at the table | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | *Friends*: advantage on your bait against that target, and they know afterwards. *Charm person*: the target makes a Wisdom save against your spell save DC. On a failure, advantage on your bait against them, and they know afterwards. *Suggestion*: the target makes a Wisdom save against your spell save DC. On a failure, one bait succeeds automatically. |
| Hidden spell: *detect thoughts* on someone within 30 ft who is looking at your card | A caster at the table | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The DM tells you your three words. Advantage on every Insight check. |
| Bardic Inspiration, passed off as a friendly word | A bard within 60 ft | Cha (Deception) to pass it off as table chat | The player adds the die to one bait or Insight check |

### Cheating dice example

Thokk tilts his spoon to read his own card in it.
- Thokk: Sleight of Hand **13** − 1 = **12**. The Watcher: **4** + 5 = **9**. Not caught.
- Mirela is watching his face: Perception **15** + 3 = **18**. She beats 12 and **sees it**. She says nothing, for now. A dwarf who owes you a secret is worth more than 5 sp.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The card deck** was printed by a local scribe. One card in it has three words that aren't common at all: a name, a place and a date.
- **A diplomat** plays every week to practise. She's never taken a token. She's never baited anyone either. She just listens.
