# Coaster Tower

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Stack the beer mats on the edge of the table, flick them up with the back of your hand, and snatch the whole stack out of the air before it scatters. Two is easy. Ten is hard. Twenty is a legend.*

**Description:** A weekly flicking contest with beer coasters. You start with a stack of **2 coasters** on the table edge, flick them up and catch them. Every catch adds **2 more coasters** to the stack. Miss and your run is over. The **biggest stack caught** wins the pot.

## How to Play

### Your run, step by step

1. **Start with a stack of 2.**
2. **Flick and catch:** make a **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check at **DC 6 + the number of coasters** in the stack.

   | Stack | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | DC | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 18 | 20 | 22 |

   - **Success:** you caught it. Add 2 coasters and go again.
   - **Failure:** coasters everywhere. Your run is over.
3. **Your score** is the biggest stack you caught. (Failing your first flick scores 0.)

### Winning

- Everyone gets **one run**. The highest score wins the pot.
- **Ties:** the tied flickers each try the **next stack up**. A catch beats a miss. If they all catch, or all miss, go up again (or try again) until one is left.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any flicking technique: back of the hand, palm-up, or the dwarven elbow flick.
- Using the House's coasters (stiff, dry, standard size).

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *mage hand*, *guidance* or Bardic Inspiration.
- Sticky coasters, damp coasters slipped into a rival's stack, or a bumped table.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Floor Watcher's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Watcher.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Watcher | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tacky resin on your stack | Flicker | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on every catch this run |
| Damp coaster in a rival's stack | Anyone | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's next catch has disadvantage |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*, *mage hand*) | A caster nearby | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Before the tiebreak, Mirela slips a damp coaster into Quill's stack.
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **17** + 2 = **19**. The Watcher: **9** + 5 = **14**. Not caught.
- Quill rolls with disadvantage: **11** and **4**, keeps 4: 4 + 7 = **11**. ✗ Mirela tries her own stack 10: **14** + 2 = **16**. ✓ **Mirela wins.**

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The record stack** of 20 has stood for eleven years. The halfling who set it is now the city's most notorious pickpocket.
- **A brewer** is handing out free coasters printed with a map. Each week's coasters show a different piece.
