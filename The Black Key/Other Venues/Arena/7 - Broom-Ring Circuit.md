# Broom-Ring Circuit

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Five glowing hoops hang in the sky, each smaller and higher than the last. The riders kick off one at a time on speed-limited training brooms, weaving through the rings while the crowd counts the seconds.*

**Description:** A monthly flying race through **five rings**, each harder than the last. Riders fly one at a time on **speed-limited training brooms**. Missing a ring doesn't crash you; you just loop back, and it costs **5 seconds**. **Fastest time wins.**

*Flying isn't allowed in the city, so this game doesn't fit the arena. It's kept here for a future venue outside the walls.*

## How to Play

### The rings

| Ring | DC |
|---|---|
| 1 | 12 |
| 2 | 14 |
| 3 | 16 |
| 4 | 18 |
| 5 | 20 |

### A run, step by step

1. **Every run starts at 60 seconds.**
2. **At each ring**, make a **Dexterity check**. Add your proficiency bonus if you're proficient with **air vehicles**.
   - **Success:** clean through.
   - **Failure:** you miss and loop back through it. **Add 5 seconds.** No crash, no damage.
3. **Fastest total time wins.** If times are tied, the tied riders make a Dexterity roll-off.

The brooms are **speed-limited training brooms**. No spells and no magic items of your own.

## Dice Examples

**Mirela** (PC, Dex +2, no air vehicle proficiency).

| Ring | DC | Roll | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 12 | **13** + 2 = 15 | ✓ |
| 2 | 14 | **9** + 2 = 11 | ✗ +5 s |
| 3 | 16 | **16** + 2 = 18 | ✓ |
| 4 | 18 | **15** + 2 = 17 | ✗ +5 s |
| 5 | 20 | **18** + 2 = 20 | ✓ (meets the DC) |

**Mirela's time:** 60 + 5 + 5 = **70 seconds.**

**Edge case:** a perfect run (all five rings clean) is **60 seconds flat**. It almost never happens.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Any line through the course, as long as you go through the rings in order.
- Practice runs on the day (paid separately).

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic of your own** (no *haste*, no *feather fall*).
- A broom with its speed-limiter removed, or a bribed ring-keeper.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Remove your broom's speed-limiter | Rider, before the run | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Your time starts at 55 seconds |
| Bribe a ring-keeper to widen a ring (2 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | That ring's DC drops by 4 for you |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Caught cheating: penalties

The Broom-Ring Circuit is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Quill tries to pry the speed-limiter off his broom.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **6** + 7 = **13**. The Steward: **10** + 5 = **15**. **Caught.**
- Quill is disqualified. He's banned from the circuit for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 10 gp, 1 day serving (sweeping, with a regular broom), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The training brooms** were bought cheap from a wizards' academy that closed suddenly. One of them still answers to its old owner's whistle.
- **The ring-keeper** stands on a tower all day adjusting the rings. She's seen something big flying past at night, well above the course.
