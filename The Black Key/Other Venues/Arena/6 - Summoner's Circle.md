# Summoner's Circle

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Runes flare on the pit floor, and two spirits rise out of the summoning circles: a hulking brute and a shield-bearing guardian. Their pilots stand at the rail above, guiding them with a thought. When one spirit falls, its pilot can call up another.*

**Description:** A monthly battle of summoned spirits. The House's circle conjures **six standard spirits**, and each pilot commands one from the rail. Each spirit **beats two others and loses to two**, like rock-paper-scissors. **Win three exchanges to win the bout.** Lose an exchange, and you may **swap spirits**.

## How to Play

### The spirits

| Spirit | Beats | Loses to |
|---|---|---|
| **Brute** | Guardian, Healer | Archer, Trickster |
| **Guardian** | Archer, Skirmisher | Brute, Trickster |
| **Archer** | Brute, Healer | Guardian, Skirmisher |
| **Skirmisher** | Archer, Trickster | Guardian, Healer |
| **Trickster** | Brute, Guardian | Skirmisher, Healer |
| **Healer** | Skirmisher, Trickster | Brute, Archer |

**Any other pairing is even.** For example, Brute against Skirmisher.

### A bout, step by step

1. **Both pilots choose a spirit in secret**, then reveal.
2. **An exchange:** each pilot rolls **Intelligence (Arcana)**. **The spirit that beats the other adds +3.** **Ties are rerolled.** The higher total wins the exchange.
3. **The loser of the exchange may swap** to a different spirit before the next one. The winner keeps theirs.
4. **First to win 3 exchanges wins the bout.**

**Pilots don't have to be spellcasters.** The House circle does the summoning. No spells and no magic items.

**Netted curtain:** up.

## Dice Examples

**Orrin Vex** (NPC wizard, Arcana +5) against **Quill** (PC, Arcana +2).

Orrin picks the **Guardian**, and Quill picks the **Brute**. The Brute beats the Guardian, so **Quill adds +3**.

| Exchange | Quill | Orrin | Score | Swap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brute: **12** + 2 + 3 = 17 | Guardian: **13** + 5 = 18 | Orrin 1–0 | Quill keeps the Brute (it still has the edge) |
| 2 | Brute: **15** + 2 + 3 = 20 | Guardian: **9** + 5 = 14 | 1–1 | Orrin swaps to the **Archer** (it beats the Brute) |
| 3 | Brute: **10** + 2 = 12 | Archer: **8** + 5 + 3 = 16 | Orrin 2–1 | Quill swaps to the **Skirmisher** (it beats the Archer) |
| 4 | Skirmisher: **14** + 2 + 3 = 19 | Archer: **11** + 5 = 16 | 2–2 | Orrin swaps to the **Guardian** (it beats the Skirmisher) |
| 5 | Skirmisher: **13** + 2 = 15 | Guardian: **7** + 5 + 3 = 15. **Tie, reroll:** **16** + 2 = 18 vs **12** + 5 + 3 = 20 | **Orrin wins, 3–2** | |

**The pattern:** whoever just lost can usually swap to a spirit with the edge. Arcana decides the close ones.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Picking any spirit, and keeping your pick secret.
- Choosing whether to swap after a loss.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- A spotter who signals the rival's pick, or a tampered summoning circle.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| A spotter reading the rival's pick | A friend near the rival | Dex (Sleight of Hand) for hand signals | You see the rival's first pick before choosing yours |
| Scratch the rival's circle runes | Anyone before the bout | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival's spirit loses ties instead of rerolling |
| Hidden spell (*guidance*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Mirela stands behind Orrin and signals his first pick to Quill.
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **9** + 2 = **11**. The Steward: **3** + 5 = **8**. Not caught.
- Quill sees the Guardian coming and picks the Brute (or the Trickster). Either way, he starts with the +3 edge.
