# Clockwork Bull

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A brass bull with glowing eyes sits on a turntable in the pit, steam hissing from its nostrils. Climb on, grab the horn, and hold on. Five bucks, each one meaner than the last.*

**Description:** The arena's daily ride. Pay, climb onto the **Clockwork Bull** and try to stay on through **five buck patterns**, each harder than the last. **Your first failure throws you onto the cushions.** Last all five and you win a gold piece and your name on the board.

## How to Play

### A ride, step by step

1. **Pay 2 sp** and climb on.
2. **For each buck**, make a **Strength (Athletics)** or **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check (your choice, each time).

| Buck | DC |
|---|---|
| 1. The Rock | 10 |
| 2. The Spin | 12 |
| 3. The Kick | 15 |
| 4. The Twister | 18 |
| 5. The Widowmaker | 20 |

3. **Your first failure throws you off.** Your score is the number of bucks you lasted.

**You land on thick cushions. No damage.** No spells and no magic items.

- Buck 1: **7** + 5 = 12 ✓
- Buck 2: **9** + 5 = 14 ✓
- Buck 3: **13** + 5 = 18 ✓
- Buck 4 (DC 18): **15** + 5 = 20 ✓
- Buck 5 (DC 20): **16** + 5 = 21 ✓. **All five! Quill wins 1 gp and his name goes on the board.**

## Prizes & Stakes

- **A ride:** 2 sp.
- **Last 4 bucks:** your 2 sp back.
- **Last all 5:** 1 gp and your name on the board.
- **The House keeps:** everything else. (Most riders lose their 2 sp.)

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Switching between Athletics and Acrobatics on every buck.
- Riding again (pay again).

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- A rope looped under the bull's belly, or a bribed operator.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| A hidden leg loop | Rider | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on every buck |
| Bribe the operator to go easy (5 sp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Bucks 4 and 5 drop to DC 15 |
| Hidden spell (*enhance ability*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Mirela bribes the operator to go easy on her.
- Mirela: Persuasion **15** + 5 = **20**. The Steward: **8** + 5 = **13**. Not caught.
- Bucks 4 and 5 are now DC 15. With Acrobatics +2, she still needs a 13 or more on each.
