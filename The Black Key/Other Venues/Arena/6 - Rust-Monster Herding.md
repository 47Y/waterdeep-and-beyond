# Rust-Monster Herding

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *Two muzzled rust monsters snuffle around the pit, antennae twitching. Their handlers wave copper bait-sticks and try to steer them through coloured gates. Everyone in the front row has taken off their rings.*

**Description:** A weekly herding match. Two handlers each guide a **muzzled, trained rust monster** through **three coloured gates**. At each gate, the handlers go head to head with **Animal Handling**. **Win two gates out of three to win the match.**

## How to Play

### The rules of the pen

- **No metal in the pit.** Handlers wear leather and use **wooden poles** with standard copper bait.
- **The rust monsters are muzzled** and trained. They never attack.

### A match, step by step

1. **At each gate**, both handlers make a **Wisdom (Animal Handling)** check. **Ties are rerolled.**
2. **The higher total** gets their rust monster through that gate first and **wins the gate.**
3. **Best of three.** The first handler to win **two gates** wins the match.

No spells and no magic items.

**Netted curtain:** up (to keep the crowd's buckles away from curious antennae).

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Clicking, whistling and talking to your rust monster.
- Standing in your rival's path. (No touching.)

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic** (no *speak with animals*, no *animal friendship*).
- Hidden metal bait, or a scent smeared on the rival's pole.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| An iron nail in your glove (better bait) | Handler | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on every gate |
| Skunk oil on the rival's pole | Anyone before the match | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | The rival has disadvantage at the first gate |
| Hidden spell (*speak with animals*) | A caster in the crowd | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | The spell works normally |

### Cheating dice example

Mirela hides an iron nail in her glove.
- Mirela: Sleight of Hand **12** + 2 = **14**. The Steward: **14** + 5 = **19**. **Caught.** The rust monster finds it first, and her glove crumbles to rust in front of everyone.
- Mirela is disqualified. She's banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear her name: 5 gp, 1 day serving (mucking out the rust monster pens), or wait 3 months.
