# Casino Tables

Shared casino rules. Each casino game embeds the sections it needs, so a change here updates every game.

## Tables

Every casino game is offered at three tables. The rules and odds are the same at every table; only the size of the bets changes.

| Table | Minimum bet | Maximum bet | Who sits here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silver Table | 1 sp | 5 gp | Locals, laborers, new adventurers |
| Gold Table | 1 gp | 100 gp | Adventuring parties, merchants |
| Platinum Salon | 1 pp | No limit | Nobles, guildmasters, high-level adventurers |

## Reading Payouts

- **"2:1" (two to one)** means you win 2 for every 1 you bet, **and you keep your bet**. Bet 5 gp at 2:1 and you get back 15 gp (10 gp won + your 5 gp).
- **"3:2"** means you win 3 for every 2 you bet. Bet 2 gp, win 3 gp, and keep your 2 gp.
- **"Push"** means a tie: your bet comes back and nobody wins.
- **House edge** is the share of every bet the House keeps on average over many games. A 5% edge means that for every 100 gp bet, players get back about 95 gp in the long run.
- **Rake** is how the House earns from games where players bet against each other: it takes a small cut of each pot (usually 5%) before the winner collects.

## Floor Watcher

Every casino table is watched by a **Floor Watcher** with **Wisdom (Perception) +5**. Cheat attempts are contests against the Watcher, and ties go to the Watcher.
