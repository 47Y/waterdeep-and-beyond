# Cheating Penalties

The shared cheating rules for every game. Each game note embeds the sections that apply to it, so a change here updates every game.

**Who you cheated decides which section applies.** Cheat the House at a casino game and it's handled privately. Cheat other people at a public event and it's handled in the open.

Each game note keeps its own details: what counts as cheating, its cheat table, its fine and days table, and its ban length.

## Spotting a Cheat

Anyone watching (a PC, an NPC or a rival) can roll **Wisdom (Perception)** against the cheater's roll total. If they beat it, they saw the cheat. What they do next is up to them:
- **Report it** to the watcher on duty (the Floor Watcher in the tavern and casino, the Track Steward at every arena event). The cheater is caught and the penalties apply.
- **Demand a bribe** to keep quiet.
- **Say nothing** and keep the secret for later.

**Bribing staff:** anyone can try it, and the briber faces the same penalties as any other cheat if caught. If the House finds out an employee took a bribe, that employee is fired.

## Casino

Cheating **the House** is handled **behind closed doors**. The House never admits its games can be beaten.

### Always

1. **Quiet confiscation:** all your chips and that night's winnings are taken, and you're escorted out the back.
2. **The House's private file:** your portrait and notes on how you cheated are filed in the back office. From now on, Watchers have **advantage** to spot you.
3. **Quiet blacklist:** copies of your portrait go to every other casino and gambling den in the city.
4. **Banned** from the casino floor for one month.

### Then the House chooses one

- **Debt to the House:** you owe **five times the bet you were caught on**, but never more than the table's cap. A polite collector visits every week until it's paid.

  | Table | Debt cap |
  |---|---|
  | Silver Table | 25 gp |
  | Gold Table | 250 gp |
  | Platinum Salon | 1,000 gp |

  *Example:* a 5 gp bet means 25 gp of debt. A 200 gp bet at the Gold Table would be 1,000 gp, so it's capped at 250 gp.
- **A favour owed:** one job for the House, such as spying on a rival, recovering an item or delivering a message. This can be taken **at any time**, even partway through paying off a debt, and it wipes whatever is left.

### No clearing your name

The House always remembers. The file and the blacklist are permanent.

## Public Events

Regular events (daily, weekly or monthly) where you cheated **other people**, such as rival contestants or spectators who bet. Handled **in the open**.

### Always

1. **Disqualified:** your entry fee is forfeited, and bets on you lose.
2. **Banned** from the event for **3 months**, unless the game sets a different length.
3. **Notice board:** your portrait is pinned to the event's notice board with your name and what you did.

### Clearing your name

Clearing your name takes your portrait off the notice board. The ban still stands. Choose one:
- **Pay the fine:** ten times the entry fee.
- **Serve the event:** 1 day of work at the event (scooping stalls, sweeping, pouring) for every 10 gp of the fine (minimum 1 day).
- **Wait it out:** 3 months.

Games can set their own fine and days. If they do, the game's table wins.

## Special Public Events

Yearly events such as King of the Drunks and the Grand Derby, where you cheated **other people**. Handled **in the open, loudly**.

### Always

1. **Disqualified:** your entry fee is forfeited, and bets on you lose.
2. **Banned** from next year's event.
3. **The Cheater's Wall:** your portrait hangs in the hall with your name and what you did.
4. **Town crier:** your name and offence are read out in the city square.
5. **Known cheat:** food and drink cost double in every tavern in the city.

### Clearing your name

Clearing your name takes you off the Cheater's Wall and ends Known Cheat. The ban still stands. Choose one:
- **Pay the fine:** ten times the entry fee.
- **The stocks:** 1 day for every 10 gp of the fine. You are jeered at but take no damage.
- **Serve the event:** 1 day of work at the event for every 10 gp of the fine. A Grand Derby cheat could spend weeks mucking out stalls.
- **Wait it out:** one full year.

Games can set their own fine and days. If they do, the game's table wins.
