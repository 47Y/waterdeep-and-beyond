# Colossus Climb

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A giant of oak and iron stands forty feet tall in the pit, its head level with the second-floor balconies. It sways, it shrugs, it swings its arms. At the top, a bell hangs from its crown. Ring it, and the whole building hears.*

**Description:** A weekly climbing race up the **Colossus**, a moving construct 40 ft tall. Climbers go one at a time through **four stages** to ring the **crown bell**. There's **no harness and no padding**: fail a stage and you **fall to the sand** and take **real fall damage**. Then climb again, or quit. **Fastest bell wins.**

## How to Play

### The stages

Each stage is 10 ft higher than the last. A fall is normal fall damage: **1d6 bludgeoning per 10 ft**.

| Stage | Check | DC | Fall |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The legs | Strength (Athletics) | 12 | 10 ft: **1d6** |
| 2. The swinging arm | Dexterity (Acrobatics) | 15 | The arm hits you for **1d10** bludgeoning, then 20 ft: **2d6** |
| 3. Find the handhold on the shoulder | Wisdom (Perception) | 17 | 30 ft: **3d6** |
| 4. The head | Strength (Athletics) | 18 | 40 ft: **4d6** |

### A climb, step by step

1. **The clock starts.** Every check takes **30 seconds**, pass or fail.
2. **Make each stage's check**, in order.
   - **Success:** move on to the next stage.
   - **Failure:** you **fall to the sand** and take that stage's damage.
3. **After a fall, choose:** **climb again** or **quit**.
   - **Climb again:** you climb back up the stages you've already cleared **without rolling** (you know the route now), at **30 seconds per stage**. Then try the stage you fell from again.
   - **Quit:** your climb is over. Your result is the highest stage you cleared.
4. **Drop to 0 HP** and your climb is over. You're stable (see the Arena Ward below).
5. **Ring the bell** when you clear stage 4. Your time stops.

### Winning

- **Any climber who rings the bell beats every climber who doesn't.** Fastest bell wins.
- **If nobody rings it:** the highest stage cleared wins. Ties go to the faster time.
- **Tied times:** both climbers share the purse.

No spells and no magic items.

## Dice Examples

**Quill** (PC, HP 21, Athletics +0, Acrobatics +5, Perception +6).

| Step | Roll | Result | Clock | HP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Legs (DC 12) | **14** + 0 = 14 | ✓ | 0:30 | 21 |
| 2. Arm (DC 15) | **8** + 5 = 13 | ✗ The arm hits: **6**. He falls 20 ft: **3** + **4** = 7. **13 damage.** | 1:00 | 8 |
| He climbs again | Back up the legs, no roll | | 1:30 | 8 |
| 2. Arm (DC 15) | **12** + 5 = 17 | ✓ | 2:00 | 8 |
| 3. Shoulder (DC 17) | **13** + 6 = 19 | ✓ | 2:30 | 8 |
| 4. Head (DC 18) | **19** + 0 = 19 | ✓ **He rings the bell!** | **3:00** | 8 |

**Quill's time: 3 minutes.**

**Edge case: a fall from the head.** If Quill had rolled **9** on the head (9 + 0 = 9, a fail), he'd fall 40 ft: **2** + **5** + **3** + **4** = **14** damage. He has 8 HP, so he drops to **0 HP**. He's stable, and his climb is over. His result: **stage 3 cleared, no bell**, at 3:00.

**Different strengths:** Quill's weak Athletics makes the head his danger. Thokk (Athletics +5, Acrobatics −1, Perception +1) would usually clear the head, but he'd risk the arm, and the arm hurts most.

## Odds

If a climber has the same bonus in all three skills, and never quits:

| Bonus | +2 | +5 | +7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A perfect climb (no falls) | 1.7% | 6.9% | 14.3% |
| Rings the bell with **20 HP** | 9% | 26% | 43% |
| Rings the bell with **40 HP** | 22% | 52% | 71% |
| Rings the bell with **80 HP** | 53% | 84% | 95% |
| Average falls before the bell | 7.6 | 4.0 | 2.6 |
| Average damage before the bell | 88 | 45 | 30 |
| Average time to the bell | 13 min | 8 min | 6 min |

**The arm and the head do the damage.** A fall from the arm averages 12.5, and from the head 14. Climbers who are hurt have to decide whether one more try is worth it.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Watching other climbers to learn the Colossus's moves.
- Chalk for your hands.
- Quitting at any time after a fall.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic** (no *spider climb*, no *jump*, no *feather fall*).
- Bribing the operator to slow the Colossus, or hidden climbing claws.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hidden climbing claws | Climber | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on both Athletics stages |
| Bribe the operator to hold still (2 gp) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | The arm stage DC drops to 12 |
| Hidden spell (*spider climb*), by touch before the climb: on yourself, or a friend's pat on the back | The climber or a friend | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on both Athletics stages |

Colossus Climb is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

### Cheating dice example

Quill straps climbing claws under his sleeves.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **14** + 7 = **21**. The Steward: **16** + 5 = **21**. **A tie goes to the Steward: caught.**
- Quill is pulled off the Colossus before he starts. He's banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 5 gp, 1 day serving (oiling the Colossus's joints), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The Colossus's operator** works from a cabin in its chest. He says it sometimes moves when he hasn't touched the levers.
- **The crown bell** was cast from a real temple bell. The temple wants it back.
