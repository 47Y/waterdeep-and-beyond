# Beast Clash

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *The gates on either side of the pit grind open. A grey wolf slinks out of one; a brown bear lumbers out of the other and rears up, roaring. The handlers shout from the rim, and the crowd's money is already down.*

**Description:** A weekly fight between two creatures, one from each gate. Creatures come from the House menagerie, or owners can enter their own. Every fight is settled with **Clash**, so the DM can run a whole card in minutes. Each owner may **urge** their creature once per fight.

## How to Play

### A fight, step by step

1. **Grade each creature** by its CR, using [[Arena Combat Rules#Clash]].
2. **The bookie posts prices** from the Clash odds (see [[Proposition Betting]]).
3. **Fight:** run Clash exchanges. The first creature to win **3 exchanges** wins.
4. **Urge (once per fight, optional):** before any exchange, an owner may make a **Wisdom (Animal Handling)** check, **DC 15**.
   - **Success:** the creature has **advantage** on that exchange.
   - **Failure:** it has **disadvantage** on that exchange.

![[Arena Combat Rules#Arena Ward]]

**Netted curtain:** up.

## Dice Examples

**Gate 1:** a wolf (Green, +2), owned by Mirela (PC, Animal Handling +3).
**Gate 2:** a brown bear (Pit regular, +4), from the House menagerie.

**The bookie:** the bear (68%) pays **1 gp 2 sp** per 1 gp. The wolf (32%) pays **2 gp 7 sp** per 1 gp.

| Exchange | Wolf | Bear | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **11** + 2 = 13 | **15** + 4 = 19 | Bear 1–0 |
| 2 | Mirela urges: **14** + 3 = 17. Success: advantage. **6** and **18**, keeps 18: 18 + 2 = 20 | **12** + 4 = 16 | 1–1 |
| 3 | **9** + 2 = 11 | **9** + 4 = 13 | Bear 2–1 |
| 4 | **17** + 2 = 19 | **10** + 4 = 14 | 2–2 |
| 5 | **8** + 2 = 10 | **13** + 4 = 17 | **The bear wins, 3–2** |

The wolf drops, and the healers carry it to the kennels.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 1 gp per creature.
- **Purse:** 5 gp to the winning owner.
- **The House keeps:** the entries, the betting cut and the seats.

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Entering any creature that the House approves. No undead, no fiends, and nothing that flies.
- One urge per fight.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic**, such as *enhance ability*, *bless* or *animal friendship* on a rival creature.
- Doping your creature, drugging a rival's feed, or bribing the kennel hands.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dope your creature | Owner | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | +2 Clash bonus this fight |
| Drug a rival's feed | Anyone with kennel access | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | −2 to the rival's Clash bonus this fight |
| Hidden spell (*enhance ability*), by touch at the gate before the fight | The owner, or anyone with kennel access | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | Advantage on one exchange |

### Caught cheating: penalties

Beast Clash is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught. A doped creature is disqualified.

![[Cheating Penalties#Public Events]]

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 10 gp | 1 day |

### Cheating dice example

Before the fight, Quill slips into the kennels to drug the bear's feed.
- Quill: Sleight of Hand **18** + 7 = **25**. The Steward: **14** + 5 = **19**. Not caught.
- The bear fights at +2, the same as the wolf. It's now an even fight.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **The menagerie keeper, Old Haskel,** cries every time one of his beasts loses. He's been quietly selling the losers to someone outside the city.
- **A noble** wants to enter a "very large dog." It is not a dog.
