# Last Ring

> **Pitch (read aloud):** *A white circle, thirty feet across, painted on the sand. A dozen fighters stand around its edge, facing in. When the horn blows, there are no teams and no friends, and the last one standing inside the line takes the pot.*

**Description:** A monthly free-for-all. Every fighter starts on the edge of a **30-ft circle**. You're out if you're **pushed out of the circle** or drop to **0 HP**. The last fighter left inside wins half of all the entries.

## How to Play

### The rules of the ring

- **Weapons:** unarmed strikes, or House wooden weapons: **club** (1d4) or **staff** (1d6).
- **No armour, no shields, no spells and no magic items.**

### A fight, step by step

1. **Every fighter starts on the edge** of the circle, spaced evenly, facing in.
2. **Roll initiative** and fight with normal combat rules.
3. **You're out when:**
   - **Any part of you touches the sand outside the line.** Being **shoved** is the usual way: use the Attack action to make a **Strength (Athletics)** check against the target's **Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics)** (the target chooses). If you win, the target is pushed **5 ft**. **Ties go to the target.**
   - **You drop to 0 HP.**
   - **You step out** to yield.
4. **The last fighter inside the circle wins.**

![[Arena Combat Rules#Arena Ward]]

**Netted curtain:** down.

### The quick NPC version

When no PC is in the ring, the DM can settle it in a minute:
1. **Grade each fighter** with [[Arena Combat Rules#Clash]].
2. **Pair them off at random.** Each pair rolls a **single Clash exchange** (d20 + bonus; ties are rerolled). **The loser is out.** If there's an odd fighter, they sit this round out.
3. **Repeat** until one fighter is left.

## Dice Examples

**A PC moment:** Grit (NPC thug, Athletics +2) is 5 ft from the line. Thokk (PC, Athletics +5) shoves him.
- Thokk: **13** + 5 = **18**. Grit: **9** + 2 = **11**. Grit is pushed 5 ft, over the line. **Grit is out.**

**Edge case: resisting with Acrobatics.** Sela (NPC, Athletics +3) shoves Quill (PC, Acrobatics +5). Quill chooses Acrobatics.
- Sela: **11** + 3 = **14**. Quill: **9** + 5 = **14**. **A tie goes to the target**, so Quill stays put.

**The quick NPC version:** Grit (Green, +2), a dockhand (Green, +2), Kord (Pit regular, +4) and Sela (Veteran, +6).

| Round | Pair | Rolls | Out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grit vs the dockhand | **14** + 2 = 16 vs **9** + 2 = 11 | The dockhand |
| 1 | Kord vs Sela | **12** + 4 = 16 vs **10** + 6 = 16. Tie, reroll: **8** + 4 = 12 vs **15** + 6 = 21 | Kord |
| 2 | Grit vs Sela | **18** + 2 = 20 vs **13** + 6 = 19 | **Sela: Grit wins!** |

A single exchange is much swingier than a full Clash, so upsets like this are common.

## Prizes & Stakes

- **Entry:** 2 gp per fighter.
- **Prize:** the winner takes **half of all the entries**. With 10 fighters, that's 10 gp.
- **The House keeps:** the other half, the betting cut and the seats.

![[Spectator Betting#Betting Pool]]

## Odds

- **PC fights:** with equal fighters, each has about a **1 in N** chance (N = the number of fighters). A strong shover does better.
- **The quick NPC version:** a single exchange (ties rerolled) goes to the stronger fighter:

| Bonus difference | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The stronger fighter wins the exchange | 50% | 55% | 60% | 64.5% | 69% |

## Rulings & Cheating

### Fair Play: what's allowed

- Making and breaking alliances during the fight. (They don't last; there's only one winner.)
- Shoving, grappling and dragging rivals toward the line.

### What counts as cheating

- **Any spell or magic.**
- Greasing the sand near your own start point, or paying other fighters to gang up on someone.

### How to cheat (and how you get caught)

Each cheat is one **contest against the Track Steward's Wisdom (Perception) +5**. **Ties go to the Steward.** The party can help, but each person who tries a cheat makes their own roll and risks being caught.

| Cheat | Who can do it | Roll vs the Steward | If it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rough boots (rosin on the soles) | Fighter | Dex (Sleight of Hand) | Advantage on checks to resist being shoved |
| Pay fighters to gang up (2 gp each) | Anyone | Cha (Persuasion) | Each paid NPC attacks your chosen rival first |
| Hidden spell: *bless* from within 30 ft, or *enhance ability* by touch before the horn | A caster in the crowd (*bless*), or the fighter or a friend (*enhance ability*) | Cha (Deception) to disguise the casting. Subtle Spell gives advantage. | *Bless*: +1d4 on attacks and saves for up to 1 minute. *Enhance ability* (Bull's Strength): advantage on your Strength checks to shove or resist a shove. |

Last Ring is a **public event**. Penalties apply to the cheater **and** to any accomplice who is caught.

| Fine | Or days serving the event |
|---|---|
| 20 gp | 2 days |

### Cheating dice example

Thokk rubs rosin on his boot soles before the horn.
- Thokk: Sleight of Hand **12** − 1 = **11**. The Steward: **6** + 5 = **11**. **A tie goes to the Steward: caught.**
- Thokk is disqualified before the fight starts. He's banned from the arena for 3 months and pinned to the notice board. To clear his name: 20 gp, 2 days serving (repainting the ring), or wait 3 months.

## Hooks / NPCs

- **Three fighters** have entered together every month and always take out the strongest fighter first. One of them always wins.
- **The painter** who marks the ring has been making it a little smaller on one side.
