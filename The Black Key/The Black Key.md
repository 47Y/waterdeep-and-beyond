# The Black Key

The Black Key is a tavern, casino, arena, and lodging business created by [[Joe Bauers]] during the six-month downtime. It is near Waterdeep's [[Market]]. Its guest rooms are intended to support the information network Joe began developing.

## Grand Opening

The grand opening followed [[Dick's Investiture as Open Lord]]. Extensive advertising turned the journey from the ceremony into an informal parade.

[[Dick Mixon]] paid Market food vendors to serve everyone for free and paid nearby taverns to close for the day, making The Black Key the only nearby establishment serving alcohol. He received visitors from a second-floor VIP booth.

Opening-day events included:

- [[1 - Sausage Roulette|Sausage Roulette]].
- Pit fighting in the arena.
- [[1 - Beholder's Eye Darts|Beholder's Eye Darts]].
- [[1 - Lizard Races|Lizard Races]].
- [[Bagman's Bet]].
