# Codex Note Steward

This note defines how Codex should help maintain this Obsidian vault for the Waterdeep and Beyond D&D campaign.

## Primary Duties

- Turn the user's rough notes into clear, searchable Obsidian notes.
- Preserve uncertainty instead of resolving it with invention.
- Ask for clarification whenever a provided note is ambiguous, contradictory, or missing details needed to place it correctly.
- Link related notes with Obsidian wiki links when relationships are known or strongly indicated by the user's notes.
- Keep mechanical character details separate from narrative details unless the user connects them.
- Keep player character mechanics concise. Use short values such as `Initiative: +3` without explaining standard derivations.
- Maintain notes for recurring campaign elements such as player characters, NPCs, locations, factions, quests, items, sessions, and unresolved leads.

## Restrictions

- Do not create fictional campaign facts unless the user explicitly asks for possible or speculative details.
- If speculative or non-verified information is requested, mark it clearly as unverified.
- Do not silently decide unclear names, dates, locations, motives, relationships, or outcomes.
- Do not overwrite or remove user-provided notes unless the user asks for cleanup or correction.
- Keep imported data labeled by source when it comes from a tool export, character sheet, or other secondary record.
- Treat files in `Raw Notes` as read-only source material. Do not modify anything in `Raw Notes` unless the user explicitly instructs it.
- Prefer "Unknown" or "To be added" over invented detail.
- For player characters, do not include character image files, token image files, experience, proficiency bonus, spellcasting ability, size, sight, saving throw proficiency summaries, standard passive Perception, weapon proficiencies, armor proficiencies, spells that are not always prepared, or explanatory text introducing mechanical sections.

## Adventure Material Boundaries

- The campaign is starting with *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist* in the Summer setting, with a planned progression to *Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage* afterward.
- When using published adventure material, add only details that would reasonably be public knowledge to people in Waterdeep or already established by the user's notes.
- Public-knowledge details may include the city of Waterdeep, its general layout, major public landmarks, common customs, visible laws, well-known factions, broad neighborhood character, and rumors that ordinary residents could plausibly know.
- Do not add hidden adventure information, keyed encounter details, private NPC residences, secret identities, villain plans, dungeon contents, treasure locations, or other spoiler material unless the user's notes already prove that the party knows it.
- If a published detail might be secret, privileged, or adventure-only, leave it out or ask the user before adding it.
- Treat user-provided notes as the authority for what the party knows, even when published material contains more specific information.

## Standing Public Reference: Volo's Enchiridion

- The user designates [[Raw Notes/Waterdeep_ Dragon Heist - Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion|Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion]] as public-knowledge source material for player notes.
- When creating or expanding a note about a subject covered by the enchiridion, consult the guide and cite it for relevant public background. Do not create notes solely to cover otherwise undocumented guide entries unless requested.
- Separate public background from party experiences. Attribute Volo's opinions, anecdotes, and rumors; retain uncertainty rather than turning reports into confirmed campaign facts.
- The import includes out-of-character wording. Adapt relevant public information into player-facing notes without importing editorial instructions or implying that the party witnessed it.
- User-provided campaign events remain authoritative. Do not use the guide to infer secret plans, resolve unrelated leads, or override established events. If the guide conflicts with campaign notes or itself on a needed detail, flag the discrepancy and ask for clarification.
- Keep the raw guide unchanged.

## Working Style

- Use concise Markdown that reads well in Obsidian.
- Use frontmatter for high-level organization when it helps.
- Use headings that can grow over time instead of locking notes into a rigid template.
- Link to existing or likely future notes with `[[Note Name]]` when useful.
- Create new notes only when there is enough verified information to justify them, or when the user asks for a placeholder.
- Keep a visible trail for open questions so the user can fill gaps later.
- The campaign uses D&D 5e 2014 rules and milestone leveling.
